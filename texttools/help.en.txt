# Text Tools
Text Tools cleans up and rearranges plain text and simple tables: sort lines, change capitals and accents (Greek done right), remove duplicates, trim spaces, number lines, change the separator of a CSV, find and replace, pull out e-mails or links, encode text, compare two texts and translate between Greek and English on your own device. Paste your text, pick a tool and the result appears straight away.

## Getting started
1. Paste or type your text in the `Text` box, or tap or click the folder button to open a file (.txt, .csv, .tsv, .md and similar, up to 5 MB). The clipboard button pastes what you copied.
2. Pick a group at the top (`Lines`, `Letters & accents`, `Clean up`, `Numbers`, `Columns`, `Find`, `Encode`, `Compare`, `Translate`) and then a tool.
3. Set its options. The `Result` box follows as you type.
4. `Copy` the result, `Save` it as a file, or press `Use as text` to move it up into the `Text` box and work on it again.

You can also open a .txt, .csv or .tsv file with Text Tools straight from your computer's `Open with` menu or from `Share` on Android, when orOS is installed. Writer, Notes, Mind Map and Spreadsheet open those files too, so orOS asks which app you want; tick the `Always` box to stop asking. [Settings](app:settings) › `Opening files` changes that choice.

Under the text you see how many characters, words and lines it has. Tap or click that line for more: sentences, paragraphs, reading time and the most used words.

## Tools
- `Lines`: `Sort` (A → Z with Greek and English in the right order, natural order so that 2 comes before 10, by number, by length or at random), `Reverse order`, `Remove duplicates`, `Only duplicates`, `Count lines`, `Remove empty lines`, `One empty line`, `Keep / remove lines` that contain, start with or end with a text, `Join lines` and `Split into lines`.
- `Letters & accents`: capitals, small letters, accents and Greek in Latin letters (see below).
- `Clean up`: `Trim spaces`, `Collapse spaces`, `Tabs ↔ spaces`, `Unwrap lines` (one line per paragraph), `Wrap lines`, `Hidden characters` (invisible characters, odd spaces, curly quotes) and `Line endings` (LF or CRLF for Windows).
- `Numbers`: `Number lines` with your own start, step and style, `Remove numbers / bullets`, and `Prefix / suffix` with ready choices such as bullets or «…».
- `Columns` for CSV-like tables: `Change separator` (comma, semicolon, tab, pipe or your own; quotes are handled), `Pick columns` (for example 3,1 or 2-4, in the order you write them), `Rows ↔ columns`, `Markdown table` and `JSON`.
- `Find`: `Find and replace`, with options to ignore case, ignore Greek accents, match whole words, or use a pattern (regular expression, with $1 for groups). `Extract` lists the e-mails, links, phone numbers, numbers, #hashtags or @names in the text, one per line, each one once if you like.
- `Encode`: `URL` (Ελλάδα becomes %CE%95… and back), `Base64` (the whole text or each line), `HTML entities` (&amp; and &#233; and back) and `Slug`, which turns a title into a web-friendly name such as kali-chronia.
- `Compare`: `Compare texts` puts your text next to a second one, line by line. Paste the second text in the `Compare with` box under the options, or open a file for it; the arrows button swaps the two texts. Lines marked `-` are only in your text, lines marked `+` only in the second one. You can show only the changes, only the added or removed lines, or only the lines both texts share.

`Ignore case` and `Ignore accents` only change how lines are compared; your text keeps its own letters. To change capitals or accents, use `Letters & accents`.

In separator, prefix and suffix fields, `\t` means a tab and `\n` a new line.

## Letters & accents
This group is the former Case Converter app: an old Case Converter shortcut, link or bookmark opens it here, and your `My words` are the same.
- `UPPERCASE`, `lowercase`, `Sentence case` (a capital at the start of each sentence), `Title Case` (small words such as "of", "the", "και", "του" stay small), `Capitalize Each Word`, `tOGGLE cASE` (fixes text typed with Caps Lock on) and `aLtErNaTiNg`. Greek is done right: capitals lose their accents but keep the diaeresis (τσάι → ΤΣΑΪ, Μάιος → ΜΑΪΟΣ), and a final ς comes back in small letters.
- `Remove accents` (also diaereses and the accents of other languages), `Greek → Latin` as ELOT 743 on Greek passports (Θεσσαλονίκη → Thessaloniki) or casual Greeklish, and `Fix mixed letters`, which finds Latin letters hiding in Greek words (an English A in ΑΘΗΝΑ) and the other way round.
- `Names for code`: camelCase, PascalCase, snake_case, kebab-case, CONSTANT_CASE, dot.case, path/case and Train-Case, one name per line. Greek is written in Latin letters unless you tick `Keep Greek letters`.
- `Fancy letters`: bold, italic, monospace, double-struck, small caps, fullwidth, circled, strikethrough and underline letters for posts. `Plain text` turns them back.

`Language` is `Automatic` at first: the letters decide. Choose `Türkçe` for the Turkish i and İ, or `Deutsch`, when the text needs it. Tick `Selection only` and select part of the text: only that part changes, the rest of the result stays as it is.

`My words`, under the options of `Sentence case`, `Title Case` and `Capitalize Each Word`, holds words that always keep their capitals, such as iPhone, NASA or ΔΕΗ. Type a word and press `Add`; the × next to a word removes it (`Undo` brings it back). A few common words are there at first.

## Translate
`Translate` turns the whole text into another language right on this device, with Mozilla's Firefox Translations models (the ones the Firefox browser uses). Your text never leaves the device and no account is needed. It knows English and 21 more languages, among them Greek, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Turkish, Bulgarian, Albanian, Romanian, Ukrainian and Arabic; two languages other than English go through English (two models, as in Firefox). Russian translates only into other languages for now, Chinese and Japanese only from them.
1. Pick `From` (`Detect` finds the language by itself) and `To`. The arrows between them swap the languages and move the translation up into the `Text` box, so you can check it the other way round.
2. The first time for a direction, the button says how much it downloads (15 to 30 MB per model, plus about 5 MB once for the translator itself). Press it: the files come from orOS's own copy, are checked, and stay on the device, so it works offline from then on.
3. Once the language is on the device, a short text translates as you type; a long one when you press `Translate`. `Stop` stops a long translation or a download.

Line breaks, empty lines and spaces at the start of a line stay where they were. `Languages on this device` lists what is downloaded and how much room it takes; `Remove` (tap twice) deletes it. The same list is in [Settings](app:settings) › `Language & search` › `Translation on this device`. Translation is not a step, so it does not go into recipes.

`Where no model fits, use the Assistant's AI` (off until you turn it on, this device only) lets the AI you set up in the [Assistant](app:assistant) translate the directions that have no model, such as Greek into Russian. Then that text goes to that AI service (your own key or your own computer), the button says `Translate with the AI` and the line under the languages names the service. It never translates as you type, only when you press the button.

>i The models are small and quick, made for understanding a text and for a first draft. Read the translation before you send it: names and rare words can come out wrong. For a book, [Writer](app:writer) will offer the same engine sentence by sentence.

## Steps and recipes
Each tool is a step. Press `+ Step` to add another one: the text goes through the steps in order, for example `Trim spaces`, then `Remove duplicates`, then `Sort`. Tap or click a step to change it, the arrow moves it up, × removes it (`Undo` brings it back).

`Save as recipe` keeps the steps under a name. Open `Recipes` at the top to use a recipe again, rename it or delete it. When you change the steps of a recipe you opened, `Update` saves the change.

## Send to other apps
`Send` hands the result to another orOS app:
- [Notes](app:notes): a new page with the result.
- [Spreadsheet](app:spreadsheet): a new sheet, one row per line and one column per field.
- [To-Do](app:todo): one task per line. To-Do shows them first and adds them only when you confirm.

## Sync and backup
Your recipes and `My words` sync between your devices through [orOS sync](help:t/sync). In `Recipes` you can also `Export` them to a file and `Import` them on another device; imported recipes are added next to yours.

>! Your text is never saved on the device and never synced. It stays only while orOS is open, so save or send the result before you close the browser.

## Keyboard shortcuts
- [[Ctrl+Enter]]: run the steps now (in `Translate`: translate now).

## Tips
- Cleaning a list from an e-shop or an e-mail? Try `Hidden characters`, then `Trim spaces`, then `Remove duplicates`.
- For an Excel file in Greek, change the separator to `Semicolon ;` before you open it there.
- Writing a title in Greek capitals? Use `UPPERCASE`: the accents go away by themselves and the diaereses stay where they belong.

## Limits
- Up to 2,000,000 characters of text. Above 200,000 the result is not live: press `Apply` (or [[Ctrl+Enter]]).
- A pattern that runs for more than two seconds is stopped.
- The `Compare with` text is not kept in recipes; like your text, it stays only while orOS is open. When two long texts differ almost everywhere, the comparison may mark a few more lines as changed than strictly needed.
- Up to 100 recipes with up to 30 steps each.
- Small letters cannot get back the accents of a text written in capitals (ΚΑΛΗΜΕΡΑ → καλημερα), and Greeklish cannot be turned back into Greek.
- Many screen readers read `Fancy letters` badly; keep them for short titles. Letters a style has no form for, such as Greek in monospace, stay as they are.
- `Translate` knows Greek and English for now; more languages will come.
- Notes gets up to 20,000 characters, Spreadsheet up to 500 rows and 64 columns, To-Do up to 200 tasks.
