# Habits
Habits helps you build routines by ticking them off day by day. You see your week at a glance, a month calendar and stats with streaks.

## Getting started
Tap or click `Create Habit` (or the **+** button at the top), type a `Name` and press `Save`. Your habit appears in the `List` with one dot for each day of the week. Tap a dot to mark that day done; tap it again to undo.

## Habits and schedules
In the `Add habit` window you pick a `Name`, an `Icon`, a `Color` and a `Schedule`:
- Pick all seven days for `Every day`, a few days for a set number of days a week, or none for `Any day` (shown as `Flexible`).
- On a scheduled habit you can only tick the days it's scheduled for. A flexible habit can be ticked on any day.
- You can't tick days in the future.

The pencil on a habit opens `Edit habit`. The bin, or `Delete` in the edit window, removes the habit and its full history after you confirm.

The flame next to a habit shows your current streak. Today doesn't break a streak until the day is over.

## Views
- **`List`**: one week, Monday first. The arrows move a week back or forward; the middle button returns to this week.
- **`Calendar`**: a whole month, one row per habit. Tap a day to tick it, and tap a habit's name to edit it. The arrows move by month.
- **`Stats`**: pick `30d`, `90d` or `All` to see `Check-offs`, `Active habits`, `Avg / week` and `Perfect days`, then each habit's current and longest streak and completion rate under `By habit`, and a few `Insights`.

## Rows from other apps
Some apps can add an automatic row, marked `Auto`, that ticks itself:
- [Water](app:water): days you reached your water goal. You can turn it off with `Show in Habits` in Water's settings.
- [Workouts](app:fitness): days with a finished workout. You can turn it off in Workouts' settings.

These rows are read-only and aren't counted in `Stats`. Tap one to open its app.

## Sync and backup
Your habits and check-offs sync between your devices through [orOS sync](help:t/sync). The open view and period stay on this device. Habits has no import or export; use the orOS [backup](help:t/backup).

## Keyboard shortcuts
- [[N]] opens `Add habit` (when you're not typing in a field).
- [[Enter]] in the `Name` field saves the habit.
- [[Esc]] closes the open window.

## Tips
- Ticked habits show as dots in [Calendar](app:calendar) on their day.
- Use `Any day` for things you want to do often but not on fixed days.

## Limits
- Habits are done or not done each day; there are no counts or amounts.
- Deleting a habit can't be undone.
