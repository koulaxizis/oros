# Timesheet
Timesheet tracks the time you spend on work, per project and client. Start a timer or add time by hand, then see your week and make reports with hours and amounts.

## Getting started
On the `Timer` tab, pick a project (or leave `No project`), type `What are you working on?` and press `Start`. Press `Stop` when you're done. The timer keeps running even with the app closed, and you see it on your other devices too.

Below the timer you see the entries of the day and its total. Use the arrows to go to other days; the date button brings you back to today. Tap or click an entry to edit it, or use its ▶ button to continue the same work with a new timer.

If a timer has been running for more than 10 hours, a banner asks if you forgot to stop it: `Fix end time` or `It's fine`. orOS also sends a notice once, even when Timesheet is closed.

While a timer runs, a clock chip in the orOS top bar shows how long it has been going; hover over it for the project and start time. Click it for a small menu: `Stop timer`, `Open Timesheet` and, after time away, the same choices as the banner below. So you can stop the timer from any app. Anywhere in orOS, [[Ctrl+Alt+Shift+T]] starts or stops the timer (see [Keyboard shortcuts](help:t/shortcuts)).

## Add and edit time
`Add time by hand` opens `Add time`. Give a `Start date`, `Start` and `End`, or just a `Duration` such as 1:30, 1,5 (hours) or 90m. In an entry you can also tick `Invoiced`, give a `Task` (the project's earlier tasks are suggested) and `Tags`, separated by commas (tags you already use show as buttons to add). `Delete` removes it, with an `Undo` button for a few seconds.

## Week
The `Week` tab shows a grid of hours per project and day, with totals. Tap or click a day to open it on the `Timer` tab.

## Reports
On `Reports`, choose a `Period` (this week, last month, this year or `Custom`), then filter by `Client`, `Project`, `Billable`, `Invoiced` and `Tag` (once you use tags), and `Group by` project, client, task, tag or day. An entry with several tags counts under each tag; the total counts it once. You see `Hours`, `Billable` hours and the `Amount`.

- `Export CSV` saves the report for a spreadsheet.
- Inside orOS, `Open in Spreadsheet` opens the same rows as a new sheet in Spreadsheet, with totals under hours and amount.
- `Create quote` turns the report into a new [Quote](app:quote): one line per billable project with a rate, with the hours as quantity and the hourly rate as price. The client is filled in when the period has only one. The quote takes Timesheet's currency, whatever it is.
- `Create invoice` puts the same lines in a new invoice in [Invoices](app:invoices), in any currency. Check it there, then save or issue it.
- `Mark … as invoiced` marks all billable entries of the report as invoiced, so next time you can filter for `Not invoiced`. You can unmark them the same way.

## Projects and clients
On `Projects`, add a `New project` or `New client`. A project has a `Name`, `Client`, `Colour`, `Hourly rate`, an optional `Hours budget` (e.g. 40 or 12:30) and `Billable`. With a budget, the project shows a bar of the hours used: it turns gold from 90 % and red when the budget is exceeded. Leave the rate empty to use the client's rate, or the default from `Settings`. A project with entries can't be deleted; tick `Archived` instead to hide it from the timer and keep its time.

## Day rate, minimum charge, overtime and extra charges
Open a project (or a client, for all its projects without their own) on `Projects` and unfold `Day rate, minimum and overtime`. Choose `Per` `Day worked`, `Week` or `Month`, then any of:
- `Minimum charge (day rate)`: an amount charged for every day with work, even a short one. A project with a day rate uses only its own hourly rate; leave that empty to charge the day rate alone.
- `Minimum billable hours` (e.g. 2 or 1:30): charged even when you work less.
- `Overtime after (hours)` (e.g. 8): time beyond it is overtime at `Overtime hourly rate` (empty: the project's usual rate). Without it, overtime starts after the minimum hours.

For example a day rate of 100 € with overtime after 8 hours at 15 €/h: 3 hours or 8 hours cost 100 €, 12 hours cost 100 € + 4 × 15 €. Reports show the parts under the table: `Work time`, `Overtime`, `Up to the minimum charge` and the `Total to charge`.
- Only billable time in the report's filters counts. A day, week or month without work charges nothing.
- A minimum per week or month counts only for whole weeks or months inside the report's period (for a monthly minimum, pick `This month` or `Last month`). The note above the table says when one was left out.
- Your entries keep their real times: the minimum and the overtime only change reports, CSV, quotes and invoices.

`Add an extra charge` under the day's entries records a cost beyond the hours, such as fuel or work away from the office: a `Date`, a `Project` (or just a client), `What it is for` and the `Amount`. Extra charges show under the day's entries, are added to the report's amount and listed under it (tap one to edit it), come into the CSV as rows without times, and become their own lines in `Create quote` and `Create invoice`. `Mark … as invoiced` marks them too.

## Settings
The gear button opens `Settings`: `Currency`, `Default hourly rate`, a `Daily goal` (shown as a bar under the day) and `Rounding in reports`. Rounding only changes reports and CSV; your entries keep their exact times.

### Time away
Tick `Ask about time away while a timer runs` and set `Away for at least (minutes)` (10 by default). orOS then notices when the device slept or the lid was closed and, in Chrome and Edge, when you left the keyboard and mouse (allow "idle detection" when the browser asks). When you come back, a banner says when you were away, with three choices: `Keep` leaves the time in, `Take it out` ends the entry when you left and carries on with a new one from when you came back, and `Take out and stop` ends it when you left. The clock chip in the top bar shows a dot and offers the same choices from any app. The setting stays on this device.

### Move from another app
`Import from another app` in `Settings` brings in an export from Toggl Track, Clockify, Harvest, Kimai or Jira Tempo, as CSV or Excel (.xlsx), with tasks and tags. First you see what the file holds: new entries, entries already here, new projects and clients, and rows that could not be read. Nothing changes until you tap `Import`.
- Importing only adds entries. The same file twice adds nothing, and entries you delete stay deleted if you import again.
- Projects and clients with the same name as yours are joined to them.
- If the dates could be either way round, choose Day/Month or Month/Day in `Dates in the file`.
- Harvest has hours but no start times, so each day's entries are placed one after another from 09:00.
- Kimai: `Times` › `Export`, choose CSV or Excel. Customer becomes the client, Activity the task, and the hourly rate the rate of a new project.
- Jira Tempo: open a worklogs report (`Tempo` › `Reports` › `Logged time`), then `Export` › CSV. The issue key and summary become the task; days without a start time are placed from 09:00.
- A file with several people's time (Kimai, Tempo) lets you pick `Whose entries`.
- The message after an import has `Undo`, which takes the imported entries out again on every device.

## Sync and backup
Entries, the running timer, projects, clients and settings sync between your devices through [orOS sync](help:t/sync). The open tab, the last project you used and the report filters stay on each device.

In `Settings`, `Back up (JSON)` saves everything to a file and `Restore from backup` merges a backup with what you have: nothing is replaced or lost. The orOS [backup](help:t/backup) includes Timesheet too.

## Save and open an orOS file
In `Settings`, `orOS file (.oroshours)` saves every client, project and finished entry as one `.oroshours` file, for example to move your time to another device without sync or to keep a copy. A timer that is still running is not saved: stop it first to include it. The file is a plain ZIP: unzipped it holds your time as `content.json` plus `preview/hours.csv` (the same columns as `Export CSV`) for spreadsheets. It is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere safe.

`Open orOS file…` (or `Unzipped orOS folder…`, or "Open with" orOS on the computer) shows how many records are new and how many are already here, then `Add` adds only the new ones, with their own ids: opening the same file again adds nothing. An entry already here is kept as it is, and one you deleted here stays deleted; a file never deletes anything, and your running timer and settings stay as they are. Tick `Update with newer versions` to let entries that were changed later in the file replace your copy. `Undo` in the message removes what was added.

## Pomodoro
In [Time](app:time) → `Pomodoro`, pick a project under `Log focus in Timesheet`: every focus session then appears here as the task "Pomodoro" on that project (a toast tells you when new ones arrive). Sessions are added the next time Timesheet is open on the same device; after that they sync to your other devices like any entry.

## Keyboard shortcuts
- [[S]] starts or stops the timer.
- [[N]] adds time by hand.
- [[←]] / [[→]] go to the previous or next day (or week on `Week`).
- [[Enter]] in the description box starts the timer.
- [[Ctrl+Alt+Shift+T]] starts or stops the timer from anywhere in orOS.
- [[Ctrl+Z]] undoes the last delete or change that offered `Undo`.

## Tips
- [Calendar](app:calendar) can show your time under the `Work time` chip: one row per project per day, such as "Website · 2:15". Tapping a row opens that day here.
- `Track time` on an event in [Calendar](app:calendar) starts a timer here named after the event (with the project of the same name, if you have one). If a timer is already running, it keeps running.
- You don't need a project to start: time without a project is kept, and you can assign it later.
- If timers were started on two devices at once, both keep running and you can stop each one.

## Limits
- A single entry can be at most 366 days long.
- Keyboard and mouse idle detection works only in Chrome and Edge, and only while orOS is open in a tab or window; other browsers notice only a sleeping device.
