# Health
Health keeps your home measurements in one place: blood pressure, weight, blood sugar, sleep, resting heart rate, temperature, oxygen and any kind you add yourself. It also keeps a list of the medicines you take, reminds you of each dose, counts what's left and lets you log symptoms. Its `Record` keeps your Medical ID for emergencies, your doctor's appointments, vaccinations and documents such as lab results. It shows trends and makes a tidy report for your doctor.

>! Health records, it does not diagnose and it is not medical advice. The Medical ID shows only what you typed: it does not replace your doctor's records. It does not check doses or interactions between medicines. Take your medicines as your doctor or pharmacist told you, and for anything that worries you, talk to them.

## Getting started
The `Measurements` tab has one card per measurement. Tap or click **+** on a card to add a reading: the date and time are filled in with now, so you only type the value. Tap or click the card itself to see its chart.

Each reading can have a `Note` and `Tags` (separate them with commas, e.g. after coffee). Open `Settings` first if you want other units (kg or lb, mg/dL or mmol/L, °C or °F) or your height, which is only used for the BMI.

## Measurements
- `Blood pressure`: upper and lower value, optional pulse, `Arm` and `Position`.
- `Weight`: with your BMI once your height is set.
- `Blood sugar`: pick `When` (`Fasting`, `Before a meal`, `2 h after a meal`, `Bedtime` or `Random`); the ranges depend on it.
- `Sleep`: when you went to bed, when you woke up and an optional `Quality` from 1 to 5.
- `Resting heart rate`, `Temperature`, `Oxygen (SpO₂)`.

The badge on each reading (`In range`, `Borderline`, `High` and so on) follows common tables: ESC/ESH 2023 for blood pressure, ADA for blood sugar, WHO for BMI. If your doctor gave you other targets, type them in the measurement's settings and they win.

### Your own kinds
Tap or click `Add your own kind` for anything else, such as cholesterol or waist size. Give it a `Name`, an optional `Unit`, the number of `Decimals` and, if you like, a target range. Deleting an own kind also deletes its readings on every synced device; `Undo` brings both back.

### Reminders
In a measurement's settings, `Add a time` (up to four). A bell on the card shows it has reminders. The reminder appears in your orOS notifications from that time on for four hours; a reading taken up to an hour before the time counts, so you aren't reminded twice. Tap the notification to open a new reading. See [notifications](help:t/notifications).

## Medicines
The `Medicines` tab has three views: `Today`, `My medicines` and `History`.

### Adding a medicine
Tap or click `Add a medicine` and fill in:
- `Name` and, if you like, the `Strength` (e.g. 500 mg) and the `Form` (tablet, capsule, liquid in ml, drops, inhaler, injection, cream, patch and more).
- `Dose`: how much you take each time, e.g. 1 or 0.5 tablets.
- `How often`: `Every day`, `On certain days` (pick the weekdays), `Every few days` (every 2 days, every 3 days…) or `As needed`. For the first three, add the `Times` you take it (up to eight).
- `Start` and an optional `End` for a course, such as an antibiotic for 7 days.
- `Remind me at these times` and a `Note` (e.g. with food).

Tap or click a medicine in `My medicines` to change it. When you change its times or days, the days before keep the old schedule, so your history stays right. `Stop taking` ends the course today and keeps its history; `Taking it again` brings it back. Deleting a medicine also deletes its dose history on every synced device; `Undo` brings it back.

### Today
`Today` lists every dose of the day by time. For each one choose `Taken`, `Skip` or `Snooze` (10 minutes, 30 minutes or 1 hour). A dose more than 30 minutes past its time is marked `Late` in red. After you mark a dose, `Change` lets you fix the time you took it or the amount, and `Undo` takes the mark back. Medicines you take `As needed` have their own list with `Take a dose` and today's count.

### Stock and refills
Tick `Count what's left` in a medicine and type how many you have now. Each dose marked `Taken` takes its amount off, and the list shows how many are left and for about how many days. When a medicine will run out within the days you chose in `Warn me this many days before it runs out` (7 by default), a warning appears at the top of `Today` and in your orOS notifications. `Refilled +30` (your pack size) adds a new pack. If the count drifts, type the right number in the medicine again.

### History and adherence
`History` shows, for the last 7, 30 or 90 days, how many doses you took out of those that were due, per medicine and in total, and every dose by day: `Taken at` a time, `Skipped` or `Missed`. A dose counts once it is 30 minutes past its time or marked. Tap or click a dose to mark it later or to change it.

### Medicine reminders
At each dose time you get an orOS notification for four hours, unless you have already marked it; after `Snooze` it comes back when the snooze ends. Tap it to open `Today`. In the orOS Android app with phone reminders allowed (Settings › `Connected services`), doses ring on the phone even when orOS is closed. Doses of a medicine you just added that were due before you added it are not reminded or counted as missed. Today's open doses and medicines running low also appear in [Today](app:today).

## Symptoms
The `Symptoms` card on `Measurements` keeps how you feel: tap or click **+**, pick the symptom, set the `Intensity` from 1 to 10 and add a note or tags if you like. Headache, nausea, dizziness, tiredness, pain, cough, shortness of breath and palpitations are ready; pick `Your own symptom…` to add any other. In `Settings` you can hide symptoms you don't need, and rename or delete your own (with their entries; `Undo` brings them back).

Symptoms appear in `History`, have their own chart in `Charts` (pick the symptom in the list) and can go in the report.

## History, charts and report
- `History` lists every reading and symptom by day, newest first, with a filter per measurement and one for `Symptoms`. Tap or click a row to edit or delete it.
- `Charts` shows 7, 30, 90 days, 1 year or all, with the target range shaded. Blood pressure splits mornings and evenings, blood sugar can be shown per context, weight adds a 7-day average. Below the chart you get averages, lowest, highest and `Numbers as a table`. Tick `Show symptoms and doses` to see small marks under a measurement's chart: dots for symptoms (blue mild, amber moderate, red strong) and ticks for doses taken; hover over a mark to see what it is.
- `Report` builds a sheet for your doctor: pick the period, the measurements, `Medicines (list and doses taken)`, `Symptoms` and whether to include notes. `Print or save as PDF` opens your browser's print window; `Spreadsheet (CSV)` saves the same readings, symptoms and doses as a file. Inside orOS, `Open in Spreadsheet` (here and in `Settings` → `Export`) opens the same rows as a new sheet in Spreadsheet.

## Record
The `Record` tab has four views: `Medical ID`, `Appointments`, `Vaccines` and `Documents`.

### Medical ID
Like the Medical ID on a phone: what someone helping you in an emergency should know. Tap or click `Edit` for your name, date of birth, `Blood type`, `Organ donor` and `Medical notes` (e.g. pacemaker, contact lenses). Then add your `Allergies` (with `Severity` and the reaction), your `Conditions` and your `Emergency contacts`. `From Contacts` picks a person and a phone number from [Contacts](app:contacts); you can also type them. The medicines you take now are shown too, unless you untick `Show the medicines I take now`.

- `Show Medical ID` fills the screen with big, clear text to show to a doctor or a first aider, with a `Call` button for each emergency contact. The screen stays on while it is open, where the browser allows it.
- `Show a QR code` adds a QR code with the essentials as plain text: a phone camera reads it without going online. When there is a lot, the notes and the last medicines are left out of the code (the screen above still shows everything).
- `Emergency card` prints a wallet-size card, front and back side by side, to cut out and fold; it carries the QR code when you have it switched on. In the print window you can also save it as a PDF.

### Appointments
`Add an appointment` with the `Doctor`, `Specialty`, date and time, an optional `Duration`, the `Place` and a note. `Remind me` from the time itself up to two days before. `Show on the map` opens the place in [Maps](app:maps). Appointments appear in [Today](app:today) and, if you turn it on, in Calendar.

### Vaccines
Add each vaccination with the day it was given and, if there is one, when the `Next dose` is due. You get a reminder a week before and on the day. When you get the next dose, tap or click `Given again`: it adds a new entry with the same name and the old one stops reminding. A vaccine due soon also appears in Today.

### Documents
Keep lab results, prescriptions, imaging, letters and other papers: a `Title`, the `Kind`, the date, a note and up to six photos or PDFs. Photos are made smaller and lose their location data; PDFs can be up to 10 MB. The files are kept in [Files](app:files), folder `Health`, not in the sync of your readings: they reach your other devices through Vault Drive. A file that is not on a device yet shows `Not on this device`. Deleting a document removes its files once the `Undo` time has passed.

## Calendar
In [Calendar](app:calendar), the `Medicines` chip shows the first and last day of a course and the day a medicine you count runs out. The `Doctor visits` chip shows your appointments at their time and the day a vaccine's next dose is due. Both start off: tap or click a chip to turn it on.

## Workouts weights
If you log body weight in [Workouts](app:fitness), those weights appear in Health as hollow dots marked `from Workouts`. Health only reads them: to change one, open Workouts. You can turn this off in `Settings`.

## Steps and Health Connect
`Steps` holds one reading per day. You can type it, or let the orOS Android app bring it: switch on `Health Connect` in Settings › `Connected services` and allow it. orOS then takes each day's steps from Health Connect, which collects them from your phone, watch or other health apps. Today's count goes up while you use orOS.

The same switch also brings readings that other apps and devices put in Health Connect: weight, resting heart rate, sleep, blood pressure, blood sugar and oxygen. Each one becomes a normal reading here, once; if you change or delete it in Health, it stays that way. orOS only reads these from Health Connect and never writes them there. If you allowed Health Connect before, open the switch once more to allow the new kinds. The same switch sends your [Water](app:water) drinks and [Workouts](app:fitness) to Health Connect, and brings back drinks and workouts from your other apps, for the last 30 days.

## Import from another app
In `Settings` → `Import` → `From another app…` pick an export from another health app. Health shows what it found first (how many readings of each kind, the dates, what is already here and what it leaves out) and imports only when you confirm. Your own readings are never changed, imported ones get the app's name as a tag, and importing the same file again adds only what is missing. `Undo` on the message right after removes the whole import.
- **Withings**: in Health Mate, open your profile or Settings and choose `Download my data`; you get an email with a ZIP. Pick the ZIP, or just `weight.csv` or `bp.csv` from it.
- **OMRON connect**: in the app's menu or Profile, look for `Export data` / `Share as CSV` and pick that CSV. Dates written as day/month or month/day are both understood.
- **Samsung Health**: in Samsung Health › Settings, `Download personal data`; the phone saves a folder of CSV files (Internal storage › Download › Samsung Health). Zip the folder, or pick one file such as `com.samsung.health.weight….csv`.
- **Apple Health**: in the Health app tap your picture, then `Export All Health Data`, and send `export.zip` to this device. Pick the ZIP, or the `export.xml` inside it. Large exports are read piece by piece, so they can take a minute.

Health takes blood pressure, weight, blood sugar, resting heart rate, temperature and oxygen. Pounds become kilograms, mmol/L becomes mg/dL and °F becomes °C (you still see your own units). Body fat, muscle, water and height are not kept. When a file has only heart-rate samples, each day becomes one resting value: the day's lowest.

## Sync and backup
Readings, your own kinds, targets, reminders, medicines, doses, refills, symptoms, your Medical ID, appointments, vaccinations, documents (without their files) and settings sync between your devices through [orOS sync](help:t/sync). Each dose is one entry, so if you mark the same dose on two devices, they agree on the later mark; refills from two devices both count. The open tab, chart choices, report options and whether the QR code is shown stay on this device.

In `Settings` → `Export` you can save `All readings (CSV)` or a `Backup (JSON)` (with medicines and symptoms too). `Restore from backup (merge)` merges a backup with what you already have. For a full copy of orOS, see [backup](help:t/backup).

## orOS file (.oroshealth)
In `Settings` → `Export`, `orOS file (.oroshealth)` saves all of Health as one file: readings, your own kinds and targets, medicines, doses, refills, symptoms, allergies, conditions, emergency contacts, vaccinations, appointments, documents with their photos and PDFs, your Medical ID and settings. Inside are also `readings.csv` for a spreadsheet and `health.md` to read. The file is not encrypted: it holds your health data, so keep it somewhere safe and send it only to people you trust.

`Open orOS file…` (or a double click on the file in Files) adds what this device does not have yet, with nothing deleted: the dialog shows how many records are new. A record that is already here, or that you deleted here, stays as it is; tick `Update with newer versions` to take the file's newer copies when you move your own data between devices. Your settings and Medical ID here stay as they are. Reminders come in off: turn them on for the medicines, kinds, vaccines and appointments you want reminding about. `Undo` in the message takes the added records out again. Opening the same file twice adds nothing. `Unzipped orOS folder…` opens a file someone has unzipped.

## Keyboard shortcuts
- [[1]] to [[6]] switch between the six tabs (not while typing).
- [[Esc]] closes a dialog.

## Tips
- A note like "after coffee" or a tag like "with medicine" makes odd readings easier to explain at the doctor's.
- Hide measurements you never use in `Settings`; hidden kinds don't remind you.
- Bring the report with `Medicines` ticked to your appointment: it lists what you take and how regularly you took it.
- Print the `Emergency card` again after you change a medicine or an allergy: the paper does not update itself.

## Limits
- Measurement reminders only appear while orOS is open; a time missed by more than four hours is skipped. Medicine doses, appointments and vaccines also ring through the orOS Android app with orOS closed, for the next two days from the last time orOS was opened; in a browser they too need orOS open.
- The Medical ID lives in orOS: it is not on your phone's lock screen, so whoever helps you needs your phone unlocked, or the printed card.
- Health has no medicine database: it does not know doses, interactions or side effects, and it does not check what you type.
- The count of what's left is only as right as the doses you mark; check it against the box now and then.
- Apart from what comes through Health Connect in the orOS Android app, readings are typed in by hand or imported from another app's export: no direct link to devices.
- The `Backup (JSON)` keeps the names of document files, not the files: copy the `Health` folder in Files, or use Vault Drive, to keep them.
