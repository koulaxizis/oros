# Stock
Stock keeps the inventory of a business: your products, what comes in, what goes out and what is left. For things at home (where you put the winter blankets), use [Storage](app:storage) instead.

## Getting started
1. Add a product with `New` › `Product`, or bring all of them at once with `Import a CSV` (Settings › `Import products (CSV)`).
2. Give it a `Name` and, if it has one, a `SKU` and a `Barcode`. A `Minimum` tells Stock when it's time to reorder.
3. Type an `Opening quantity`, or record your first `Receive stock` later.

The quantity of a product is never typed in directly: it is always the sum of its movements. That's why it stays right when two of your devices sell at the same time.

## Products
The `Products` tab shows what you have, with the total `Stock value` and how many products are `To reorder` or `Out of stock`. Filter with `Low`, `Out of stock` or `Archived`, pick a category and choose the order.

Tap or click a product to see its quantity, average cost, value, prices and every movement it ever had. From there: `+ Receive`, `− Sell`, `Count`, `Edit`, `Make a label`, `Archive`.

> A barcode scanner (USB or Bluetooth) works in the search field: scan a code and the product opens. An unknown code offers `New product` with the SKU filled in.

On a phone, the 📷 button next to a search or scan field reads barcodes with the camera (Chrome on Android). In a receipt, a sale or the counter it stays open, so you can scan one product after another; tap `Done` when you are finished. Where the button doesn't show, the browser can't read barcodes, but a scanner still works.

A product can have a photo: `Add photo` in its sheet. The picture is made smaller and kept in [Files](app:files), folder Stock/Photos (without location or camera data); it reaches your other devices with Vault Drive.

### Barcode labels
`New` › `Barcode labels` (or the button in a product's sheet) prints labels on an A4 sheet of 3 × 8 (70 × 37 mm): name, barcode and, if you want, the price. Choose the products and how many copies of each. A product with a 13-digit barcode gets an EAN-13; any other gets a Code 128 of its barcode or SKU. Print to paper or save as PDF from the print window.

## Movements
Every change in stock is a movement. The `Movements` tab lists them day by day; filter by type and period.

### Receive and sell
Open `New` › `Receive stock` or `Sell / issue`. Pick a `Date`, a `Ref.` (your invoice or receipt number) and, for receipts, a `Supplier`. Then add products: type a name, or scan. Scanning the same product again adds one more. Receipts take a `Unit cost`, sales a `Unit price`, and the `Total` is shown.

Other types: `Customer return`, `Return to supplier`, `Loss / damage` and `Correction` (a + or − quantity).

A sale can also come from another orOS app, already filled in, for example the products of an invoice. Stock matches them by SKU or barcode, lists any code it couldn't find, and warns if the same document was taken out of stock before. Nothing is saved until you press `Save`.

### Lots and expiry dates
For food, cosmetics or anything that expires, tap `+ Lot / expiry` on a receipt line and type the `Lot` and the date it `Expires` (a date alone is enough; it becomes the lot's name). From then on that product's receipts ask for them every time.

When you sell, a product with lots offers a `Lot` choice; leave it on `First to expire` and Stock takes the lot that expires first. A product's sheet shows what is left of each lot, the `Expiring` filter on Products lists what expires within 30 days, Reports shows them too, and [Today](app:today) reminds you a week before.

### Counter
`New` › `Counter (quick sale)` is made for a shop with a scanner: scan products (each scan adds one), change quantities with − and +, and press `Complete sale`. It saves one sale at the products' sale prices, and you can `Undo` it right away. A sale you leave open stays in the counter until you complete or clear it.

### Point of sale
`New` › `Point of sale (till)` is a full-screen till for a shop. On the left: a search box (a scanner or the 📷 camera adds the product straight away), category chips and a grid of products with their price and what is left. On the right: the sale, with − and + or a typed quantity per line, the net, the VAT per rate and the total.

1. Pick a `Price list` at the top. `Retail` uses each product's sale price. Under `Price lists` you can add others, such as `Wholesale`: a percentage on the sale price (−10 means 10% off) and, with `Prices…`, a price of its own for any product.
2. Choose `Cash`, `Card` or `Other`. For cash you can type `Cash given` to see the change.
3. Press `Charge`. The products come off stock (from the warehouse shown at the top, when you have several), and the receipt opens: print it on 58 mm or 80 mm roll paper. `Undo` in the message voids the sale right away.

`Receipt settings` holds the receipt's header (shop name, address, VAT number) and footer, the paper width and the `Register code`: two characters at the start of the receipt numbers (like AB-261014-001), so two tills never print the same number. These settings stay on each device.

`Z report` shows a day's sales: how many, net, VAT per rate, by payment method and price list, the best sellers and every receipt (reprint or `Void` from there; a voided sale's products go back into stock). `Close the day` keeps the day's totals as a numbered Z; sales made after it show apart. `Print Z report` prints it on the roll.

Every sale is also an ordinary `Sale` movement, so stock, reports and sync work as with any sale. Two tills selling at the same time never lose a sale.

### Stock count
In a `Stock count` you type what you `Counted`. Stock saves the difference from what it expected, so a sale on another device at the same moment still counts.

### Void a movement
A movement is never edited or deleted. Open it and choose `Void this movement`: its quantities stop counting and it stays in the list, crossed out. Then record the right one.

>! If a sale would take a product below zero, Stock asks first. You can forbid it in Settings.

### Warehouses
If you keep stock in more than one place (a shop and a storeroom, two branches), add them in Settings › `Warehouses`. From then on:
- every receipt, sale, count and the counter has a `Warehouse` (this device remembers the last one you used);
- `New` › `Transfer` moves products `From` one warehouse `To` another; totals don't change;
- the Products tab can show one warehouse, and a product's sheet shows its quantity `By warehouse`;
- a stock count compares with what that warehouse should have;
- Reports adds the `Value per warehouse`.

Reorder, minimums and the total value stay for the whole business. A warehouse that was used can be archived, not deleted; move its stock out first.

## Reorder
`Reorder` lists every product at or below its minimum, grouped by supplier, with a suggested quantity (the product's `Reorder qty`, or enough for twice the minimum, less what is already on order). For each supplier:
- `Create order` makes a purchase order with those lines.
- `Copy list` copies the order as text; `To-Do` sends it to a new To-Do list; `E-mail` opens a message to the supplier in [Mail](app:mail) (when Mail has an account on this device).
- `Receive this order` opens a receipt with those lines when the goods arrive.

When a product reaches its minimum you also get one notice on this device (you can switch it off in Settings).

### Purchase orders
A purchase order keeps what you asked a supplier for: `Order no.`, `Supplier`, `Order date`, `Expected` day (filled in from the supplier's delivery time) and the products with quantity and unit cost. Save it as a draft or mark it sent. Open orders show at the top of `Reorder`.

When goods arrive, open the order and choose `Receive`: a receipt opens with what is still to come. Save it, even if only part arrived; the order shows `Partly received` until everything is in, then `Received`. Voiding a receipt puts its quantities back on the order. `Close order` when nothing more is coming; `Cancel order` if it never went out.

What is on order shows next to low products and lowers their suggested quantity. A late delivery shows in [Today](app:today).

## Reports
The `Reports` tab shows the stock value, sales, purchases and losses of a period (last 30 days, this month, this year or all), the value per category, the products that sold most and the ones that sold nothing for 90 days. `Open in Spreadsheet` sends the full table to a new sheet; `Report (CSV)` saves it as a file.

## Closing a period
A busy shop writes thousands of movements a year, and they take space on every device. Settings › `Close a period` archives everything up to a day you choose (for example 31 December):
1. the full data as it was goes to a file in [Files](app:files), folder Stock/Archive (it travels with Vault Drive like any file);
2. the movements up to that day are replaced by an `Opening balance` per product: quantities per warehouse, lots and average costs.

Quantities, value and reorder stay exactly the same. The Movements tab and Reports then start after that day, and new movements can't be dated inside the closed period.

If a device was offline and recorded a sale inside the period before it synced, that sale still counts when it arrives (within 90 days). `Reopen` in the same place brings the archived movements back from the file.

## Suppliers
The `Suppliers` tab keeps names, contact person, phone, e-mail, VAT number and delivery time. `From Contacts` copies a person or company from [Contacts](app:contacts) into a new supplier.

## The shop (WooCommerce)
Connect your WooCommerce shop once in Settings › [Connected services](app:settings), then open `Shop (WooCommerce)` from `New` or from Settings here.
- `Products`: `Get products from the shop` reads every product and variation. `Import into Stock` adds the new ones and updates name, SKU, sale price and category of those you already have (matched by the shop product, then SKU, then name). Tick the box under it to also set the quantities here to the shop's, as one stock count.
- `Quantities that differ` lists the products whose stock the shop manages and whose quantity is different here. `Send to shop` sets the shop's quantity to yours; `Take the shop's` counts yours at the shop's.
- `Orders`: the shop's orders of the last 30 days. For each one: `Record the sale` opens a sale with its lines (it shows `Sale recorded` once saved, so you don't record it twice), `Invoice` opens a new invoice in [Invoices](app:invoices), `Customer in CRM` logs it on the customer in [CRM](app:crm), `Add to Budget` logs the income in [Budget](app:budget), and `Mark completed` completes the order in the shop.

Nothing is sent to the shop without a button, and nothing is saved here or in another app until you save it there.

## Import and export
In Settings:
- `Import products (CSV)` reads your own export, your e-shop's product export (WooCommerce works) or any sheet whose first row names the columns, such as Name, SKU, Price, Stock. Existing products are matched by SKU, then by name. Tick `Set the quantities from the file` to save the file's quantities as one stock count.
- The same button also reads a Shopify product export (`Products` › `Export`, CSV) and a Square item library (`Items` › `Item library` › `Actions` › `Export library`, Excel or CSV). Each Shopify variant becomes its own product, named with its options; the description comes in as plain text, tags and the picture's link go into the note. Square quantities of all locations are added together. You first see how many products are new and how many are already here; products already here are never changed. Leave `Set the quantities` ticked to bring the quantities in as one stock count. The message after the import has `Undo`; if quantities came in, the products stay archived with their count voided.
- An Excel sheet (.xlsx) works too; if the Excel reader is missing, save the sheet as CSV.
- `Products (CSV)` and `Movements (CSV)` open in any spreadsheet.
- `Backup (JSON)` saves everything; `Restore backup` merges a backup in, it never wipes what you have.

## Sync and backup
Products, movements, suppliers, categories, units and settings sync between your devices through [orOS sync](help:t/sync). The low-stock notice switch and your filters stay on each device. The orOS [backup](help:t/backup) keeps a copy too.

## Tips
- Set a `Location` such as "Shelf B3": it shows in the list and in search.
- Archive products you no longer sell: their history stays.
- From a product: `New quote` starts a quote in [Quote](app:quote) with that product and its sale price (in Stock's currency). `Quote from products` in `New` takes several.
- Open a receipt or a sale and choose `Add to Budget` to log its total in [Budget](app:budget).
- Units with decimals (kg, L, m) take quantities like 2,5; you can add your own units in Settings.

## Limits
- Minimums and reorder are for the whole business, not per warehouse.
- Lots are counted for the whole business, not per warehouse; the counter always sells the first lot to expire.
- Camera scanning needs a browser that reads barcodes (Chrome on Android); elsewhere use a scanner that types.
- A product with movements can't be deleted, only archived.
- The shop: WooCommerce only for now. Orders are read live (the last 30 days, up to 50), so they need a connection. The shop keeps whole units: a quantity with decimals is rounded when sent.
- After closing a period, the Movements tab and Reports show only what came after it; the older history is in the archive file.
- The point of sale prints its own simple receipts and Z reports: they are not tax receipts and aren't sent to any tax authority (in Greece, use a certified cash register or e-invoicing provider for that). It doesn't take card payments itself: use your card terminal and choose `Card`.
