# Maps

Find places, plan routes by car, bike or on foot, follow spoken turn-by-turn directions, and help improve OpenStreetMap with notes.

## Getting started

Type a place or address in `Search a place…`. Suggestions appear from the third letter, and places near the part of the map you're looking at come first. Tap or click one to drop a pin. You can also type coordinates, such as `37.9755, 23.7348` or `37°58'32"N 23°44'05"E`.

`Use my location` shows where you are (your browser asks for permission the first time). The layers button at the top right switches between `Standard`, `Humanitarian` and `Satellite` maps.

## Places

- A pin's popup has `Save` (star), `Route` (from your location to there) and `From here`.
- Right-click the map, or long-press on a phone, to use any point: `Go here`, `Start here`, `Add as stop` (once a route is set) and `Save`.
- `Saved places` lists everything you starred. Tap a place to fly there. The pencil renames it (`Rename place`) and the × deletes it, with `Undo`.

## Plan a route

1. Tap `Plan a route`. A new plan starts `From` `My location`.
2. Type in a row to search. Your saved and recent places are offered too. Or tap `Choose on map` in that row and then tap the map.
3. Use `Add stop` for up to 3 stops. `Swap` reverses the whole trip.

The route bar shows distance and travel time (an estimate without live traffic), plus `Car`, `Bike`, `Walk`, `Directions` (the step list), `Edit route`, `Send to Calendar`, `Start navigation` and `Clear route`. `Send to Calendar` asks for a `Day` and adds a "Route to …" event to [Calendar](app:calendar) with the distance, time and mode.

## Navigation

`Start navigation` follows your GPS position and speaks each turn in the orOS language. If you're more than about 150 m from the start, the route is recalculated from where you are. Leave the route and it's recalculated automatically (when you're online).

- Move the map and it stops following you. `Recenter` brings it back.
- `Mute voice` silences the instructions.
- `Exit navigation` needs a second tap within 3 seconds.
- Where the browser allows it, the screen stays on while you navigate.
- If Maps reopens in the same tab within 30 minutes, navigation picks up again.

## OpenStreetMap notes

A note tells the volunteers who map [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org) that something is wrong or missing: a shop that closed, a new path, a wrong street name. They read it, fix the map and resolve the note.

1. Tap `Mapping (OpenStreetMap notes)` (the flag). The notes of the area appear on the map and in the panel (zoom in if you see `Zoom in to see the notes of this area.`). Red notes are open, green ones resolved in the last week.
2. To report something, long-press the map (right-click on a computer) and pick `Report to OpenStreetMap`, or use `New note at the map centre`. Describe it clearly: other people will read it. If an open note is already close by, Maps shows it first.
3. Tap a note to read it. You can `Comment`, `Resolve` or `Reopen` it.

Writing needs a free OpenStreetMap account: `Sign in with OpenStreetMap` opens the OpenStreetMap page in a small window, and you come back signed in. Reading works without one. `Mine` lists the notes you created or commented on, and Maps tells you when someone replies.

> Without a connection, or when OpenStreetMap doesn't answer, what you write goes to `Waiting` and is sent from this device as soon as it can. If you wrote it on another device, `Send from here` takes it over.

In `Maps settings` › `OpenStreetMap` you sign out, pick the `Test server` (for trying things out, it's not the real map) and set the `Upload comment`. That line, "Uploaded using orOS Maps" unless you change it, goes at the end of your new notes (you can edit it each time in `Ending line`) and will be the comment of your map edits.

## Online services and privacy

- **Map images**: OpenStreetMap (`Standard`), OpenStreetMap France (`Humanitarian`) and Esri (`Satellite`).
- **Search and place names**: Photon by komoot. It gets your search text and the centre of the map, or the point you long-pressed.
- **Routes**: the OSRM demo server (car) and FOSSGIS (bike and walk). They get the coordinates of your route points.
- **Notes**: the OpenStreetMap API. In Mapping mode it gets the area you're looking at. What you write is published under your OpenStreetMap name, with the point you chose. The sign-in key stays on this device.

Your location stays on your device unless it's a route point. More in [Privacy](help:t/privacy).

## Sync and backup

Saved places, notes waiting to be sent and the upload comment sync through [orOS sync](help:t/sync). Your OpenStreetMap sign-in does not: sign in once on each device. These stay on this device: your 10 recent places, the last route (it comes back next time you open Maps), map images saved for offline use, and the map position and style.

`Maps settings` shows how many map images are stored (`Clear tile cache`) and the saved route (`Forget saved route`). Maps has no file import or export.

## Keyboard shortcuts

- [[↑]] / [[↓]] move through the suggestions, in the search box and in the route planner.
- [[Enter]] opens the highlighted suggestion, or the first one.
- [[Esc]] closes the suggestions, open panels, the planner or a "choose on map" pick.

## Tips

- [Bookmarks](app:bookmarks) and [Calendar](app:calendar) can open a place in Maps.
- Before a trip, look around the area while you're online. Those map images then work offline too.

## Limits

- Search and new routes need a connection. Offline you get cached areas, saved places and your last route.
- Navigation needs GPS, and Maps has to stay open.
- The routing servers are free public services and can sometimes be slow or down.
