Every way to get orOS. orOS is free forever (MIT): every download here costs nothing.

Use it in your browser

Available

Open orOS and start. Nothing to download, works offline after the first visit, your data stays on your device.

Install it as an app

Available

Windows, Linux, Chromebook, Android: open useoros.online in Chrome, Edge or Brave and choose Install orOS in the address bar or the browser menu. orOS then gets its own window and icon, and can open your files.

orOS Portable (USB stick)

Not sure? Take the full edition. Pick lite only for a small download or a small stick.

Stick: 32 GB or more recommended (64 GB is ideal and usually faster), 8 GB minimum. Format it as exFAT, unzip, copy everything inside the orOS-Portable folder to the stick, then double-click orOS.exe (Windows) or run ./orOS (Linux). Optional: orOS-sticktest checks whether a stick is fast enough.

orOS Live USB (bootable)

Beta · coming soon

Start any PC straight into orOS. Write the ISO (about 1.2 GB) to a USB stick (8 GB or more) with Rufus or balenaEtcher, then boot from it (F12/F11/Esc boot menu). Works offline; your data is kept on the stick when you add a persistence partition (Rufus: “Persistent partition size”). Secure Boot supported. Wi-Fi: Ctrl+Alt+F2. The power button shuts down. For 64-bit Intel/AMD PCs; not for Macs with Apple chips.

orOS Live USB (bootable)

Beta · σύντομα

Ξεκίνα οποιονδήποτε υπολογιστή κατευθείαν στο orOS. Γράψε το ISO (περίπου 1,2 GB) σε USB stick (8 GB ή περισσότερο) με το Rufus ή το balenaEtcher και κάνε εκκίνηση από αυτό (μενού εκκίνησης με F12/F11/Esc). Δουλεύει χωρίς internet. Τα δεδομένα σου μένουν στο stick αν προσθέσεις persistence partition (Rufus: “Persistent partition size”). Υποστηρίζει Secure Boot. Wi-Fi: Ctrl+Alt+F2. Το κουμπί λειτουργίας κλείνει τον υπολογιστή. Για υπολογιστές 64-bit Intel/AMD, όχι για Mac με chip Apple.
