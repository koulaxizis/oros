# Montage
Montage is a video editor. Put your videos, pictures, music and text on a timeline, trim and arrange them, add transitions and titles, and export a video file. Everything happens in your browser, even offline.

## Getting started
1. Tap or click `New project`, give it a `Name`, pick a `Shape` (`Wide 16:9` for a screen, `Tall 9:16` for a phone story, `Square 1:1` or `Portrait 4:5`) and `Frames per second`, then `Create`.
2. Tap `Media` and pick videos or pictures. They line up one after another on the `Main` row.
3. Press `Play` to watch, then `Export` when you are happy.

Your projects are listed on the first screen. Tap a project to open it; its `More` button lets you `Rename`, `Duplicate` or `Delete` it (with `Undo` for a few seconds).

## The timeline
The timeline has five rows: `Text`, `Overlay`, `Main`, `Music` and `Voice`. The playhead stays in the middle; scroll the timeline to move through your video. `Zoom in` and `Zoom out` change how much you see; on a computer, Ctrl + mouse wheel zooms too.

- Tap a clip to select it. The tools bar then shows what you can do with it; `Done` deselects.
- Drag a clip to move it. On the `Main` row this changes the order; on the other rows it changes when the clip starts. Clips snap to the playhead and to the edges of other clips.
- Drag the left or right edge of a selected clip to trim it.
- `Split` cuts the clip under the playhead in two.

## Edit clips
With a clip selected:
- `Speed` plays it faster or slower. Speed also changes the pitch of the sound.
- `Volume` (up to 200 %), `Fade` (`Fade in`, `Fade out`) and `Mute` for its sound. `Extract sound` puts the sound of a video on the `Music` row (or `Voice`, if `Music` is taken there) so you can move it on its own.
- `Transition` into the clip: `Dissolve`, `Through black`, `Slide`, `Wipe`, `Push up`, `Zoom` or `Circle`, with a `Length`.
- `Adjust`: `Brightness`, `Contrast`, `Saturation`, `Warmth` and a `Filter` such as `Mono`, `Sepia`, `Film`, `Vintage` or `Golden`. `Open a .cube file` adds a `Colour look (.cube)`: a 3D LUT from a colour-grading app or a LUT pack, up to 64 points a side. It is kept like a picture (on this device, or synced with the project's media); `Remove the look` takes it off.
- `Frame`: `Whole picture` or `Fill the frame`, `Rotate` and `Mirror`.
- `To overlay` turns a clip into a picture in picture on top of the main video; `Position` sets `Left – right`, `Top – bottom`, `Size` and `Opacity`. `To main` puts it back.
- `Freeze frame` (videos on the `Main` row) holds the picture at the playhead for 2 seconds: the clip splits there and a still of that frame, at the video's full size, goes in between. Change its `Duration` like any picture.
- `Reverse` (videos) plays the clip backwards, without sound; `Forwards` turns it back.
- `Duplicate` and `Delete` (with `Undo`).

## Keyframes
`Keyframes` makes a clip move, grow, fade or change volume while it plays (a slow zoom on a photo, a picture that slides in, music that rises).
1. Select the clip and tap `Keyframes`. The panel shows the values at the playhead.
2. Change `Left – right`, `Top – bottom`, `Size`, `Opacity` or (for clips with sound) `Volume`: a key is set at the playhead.
3. Move with `+½ s`, `−½ s`, `Previous key` and `Next key`, and change values again. Between two keys Montage changes them at an even pace; before the first key and after the last one they stay put.

`Add key` keeps the current values at the playhead, `Remove key` takes it away, `Remove all keys` starts over. Small diamonds on the clip in the timeline show its keys. Keys belong to the clip: `Split` and `Duplicate` keep the motion as it was, but trimming the start of a clip shifts its keys with it. A clip can have up to 60 keys.

## Text, music and voice
- `Text` adds a title at the playhead. `Edit text` changes the words, `Font`, `Text size`, `Colour`, `Outline`, `Box behind the text`, `Alignment` and `Entrance` (`Fade`, `Rise`, `Pop`). Drag its edges to make it show longer or shorter.
- `Sound` adds music or a sound file at the playhead, on `Music` or `Voice`.
- `Voice` records your voice from the microphone. After a 3-2-1 count the video plays from the playhead and you speak over it; `Stop` puts the take on the `Voice` row (or `Music`, if `Voice` is taken there). Use headphones so the video's sound does not get into the recording. The first time, the browser asks to let orOS use the microphone.
- Sound clips show their waveform, so you can see where the loud and quiet parts are.
- `Overlay` adds a video or picture on top of the main video.
- Pictures and texts have a `Duration` instead of a speed.
- Drag a text onto the `Main` row to make a title card between clips; there `Background` sets the colour behind it.
- In `Project settings`, `Lower the music when someone speaks` (`A little`, `Medium` or `A lot`) makes the `Music` row quieter while anything plays on the `Voice` row, with a short smooth slope down and back up. You hear it in the preview and in the exported file.

## Subtitles
`Subtitles` (with nothing selected) works with the texts on the `Text` row:
- `Add a subtitle` puts a text at the playhead in subtitle style: small white letters with a black outline along the bottom of the picture.
- `Open an SRT file` adds every line of an SRT (or WebVTT) subtitle file as its own text at the right time. You can edit, move or delete each one like any text.
- `Save as SRT` writes all texts on the `Text` row as an SRT file, for YouTube or a video player.

## Grab a frame
With nothing selected, `Grab frame` saves the picture at the playhead as a PNG in Files › Pictures and opens it in [Photo Lab](app:photolab), where you can edit it.

## Export
`Export` opens `Export video`. Pick a `Size` (720p, 1080p or 4K, as your device allows) and, if you like, `Also keep a copy in Files › Videos`. Montage makes an MP4 file when your browser can, otherwise a WebM file.

Choose `Sound only` to get just the mixed sound of the project (music, voice, clip sound, with every volume, fade and ducking) as an M4A file, or Ogg or WAV where the browser cannot make M4A. `Also keep a copy in Files › Music` keeps it in orOS too.

Choose `GIF` for a silent looping picture for chats and web pages: `Small (320 px)` or `Medium (480 px)` on the long side, 12 frames a second, up to 30 seconds. GIF files get big quickly, so keep it short. `Also keep a copy in Files › Pictures` keeps it in orOS too. When it is ready you can `Play it` or `Save` it to your device. Keep the app open while it exports; `Cancel` stops it.

## Sync and backup
The edit itself (clips, cuts, texts, settings) travels with orOS sync to your other devices. The media files do not: by default your videos stay on the device you added them on, because big files are slow to upload.

In `Project settings` you can switch on `Sync this project's media`. Montage then copies its files to Files › Assets and Vault Drive syncs them, encrypted. On another device, a file that is not there shows as `Missing on this device`; `Find file` lets you point to it again (a different file of the same kind works too).

`Free space` removes media that no project on this device uses.

>! Clearing your browser's site data removes the media stored on this device. Keep the original files, or switch on media sync for projects that matter.

## Keyboard shortcuts
- [[Space]] or [[K]]: play or pause.
- [[←]] / [[→]]: one frame back or forward; with [[Shift]], one second.
- [[Home]] / [[End]]: start or end.
- [[S]]: split; [[Delete]]: delete the selected clip.
- [[Ctrl+Z]] undo, [[Ctrl+Y]] or [[Ctrl+Shift+Z]] redo.
- [[+]] / [[-]]: zoom the timeline.
- [[Esc]]: deselect or close a window.

## Tips
> The preview and the export use the same picture recipe, so what you see while editing is what you get in the file.

- For a phone story, start the project as `Tall 9:16`; you can still change the `Shape` later in `Project settings`.
- Adding the same file again, even to another project, does not take extra space: Montage keeps one copy.

## Limits
- One file can be up to 4 GB. Very long 4K projects need a lot of browser storage.
- Some browsers cannot make 4K or MP4 files; Montage offers only what your browser can do.
- Pictures other than JPEG and PNG are turned into JPEG when you add them.
