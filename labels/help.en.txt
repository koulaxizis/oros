# Label Maker
Type a word and it is pressed letter by letter into plastic tape, with raised white letters like the old embossed labels.

## Getting started
Type in the box under the tape. Every letter is pressed into the tape as you type, and the tape grows with it.
- The tape takes capital letters only, Latin and Greek, without accents. Small letters become capitals.
- Digits and these symbols work too: & - . , / : ! ? # + = ' ( ) * @ € and ♥ ★ ☺. Anything else is left out.
- A label takes up to 40 letters. The counter in the box shows how many you have used.
- In Greek text, ; becomes a question mark.

## The letter wheel
The wheel button at the top shows the letter wheel, as on the real machine. The keyboard still works next to it.
- Turn the wheel with a finger or the mouse, with the mouse wheel, or with the arrow keys. The letter under the pointer is the one that gets pressed.
- Tap the handle in the middle, or `Press`, to press that letter. With the wheel selected, Enter or Space presses too.
- `ABC`, `ΑΒΓ` and `123 &?` choose which letters are on the wheel.
- `Space` adds a gap between words and `Remove last` takes back the last letter.

## Tape and ends
- `Tape` sets the colour: red, black, blue, green, yellow, white or clear. Yellow and white tape have black letters.
- `Width` is 9 or 12 mm. The letters are half as tall as the tape.
- `Ends` cuts the tape straight or round, and `Margin` sets how much blank tape is left on each side.
- The letters are pressed a little unevenly, as on the real tape. The same label always looks the same.
- The speaker button turns on the click of every letter. Sound is off at first.

## My labels
- `Save label` (or Enter in the box) keeps the label under `My labels`. Changes to an open saved label are saved as you go.
- `New label` starts an empty tape with the same colour and size.
- In the list, tap a label to open it. The copy button makes a duplicate, and the × deletes it; `Undo` brings it back.
- `Recent` shows the last 10 labels you made on this device, saved or not. Tap one to start a new label from it.

## Download, copy and print
- `Download PNG` saves the label as an image with a transparent background, sharp enough to print.
- `Copy image` puts the image on the clipboard, where your browser allows it.
- `Print` makes an A4 sheet at real size, with a dashed line round each label to cut along. Tick labels in `My labels` to print them together; with none ticked, the label on the tape is printed. `Copies of each` sets how many of each.
- A label longer than the sheet is wide is printed upright.

## orOS file
- `orOS file (.oroslabels)` saves a sheet of labels in one file: the labels you ticked in `My labels`, or the label on the tape when none is ticked, with `Copies of each`.
- The file is a ZIP. Unzip it and you find the labels in content.json, a picture of them in preview/thumbnail.png and a list any spreadsheet opens in preview/labels.csv.
- `Open an orOS file…` (under `My labels`) adds the labels of a file to `My labels` as new labels, ticked and ready to print. Nothing you already have is changed. A .oroslabels file opened with orOS from your device opens the same way.
- `Unzipped orOS folder…` opens a .oroslabels file that was unzipped, even after you changed content.json by hand.

## From Storage
In Storage, the tag button on a space, a room, a piece of furniture or a shelf opens the Label Maker with its name already on the tape.

## Sync and backup
- `My labels` follow you to all your devices through orOS sync.
- Recent labels, sound, the wheel and your last colour and size stay on this device only.

## Tips
> Print a sheet of labels on sticker paper and cut along the dashed lines.
- Choose the clear tape for a label that shows what is under it.

## Limits
- Up to 300 saved labels, each up to 40 letters.
- When the same label is changed on two devices before they sync, the most recent change wins.
