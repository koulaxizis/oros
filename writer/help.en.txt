# Writer
Writer is a word processor with tabs, footnotes, comments, version history and export to Word, PDF and more.

## Getting started
Just start typing; everything saves by itself.

- Each open document is a tab. Tap `+` (`New document`) for a new one, or press [[Ctrl+Alt+T]].
- Double-click a tab, or press [[F2]], to give the document a title.
- Closing a tab doesn't delete anything. The `Documents` button left of the tabs lists every document, with search, to open or delete (with a few seconds to Undo).

## Write and format
- The toolbar has Undo and Redo, a paragraph style list (`Normal text`, `Heading 1` to `Heading 4`, `Quote`, `Code block`), bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, indent, lists, alignment, `Horizontal rule` and `Page break`.
- `Special characters` inserts Greek letters, maths, arrows, currency, punctuation, symbols and emoji.
- `Insert` (the plus button next to the paragraph style list) adds a link, an image, a table, a horizontal rule, a page break, a footnote or special characters, on phones and tablets too. On a computer, Alt+right-click in the text also opens a quick menu with formatting, `Insert link`, `Insert image` and `Insert table`.
- With the cursor in a table, a strip of table tools appears under the toolbar: `Row above`, `Row below`, `Column left`, `Column right`, `Delete row`, `Delete column`, `Header row` and `Delete table`.
- Pasted images, such as screenshots, are shrunk to save space.
- `Find and replace` (or [[Ctrl+F]]) has `Match case`, `Whole word`, a format filter (for example `Bold only`), `Replace` and `Replace all`. Undo takes a replacement back; right after `Replace all`, one Undo takes back all of it.
- In `Settings`, `Smart typography` turns -- into a dash, ... into an ellipsis and straight quotes into curly ones. `Auto-correction rules` replaces text such as (c) with © when you press space. You can add your own rules. `Typewriter sound while typing` (off at first) plays a soft key click as you type and a carriage return on Enter.

## Focus and statistics
- `View` (the eye button) has `Focus mode`: the tabs and toolbars hide and all the text dims except the paragraph you are in, or the sentence with `Focus on the sentence`. Leave it with the round × button, [[Esc]] or [[Ctrl+Alt+F]].
- `Typewriter scrolling` keeps the line you are typing in the middle of the screen.
- `Show word count` puts a small word count in the corner (with a selection, its words too). Tap it, or `Statistics…`, for words, characters with and without spaces, paragraphs, sentences, reading time and speaking time, for the document and the selection.
- These view choices are kept on each device; they don't sync.

## Spelling, tracked changes and mail merge
- The `Review` button (AB with a tick) holds spelling, tracked changes and mail merge.
- `Check spelling` underlines misspelt English and Greek words with a wavy red line. Right-click a word (or tap it on a phone) for suggestions, `Add to my dictionary` or `Ignore`. The underlines are only on screen: they are not saved, printed or exported.
- `Spelling of this document` offers four choices. `English and Greek (automatic)` checks each word in its own alphabet. The others are `English only`, `Greek only` and `Don’t check this document`. Greek words are checked with their accents: "καλημερα" is marked and "καλημέρα" is offered first. Words in capitals don't need accents.
- `My dictionary…` lists the words you added; you can add or remove words there. Your dictionary syncs to your other devices.
- The dictionaries download the first time they are needed (about 0.2 MB for English and 2.1 MB for Greek) and then work offline. Until then, or if spelling is off in `Settings`, the browser uses its own spell check, if it has one.
- `Track changes` marks what you type as an insertion (underlined) and what you delete as a deletion (struck through). Each change carries your name and the time, in one colour per author. Set your name in `Settings` under `Your name for tracked changes`; otherwise the device's name is used.
- `Changes…` opens the changes panel. Tap a change to find it in the text. Each change has `Accept` and `Reject`, and the panel has `Accept all` and `Reject all`.
- Tracked changes are saved and synced with the document. Word (.docx) export keeps them as Word tracked changes, and Word files with tracked changes open with them. The other export formats save the text as if all changes were accepted.
- `Mail merge…` fills placeholders such as {{Name}} or {{City}} in your document. The data comes from [Contacts](app:contacts) (all contacts, or one label) or from a CSV file. A field shows green when the data has it and red when it doesn't. Use the arrows to preview each record.
- The result can be `One PDF`, `A PDF for each record (ZIP)` or `One new document`, with a page break between records. From Contacts you can use FullName, FirstName, LastName, Company, JobTitle, Email, Phone, Address, City, PostalCode, Country and Birthday. Greek names such as {{Όνομα}} or {{Πόλη}} also work.

## Notes, comments and structure
- `Footnotes`: tap `Add footnote` to place a numbered note at the cursor and type it in the side panel. The footnotes and endnotes of a Word file you open come in as footnotes.
- `Comments`: select some text, then `Add comment`. A comment must stay inside one paragraph.
- `Table of contents` lists your headings. Tap one to jump to it, or `Insert inline` to put a contents list into the text.
- `Document metadata` holds `Title`, `Author`, `Tags` and `Category`.
- `Page settings` sets `Paper size`, `Margins (mm)`, `Header` and `Footer`.

## Versions, templates and goals
- `Version history` keeps automatic versions while you write (at most one every 5 minutes, the newest 8). Tap `Snapshot` to save one yourself; those are never dropped. `Restore` saves the current text as a snapshot first.
- `Templates` has built-in ones (`Essay`, `Formal letter`, `Novel chapter`, `Screenplay`, `Poem`, `Meeting notes`, `Blank`) and yours. `Use` starts a new document from one; `Save current as template` adds yours.
- `Writing goal` sets a target in words, characters, paragraphs, minutes or session words. A bar at the bottom shows your progress. With `Lock editing after goal`, typing stops once you reach it.

## Import and export
- `Export` saves the document as plain text, Markdown, HTML, RTF, Word (.docx), PDF or an orOS Writer file (.orosdoc, which keeps pictures, footnotes, comments and metadata). Word (.docx) keeps headings, bold, italic, alignment, real numbered and bulleted lists (with their start number and 1, a, A, i, I or α), pictures at the size you see them and picture captions, so it opens in Word or LibreOffice looking the same (LibreOffice shows α lists as 1, 2, 3) and comes back into Writer the same; footnotes and comments go at the end. PDF keeps the same, with the bold and italic letters of Noto Sans. An .orosdoc is an open ZIP file: unzip it and you find `content.json` (the document), `media` (its pictures) and `preview/document.html`, which opens in any browser. `Database (JSON)` saves all your documents, templates and settings.
- The PDF keeps headings, bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, links, alignment, lists, quotes, code, tables and images, and uses the paper size, margins, header and footer from `Page settings`, with page numbers.
- `Import` opens OROSDOC, DOCX, ODT, RTF, HTML, TXT, MD and PDF files, with their headings, bold, italic, underline, alignment, lists and numbering (a numbered list goes on from where it stopped, in 1, a, A, i or α). You choose `New tab (recommended)`, `Append to current` or `Replace current`. You can also drop a file onto Writer. `Open an unzipped .orosdoc folder…` brings back a .orosdoc that was unzipped, even after you changed a picture in it.
- `From Google Docs…` in the same dialog signs in to Google, lets you pick one document in Google Drive (a Google Doc, or a DOCX, ODT, RTF, TXT, MD or HTML file there) and brings it in the same way; a Google Doc comes as DOCX. orOS sees only that file, and the sign-in ends right after.
- From [Typewriter](app:typewriter), `Send to Writer` opens a page here as a new document.
- Importing a `Database (JSON)` file merges it. Documents that exist only here are kept, and if a document differs, the older text goes into `Version history`.

## The Assistant
Ask the Assistant to change your documents in words, such as "2026 instead of 2025", "make the headings centred", "turn the list into a numbered list" or "set the paper to A5". It shows the changes on a card first; `Apply` makes them here and opens the document. `Undo` on the message here, or on the Assistant's card, puts the documents back as long as they weren't changed since. See the Assistant's help for everything it can do.

## Sync and backup
With [orOS sync](help:t/sync), your documents (text, footnotes, comments, versions, goals, page settings, tracked changes, spelling language), templates, auto-correction rules, your spelling dictionary and settings reach your other devices. Which tabs are open stays on each device. Deleting a document removes it everywhere. See also [Backup](help:t/backup).

## Keyboard shortcuts
- [[Ctrl+S]] saves now (Writer also saves by itself).
- [[Ctrl+F]] find and replace.
- [[Ctrl+Alt+T]] new document, [[Ctrl+Alt+W]] close the current tab.
- [[F2]] rename the current document.
- [[Ctrl+Alt+F]] focus mode on or off.
- [[Ctrl+Z]] right after `Replace all` takes back every replacement at once.

## Tips
- With the Assistant installed, the sparkle button sends the selected text (or the whole document) to the Assistant to summarize, rephrase or correct it. Nothing is sent until you press `Send` there; copy the answer back with `Copy`.
- Use heading styles: they build the table of contents. Take a `Snapshot` before big changes.

## Limits
- If two devices change the text of the same document before syncing, the newer text wins.
- A PDF has no paragraphs or styles inside, so Writer works them out from the page: bigger letters become headings, a short line all in bold a smaller heading, and lines that reach the edge one paragraph. Repeated page headers, footers and page numbers are left out. Pictures and tables in a PDF are not brought in, and a scanned PDF (pictures of pages, no text) can't be opened: make it searchable first in [Scanner & PDF](app:scanpdf). Check the result against the PDF.
- RTF and PDF tables come as plain text.
- Table tools (adding or deleting rows and columns, the header row) can't be undone with Undo.
- Sentence focus needs a recent browser; older ones light the whole paragraph instead.
- In a PDF, bold and italic letters outside Latin and Greek (for example Cyrillic) are drawn in the regular font, with bold thickened; merged table cells are drawn as single cells; an image from another website may be shown only as a placeholder.
- Spelling covers English and Greek only. It skips words with digits, mixed capitals (such as iPhone) and capitals of four letters or fewer (such as NASA). Long compound words that are not in the dictionary are marked. `Ignore` lasts until you close Writer.
- Track changes records typed and deleted text only. Splitting or joining paragraphs and formatting changes are not tracked, and text pasted while tracking goes in as plain text. While tracking is on, Undo and Redo work in whole steps of typing or deleting.
- In mail merge, a placeholder must not be split by formatting (for example half of it in bold). PDF outputs stop at 500 records. The new document keeps the text only, without footnotes or comments.
