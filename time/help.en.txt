# Time

A big clock in five styles, a world clock, sunrise, sunset and the moon phase, plus alarms, a timer, a stopwatch, a Pomodoro timer and a stage timer for conferences. Tap the clock in the orOS top bar to open it.

## Getting started

Pick a clock style under the big clock: `Digital`, `Analog`, `Binary`, `Flip` or `Neon`. Below the clock you'll see today's date and your UTC offset. Everything uses the 24-hour format.

## World clock

- Tap a quick chip (`+ Athens`, `+ London`, `+ New York`, `+ Tokyo`), or `Add` to choose from the list of time zones.
- Double-click a zone's name to change it (`Change zone`). The × deletes it (`Delete zone`).

## Sun & Moon

This section shows `Sunrise`, `Sunset`, `Solar noon`, `Day length`, where the sun is now, `Moon age`, the moon phase and its `Illumination`. It's all calculated on your device, so it works offline.

It uses the top-bar weather city: the city pinned in [Weather](help:a/weather), or your first city there. To use another place, tap `Set location`, type `Latitude` and `Longitude` or tap `Use device location`, then `Save`. `Use weather location` goes back to the weather location. If you use your device location without saving it, it's only kept until you close the app.

## Alarms and timers

- **`Alarm`**: set a time, add a `Label…` if you like, tick `Daily` for every day, then `Add alarm`. A time that has already passed today is set for tomorrow. The × deletes an alarm.
- **`Timer`**: enter minutes and seconds and tap `Start`. The same button becomes `Stop`, and `Reset` clears it. In the last 60 seconds the countdown fills the screen. Tap it or press [[Esc]] to hide it (the timer keeps going).
- **`Stopwatch`**: `Start`, `Stop` and `Reset`.
- **`Pomodoro`**: set `Work` and `Break` minutes and tap `Start`. When a `Focus` leg ends, a `Break` is ready to start, and so on. The counter shows how many sessions you've finished. `Reset` goes back to `Focus`. With `Log focus in Timesheet` set to a project (or `No project`), each `Focus` leg is added to [Timesheet](app:timesheet) as the task "Pomodoro", even if you close Time; stopping early logs the minutes you focused. The choice stays on this device.

When an alarm, the timer or a Pomodoro leg ends, orOS shows a box with `Snooze 9m` and `Dismiss` and plays a beep. It stops by itself after 30 seconds and also lands in your [notifications](help:t/notifications). It rings in any app, not only in Time.

## Stage timer for conferences

The `Stage` tab is a timer for talks, panels and shows, with a rundown and a big countdown the speaker can read from far away.

- **Event:** tap `New event`, give it a `Name`, a `Date` and, if you like, the time it `Starts at`. Set when the clock turns yellow and red (`Yellow at (min left)`, `Red at (min left)`) and whether it should `Flash when time is up`. `Event settings` changes them later; `Duplicate event` copies an event with its rundown (handy as a template).
- **Rundown:** `Add item` adds a talk or break with a `Title`, a `Speaker` and a `Length`. The `Type` is a `Countdown`, a `Count up`, `Until a clock time` (for example a coffee break until 11:00) or the `Time of day`. Each item can have its own yellow and red times and `Notes (only you see them)`. Tick `Start the next item by itself at zero` for breaks that should flow on. Tap an item to edit it, ↑ and ↓ to move it, ▶ to start from it.
- **Running:** `Start`, `Pause` and `Resume` run the clock of the current item, `Next ▶` moves on (and starts the next one if the clock was running), `◀ Previous` goes back, `−1 min` and `+1 min` change the time left (hold the button for five minutes), and `Restart item` starts the current item again. When the time is up the clock keeps counting in red with a minus sign, so you see how far a speaker ran over.
- **Schedule:** with a start time, each item shows when it was planned and, in colour, when it is now expected to start. The line above the clock shows the planned and expected end of the day and how many minutes you are behind or ahead.
- **Stage view:** `Stage view` fills the screen with only the big clock, the title, the speaker, a progress bar, the next item and the time of day, always on black. Move the mouse or tap to show `Black screen`, `Full screen` and `Close`.
- **Keys and clickers:** [[Space]] start or pause, [[→]] or [[Page Down]] next, [[←]] or [[Page Up]] previous, [[+]] and [[−]] one minute, [[B]] black screen, [[M]] message on or off, [[F]] stage view, [[Esc]] close it. Presenter clickers send Page Up and Page Down, so they work as previous and next.
- **Message to the speaker:** type a message and press `Show` (or [[Enter]]), or tap a ready one such as `Please wrap up` or `Questions now`. Pick a `Colour` and tick `Flash` to make it stand out; `Hide` takes it away. `Save` keeps your own messages as chips (they sync); the × deletes one.
- **Stage display on a second screen:** `Open stage display` opens the speaker's picture in a window of its own. On Chrome or Edge with two monitors it moves to the other screen by itself (the browser asks once); elsewhere drag it there. Double-click it or press [[F]] for full screen. It keeps going even if you close Time or open another app, and `Black screen` blanks it.
- **Stage output:** `Layout` is `Clock, titles and next`, `Clock only` or `Clock and rundown`. `Mirror (for a screen seen in a reflection)` flips the picture. `Logo…` puts your logo in the corner; it stays on this device. The box above the buttons shows the message and the black screen as the stage sees them.
- **Sound:** tick `Sound at yellow, red and zero (this device)` for a short beep at each change. It is off by default, because the laptop on stage is often connected to the speakers.

## Sync and backup

These sync through [orOS sync](help:t/sync): the clock style, world clock zones, Pomodoro lengths and session count, the manual Sun & Moon location, and your alarms (they travel with the orOS settings). Stage events and their rundowns sync too; the item that is running, its clock and the sound choice and what the stage screen shows (message, black screen, layout, logo) stay on this device, so a phone at home never moves the clock on stage. The stopwatch and a Pomodoro leg in progress stay on this device. Time has no import or export.

## Tips

- A Pomodoro leg that ends while Time is closed still counts when you open it again.
- Daily alarms are good for routines like medicine or a stretch break.

## Limits

- Alarms ring only while orOS is open (in a browser tab or as an installed app). If orOS was closed at the alarm time, it rings as soon as you open it again.
- The stopwatch resets when you close the app.
- The world clock offers a fixed list of 21 time zones.
- The Stage clock runs while Time is open. If you close Time, the clock goes on from the right time when you open it again, but sounds and "start the next item by itself" only happen while it is open.
- The stage display window needs pop-ups allowed for orOS. It shows what this device stores, so it belongs on a screen connected to the same computer.
