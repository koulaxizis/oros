# Name Generator
Name Generator comes up with names for your corners of the web, your bio and your stories: usernames, playful titles and fantasy names with an epithet. Keep the ones you like as favourites.

## Getting started
When you open the app you already get 8 new names. Tap or click `New names` (or press [[Enter]] or [[Space]]) for another batch of 8, all different.

Next to each name:
- the copy button puts it on the clipboard;
- the star (`Keep`) adds it to your `Favourites`. Tap the star again to remove it.

## Modes
Pick a mode from the tabs at the top:
- `Handles`: usernames for the web, an adjective plus a noun in Latin letters, up to 24 characters. They show with an "@" but copy without it.
- `Titles`: epithets for your bio, such as a role with an adjective or "of something".
- `Regal pairs`: a made-up fantasy name plus an epithet.

Each mode keeps its own batch while the app is open, so switching tabs doesn't throw away what you had.

## Words and style
Under `Words` choose `English` or `Greek`. It starts in the orOS language. With `Greek`, handles are written in plain greeklish (for example fengari), and Greek titles and epithets match the gender of the name.

For `Handles` only, `Style` adds optional touches; turn on any mix:
- `Leet (a→4)` swaps some letters for numbers (a→4, e→3, i→1, o→0, s→5, t→7).
- `Separators (_ -)` puts `_` or `-` between the two words.
- `Numbers` adds a number at the end.

Changing a word or style option makes a new batch straight away.

## Favourites
`Favourites` are grouped by mode, newest first, with a count at the top. Copy a single name, or `Copy all` to copy every favourite, one per line. The × button removes a favourite; an `Undo` button stays for a few seconds.

## Sync and backup
Your favourites sync between your devices through [orOS sync](help:t/sync); the same name starred on two devices counts once. The mode, language and style choices stay on each device, and the batches themselves are never saved.

There's no import or export; use the orOS [backup](help:t/backup) to keep a copy.

## Keyboard shortcuts
- [[Enter]] or [[Space]]: new names (when no button has focus).
- Arrow keys on the mode tabs: move to the next or previous mode.

## Tips
- Check that a handle is free on the site you want before you get attached to it.
- Use `Copy all` to paste your shortlist into [Notes](app:notes) or a message.

## Limits
- You can keep up to 1,000 favourites.
- You can't edit a name or type your own words; the app picks from its built-in word lists.
