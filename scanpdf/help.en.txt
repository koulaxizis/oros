# Scanner & PDF
Scan paper pages with the camera, put PDFs together, take them apart and set their pages in order, sign them, number their pages, make them smaller, read the text of scanned pages so the PDF can be searched, and make a PDF from pictures. Everything happens on your device: nothing is uploaded, and your own files are never changed.

## Getting started
1. Drop PDFs or pictures on the app, or press `Add PDFs or pictures`.
2. Every page shows as a card with its number. When the pages come from more than one file, a coloured line on top tells the files apart and the card names its file.
3. Put the pages the way you want them (see below).
4. Press `Save PDF` to save every page, in that order, as one PDF.

## Scanning with the camera
Press `Scan with the camera` (the camera button at the top, or on the empty screen). The first time, the browser asks whether the app may use the camera.
1. Lay the page on a darker surface, in good light, and point the camera at it. A gold outline shows the page the app has found.
2. With `Auto` on, the picture is taken by itself once the page is held still. With it off, press the round button.
3. The page is straightened and cleaned up. Choose how it looks:
   - `Document`: white paper and dark ink, shadows taken out. The best choice for most pages.
   - `Grey`, `Black & white` (sharpest text, smallest file) or `Photo` (the colours as they are).
4. Turn it with the arrows if needed, then press `Keep page`. The next page can be scanned right away.
- When the app can't find the page, or found the wrong shape, press `Corners`: drag each corner onto a corner of the page. A magnifier shows the spot under your finger. `Whole picture` uses all of it; `Take again` goes back to the camera.
- The pages scanned so far show in a row at the bottom. Tap one to change its look, corners or turn, or to delete it.
- The light button turns on the phone's flash, when it has one.
- `Choose photos` takes pages from photos you already have, one after the other, the same way.
- When the camera is not allowed or not there, the scanner says so; you can still choose photos.
Press `Add … pages` to put them in the list, after the pages already there. A scanned page keeps its own shape in the PDF, with no margin. Close the scanner with the cross to let the scanned pages go. The camera turns off as soon as you leave the scanner or the app.

## What it reads
- PDF files (.pdf).
- Pictures: JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP and AVIF. Each picture becomes one page.
A photo that the phone stored sideways is turned upright first.

## Selecting pages
- Tap a page to select it; tap it again to let it go. The tick on the corner shows it.
- [[Shift]] and a click selects every page between the last one you tapped and this one.
- The first button in the bar above the pages selects all of them, or none.
The bar says how many pages there are and how many are selected.

## Arranging pages
The buttons of the bar work on the selected pages:
- `Move earlier` and `Move later` move them one place.
- `Rotate left` and `Rotate right` turn them by a quarter.
- `Duplicate` puts a copy right after each one.
- `Save the selected pages as a new PDF` saves only them, in a new file.
- `Delete` takes them out of the list.
To move a page by dragging, take it with the mouse, or on a touch screen by the handle with the six dots in its corner. Selected pages move together when you drag one of them.
`Undo` and `Redo` at the top step back and forward through every change.

## Signing and adding text
Every page has a pen button in its corner. It opens the page large, in the page editor:
- `Signature` puts your signature on the page. The first time, you make one:
  - `Draw`: sign in the box with your finger, a pen or the mouse.
  - `Type`: write your name and it is set in a handwriting-like font.
  - `Photo`: choose a photo of your signature on white paper; the paper becomes see-through and only the ink stays.
  Choose black or blue ink. With `Keep it on this device for next time` ticked, the signature is saved for the next document (up to 6). The next time, `Signature` shows them to choose from, and the bin on each one deletes it.
- `Text` adds a line of text. Type it in the field above the page and choose black, blue or red.
- `Date` adds today's date.
Drag what you added into place. Drag the round handle on its corner to make it bigger or smaller. `Put on every page` copies it to the same place on every other page (for initials on each page). `Remove` takes it away.
The arrows next to the title go to the previous and next page. `Done` keeps everything you added; the cross at the top leaves without keeping it. `Undo` on the main screen takes back everything one `Done` added.
A page with additions has a filled pen button. Additions turn with the page when you rotate it later.
Text is written into the PDF as real text (with the Noto Sans font, Greek included); a signature is a picture.

## Page numbers, watermark and details
The `Options` button at the bottom sets what goes on every page of the PDFs you save or split:
- `Page numbers`: where (bottom or top, left, centre or right), the style (3 or 3 / 12) and the first number. Each part of a split is numbered on its own.
- `Watermark`: a word or two written large and pale across every page, such as COPY or DRAFT. Choose how strong it is.
- `Document details`: the title and author shown in the PDF's properties.
The cards show the page numbers and the watermark as they will be. These choices last until you close the app; picture page choices (below) are also here.

## Merging PDFs
Add two or more PDFs (and pictures, if you like): their pages are put one after the other. Arrange them, then press `Save PDF`. The text, pictures and links of each page stay as they were. Bookmarks and form fields of the original files are not carried into the new PDF.

## Splitting a PDF
Press `Split` and choose how:
- `Each page on its own`: one PDF per page.
- `Every` 2, 3 or more `pages`: a new PDF every few pages.
- `Page ranges`: type them, such as `1-3, 5, 8-`. `8-` means from page 8 to the end. Each range becomes its own PDF.
The window shows how many PDFs come out. They go into one .zip on your device, or into a folder in Files › PDF named after the file. The split always follows the list as it is now, so you can arrange the pages first.

## Pictures to PDF
Add pictures and press `Save PDF`. `Picture pages` in `Options` sets how a picture becomes a page:
- Page size: A4, Letter, or the picture's shape (no empty space around it).
- Orientation: as the picture, portrait or landscape.
- Margin: none, small or normal.
The picture is placed in the middle, as large as fits. The app remembers these choices on this device.

## Saving
`Save PDF` asks for a file name and where it goes:
- `On this device`: the usual save window of your browser, or a download.
- `In Files › PDF`: the PDF goes into Files. When the name is taken, it gets a number, such as "Report (2).pdf".

## Making a PDF smaller
`Make it smaller` in the save window shrinks a PDF for email or upload forms:
- `A little (sharpest)`, `Medium` or `A lot (for email)`: the more it shrinks, the softer the pages look.
Every page becomes a picture, so its own text can no longer be selected. With `Make it searchable` ticked, the text is read first and kept under the pictures, so the smaller PDF can still be searched. Scanned pages and photos shrink the most. When the result would not be smaller (a PDF of plain text often is not), the PDF is saved as it was and the app tells you. Your choice is remembered on this device.

## Reading text: searchable PDFs
A scanned page or a picture is only a picture of words: you can't search it or copy from it. The app can read the words on the device and put them under each page as invisible text, the way office scanners do.
- `Read text` at the bottom reads the pages and shows their text, page by page. Choose the language (`Greek and English`, `Greek` or `English`) and, when pages are selected, whether to read only those.
- `Copy the text` puts it on the clipboard; `Send to an app` hands it to Notes, Writer or another orOS app.
- `Make it searchable` in the save window reads what is not read yet and puts the text under each page. It is ticked by itself when the list has scanned pages or pages already read; the app remembers when you change it.
- A page that was read shows a small T next to its number.
Reading takes a few seconds a page. A bar shows how far it got; `Stop` (or [[Esc]]) ends it and keeps the pages read so far. Pages of a PDF that already has text are not read again: their own text is used.
The first time, the text recognition engine (Tesseract, about 7 MB with Greek and English) is downloaded from orOS's own site; after that it works offline. The pictures never leave your device. Recognition is good on clean, straight, well-lit pages and weaker on handwriting, small print or photos taken at an angle, so check what matters.

## Finding saved PDFs in orOS search
PDFs you save into Files › PDF can be found in the orOS search by their name and by the text read from them. Choosing one opens it here. The list stays on this device (up to 100 PDFs) and is never synced; a PDF you delete or move in Files drops out. `Forget them` in `Options` empties it.

## From Files
In Files, select one or more PDFs, or several pictures, and choose `Open in Scanner & PDF` in the menu or in the selection bar. The app opens with their pages, in the order of the selection.

## Keyboard shortcuts
- [[Ctrl+O]]: add files.
- [[Ctrl+S]]: save PDF.
- [[Ctrl+A]]: select all pages, or none.
- [[Ctrl+Z]] and [[Ctrl+Y]]: undo and redo.
- [[Left]] and [[Right]]: go to the page before or after.
- [[Ctrl+Left]] and [[Ctrl+Right]]: move the page (or the selected pages) one place.
- [[Space]]: select the page; [[Delete]]: delete the selected pages; [[Esc]]: select none.
- [[E]]: open the page editor for the page.
In the page editor:
- [[Tab]] goes from one addition to the next; [[Left]], [[Right]], [[Up]] and [[Down]] move it ([[Shift]] for bigger steps); [[+]] and [[-]] make it bigger or smaller; [[Delete]] removes it.
- [[PageUp]] and [[PageDown]]: the previous and next page. [[Esc]]: let go of the addition, or leave.
In the scanner:
- [[Tab]] goes through the buttons; on a corner, [[Left]], [[Right]], [[Up]] and [[Down]] move it ([[Shift]] for bigger steps).
- [[Esc]] steps back, and on the camera leaves the scanner.
While text is read, [[Esc]] stops it.

## Privacy and sync
Everything happens on your device; no file is sent anywhere. The app keeps no data of its own and has nothing to sync: PDFs you save into Files are synced like the rest of Files when Vault Drive is on. Only your choices (picture pages, where to save, how much smaller, Auto in the scanner, the reading language, Make it searchable), the list of saved PDFs for search and the signatures you keep are kept, on this device. Signatures are never synced, on purpose: they stay on the device where you made them.

## Limits
- PDFs locked with a password can't be changed: their pages can't be copied without it.
- A file can be up to 200 MB, and the list can hold up to 2,000 pages.
- Large files take a moment to read and to save; keep the app open until it is done.
