# Scanner & PDF
Put PDFs together, take them apart and set their pages in order, and make a PDF from pictures. Everything happens on your device: nothing is uploaded, and your own files are never changed. Scanning with the camera and making scanned pages searchable come in later versions.

## Getting started
1. Drop PDFs or pictures on the app, or press `Add PDFs or pictures`.
2. Every page shows as a card with its number. When the pages come from more than one file, a coloured line on top tells the files apart and the card names its file.
3. Put the pages the way you want them (see below).
4. Press `Save PDF` to save every page, in that order, as one PDF.

## What it reads
- PDF files (.pdf).
- Pictures: JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP and AVIF. Each picture becomes one page.
A photo that the phone stored sideways is turned upright first.

## Selecting pages
- Tap a page to select it; tap it again to let it go. The tick on the corner shows it.
- [[Shift]] and a click selects every page between the last one you tapped and this one.
- The first button in the bar above the pages selects all of them, or none.
The bar says how many pages there are and how many are selected.

## Arranging pages
The buttons of the bar work on the selected pages:
- `Move earlier` and `Move later` move them one place.
- `Rotate left` and `Rotate right` turn them by a quarter.
- `Duplicate` puts a copy right after each one.
- `Save the selected pages as a new PDF` saves only them, in a new file.
- `Delete` takes them out of the list.
To move a page by dragging, take it with the mouse, or on a touch screen by the handle with the six dots in its corner. Selected pages move together when you drag one of them.
`Undo` and `Redo` at the top step back and forward through every change.

## Merging PDFs
Add two or more PDFs (and pictures, if you like): their pages are put one after the other. Arrange them, then press `Save PDF`. The text, pictures and links of each page stay as they were. Bookmarks and form fields of the original files are not carried into the new PDF.

## Splitting a PDF
Press `Split` and choose how:
- `Each page on its own`: one PDF per page.
- `Every` 2, 3 or more `pages`: a new PDF every few pages.
- `Page ranges`: type them, such as `1-3, 5, 8-`. `8-` means from page 8 to the end. Each range becomes its own PDF.
The window shows how many PDFs come out. They go into one .zip on your device, or into a folder in Files › PDF named after the file. The split always follows the list as it is now, so you can arrange the pages first.

## Pictures to PDF
Add pictures and press `Save PDF`. The button `Picture pages` at the bottom sets how a picture becomes a page:
- Page size: A4, Letter, or the picture's shape (no empty space around it).
- Orientation: as the picture, portrait or landscape.
- Margin: none, small or normal.
The picture is placed in the middle, as large as fits. The app remembers these choices on this device.

## Saving
`Save PDF` asks for a file name and where it goes:
- `On this device`: the usual save window of your browser, or a download.
- `In Files › PDF`: the PDF goes into Files. When the name is taken, it gets a number, such as "Report (2).pdf".

## From Files
In Files, select one or more PDFs, or several pictures, and choose `Open in Scanner & PDF` in the menu or in the selection bar. The app opens with their pages, in the order of the selection.

## Keyboard shortcuts
- [[Ctrl+O]]: add files.
- [[Ctrl+S]]: save PDF.
- [[Ctrl+A]]: select all pages, or none.
- [[Ctrl+Z]] and [[Ctrl+Y]]: undo and redo.
- [[Left]] and [[Right]]: go to the page before or after.
- [[Ctrl+Left]] and [[Ctrl+Right]]: move the page (or the selected pages) one place.
- [[Space]]: select the page; [[Delete]]: delete the selected pages; [[Esc]]: select none.

## Privacy and sync
Everything happens on your device; no file is sent anywhere. The app keeps no data of its own and has nothing to sync: PDFs you save into Files are synced like the rest of Files when Vault Drive is on. Only your choices (picture pages, where to save) are kept, on this device.

## Limits
- PDFs locked with a password can't be changed: their pages can't be copied without it.
- A file can be up to 200 MB, and the list can hold up to 2,000 pages.
- Large files take a moment to read and to save; keep the app open until it is done.
