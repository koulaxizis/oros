# Whack-a-Mole
Moles pop out of nine holes for a moment. Whack as many as you can before the 30 seconds run out.

## How to play
Pick a level and press `Start`. A 3-2-1 countdown runs, then moles start popping up. Tap or click a mole to whack it; a hit counts the moment you press, not when you let go.

- A mole is worth 1 point.
- A golden mole is rarer and hides faster, but it's worth 3 points.
- On `Medium` and `Hard` a hedgehog shows up now and then. Don't hit it: it costs 2 points (your score never drops below 0).

The last 10 seconds run a little faster. When the time is up, a window shows your points, the moles you whacked, your accuracy and, where they appear, the hedgehogs you hit.

## Controls
- Tap or click a hole.
- Keys [[1]] to [[9]] whack a hole, laid out like a numeric keypad: [[7]] [[8]] [[9]] is the top row, [[1]] [[2]] [[3]] the bottom.
- [[Space]] starts a round.
- [[N]] starts a round, or stops the one that's running (`Stop the round (N)`). A stopped round doesn't count.

If you switch to another app, the round pauses and resumes after a short countdown. Closing the app drops the round.

## Levels and scoring
`Easy`, `Medium` and `Hard` change how long a mole stays up and how many can be up at once; `Easy` has no hedgehog. Finish or stop a round before changing the level. `Records` shows your best score and the number of rounds for each level.

## Sync
Your best scores and round counts sync between your devices through [orOS sync](help:t/sync). `Reset records` in `Records` deletes them on every device, after you confirm. The level you picked and the sound button stay on this device.

## Tips
- On a keyboard, rest your fingers on the numeric keypad: the keys match the holes.
- On `Hard` up to three moles can be up at once, so go for the golden ones first.
- The sound button in the toolbar turns the sound effects on or off.
