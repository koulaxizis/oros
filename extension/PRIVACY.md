# Send to orOS: privacy

The "Send to orOS" add-on does not collect, store, sell or send any
personal data.

- When you press its button, use its shortcut or its right-click menu,
  it reads the address and title of that one page (or the address of
  the link you clicked) and opens orOS in a new tab with them in the
  address. Nothing is saved until you press Save in orOS.
- It stores one setting in your browser: the orOS address to open.
- It makes no network requests of its own, has no analytics and no
  access to any other tab or site.

orOS itself keeps your data on your device; its optional sync is
encrypted on your device before it leaves it.
