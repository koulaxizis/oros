# Together
Count the time you have spent together with someone you love: days, weeks, months and years, a live counter, and the milestones coming up (every month, every anniversary, every 100 days, every 50 weeks and a few fun numbers). Inspired by the Android app Since Us.

## Getting started
- Tap `Start` (or `+` at the top) and choose who: `From Contacts` picks people from your [Contacts](app:contacts), or write a name and tap `Add`.
- Choose the day it started (`Together since`) and, if you like, the time. With a time the hours, minutes and seconds count from that moment.
- `Save`. The home screen shows the time together, the next milestone and what comes after it.
- A day in the future works too: the counter then counts down to it (a wedding, a move together).

## Several relationships
- `+` adds another relationship, each with its own day and photo. Tabs at the top switch between them.
- A relationship can have more than one person (up to eight).
- `Archived` keeps a relationship but shows it last.
- `Delete` in the editor removes the relationship from all your devices. Its photos stay in Files.

## Names, pronouns and discreet mode
- A person picked from Contacts always shows the name Contacts has now. Nothing is written into Contacts.
- `A name for the two of you` replaces the list of names, for example "Anna & Chris".
- `Settings` › `Your name` puts your own name next to theirs.
- `Their pronouns` is optional and shown under the names.
- `Discreet` shows initials only, for when other people can see your screen.

## Photo
- `From this device` picks a picture: it is redrawn at most 2048 pixels wide and saved as a JPEG in Files › `Pictures` › `Together`, so the camera data inside it (EXIF, location) is not kept and the browser storage does not fill up.
- `From orOS Files` picks a picture you already have in Files › `Pictures`.
- `Framing` moves the picture up or down in the card.
- Sync carries the relationship, not the picture: Vault Drive brings the file to your other devices.

## Milestones
- Months (1 month, 2 months…), anniversaries, every 100 days, every 50 weeks and fun numbers: 222, 1111 or 12345 days, 10 000 hours, a million minutes, a billion seconds.
- A date that does not exist in a month moves to its last day: together since 31 January means 1 month on 28 or 29 February, and 29 February becomes 28 February in other years.
- `Settings` › `Milestones to show` switches each kind on or off.

## Moments
- `Moments` (next to `Time together`) is your timeline: the first date, the first trip, the day you said yes. Newest first.
- `+ Add a moment`: a day, what happened, a note, a place and a photo (the same way as the cover photo, kept in Files).
- `Remember it every year` brings it back in the Calendar, in Today and in the morning notice, with how many years ago it was.
- Tap a moment to change or delete it. Its photo stays in Files.

## Special days
- Under the milestones, `+ Special day` adds a day to count down to: the first kiss, a concert, the day you move in. `Every year` makes it come back each year.
- Days that have passed and do not repeat stay in the list as `Was on …`.

## Calendar, Today and reminders
- The Calendar shows anniversaries, every 100 days, every 50 weeks, fun numbers, moments and special days marked every year (chip `Together`). A tap opens Together there.
- [Today](app:today) can list them too: Together starts switched off there, because it is private; switch it on in Today's settings.
- `Settings` › `Reminders`: one notice in the morning (from the hour you choose) on a day with something to celebrate, while orOS is open. Choose which kinds remind you; months and fun numbers start off.
- Discreet relationships show initials in all of these.

## Gifts and other apps
- `Gift ideas`: write ideas as they come. `Send to To-Do` puts the open ones in a new To-Do list "Gifts · …" (To-Do asks before adding). With no ideas it sends one task per birthday or anniversary in the next 60 days.
- `Bought` ticks an idea and offers to put what it cost in [Budget](app:budget) as a gift expense (Budget opens the form; you save it there).
- The birthdays of the people linked from Contacts show among the special days. `Settings` › `Reminders` adds a gift reminder 3 to 21 days before each birthday and your anniversary.
- `Plan a date` opens a new Calendar event; `Anniversary in Contacts` opens the person's contact with your anniversary added.
- In a moment: `Write in Notes` starts a new page with it, `Send to an app…` sends it anywhere in orOS, `Cost to Budget` notes what it cost.

## PIN lock
- `Settings` › `PIN lock` adds a PIN screen when Together opens, and again after a minute away.
- It is a privacy screen on this device, not encryption. The PIN lives only on this device.

## Your data
- Everything stays in orOS on your devices. With sync on in [Settings](app:settings), it follows you to your other devices, encrypted.
- The menu search can find your relationships once you switch Together on in the search settings (it starts switched off, because it is private).
