# Budget
Budget tracks your income and expenses one month at a time, with categories, monthly limits, recurring entries and charts.

## Getting started
Tap or click `New`, choose `Expense` or `Income`, type the `Amount`, pick a `Category` and press `Save`. You can also set the `Date` and add a `Note`. Amounts can be typed as `12,50` or `12.50`.

The top shows the month, with arrows to move between months; `This month` brings you back. Under it, `Income`, `Expenses` and `Balance` add up the month you're looking at.

## Entries
The `Entries` tab lists the month's entries by day, with a `Day total`. Tap an entry to edit or delete it; a delete shows `Undo` for a few seconds.

`Search all entries…` looks through every month, not just the open one. The menu next to it shows `All entries`, `Expenses only` or `Income only`.

## Monthly limits
On `Limits`, `Set limit` gives an expense category a monthly limit, and `All expenses` takes an overall one. Limits apply to every month. Each bar shows how much is spent and how much is left, and turns to `Close to the limit` at 80% and `Over the limit` after 100%. When you pass a limit in the current month, orOS sends you one [notification](help:t/notifications).

## Recurring
Rent, salary, subscriptions: on `Recurring`, add them once with `Every week`, `Every month` or `Every year`, a `First date` and an optional `Last date`. When Budget is open, each one becomes a normal entry on its day, marked `Recurring`. Deleting a recurring entry stops new ones; entries already added stay. Upcoming recurring entries also show in [Calendar](app:calendar) under `Budget`; tap one to open it here.

## Accounts and transfers
Accounts are optional. In the `More` menu, `Accounts` lets you add cash, a card or a bank account with a `Starting balance` (a minus sign for a debt). Pick an account in an entry or a recurring entry to keep its balance; `Balances today` shows under the totals. Entries without an account still count in the totals.

`Transfer` moves money between two accounts, `From` one `To` the other. A transfer shows as "Bank → Cash" and counts as neither income nor expense. The filter `Transfers only` lists them.

## Charts
`Charts` shows `Expenses by category` for the month, `Income and expenses, 12 months` and `Balance at the end of each month`. Tap a category to see its entries, or a month to open it. `Show as a table` gives the numbers as text.

## Categories and currency
From the `More` menu (the three dots), `Categories` lets you add your own (`New category…`), rename them, change colours and delete them. Entries of a deleted category show as `Uncategorized`.

`Currency` sets one currency for every entry. Changing it doesn't convert amounts.

## Sync and backup
Entries, accounts, transfers, categories, limits, recurring entries, rules, bank profiles and the currency sync through [orOS sync](help:t/sync). The open tab and your CSV choice stay on this device.

From the `More` menu:
- `Export`: pick a period (`This month`, `This year` or `Everything`) and save as `CSV`, `Excel` or `PDF report`. For CSV, choose Greek Excel or other apps, such as the orOS [Spreadsheet](app:spreadsheet). `Open in Spreadsheet` opens the entries and a summary straight in [Spreadsheet](app:spreadsheet).
- `Backup (JSON)` saves everything to a file.
- `Restore from backup` merges a backup into your data; it never overwrites newer items.

## Bank statements
`More` › `Import bank statement` reads a file you download from your bank's e-banking: CSV from any bank, OFX / QFX, QIF or CAMT.053 (XML). Nothing is added before you see it.

- **Columns.** For a CSV, Budget finds the date, the description and the amount itself, including Greek files (`1.234,56`, `dd/mm/yyyy`, a `Χ/Π` marker, Windows-1253 text). If something is off, open `Columns and format`: pick the columns, one `Amount` column or separate `Debit` and `Credit` columns, the date order, the number format, the text encoding, or reverse the signs.
- **Bank profile.** Type a name in `Remember as` (for example "Eurobank") and the columns and the `Budget account` are remembered on all your devices. The next file from that bank is recognised by itself.
- **Preview.** Every row shows `New`, `Looks like an entry already here` (same account, date and amount with a similar description, or the same bank transaction id), `Already imported`, `Deleted here earlier` or `Skipped` (no date, no amount, another currency). Only ticked rows are imported; you can tick a duplicate if it really is a second one.
- **Categories.** Pick a category on any row. After a correction, `Always do this` makes a rule from it, so the next import does it by itself.
- `Import` adds them as normal entries, marked with a small bank icon. `Undo` takes back the whole import. The same file imported again, here or on another device, adds nothing twice.

## Rules
`More` › `Rules`: "if the description contains LIDL → Groceries". A rule can also look at an amount range (`Amount from`, `Amount to`), rename the entry (`Rename to`) and add a note. When several rules match, the one with the longest text wins. `Apply to uncategorised entries` runs them on what is already here; `Undo` puts it back.

## Uncategorised imports
When imported entries have no category, a bar above the list says how many. Tap it, or pick `Imported, no category` in the menu next to the search: you see them from every month, each with its category right there. `Apply rules` and `Rule…` help you clear them faster.

## Stripe and Invoice Ninja
`More` › `Import from Stripe or Invoice Ninja` brings in an account you connected in [Settings](app:settings) › `Connected services` (for Stripe, a restricted key that can only read Balance). Pick the account, a start date, the categories and, if you use them, a Budget account, then `Read the account`: you see what will be added before anything is.

- A charge becomes income (the full amount) and its Stripe fee a separate expense. Refunds, Stripe's own fees and lost disputes become expenses.
- Payouts to your bank are left out: they are your own money moving, not income.
- From Invoice Ninja, each payment received becomes income, less what was refunded from it.
- Only Budget's currency comes in; the others are counted and left out.
- A second import adds only what is new. An entry you delete here doesn't come back. `Undo` takes back the whole import. orOS only reads Stripe and Invoice Ninja.

## Keyboard shortcuts
- [[N]] opens a new entry.
- [[←]] / [[→]] move to the previous or next month (when you're not typing).
- [[Esc]] closes the `More` menu or the open window.

## Tips
- [Garage](app:garage) (`Add to Budget`) and [Split](app:split) (`Add my share to Budget`) can open a new entry here already filled in. Nothing is saved until you check it and press `Save`.

## Limits
- One currency for everything; there's no conversion.
- Recurring entries are added only while Budget is open, and back-filled for up to two years.
- Bank files are read on this device only; orOS never connects to your bank. Up to 5,000 rows and 8 MB per file; the preview shows the first 300 rows.
- Amounts in another currency than Budget's are left out, not converted. A bank transfer between two of your accounts comes in as an expense in one and income in the other: change them into a `Transfer` by hand if you want.
