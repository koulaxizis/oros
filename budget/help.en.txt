# Budget
Budget tracks your income and expenses one month at a time, with categories, monthly limits, envelopes, savings goals, subscriptions, recurring entries, several currencies, receipts and reports.

## Getting started
Tap or click `New`, choose `Expense` or `Income`, type the `Amount`, pick a `Category` and press `Save`. You can also set the `Date` and add a `Note`. Amounts can be typed as `12,50` or `12.50`.

The top shows the month, with arrows to move between months; `This month` brings you back. Under it, `Income`, `Expenses` and `Balance` add up the month you're looking at.

## Entries
The `Entries` tab lists the month's entries by day, with a `Day total`. Tap an entry to edit or delete it; a delete shows `Undo` for a few seconds.

`Search all entries…` looks through every month, not just the open one. The menu next to it shows `All entries`, `Expenses only` or `Income only`.

If you use [CRM](help:a/crm), an entry can name the customer, supplier or other partner it is with (`Partner in CRM`); that partner's card then shows this year's and all-time income and expenses with them. Payments sent from Invoices bring their partner along.

Payments you record in [Invoices](app:invoices) come in here by themselves as freelance income, with the invoice number and the client in the note. If the payment's amount or date changes there, the entry follows; if the payment is deleted, so is the entry. An entry you change or delete here stays as you left it. Invoices › Settings turns this off.

## Monthly limits
On `Limits`, `Set limit` gives an expense category a monthly limit, and `All expenses` takes an overall one. Limits apply to every month. Each bar shows how much is spent and how much is left, and turns to `Close to the limit` at 80% and `Over the limit` after 100%. When you pass a limit in the current month, orOS sends you one [notification](help:t/notifications).

## Recurring
Rent, salary, subscriptions: on `Recurring`, add them once with `Every week`, `Every month` or `Every year`, a `First date` and an optional `Last date`. When Budget is open, each one becomes a normal entry on its day, marked `Recurring`. Deleting a recurring entry stops new ones; entries already added stay. Upcoming recurring entries also show in [Calendar](app:calendar) under `Budget`; tap one to open it here.

## Accounts and transfers
Accounts are optional. In the `More` menu, `Accounts` lets you add cash, a card or a bank account with a `Starting balance` (a minus sign for a debt). Pick an account in an entry or a recurring entry to keep its balance; `Balances today` shows under the totals. Entries without an account still count in the totals.

`Transfer` moves money between two accounts, `From` one `To` the other. A transfer shows as "Bank → Cash" and counts as neither income nor expense. The filter `Transfers only` lists them.

## Envelopes
Envelope budgeting (zero-based, as in YNAB) is optional. Open the `Plan` tab and press `Start envelope budgeting`. From then on:
- `To be assigned` is the money that has no job yet: what your accounts held when you started, plus the income since, minus what you have assigned. Give it out until it reads 0.
- Each expense category is an envelope. Tap one to set what is `Assigned this month`; it shows `Spent` and `Available`.
- What is left in an envelope moves on to the next month. So does overspending: the envelope starts the next month in the red (`Overspent`) until you cover it. `Cover the overspending` fills in the amount.
- `Move money` takes money from one envelope (or `To be assigned`) to another. `Same as last month` copies last month's amounts into the envelopes that have none yet.
- Your monthly limits keep working next to the envelopes, and each envelope shows its limit.
- `Stop envelope budgeting` turns it off; what you assigned stays for when you start again.

## Savings goals
On `Plan`, `New goal`: a `Name`, a `Target` and, if you want, a date (`By`). Progress comes from `What I put aside by hand` (use `Add money`; a minus sign takes money out) or from the balance of one of your accounts. Each goal shows how far along it is and how much to put aside each month to make it by its date.

## Subscriptions
On `Recurring`, `Subscriptions` lists your recurring expenses by their next charge, each with what it costs `A month` and `A year`, and the totals on top. In a recurring expense, `Remind me` (`On the day of the charge`, or 1 to 14 days before) sends you a [notification](help:t/notifications) before the charge. `Reminders at` sets the hour on this device; `Off on this device` silences them here only.

## Reports
`Charts` also shows `Where the money goes` for the year of the month you are looking at: a bar per month split by category, and a table of every category per month with totals, income and expenses. The arrows change the year. `Net worth` adds up your accounts at the end of each of the last 12 months.

## Currencies
The main currency (`More` › `Currency`) is the one every total is in. An account can have its own (`Currency of the account`), and so can an entry: pick it next to the `Amount`. Budget then fills in the amount `In` the main currency from the European Central Bank's rates; change it to what your bank actually charged. The entry keeps both the amount you typed and its currency. Rates come from the same place, and the same saved copy, as the [Unit Converter](app:units), only when something is in another currency, at most every 6 hours. Offline, the last saved rates are used and the window says so. Account balances show in their own currency; net worth converts them at today's rates.

## Receipts
In an entry, under `Receipt`, the `+` adds a photo or a PDF of the receipt (up to 3). Photos are redrawn on this device, so no location or camera data is kept; a PDF is kept only if it really is one (up to 10 MB). The files sit in [Files](app:files), folder Budget/Receipts, and reach your other devices with Vault Drive, not with sync. `Read the total from text` takes the receipt's text (for example from your phone's "copy text from photo") and puts its total into `Amount`.

## Charts
`Charts` shows `Expenses by category` for the month, `Income and expenses, 12 months` and `Balance at the end of each month`. Tap a category to see its entries, or a month to open it. `Show as a table` gives the numbers as text.

## Categories and currency
From the `More` menu (the three dots), `Categories` lets you add your own (`New category…`), rename them, change colours and delete them. Entries of a deleted category show as `Uncategorized`.

`Currency` sets the main currency, the one every total is in (see Currencies). Changing it doesn't convert the amounts already there.

## Sync and backup
Entries, accounts, transfers, categories, limits, envelopes, savings goals, recurring entries and their reminders, rules, bank profiles and the currency sync through [orOS sync](help:t/sync). The open tab, your CSV choice and the reminder hour stay on this device. Receipt files travel with Vault Drive.

From the `More` menu:
- `Export`: pick a period (`This month`, `This year` or `Everything`) and save as `CSV`, `Excel` or `PDF report`. For CSV, choose Greek Excel or other apps, such as the orOS [Spreadsheet](app:spreadsheet). `Open in Spreadsheet` opens the entries and a summary straight in [Spreadsheet](app:spreadsheet).
- `Backup (JSON)` saves everything to a file.
- `Restore from backup` merges a backup into your data; it never overwrites newer items.

## Save and open an orOS file
`More` › `orOS file (.orosbudget)` saves every entry, account, transfer, category, limit, envelope, savings goal, recurring entry and rule as one `.orosbudget` file, with the receipt photos and PDFs inside: for example to move your budget to another device without sync, or to keep a copy. Bank profiles stay on this device. The file is a plain ZIP: unzipped it holds the records as `content.json`, the receipts in `media/` and `preview/entries.csv` (the same CSV as `Export`) for other programs. It is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere safe.

`Open orOS file…` (or `Unzipped orOS folder…`, or "Open with" orOS on the computer) shows how many records are new and how many are already here, then `Add` adds only the new ones, with their own ids: opening the same file again adds nothing. An entry already here is kept as it is, and one you deleted here stays deleted; a file never deletes anything. Tick `Update with newer versions` to let records that were changed later in the file replace your copy. Receipts go to Budget/Receipts only when they are not there yet, with the same checks as adding one by hand. `Undo` in the message removes what was added.

## Bank statements
`More` › `Import bank statement` reads a file you download from your bank's e-banking: CSV or Excel from any bank, OFX / QFX, QIF, CAMT.053 (XML) or MT940, and PayPal's activity file. Nothing is added before you see it.

- **Columns.** For a CSV, Budget finds the date, the description and the amount itself, including Greek files (`1.234,56`, `dd/mm/yyyy`, a `Χ/Π` marker, Windows-1253 text). If something is off, open `Columns and format`: pick the columns, one `Amount` column or separate `Debit` and `Credit` columns, the date order, the number format, the text encoding, or reverse the signs.
- **Bank profile.** Type a name in `Remember as` (for example "Eurobank") and the columns and the `Budget account` are remembered on all your devices. The next file from that bank is recognised by itself.
- **Preview.** Every row shows `New`, `Looks like an entry already here` (same account, date and amount with a similar description, or the same bank transaction id), `Already imported`, `Deleted here earlier` or `Skipped` (no date, no amount, another currency). Only ticked rows are imported; you can tick a duplicate if it really is a second one.
- **Already here another way.** A row is left unticked when the same money is already in Budget: `Already here from Invoices` (a payment Invoices recorded, same amount within 10 days), `Already here as a transfer between your accounts` (a transfer that left or reached this account within 3 days) or `Already here from PayPal` (see below).
- **PayPal.** On paypal.com › `Activity` › `Download`, pick CSV. Budget recognises the file by itself, in English or Greek. Each payment comes in with the name of the shop; one in another currency comes in with the amount that left in your Budget currency; income comes in after PayPal's fee. Holds, pending payments and money that only moves between PayPal and your own card or bank are skipped. Your bank shows a PayPal payment again as "PAYPAL *…": whichever of the two files you import second marks that line `Already here from PayPal`, so it is not counted twice.
- **Viva.** In viva.com › `Reports` › `Monthly Account Statement`, download the Excel file and import it here.
- **Categories.** Pick a category on any row. After a correction, `Always do this` makes a rule from it, so the next import does it by itself.
- `Import` adds them as normal entries, marked with a small bank icon. `Undo` takes back the whole import. The same file imported again, here or on another device, adds nothing twice.

## Other budgeting apps
`More` › `Import from another app` (or `Import bank statement`, it's the same picker) recognises the export of these apps by itself and brings in categories and accounts too, not only amounts:
- **YNAB**: on the web, open an account or All Accounts and use `Export` (the register CSV; YNAB 4's register CSV works too). Transfers and the starting balance are left out; money into `Ready to Assign` comes in without a category.
- **Wallet by BudgetBakers**: on the web app, `Records`, then `Export` as CSV. `Labels` go into the note as #tags.
- **Spendee**: in the app's settings, `Export data` to CSV.
- **Money Lover**: in the wallet's menu, `Export to CSV` (comma or semicolon, dd/mm/yyyy dates both work).
- **Monefy**: `Settings` › `Data export` › CSV. Its converted amount is used when it is in Budget's currency.
- **Money Manager** (Realbyte): `More` › `Backup` › `Export to Excel` (.xls or .xlsx), or send the file to yourself by email.
- **Firefly III**: `Options` › `Export data` › all transactions (CSV). Withdrawals, deposits, categories and tags come in; transfers and opening balances are left out.

In the preview:
- **Accounts.** Each account of the file goes to a Budget account with the same name, a `New account` (when you already use accounts), or `No account`: then its name goes into the note.
- **Categories.** Names are matched with yours (also the ready ones in English or Greek). `Make the categories that are missing` adds the rest, up to 60 categories; untick it to leave them without a category, where your rules can still help.
- **Currencies.** A row in another currency keeps its own amount and gets the amount in Budget's currency: the app's own converted amount when the file has one, otherwise today's rate. Without a rate (offline, never fetched), those rows are left out and the window says which.
- Duplicates, `Already imported` and `Deleted here earlier` work as for bank statements. Imported entries carry a small import icon; `Undo` takes back the entries and the new categories and accounts.

## Rules
`More` › `Rules`: "if the description contains LIDL → Groceries". A rule can also look at an amount range (`Amount from`, `Amount to`), rename the entry (`Rename to`) and add a note. When several rules match, the one with the longest text wins. `Apply to uncategorised entries` runs them on what is already here; `Undo` puts it back.

## Uncategorised imports
When imported entries have no category, a bar above the list says how many. Tap it, or pick `Imported, no category` in the menu next to the search: you see them from every month, each with its category right there. `Apply rules` and `Rule…` help you clear them faster.

## Stripe and Invoice Ninja
`More` › `Import from Stripe or Invoice Ninja` brings in an account you connected in [Settings](app:settings) › `Connected services` (for Stripe, a restricted key that can only read Balance). Pick the account, a start date, the categories and, if you use them, a Budget account, then `Read the account`: you see what will be added before anything is.

- A charge becomes income (the full amount) and its Stripe fee a separate expense. Refunds, Stripe's own fees and lost disputes become expenses.
- Payouts to your bank are left out: they are your own money moving, not income.
- From Invoice Ninja, each payment received becomes income, less what was refunded from it.
- Only Budget's currency comes in; the others are counted and left out.
- A second import adds only what is new. An entry you delete here doesn't come back. `Undo` takes back the whole import. orOS only reads Stripe and Invoice Ninja.

## Keyboard shortcuts
- [[N]] opens a new entry.
- [[←]] / [[→]] move to the previous or next month (when you're not typing).
- [[Esc]] closes the `More` menu or the open window.

## Tips
- [Garage](app:garage) (`Add to Budget`) and [Split](app:split) (`Add my share to Budget`) can open a new entry here already filled in. Nothing is saved until you check it and press `Save`.

## Limits
- An entry in another currency keeps the main amount it was saved with; later rates don't change it. Net worth converts foreign accounts at today's rates, also for past months. Bank, Stripe and Invoice Ninja imports still take only the main currency.
- Receipt text is not read from the photo itself: paste the text to fill in the total.
- Subscription reminders come while orOS is open in a browser on that device; a closed orOS catches up when it opens.
- Recurring entries are added only while Budget is open, and back-filled for up to two years.
- Exports of other apps: up to 20,000 rows and 20 MB per file.
- Bank files are read on this device only; orOS never connects to your bank. Up to 5,000 rows and 8 MB per file; the preview shows the first 300 rows.
- Amounts in another currency than Budget's are left out, not converted. A bank transfer between two of your accounts comes in as an expense in one and income in the other: change them into a `Transfer` by hand if you want.
