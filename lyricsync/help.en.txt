# Lyric Sync
Lyric Sync times the words of a song to the music, line by line, the way distributors and streaming services want them. You tap along while the song plays, fix any line that came out early or late, check the result in a preview and save it as LRC, SRT, WebVTT, TTML, JSON or plain text. The song never leaves your device and you need no account.

## Getting started
- Press `New song`, then `Choose the song` to pick the audio file (MP3, M4A, WAV, FLAC, Ogg and more).
- In the `Lyrics` tab, write or paste the words, one line per line. A blank line starts a new verse. Title, artist, album and language code go into the exported files.
- `Open file` on the first screen starts a song from an audio file or from a lyrics file you already have (LRC, Enhanced LRC, SRT, WebVTT, TTML, ASS, our JSON or text). You can also drop a file on the window.

## Sync
Open the `Sync` tab. The first press of the big button (or [[Space]]) starts the song. Then:
- `Tap`: press once as each line starts. A line ends where the next one starts; `End line` (or [[E]]) marks a pause after the last line you timed, for an instrumental break.
- `Hold`: hold the button (or [[Space]]) while each line is sung and let go when it ends, like DistroKid's lyric tool.
The marked line (▸) is the next one to time, and the line playing now is lit. `Undo` (or [[Backspace]]) takes back the last step and `Redo` brings it back.

For a fast song, set the speed to 75% or 50%: the pitch stays the same and the times are still right.

## Word by word (karaoke)
Choose `Words`. The line being timed shows above the list, one button per word; tap the big button (or [[Space]]) as each word starts. The first word also starts the line, and after the last word the next line follows. Tap a word button to time again from that word, or a line in the list to move to it. A line shows how many of its words have a time; `Clear word times` takes them off. Lines with every word timed fill word by word in the preview and in the karaoke exports.

## The waveform
Under the play bar the song is drawn as a waveform with a mark at the start of each line (and, for the selected line, each word). Drag a mark to move it; tap anywhere else to jump there. `+` and `−` zoom; `Loop line` plays the selected line again and again while you fine-tune it.

## Tap delay, shift, singers
- `Tap delay` measures how late you tap: press `Start` and tap on the 8 clicks. The delay is taken off every tap on this device.
- `Shift all` moves every time by the same number of seconds, for example when another version of the song has a longer intro.
- For a duet, write the singers' names in the `Lyrics` tab (comma between them). Then a selected line has a singer menu, and TTML and ASS name the singer of each line.

## Check before you send
`Check` lists what distributors usually refuse: lines with no time, lines that start before the line above, lines on screen for less than half a second or for 15 seconds or more, lines over 80 characters, labels and repeat marks such as [Chorus] or x2, and lines with only some words timed. Tap an item to go to its line.

## Rough sync from the song
`Rough sync` (Sync tab) listens to the song on this device with Whisper, the same speech model as Montage's automatic subtitles, and gives each line a rough start. Your words stay exactly as you wrote them; only the times are guessed. Then play the song and fix the lines by ear. It works best with clear vocals; set the language code in `Lyrics` for a better result.
- The first time it downloads the speech model once (about 42 MB from Hugging Face). Montage uses the same copy, and Montage's subtitles dialog can remove it. The song itself is never sent anywhere.
- It times only the lines without a time, unless you tick `Also move lines that already have a time`. `Undo` takes it all back.

## Fix a line
Tap a line in the list to select it. Then:
- Write its start time in the box (minutes:seconds, for example `1:02.50`), or move it with `−0.1`, `−0.01`, `+0.01` and `+0.1` seconds (keys [[,]] and [[.]], with [[Shift]] for 0.1 s).
- `Play` plays the song from that line.
- `Sync from here` jumps back a little and makes the next taps time this line and the ones after it again.
- `Clear time` takes the time off the line.
Changing the words in the `Lyrics` tab keeps the times of the lines that did not change.

## Keys
- [[Space]]: tap or hold (Sync), play or pause (Preview). [[P]]: play or pause.
- [[←]] [[→]]: 2 seconds back or forward ([[Shift]]: 10 seconds). [[↑]] [[↓]]: select a line.
- [[Ctrl+Z]] and [[Ctrl+Shift+Z]]: undo and redo.
- [[E]]: end line. [[,]] [[.]]: move the selected line by 0.01 s.

## Preview and export
`Preview` shows the lines as they will appear in a player, with the current line large. `Full screen` makes it a karaoke screen: tap it or press [[Space]] to play and pause, [[Esc]] to leave. `Export` saves the file you need:
- `LRC` for music players and LRCLIB; `Enhanced LRC` (shown when lines have word times) adds a time to every word for karaoke players.
- `SRT` and `WebVTT` for video and the web.
- `TTML` for Apple Music, iTunes, YouTube and distributors such as DistroKid, with word timing and singers when you have them.
- `ASS karaoke` for video tools such as Aegisub, ffmpeg and VLC: the words fill as they are sung.
- `JSON (orOS)` keeps everything, to open it again later; `Text` is just the words.

## With other apps
- In [Music](app:music), open a song's menu and choose `Sync lyrics`. The song opens here straight from your Music folder, with the lyrics it already has. In `Export`, `Lyrics for Music` saves the .lrc file next to the song, and Music shows the lyrics as it plays (on your other devices too, with Vault Drive).
- In [Studio](app:studio), `Export` → `Sync lyrics` puts your mix in Music and opens it here.
- `MP3 with the lyrics inside` (for MP3 songs) saves a copy of the song with the words in its tags, plain and synced, for phones, car stereos and players that read them. Your song file is not changed.
- `Lyric video in Montage` (for songs on the orOS disk) opens a new [Montage](app:montage) project with the song and every line as a text clip.
- `.lrc`, `.srt`, `.vtt`, `.ttml` and `.ass` files open here from [Files](app:files), or from your computer's "Open with" when orOS is installed.

## Songbook: chords
Lyric Sync is also a songbook: a song can have chords over its words, a key, a capo, a tempo and a time signature, with or without a song file and with or without times.
- In the `Lyrics` tab, tick `Chords`. The box then takes words and chords: put a chord in brackets just before its syllable, as in `[Am]Σε θυ[F]μάμαι`, or paste a sheet with the chords written over the words (the way most chord sites show them); it is turned into the same thing. Greek names such as `Λα-` or `Ρε7` are read too.
- `[Verse]`, `[Chorus]` and `[Bridge]` (or `[Κουπλέ]`, `[Ρεφρέν]`, `[Γέφυρα]`) start a section; `[Intro]`, `[Solo]` and other labels, and `{c: text}`, are notes for the player. A line of chords only (an intro, a solo) is played, not sung.
- Under the box the song shows with the chords over the words. Tap a word to put a chord on it or change it; the `+` at the end of a line puts one after the last word. The picker offers the song's own chords first, then the chords of its key.
- `Transpose` moves every chord by semitones, or keeps your chord shapes and moves the capo instead. `Undo` takes it back.
- `Chord names` shows `C D E` or `Ντο Ρε Μι` on this device. The song keeps `C D E`, so files open everywhere.
- Chords never reach the timing files (LRC, SRT, TTML and the rest): distributors get only the sung words. `Export` adds `ChordPro` (.cho, for OnSong, SongbookPro and other songbook apps) and `Chords over the words` (plain text, to print or send).
- ChordPro files (.cho, .chopro, .chordpro, .crd) and chord sheets in .txt open here, from `Open file`, from [Files](app:files) or from your computer's "Open with". Only the first song of a file with several is read.
- Lyric Sync downloads no chords from any site: you write them or open your own files.

## Stage: playing live
`Stage` (next to `Transpose`, and in `Preview`) fills the screen with the song for playing it live, and keeps the screen on while it is open.
- With the song file here and the lines timed, it follows the song: press ▶ (or tap the words) and the line being sung lights up and stays in view.
- Otherwise ▶ scrolls at a steady speed: the whole song in its length (or about two bars a line at the song's tempo). `−` and `+` make it slower or faster; tap the words to pause. With a tempo set, `Count-in` clicks one bar first.
- ▲ ▼, the arrow keys and Page Up / Page Down move a screen back or on; most page-turn foot pedals send these keys. [[Esc]] closes the stage.
- `A−` `A+` set the size, and `Show` picks words and chords, words only (for singers) or chords only. The stage remembers these on this device.

## Sets, PDF and print
- On the first screen, `Sets` holds your setlists. `New set` makes one: give it a name, a date if you like, and notes, then add songs with `Add song`. ↑ ↓ change the order, ✕ takes a song out (the song itself stays).
- `Key here` plays a song of the set in another key; the song keeps its own key everywhere else.
- `Stage` on a set plays its songs one after the other. At the end of a song the pedal (or Page Down) goes on to the next one; ⏮ ⏭, a swipe left or right, and the keys [[N]] and [[P]] move between songs. The bottom shows the song that comes next.
- `PDF` makes a sheet to print: one song from `Export`, or the whole set with a first page that lists its songs. Pick A4 or Letter, one or two columns and the font size; `Print` sends the same pages to your printer. Greek and polytonic print in Noto.
- `Export` on a set saves all its songs in one ChordPro file, or the list as text. Opening a ChordPro file with several songs (`{new_song}` between them) adds every song and a set of them.
- `Add to Calendar` puts a dated set in Calendar as a day event; Calendar shows it before it adds it.
- Sets sync with your devices like the songs.

## Your data
- The lyrics and their times are saved as you go. With orOS sync switched on in [Settings](app:settings), they follow you to your other devices, encrypted.
- The song file stays only on the device where you chose it. On another device the song shows "song file not on this device" until you choose it there too. A song opened from Music is read from your Music folder, so it is there on every device that has the file (Vault Drive).
- `Delete` on the first screen removes the lyrics and times from all your devices; the song file itself is not touched.
- The menu's search finds your songs by title, artist or words.
