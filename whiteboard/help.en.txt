# Whiteboard
Whiteboard is an endless board for ideas: sticky notes, text, shapes, arrows that join them, pen lines, pictures and frames. Keep as many boards as you like, move around and zoom freely, and export a board or one frame as PNG, SVG or PDF.

## Getting started
- Pick a tool in the bar on the left, then tap the board. `Sticky note` (N) and `Text` (T) open the text box right away; type and tap anywhere else to finish.
- `Shape` (S) and `Frame` (F): drag to draw one, or tap for the usual size.
- `Arrow` (A): drag from one object to another. The arrow sticks to both and follows them when they move. An arrow can also start or end on an empty spot.
- `Pen` (P) draws with your finger, a pen or the mouse until you pick `Select and move` (V) again.
- `Add a picture` in the top bar puts a picture in the middle of the screen. You can also drop pictures on the board or paste one with [[Ctrl+V]].
- The first object you add makes your first board. The name at the top left opens the list of `Boards`, with `New board`, `Rename` and `Delete board`.

## Select, move and change
- With `Select and move`, tap an object to select it and drag it to move it. [[Shift]] + tap adds to the selection, and dragging an empty spot with the mouse draws a box around several objects.
- Moving a frame moves everything inside it.
- The square handle at the bottom right of a selected object resizes it. A selected arrow shows a dot at each end: drag one onto another object to stick it there, or onto an empty spot to free it.
- The bar at the top shows what the selection can change: `Colour`, `Shape`, `Fill`, `Arrow heads`, `Dashed line`, `Line width`, `Smaller text` and `Bigger text`, then `Edit text`, `Bring to front`, `Send to back`, `Duplicate` and `Delete`. With a tool chosen and nothing selected, it sets the colour of the next object.
- Double-tap an object, or press [[Enter]], to change its text. A note makes its text smaller as it fills up. Double-tap an empty spot to add a sticky note there.
- `Undo` and `Redo` in the top bar ([[Ctrl+Z]] and [[Ctrl+Y]]) go back through your changes on this board.

## Move around
- Drag an empty spot with your finger, or use `Move around` (H), the middle mouse button or [[Space]] + drag. The mouse wheel scrolls; [[Ctrl]] + wheel or a pinch zooms.
- The buttons at the bottom right zoom out, show the zoom (tap it for 100%), zoom in and `Show everything`.

## Keyboard
- [[V]] [[H]] [[N]] [[T]] [[S]] [[A]] [[P]] [[F]] choose a tool, [[I]] adds a picture.
- [[Tab]] goes from one object to the next and the screen reader reads it out. Arrow keys move the selection (with [[Shift]] one step at a time), [[Delete]] removes it.
- [[Ctrl+A]] selects everything, [[Ctrl+D]] duplicates, [[Ctrl+C]], [[Ctrl+X]] and [[Ctrl+V]] copy, cut and paste on this board.
- [[+]] and [[-]] zoom, [[0]] goes to 100%, [[1]] shows everything, [[Esc]] lets go of the selection.

## Export
- `Export` in the top bar saves the `Whole board`, the `Selection` or, with one frame selected, `This frame`, as PNG, SVG or PDF, on a `Paper` or `Transparent` background. `Save in Files` keeps it in Files › Whiteboard.
- A selected frame also has `Export this frame` in its bar, which makes frames handy as pages.

## Your data
- Everything stays on this device and is saved as you go. Pictures are made smaller (at most 1024 pixels) so the boards stay light.
- With orOS sync switched on in [Settings](app:settings), your boards follow you to your other devices, encrypted. Each object syncs on its own, so edits to different objects on two devices both stay; for the same object the newer edit wins.
- There is no live collaboration: that would need a server that sees your boards.

## Find it from anywhere
- The menu's search box finds board names and the words on notes, texts, shapes, arrows and frames. Choosing one opens the board on that object.
