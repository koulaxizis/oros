# Micro-Zen
Micro-Zen is a breathing circle for a minute or a few of quiet. Follow the circle as it grows and shrinks, and watch your quiet minutes add up.

## Getting started
Pick a `Pattern` and a `Length`, then tap or click the circle or the play button. After a short `Get ready` countdown, the circle grows while you `Breathe in`, rests while you `Hold`, and shrinks while you `Breathe out`. The words in the circle tell you which phase you're in.

When the session ends you see `Well done` with your breaths and time. `Again` starts another one; `Close` goes back.

## Patterns and length
- `Box`: 4-4-4-4, in, hold, out, hold. Good for balance.
- `Relax`: 4-7-8, in for 4, hold for 7, out for 8. Made for winding down before sleep.
- `Coherent`: 5.5 seconds in and 5.5 out, a steady rhythm with no holds.
- Your own: `+ My pattern` opens a small editor. Give it a name and the seconds for `Breathe in`, `Hold (full)`, `Breathe out` and `Hold (empty)`, in half seconds (a hold of 0 is skipped). It joins the list with its rhythm, like 4-2-6. Select it and tap `Edit this pattern` to change or delete it (`Undo` brings it back). You can have up to 12, and they sync to all your devices.

`Length` can be 1, 3, 5 or 10 minutes. A session always ends on a whole breath, so the time is close to, not exactly, what you picked. You can't change the pattern or length mid-session; pause first.

## During a session
- Tap the circle or the play button to pause and resume. The session also pauses by itself when you switch away from the app.
- The end button finishes early. Every whole breath you completed still counts; if you stop before the first breath is done, nothing is saved.
- The speaker button at the top turns `Soft tones` on or off, a gentle sound at each phase change and a chime at the end.
- On phones that support it, a `Vibration` button adds a short buzz at each phase change.
- While a session runs, orOS tries to keep your screen awake.

## Your quiet
`Your quiet` shows your minutes `Today` and `This week`, your `Streak` of days in a row with a session, your total `Sessions`, and a small chart of the last 7 days. A streak stays alive until midnight even if you haven't breathed yet today.

`Reset stats` sets minutes, sessions and the streak back to zero, after you confirm.

With `Show in Habits` ticked (at first it is), [Habits](app:habits) shows a `Micro-Zen` row that ticks itself on each day with a finished session. Untick it to hide the row on all your devices.

## Import sessions
Bring your meditation time over from another app. Under `Your quiet`, press `Import sessions` and pick the file. Micro-Zen shows how many sessions it found, on how many days, the dates they cover and how many days are already here. If the file has activities (Meditation, Yoga…), untick the ones you don't want counted. Press `Import`; a message tells you how many sessions were added, and its `Undo` button takes the import back on all your devices.

How to export:
- **Insight Timer**: on the website or in the app, open your profile settings and look for exporting your sessions as CSV. The file has `Started At`, `Duration`, `Preset` and `Activity`; day-first and month-first dates are both understood.
- **Any CSV**: a date (or date and time) column and a minutes column, like `date,minutes`. A `seconds` column, or a length like `0:10:00`, works too.

Imported time counts in `Today`, `This week`, the streak and `Sessions`, and in Habits if Micro-Zen shows there; breaths aren't counted. Importing the same file again, even on another device, never counts a session twice, and a newer export only adds what's new. `Reset stats` clears imported time too. The file is read on your device; nothing is uploaded anywhere except through your own sync.

## Sync and backup
Your stats sync through [orOS sync](help:t/sync) and add up across all your devices, and a reset clears them everywhere. Your own patterns and `Show in Habits` sync too. The chosen pattern, length, tones and vibration stay on each device. Micro-Zen has no export of its own; your stats are part of the orOS [backup](help:t/backup). To bring in sessions from another app, see the section above.

## Keyboard shortcuts
- [[Space]] starts, pauses or resumes.
- [[Esc]] ends the session.

## Tips
- `Relax` is the slowest pattern, with a long out-breath. Try it in bed with the screen dimmed.
- If your system asks to reduce motion, the circle stays still and only its light and words change.

## Limits
- Micro-Zen keeps only totals per day, not a list of single sessions.
