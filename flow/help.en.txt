# Flow Free
Join each pair of matching coloured dots with a line. Lines can't cross, and every cell of the grid must be filled.

## How to play
Draw a line from one dot to the other dot of the same colour. A puzzle is solved when every pair is joined and no cell is left empty. If all pairs are joined but some cells are still empty, a message tells you so.

- Drawing back over your own line takes it back.
- Drawing over another line cuts that line at that point.
- A line can't pass through another colour's dot.

Every dot also carries a letter, so you never have to rely on colour alone. Every puzzle is made on your device and has exactly one solution.

## Controls
- Drag from a dot, or from the end of a line, with your finger or the mouse.
- [[←]] [[↑]] [[→]] [[↓]] move the cursor, [[Enter]] or [[Space]] picks the dot or line under it, the arrows then draw, [[Backspace]] takes back a cell, and [[Enter]] or [[Esc]] lets go.
- [[U]] or `Undo (U)` takes back your last stroke.
- [[R]] or `Clear the board (R)` clears every line.
- [[H]] or `Hint (H)` draws one line of the solution.
- [[N]] or `New puzzle (N)` makes a new puzzle.

## Levels and scoring
Pick a grid size from `5×5` to `9×9`; bigger grids have more colours. The status bar shows how many `Lines` are joined and your `Time`. The clock starts with your first line and only runs while the app is on screen.

A solved puzzle shows your time, moves and best time for that size. A puzzle solved with a hint counts as solved but doesn't set a best time. `Records` shows the best time and puzzles solved for each size.

## Sync
Your records sync between your devices through [orOS sync](help:t/sync). `Reset records` deletes them on every device after you confirm. The puzzle in progress, the grid size and the sound setting stay on this device.

## Tips
- A puzzle in progress is kept, undo history included.
- Cleared the board, started a new puzzle or changed size by mistake? Tap `Undo` on the message that appears.
- Start with dots near corners and edges: they usually have only one way to go.
