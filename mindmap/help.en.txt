# Mind Map
Mind Map helps you sort out ideas: one idea in the centre, branches around it. The layout is always tidy (as a map, an org chart, a tree or a timeline), so you only think about the ideas. You can keep as many maps as you like, style them and present them branch by branch.

## Getting started
- Tap `New map`, type the `Central idea` and press `OK`. Or tap `Show an example` to look around first.
- Tap or click a node to select it. The bar at the bottom has `Child`, `Sibling`, `Edit`, `Details`, `Style`, `Fold` and `Delete`.
- To change the text, double-tap or double-click the node, or tap it again once it is selected. [[Shift+Enter]] starts a new line.
- The text of the centre is the map's title.

## Build the map
- Drag a node onto another node to move it there. Drop it on the middle of a node to make it a child, or just above or below it to put it before or after. The whole branch moves with it.
- Drag a node to an empty spot and it stays there, with its whole branch. `Back to its place` in its menu undoes that, and `Tidy up (all back in place)` in the `More` menu puts every node back.
- Long-press a node (or right-click it) for more: `Mark as done`, `Move up`, `Move down`, `Copy style`, `Paste style`, `Add a boundary or summary…`, `Copy as text`, `Paste a list here…`, `Export this branch…`, `Present from here` and `Delete branch`.
- `Details…` holds an `Emoji or symbol` (with quick icons such as a star, a question mark or priority 1 to 3), a `Branch colour`, a `Note`, a `Link (web address)` and a `Picture`.
- `Paste a list…` turns a list into branches: one idea per line, with indents (or Markdown `- ` lists and `#` headings) for the levels.
- Drag the empty space to move around, pinch or use the zoom buttons, and tap `Fit to screen` to see the whole map.

## Style and layout
- `Style` in the bottom bar (or `Style…` in a node's menu) sets the `Shape` (rectangle, rounded, pill, ellipse, diamond, hexagon, underline or text only), the `Fill` (none, light or solid), the `Border` (none, thin, thick or dashed), the `Text size` and `Bold text`. `Apply to the whole branch` gives everything under the node the same look, and `Reset style` brings back the automatic one.
- `Copy style` in a node's menu, then `Paste style` on another node, gives it the same look.
- `Layout…` in the `More` menu arranges the whole map as a `Map` (branches on both sides), a `Map to the right`, an `Org chart` (from the top down), a `Tree` (an indented list) or a `Timeline` (main branches in a row, their ideas below). Every layout stays tidy and the arrow keys follow it.
- In the org chart and on the timeline, drop a dragged node on the left or right part of another one to put it before or after it.

## Boundaries and summaries
- `Add a boundary or summary…` in a node's menu groups ideas that sit side by side. Tick the ideas that belong in it, and give it a label (such as "Phase 1") and a colour if you like.
- A `Boundary` draws a box around the ideas and everything under them. A `Summary` draws a brace beside them with its label.
- Tap the line or the label of a boundary to select it. The bar at the bottom then has `Edit` and `Delete`.

## Present
- `Present` in the `More` menu (or `Present from here` in a node's menu) shows the map full screen, one idea at a time: the centre first, then each branch from top to bottom. The last step shows the whole map.
- Tap the right side of the screen or swipe left to go on, tap the left side or swipe right to go back. The buttons at the bottom do the same, and the X ends it.
- With a keyboard: [[→]], [[↓]] or [[Space]] go on, [[←]] or [[↑]] go back, [[Home]] and [[End]] jump to the start or the end, [[Esc]] ends it.
- A folded branch shows as one step. Unfold it first if you want to present what is inside.

## Links and pictures
- To join two ideas on different branches, choose `Link to another node…` in a node's menu and then tap the other node. You can give the link a label, such as "depends on".
- Tap a link to select it. The bar at the bottom then has `Label`, `Reverse` and `Delete link`.
- `Choose picture` in `Details…` adds a small picture above the text. Pictures are made smaller when you add them, and all pictures together can use about 1 MB.

## Use it in other apps
- `Send to To-Do…` in a node's menu makes a new list in [To-Do](app:todo) from that branch. Each idea becomes a task and its note comes too. Ideas marked done are left out, and To-Do asks you before it adds anything.
- `Save as a note` makes a new page in [Notes](app:notes) with the branch as an indented list, including notes and links. `Save the map as a note` in the `More` menu does the same for the whole map.
- `Make slides from this branch…` (or `Make slides from this map…` in the `More` menu) starts a presentation in [Slides](app:slides). The branch becomes the title slide, each main idea a slide, deeper ideas bullets and notes speaker notes. Slides asks you for a theme first.

## Outline and find
- `Outline` shows the same map as an indented list, which is quicker for typing. [[Enter]] adds a line, [[Tab]] and [[Shift+Tab]] change the level, and [[Backspace]] on an empty line removes it.
- `Find` searches the text and notes of the open map. The arrows step through the matches and open folded branches on the way.

## Your maps
Tap the map name at the top left for `Your maps`. There you can open, rename, duplicate or delete a map. The `More` menu also has `Layout…`, `Unfold all` and `Fold main branches`.

## Sync and backup
With [orOS sync](help:t/sync), your maps and every node reach your other devices, with their styles, boundaries, summaries and layout. Which map is open, the view and which branches are folded stay on this device.

- `Export…` makes a picture (PNG or SVG), Markdown, OPML, JSON or an `orOS file (.orosmind)` of the whole map or one branch. You can save it, save it to [Files](app:files), print it or copy it as text.
- An `orOS file (.orosmind)` holds one whole map with everything in it: links, pictures, styles, boundaries, summaries and layout. It is an open ZIP file: unzip it and you find `content.json` (the map), `media` (its pictures) and `preview/map.md` and `preview/map.opml`, the outline for other programs. Opening it makes a new map; your other maps are not touched. `Unzipped orOS folder…` in the `More` menu opens such a file after it was unzipped, even if you changed a picture in it.
- `Import (orOS, FreeMind, XMind, SimpleMind, MindManager, OPML, Markdown, text, JSON)…` brings in maps from FreeMind, Freeplane, XMind (only the first sheet), SimpleMind and MindManager, and outlines from other apps, as a new map (see below). Their arrows become links. A JSON backup is merged into your maps: newer changes are kept and nothing you deleted comes back.
- `Back up all maps (JSON)` saves everything in one file. Your maps are also part of orOS [backups](help:t/backup).

## Import from other apps
Every import makes a new map and opens it; your other maps are not touched. The message that follows has an `Undo` button, which removes the new map again.

- **FreeMind / Freeplane** (.mm): save the map as usual and import the .mm file.
- **XMind** (.xmind): import the .xmind file as it is. Only the first sheet comes in.
- **SimpleMind** (.smmx): in SimpleMind, share or export the mind map as a SimpleMind file (.smmx), or use the file from its storage folder. Ideas, notes, links, ticks and cross-links come in.
- **MindManager** (.mmap): save the map as a .mmap file (the usual format). Topics, notes, links, colours, finished tasks and relationships come in; floating topics don't.
- **Markdown** (.md): files from Markmap, Obsidian, Logseq and other Markdown notes. Headings and nested lists become branches; the first heading becomes the centre (or the file name, if there are several). Links, bold and other formatting become plain text (a node's first web link is kept as its link), and Logseq properties such as "collapsed:: true" are left out. In Logseq, use `Export page` and choose text, or take the .md file from the pages folder.
- **Text** (.txt): one idea per line, with tabs or spaces for the levels. You can also paste such a list with `Paste a list…`.
- **OPML** (.opml): the outline export of most outliners.

## Keyboard shortcuts
- [[Tab]] adds a child, [[Enter]] adds a sibling, [[F2]] or simply typing edits the selected node.
- [[Delete]] deletes the branch, [[Space]] folds or unfolds it, the arrow keys move around the map.
- [[Alt+↑]] and [[Alt+↓]] move a node up or down among its siblings.
- [[Ctrl+Z]] and [[Ctrl+Y]] undo and redo, [[Ctrl+F]] finds, [[Ctrl+C]] and [[Ctrl+V]] copy a branch as text or paste a list.
- While presenting: [[→]] and [[←]] go on and back, [[Esc]] ends.

## Tips
- Start fast in `Outline`, then switch to `Map` to see the shape.
- Use one colour per main branch; its children follow it.
- A diamond or a solid fill makes the key ideas stand out; a summary brace is a quick way to say what a group of ideas adds up to.
- Deleted something by mistake? The message has an `Undo` button, and [[Ctrl+Z]] works too.

## Limits
- Nodes you place yourself can overlap other nodes; `Tidy up (all back in place)` sorts that out.
- Links, pictures, styles, boundaries and summaries don't show in the `Outline` view, and Markdown and OPML exports leave them out (JSON keeps everything; PNG and SVG show them).
- A boundary covers everything between the first and the last idea you ticked, even an idea in between that you left out.
- Shapes, boundaries, summaries and pictures in FreeMind, XMind, SimpleMind and MindManager files are not imported (the ideas, notes, links and colours are). A very large MindManager map may not fit within the import limits.
- A device still on an older version of orOS shows these maps in the classic look until it updates; nothing is lost.
- If two devices move nodes at the same time in a way that conflicts, a node may come back under the centre with a dashed line. Drag it where it belongs, or choose `Keep it here`.
- A very large map may be too big for PNG; export SVG instead.
