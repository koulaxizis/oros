# Nonogram
Paint the hidden picture by following the numbers. Every puzzle can be solved by logic alone, without guessing.

## How to play
The numbers beside each row and above each column are the runs of filled cells in that line, in order, with at least one empty cell between runs. For example, `3 1` means a run of three filled cells, a gap, then one more.

Fill the cells you're sure of and mark known empty ones with ✕. When a row or column matches its numbers, they dim. The puzzle is solved when every line matches; marks don't matter.

Every picture is made on your device and has only one solution.

## Controls
- Choose `Fill` or `Mark` in the toolbar ([[M]] switches). Tap or click a cell to paint it in that mode; do it again to clear it.
- Drag to paint a straight row or column in one go.
- With a mouse, right-click marks a cell.
- [[←]] [[↑]] [[→]] [[↓]] move the cursor, [[Space]] or [[Enter]] paint in the current mode, [[F]] fills, [[X]] marks, [[Backspace]] clears.
- [[U]] or [[Z]] (or `Undo (U)`) takes back the last stroke.
- [[H]] or `Hint (H)` fixes one cell: the one under the cursor if it's wrong, else another wrong cell, else a missing fill.

On a small screen, the zoom button (`Bigger cells (scroll the board)`) makes the cells bigger. The board then scrolls; drag the numbers to move around, while a drag on the cells still paints.

## Levels and scoring
Pick `5×5`, `10×10` or `15×15`. The status bar shows your `Time` and your `Best` for that size. A puzzle solved with a hint counts as solved but sets no best time. `Records` shows the best time, puzzles solved and puzzles solved without hints, for each size.

## Sync
Your records sync between your devices through [orOS sync](help:t/sync). `Reset records` deletes them on every device after you confirm. The puzzle in progress, the size, the zoom and the sound setting stay on this device.

## Tips
- Start with lines the numbers almost fill, like `8` in a 10-cell row: its middle cells must be filled.
- A puzzle in progress is kept; come back to it any time.
- Started a new puzzle or changed size by mistake? Tap `Undo` on the message that appears.
