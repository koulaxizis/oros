# One-Sentence Diary
One sentence a day, written in ten seconds. Under each day's line you see the same date in the years before, so in a year's time today comes back to you.

## Writing today
Open the app and type. The line saves itself as you type (`Saved` appears under it); [[Enter]] saves at once.
- A line can be up to 280 characters, on one line. The counter appears when you are close to the end.
- Pick an emoji for the day if you like: 😊 😐 😔 😴 🎉 ❤️. Tap the same emoji again to take it away.
- Writing after midnight? Until 03:00 the app asks whether the line is for `Yesterday` or `Today`.
- Clearing the text, or `Delete this day`, removes the day; `Undo` in the message brings it back.

## Other days
- The arrows next to the date go to the day before or after; `Back to today` returns. You can write or fix any past day, but not a day that has not come yet.
- The star button at the top opens `A random memory` from your diary.

## More about a day
Under the line, `✎ Write more about this day` opens a longer entry for the same day. The sentence stays the heart of the diary; the longer entry is optional.
- It saves itself as you type. `Preview` shows it formatted, `Write` goes back to the text.
- Simple formatting: `**bold**`, `*italic*`, `# heading`, `- list`, `1. list`, `> quote`, `---` for a line and `[text](https://…)` for a link. Only web and e-mail links become links; anything else (HTML, scripts) stays plain text and is never run.
- `Clear the longer entry` removes the text, photos, place and weather of that day; `Undo` brings them back.
- In `My days` and `Year` a ✎ marks the days with a longer entry. Search finds words in it too.

## Questions and templates
- `Question of the day` offers one question each day (the same on every device), in English or Greek with the language of orOS. `Answer it` puts the question as a heading in the longer entry; `Another question` shows the next one.
- `Templates` add a ready outline: gratitude, a daily review, a morning page.
- Your own questions: in `Diary settings` › `Your questions` add any question you like. They join the question of the day and sync with your days.

## Photos
- Up to four photos a day. `+` picks an image; it is drawn again at most 1600 pixels wide and saved as JPEG, so the camera data inside it (EXIF, the location where it was taken) is not kept.
- Photos are files on the orOS disk, in Files › `Diary` › `Photos`. The entry keeps only their names. Sync does not carry the images themselves: run Vault Drive to bring them to your other devices; until then those devices show `Not on this device`.
- Tap a photo to see it large; `Remove this photo` takes it off the entry (with `Undo`), and the file leaves the disk a few seconds later.

## Place and weather
- `📍 Add a place`: type a place name, or tap `Use my position` once. The position is asked only when you tap it, and is kept rounded to about 1 km.
- `⛅ Add the day's weather` fetches the weather of that day from Open-Meteo (the same free service as the Weather app): the sky and the highest and lowest temperature. It needs a place (a typed name is looked up first); with no place it uses the city of the Weather app. Nothing is fetched unless you tap it.
- The temperatures follow the units of the Weather app.

## PDF book
In `Diary settings` › `PDF book` choose a year (or all years) and tap `Make the PDF book`: one PDF with each day's date, line, longer entry, place, weather and photos. It is made on this device, with fonts that ship with orOS.

## PIN lock
- In `Diary settings` › `PIN lock` set a PIN of 4 to 8 digits. The diary then asks for it when it opens and again after a minute in the background. `Lock now` locks at once.
- Only a salted hash of the PIN is kept (PBKDF2), on this device only; other devices have their own PIN, or none.
- After five wrong tries you wait a little longer before each new try.
- It is a privacy screen, not encryption. Your days stay readable in this browser's storage, sync to your other devices as usual, and the diary is left out of the menu's search while a PIN is set. Forgot the PIN? Clearing orOS's site data in the browser removes it (with every other local setting); with sync on, your days come back from the cloud.

## On this day
Under the line, `On this day` shows what you wrote on the same date in earlier years, newest first. A line from 29 February also shows on 28 February in the years without one. Tap a line to open that day.

## My days
- A month calendar: the days with a line are coloured and show their emoji. Tap a day to open it.
- Under the calendar are the month's lines.
- The search box finds days in every year. Every word you type must be in the line; capitals and accents do not matter.

## Year
`Year` shows every line of one year on one page, month by month. `Print` prints it, or saves it as a PDF from the print window.

## Streak
The header and the line under `On this day` show your streak: the days in a row you have written, up to today (or yesterday, while today is still empty), and your longest one.

## Evening reminder
In `Diary settings` (the button at the top right) tick `Remind me when I have not written today` and choose the time (21:30 at first). The reminder comes only on days with no line yet, also with the app closed, while orOS is open. Tap it to write. It is off at first and stays on this device.

## Export and import
- `Text`, `CSV` and `JSON` in `Diary settings` download every line.
- `Choose a file` reads a CSV, JSON or text file with a date and a line per day, like the exports. Dates can be 2026-10-11 or 11/10/2026. Days that already have a line keep it.

## Save and open an orOS file
In `Diary settings`, `orOS file (.orosdiary)` saves every day's line, longer entry (with its place and weather), photo and own question as one `.orosdiary` file, for example to move your diary to another device without sync or to keep a copy. The file is a plain ZIP: unzipped it holds the diary as `content.json`, the photos in `media/`, plus `preview/diary.md` and `preview/diary.csv` for other programs. It is not encrypted, and the PIN lock does not cover it, so keep it somewhere safe. The reminder, the PIN and the view stay on this device.

`Open orOS file…` (or `Unzipped orOS folder…`, or "Open with" orOS on the computer) shows how many days are new and how many are already here, then `Add` adds only the new ones: opening the same file again adds nothing. A day that already has a line or a longer entry here keeps it, and a day you deleted here stays deleted; a file never deletes anything. Tick `Update with newer versions` to let days that were changed later in the file replace your copy. The photos go to Files › `Diary` › `Photos` when they are not there yet. `Undo` in the message removes what was added. With the PIN lock on, a file opened from outside waits until you unlock.

## Import from other diary apps
`Choose a file` also reads the exports of four journaling apps. A preview shows the dates, the entries and what will happen before anything is written; `Undo` in the message takes the whole import back.
- Day One: in Day One's settings, `Import/Export` › `Export` › `JSON`. Choose the ZIP (with its photos) or the `Journal.json` inside it.
- Journey: in Journey's settings, export the journal as a ZIP. Choose the ZIP.
- Diaro: in Diaro's settings, `Backup & Restore` › make a backup (ZIP or XML). Choose the ZIP or `DiaroBackup.xml`.
- Daylio: in Daylio, `More` › `Export entries` › `CSV`. Only the note title and the note come in; the moods belong to [Mood](app:mood).
- The first sentence of each day becomes the day's line (at most 280 characters) and the whole text goes into the longer entry. Several entries on one day are joined in time order, with a line between them.
- Place and weather come along when the file has them and the day has none. With `Import the photos too` up to four photos a day are saved in Files › `Diary` › `Photos`, drawn again without their camera data; pictures this browser cannot read (such as HEIC) are skipped.
- Your own text always wins: a day with a line keeps it and gets the imported text only when its longer entry is empty; a day whose longer entry has text is skipped. Importing the same file again adds nothing.
- Formatting is turned into plain text and the diary's simple formatting; nothing from the file is ever run.

## Sync and privacy
- Your lines follow you to all your devices through orOS sync, encrypted like the rest of your data. So do the longer entries, places, weather and your own questions; each part of a day syncs on its own, so writing the text on one device and adding a place on another keeps both.
- The PIN and the reminder stay on each device.
- The diary is personal, so it is not in the menu's search at first. To find your lines from the menu, tick it in [Settings](app:settings) › `Language & search`. The search choices stay on each device.
- When the same day is changed on two devices before they sync, the most recent change wins.

## Keyboard shortcuts
- [[←]] and [[→]]: the day before or after. While typing in the line, [[Alt+←]] and [[Alt+→]].
- [[Enter]] in the line: save; on a past day it also moves to the next day.
- [[Esc]]: close `Diary settings` or the photo.

## Limits
- The longer entry knows only the simple formatting above: no tables, no pictures inside the text, no colours. Up to 20,000 characters a day.
- Photos sync only through Vault Drive (they are files on the disk), and only four fit in a day. Very large images (over 40 MB) are refused.
- The weather comes from Open-Meteo and needs the internet. For days long ago it comes from their archive and may be missing.
- The PIN lock is a screen, not encryption; anyone who can open the browser's storage or your other devices can read the diary.
- The PDF book of many years with many photos can take a while and use much memory on a phone.

## Tips
> Write the first thing that comes to mind. One honest sentence is worth more than a long page you never write.
