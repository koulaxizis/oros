# Mail
Mail reads and sends your e-mail inside orOS. Add one or more accounts from any provider that offers IMAP, browse your folders, read your messages (even offline once they've been opened) and write new ones.

## Getting started
Press `Add account`, enter `Your name`, `E-mail address` and `Password`, and press `Connect`. For Gmail, iCloud, Yahoo, GMX and Zoho the server is filled in for you. For other providers, open `Server settings` and check the `Incoming (IMAP) server`, `Port / security` and `Username`; most providers use mail.yourdomain.

Gmail, iCloud and Yahoo need an app password: create one in your account's security settings and use it instead of your normal password.

## Read your mail
- The side panel lists your accounts and their folders (`Inbox`, `Sent`, `Drafts`, `Spam`, `Trash` and the rest) with unread counts. On a phone, open it with the menu button at the top left.
- Messages are listed newest first; unread ones have a dot. `Load older messages` at the bottom fetches more.
- Tap or click a message to read it. Opening it marks it as read on the server too.
- Replies are grouped with the message they answer into one conversation row, with the number of messages next to the sender. Click it to read the newest and see the rest under it; click again to fold it. While it is folded, selecting it selects the whole conversation. Turn grouping off with `Group messages by conversation` in the settings.
- With two or more accounts, `All inboxes` at the top of the side panel lists the Inbox of every account together, newest first; the coloured stripe on the left of each message shows its account. Selecting, swiping, search and older messages work in each account's own Inbox.
- Mail checks for new messages every couple of minutes while it's open; the refresh button (`Check for new mail`) checks now.
- While orOS is open, a new message in an Inbox also shows a notification, even with Mail closed (checked every 3 minutes). Click it to open the message. Turn it off under Settings › Notifications › Mail.

### Search
- The magnifier above the list (or [[/]]) searches the open folder. As you type, the messages already on this device are filtered, by sender, recipients and subject.
- Press [[Enter]] or `Search the whole folder on the server` to search every message in the folder, older ones and the text of the messages too (with `Everywhere`). The newest 100 hits are shown.
- `Sender` or `Subject` narrows the search. Accents don't matter on the device; on the server it depends on your provider.
- The buttons under a message work on search results too. [[Esc]] or the × closes the search.

### Pictures and formatting
Pictures from the internet are hidden at first, so the sender can't tell you opened the message. Press `Show pictures` to load them once, or `Always for` a sender to trust them from now on. In settings, `Always show plain text` shows every message as text; `Show the formatted version` switches one message back.

### Sender and link checks
- `Verified sender` next to the sender means the receiving server checked that the message really comes from that address's domain (SPF, DKIM, DMARC).
- A red `Careful` box means a check failed, or the sender's name shows a different address than the real one. Treat such a message as a possible fake.
- `Links that may mislead` lists links whose text names one site but open another, imitate a known name (like paypa1), or hide the real address. Inside the message each one is marked with ⚠ and the site it really opens.
- The checks run on this device and send nothing anywhere. No badge doesn't mean a message is fake; many servers don't record the checks.

### Attachments and invitations
Attachments are listed under the message. Tap one to open it: pictures and PDFs show a preview (PDFs up to 20 pages), and every file has `Save to device` and `Save to Files` (into the Mail folder of [Files](app:files)). An invitation shows a card with `Add to Calendar`, which opens a new event in [Calendar](app:calendar) already filled in, and `Show on the map`, which opens its place in [Maps](app:maps).

## Organise your mail
- Under an open message, the buttons `Archive`, `Delete`, `Spam` (`Not spam` inside Spam), `Star`, `Mark as unread` and `Move to…` act on it. `Archive` uses the account's Archive folder and creates one if there is none.
- `Delete` moves a message to Trash. Inside Trash or Spam, or on an account without a Trash folder, it becomes `Delete for good` and asks first.
- After a move, `Undo` appears for a few seconds; the change reaches the server only after that.
- `Select` above the list adds checkboxes: tick several messages (or `Select all`) and use the same buttons on all of them. `Done` leaves selection.
- On a phone, swipe a message right to archive it or left to delete it.
- Marking a message as spam also teaches your provider's spam filter.

### Folders
The folder button next to an account in the side panel opens `Folders`. `New folder` creates one at the top level or `Inside` another folder. The pencil renames a folder (its subfolders move with it) and the bin deletes it together with the messages in it, after a question; a folder with subfolders is deleted after them. Inbox, Sent, Drafts, Trash, Spam and Archive stay as they are. Rules that move mail into a renamed folder follow it; those for a deleted folder are switched off.

## Rules
Rules sort new mail in the Inbox by themselves: for example, move newsletters to a folder, send a sender to Spam, or star mail from your boss.
- Open the gear button and press `Add rule`, or press `Create a rule…` under a message to start with its sender.
- Choose what to look at (`the sender`, `a recipient`, `the subject`, or all three) and the words it `contains`. Several words separated by commas mean any of them.
- Choose what happens: move to Spam, Trash, Archive or another folder, `mark as read` or `star it`.
- `Also run it on the Inbox now` applies a new rule to the messages already there, with `Undo`.
- Tick or untick a rule in settings to switch it on or off; `Edit` changes or deletes it.

>! Rules work while orOS is open on any of your devices, even with Mail closed. Mail that arrives while orOS is closed everywhere is sorted only if it is still among the newest when orOS opens. A message a rule moves or marks as read shows no notification.

## Write and reply
- The pen button at the top (`New message`) opens a new message. Fill in `To` (several addresses separated by commas), press `Cc / Bcc` for copies, add a `Subject` and write in `Message`, then press `Send`.
- Under an open message, `Reply` answers the sender, `Reply all` everyone else in it too, and `Forward` sends it on to someone new. The original text is quoted under yours.
- `Attach files` adds files from this device (or drop them on the message), `From Files` from [Files](app:files); the × removes one. Files can be up to 7 MB together. `Forward` brings the message's files along.
- With several accounts, `From` chooses which one sends.
- A copy of what you send goes to the account's `Sent` folder.
- `Discard` (or [[Esc]]) asks first when you have written something, and offers `Save draft`.

### Drafts
`Save draft` files the message in the account's `Drafts` folder on the server (created if there is none), so you can finish it later, on another device or in webmail. Saving again replaces the earlier copy, and sending erases it. Open a message in `Drafts` and press `Continue writing` to carry on. Drafts keep `Bcc`; it never goes out with the sent message.

### Formatting
The bar above `Message` adds **bold** ([[Ctrl+B]]), italic ([[Ctrl+I]]), underline ([[Ctrl+U]]), lists, links ([[Ctrl+K]]) and colour; `Clear formatting` removes them. A formatted message also carries a plain-text copy for people whose mail app shows only text. A message with no formatting goes as plain text. Pasted text keeps only its words, not its look.

### Signature and outgoing server
In an account, `Signature` adds a few lines under each new message and reply. It can be formatted like a message and hold a small picture, such as a logo (`Picture or logo`; it is made smaller to fit). With several accounts, changing `From` swaps the signature. Sending uses the `Outgoing (SMTP) server` in `Server settings`, with the same username and password as incoming mail; it is filled in with the incoming one and checked when you save.

### Offline: the Outbox
Messages you send while offline wait in `Outbox` in the side panel and go out by themselves when you are back online. If one is refused (for example a wrong address), it stays there with the reason: open `Outbox` to `Edit`, `Send now` or `Delete` it.

## Accounts and settings
The gear button opens `Mail settings`: your `Accounts` (with `Edit`), `Always show plain text`, `Group messages by conversation` and the `Relay address`. In an account you can change its details or `Remove account`; your mail stays on the server.

## Sync and backup
Your accounts (without passwords), your rules, the relay address and the senders whose pictures you allow sync between your devices through [orOS sync](help:t/sync). Passwords stay sealed on each device and are never synced or exported, so a new device shows `Password needed on this device`: press `Enter password` once there. Downloaded messages, the open folder and `Always show plain text` stay on this device.

>! Removing an account removes it from orOS on all your devices.

## Keyboard shortcuts
- [[J]] or [[↓]] opens the next message, [[K]] or [[↑]] the previous one.
- [[Esc]] goes back to the list on a narrow screen.
- [[/]] searches the open folder.
- [[C]] writes a new message; with a message open, [[R]] replies, [[A]] replies to all and [[F]] forwards.
- On the open message, or on the selected ones: [[E]] archives, [[Delete]] or [[#]] deletes, [[!]] marks as spam (or not spam), [[S]] stars, [[U]] marks as read or unread and [[V]] moves to a folder.

## Tips
- Browsers can't talk to mail servers directly, so Mail goes through the orOS mail relay. It passes your mail through and keeps nothing.
- The relay you set here also lets [Reader](app:feeds) and [Podcasts](app:podcasts) read sites that block browsers.

## Limits
- Attached files can be up to 7 MB together; previews show pictures and up to 20 pages of a PDF.
- The Outbox stays on the device where it was written; use `Save draft` to continue on another device.
- Outlook.com and Hotmail accounts don't work yet; they no longer accept passwords from other apps.
- Up to 10 accounts. Only the most recent messages are kept for offline reading.
