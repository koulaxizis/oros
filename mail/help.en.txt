# Mail
Mail reads and sends your e-mail inside orOS. Add one or more accounts from any provider that offers IMAP, browse your folders, read your messages (even offline once they've been opened) and write new ones.

## Getting started
Press `Add account`, enter `Your name`, `E-mail address` and `Password`, and press `Connect`. For Gmail, iCloud, Yahoo, GMX and Zoho the server is filled in for you. For other providers, open `Server settings` and check the `Incoming (IMAP) server`, `Port / security` and `Username`; most providers use mail.yourdomain.

Gmail, iCloud and Yahoo need an app password: create one in your account's security settings and use it instead of your normal password.

## Read your mail
- The side panel lists your accounts and their folders (`Inbox`, `Sent`, `Drafts`, `Spam`, `Trash` and the rest) with unread counts. On a phone, open it with the menu button at the top left.
- Messages are listed newest first; unread ones have a dot. `Load older messages` at the bottom fetches more.
- Tap or click a message to read it. Opening it marks it as read on the server too.
- Mail checks for new messages every couple of minutes while it's open; the refresh button (`Check for new mail`) checks now.

### Pictures and formatting
Pictures from the internet are hidden at first, so the sender can't tell you opened the message. Press `Show pictures` to load them once, or `Always for` a sender to trust them from now on. In settings, `Always show plain text` shows every message as text; `Show the formatted version` switches one message back.

### Attachments and invitations
Attachments are listed under the message; tap one to save it. An invitation shows a card with `Add to Calendar`, which opens a new event in [Calendar](app:calendar) already filled in, and `Show on the map`, which opens its place in [Maps](app:maps).

## Write and reply
- The pen button at the top (`New message`) opens a new message. Fill in `To` (several addresses separated by commas), press `Cc / Bcc` for copies, add a `Subject` and write in `Message`, then press `Send`.
- Under an open message, `Reply` answers the sender, `Reply all` everyone else in it too, and `Forward` sends it on to someone new. The original text is quoted under yours.
- With several accounts, `From` chooses which one sends.
- A copy of what you send goes to the account's `Sent` folder.
- `Discard` (or [[Esc]]) asks first when you have written something.

### Signature and outgoing server
In an account, `Signature` adds a few lines under each new message and reply. Sending uses the `Outgoing (SMTP) server` in `Server settings`, with the same username and password as incoming mail; it is filled in with the incoming one and checked when you save.

### Offline: the Outbox
Messages you send while offline wait in `Outbox` in the side panel and go out by themselves when you are back online. If one is refused (for example a wrong address), it stays there with the reason: open `Outbox` to `Edit`, `Send now` or `Delete` it.

## Accounts and settings
The gear button opens `Mail settings`: your `Accounts` (with `Edit`), `Always show plain text` and the `Relay address`. In an account you can change its details or `Remove account`; your mail stays on the server.

## Sync and backup
Your accounts (without passwords), the relay address and the senders whose pictures you allow sync between your devices through [orOS sync](help:t/sync). Passwords stay sealed on each device and are never synced or exported, so a new device shows `Password needed on this device`: press `Enter password` once there. Downloaded messages, the open folder and `Always show plain text` stay on this device.

>! Removing an account removes it from orOS on all your devices.

## Keyboard shortcuts
- [[J]] or [[↓]] opens the next message, [[K]] or [[↑]] the previous one.
- [[Esc]] goes back to the list on a narrow screen.
- [[C]] writes a new message; with a message open, [[R]] replies, [[A]] replies to all and [[F]] forwards.

## Tips
- Browsers can't talk to mail servers directly, so Mail goes through the orOS mail relay. It passes your mail through and keeps nothing.
- The relay you set here also lets [Reader](app:feeds) and [Podcasts](app:podcasts) read sites that block browsers.

## Limits
- Messages are plain text, without attachments; forwarding sends the text only. Deleting and moving messages aren't there yet.
- Drafts are not saved on the server; the Outbox stays on the device where it was written.
- Outlook.com and Hotmail accounts don't work yet; they no longer accept passwords from other apps.
- Up to 10 accounts. Only the most recent messages are kept for offline reading.
