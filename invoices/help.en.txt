# Billing
Billing keeps your quotes and your invoices in one app. The `Quotes` tab holds the quotes you used to write in Quote (they are all here, nothing moved); the other tabs are your invoices, received invoices, clients and dashboard. Billing opens on the tab you used last.

Invoices makes invoices, receipts, proformas and credit notes with VAT and automatic numbering, as PDF. It tracks who has paid and who still owes, shows your revenue, and keeps your clients. Invoices you issue elsewhere (for example in myDATA) can be recorded here too, so all payments are in one place.

>! orOS does not send anything to ΑΑΔΕ / myDATA. Greek business-to-business invoices that must be issued electronically are issued in myDATA or with your provider; record them here with `New` › `Record an invoice issued elsewhere`. Your accountant has the final word on what you must issue.

## Getting started
The first time, a banner asks you to `Add details`: your name or company, tax number (ΑΦΜ), tax office (ΔΟΥ), address, bank accounts (IBAN) and, if you like, a logo. They go on every invoice you issue from then on.

Press `New` and choose what to make: `Invoice`, `Receipt`, `Proforma`, `Credit note`, `Record an invoice issued elsewhere`, or `From a template`. Pick a `Client` (or add one with +), the `Date`, when it is `Due` (the buttons set 15, 30 or 60 days), and the `Currency`. Then add the `Items`: description, quantity, unit price, discount and VAT %. The totals update as you type.

`Save draft` keeps it to finish later. `Preview PDF` shows how it will look, with a DRAFT mark. `Issue` gives it its number and freezes it.

## Issuing and numbering
When you issue, the document gets the next number of its series, such as INV-2026-0001. The number goes up by one and starts again each year. After issuing, the items and the client can't change: a mistake is corrected with a `Credit note`, and a document that should not exist is cancelled with `Cancel` (its number stays used and it counts as zero).

If you issue on two devices before they sync, both may take the same number. Invoices then shows a banner with a button to give one of them a new number. To avoid it, add a second series in `Settings` › `Numbering` (for example with the prefix INV-B) and tick `Use on this device` on the second device.

## Withholding, discount and VAT exemption
- `Discount on the total (%)` is taken off before VAT.
- `Withholding tax (%)` (for example 20 % on services by a freelancer) is taken off the amount payable; the invoice still shows the full total.
- Under `Terms, notes and more` you can write a `VAT exemption reason` (printed when an item has 0 % VAT), the `Payment terms`, `Notes`, and choose the `PDF language` (Greek or English) for this document.

## Payments and statuses
Open an issued document to see it and its actions. `Mark as paid` records the whole balance as one payment; `Add payment` records part of it with its date and method (bank transfer, cash, card, IRIS, PayPal). Each document shows its status: `Unpaid`, `Partly paid`, `Paid` or `Overdue` (after its due date). A credit note lowers what the invoice it corrects still owes. Tap a payment to change or delete it.

A receipt is usually paid at once: leave `Paid now` ticked when you issue it.

## Proformas, credit notes and records
- A `Proforma` asks for payment without being an invoice. `Make invoice` turns it into an invoice draft with the same items.
- A `Credit note` corrects an invoice you issued: choose it under `Corrects`.
- A recorded invoice (`Record an invoice issued elsewhere`) keeps the number, net and VAT amounts and the ΜΑΡΚ of an invoice issued in myDATA or by your provider. `Attach` keeps its official PDF on the orOS disk, under Invoices. Payments and statuses work as for any invoice.

## Import invoice PDFs (Novus / timologisi.online)
If you issue invoices in Novus (timologisi.online) or with another provider, you don't need to type them again: `New` › `Import invoice PDFs` and pick one or more PDFs (or drag the PDFs onto Invoices). Only the text of each PDF is read; nothing in the file is ever run.
- For each PDF you see the number, client, date, payable amount and due date. Tick what you want and press `Import`. Nothing is saved before that.
- Each invoice becomes a `Record an invoice issued elsewhere` with its net amount, VAT, withholding (the PDF's deductions), ΜΑΡΚ and its lines in the notes. The PDF itself is kept as the official file in the Invoices folder.
- The client is found by ΑΦΜ. If there is none, a new client is made from the PDF, linked to the [CRM](app:crm) partner with the same ΑΦΜ.
- The due date is the invoice date plus the payment days in `Settings` (30 by default). An invoice paid in cash or by card comes in already paid.
- A PDF you already imported (same ΜΑΡΚ) shows as `Already here` and never comes in twice, not even on another device.
- An invoice another business issued to you becomes a `Received invoice`. To tell the two apart, put your ΑΦΜ in `Settings`.
- A ⚠ means the PDF's amounts do not agree with each other: that invoice is not ticked for you, check it first. A PDF that could not be read (a scan, for example) or a credit note opens with `Record by hand`, with what was found already filled in.

## Received invoices
The `Received` tab keeps the invoices your suppliers send you, so you see what you owe, to whom and by when. `New` › `Record a received invoice` (or `+ Received invoice` in the tab) asks for the supplier, their ΑΦΜ, the invoice number, its date, the pay-by date and the net and VAT amounts. If you use [CRM](help:a/crm), pick the supplier's record under `Partner in CRM`: the invoice then shows on that partner's card (a received invoice without a partner shows on the card whose ΑΦΜ matches). Open one to add a payment, mark it paid, edit or delete it. Received invoices never count in your revenue, VAT, the dashboard or myDATA: they only keep track of what you owe. One still to pay shows in [Today](app:today) from a few days before its pay-by date.

## PDF, sending and sharing
`PDF` saves the document as a PDF with your logo and details; `Share PDF` hands it to another app where your device allows it. `Email` opens a new message in [Mail](app:mail) (or your email app) with the subject and text filled in. In Mail the PDF is already attached (a recorded invoice brings its official PDF) and a copy stays on the orOS disk under Invoices; in another email app, attach the PDF you saved. `Mark as sent` notes the date you sent it.

`Send the lines to an app…` on an invoice, and the same button under a quote in `Quotes`, sends the lines as a table (description, quantity, unit price, discount, VAT and amount, then net, VAT and total) to another orOS app, such as [Spreadsheet](app:spreadsheet), [Writer](app:writer) or [Mail](app:mail). The other app only opens with it filled in; the document doesn't change.

### Pay online
Put your own payment link in `Settings` › `Pay online link`: a Stripe Payment Link (https://buy.stripe.com/…, made in your Stripe dashboard) or any other https payment page. Every invoice or proforma that still has something to pay then shows `Pay online` on its PDF, as a link and a QR code, and the email gets a line with the link. With a Stripe link, the invoice number goes along as the payment's reference and the client's email is filled in on the payment page. orOS never asks for a payment key and never sees the payment: when the money arrives, record it with `Mark as paid` or `Add payment` as usual. Each business profile can have its own link.

## Send to myDATA (timologisi.online)
If you issue through timologisi.online, connect your account first in [Settings](app:settings) › `Connected services` (environment, your ΑΦΜ and the API key from the timologisi.online developer portal). An issued invoice, receipt or credit note then shows `Send to myDATA`. Pick the account, the document type (orOS suggests one from the client and the items), the payment method and, when needed, the VAT exemption reason or the withholding category, then `Send`. The document gets its ΜΑΡΚ, UID, authentication code and a link to the official PDF, shown on the document.
- myDATA only takes documents dated today and in euro. VAT rates 24, 13, 6, 17, 9, 4 and 0 %.
- Each document uses the account's credits (1 for an invoice, 0.5 for a receipt).
- A sent document can't be cancelled or renumbered. A mistake is corrected with a `Credit note`, which is sent the same way and points to the original ΜΑΡΚ.
- If no answer comes, the document shows that it is waiting: press `Check in myDATA` before anything else. orOS never sends the same document twice by itself.
- If AADE was unreachable, the provider issues it anyway and the ΜΑΡΚ comes later: `Check in myDATA` fetches it.
- A test (sandbox) account issues nothing real, but the provider still e-mails the client's registered addresses: use the test ΑΦΜ 987654324 for test clients.
- Nothing is sent unless you press `Send`. Your key never leaves your devices in clear.

## Clients
On `Clients`, add a `New client` with name, profession, ΑΦΜ, ΔΟΥ, address, country, email and phone. `From Contacts` and `From Quote` bring in people you already have. A client shows how many documents it has and what it still owes; open it to make a `New invoice` or `Show documents`. Changing a client never changes documents already issued: they keep the details they were issued with.

## Templates and recurring invoices
`Save as template` in the editor keeps the client and items to reuse with `New` › `From a template`. Set `Repeat` to every month, every 3 months or every year and a draft is made on that day for you to check and issue. Nothing is issued by itself.

## Dashboard
`Dashboard` shows, for a `Period`, how much you `Invoiced` and `Collected`, what is `Outstanding` and `Overdue`, a bar per month, your top clients and the VAT per rate. Choose the `Currency` when you invoice in more than one. `Export documents (CSV)` and `Export payments (CSV)` save them for a spreadsheet or your accountant. Inside orOS, `Open in Spreadsheet` opens the documents of the period straight in a new sheet in [Spreadsheet](app:spreadsheet), with net, VAT and total as numbers and their totals at the bottom when the period has a single currency.

The `Quotes` part of the Dashboard follows your quotes of the same period and currency to the money: how many you sent and how many were accepted, invoiced and paid, month by month, the share accepted, what is accepted but not yet invoiced, and what their invoices still owe. It is worked out each time you look; nothing is stored. A quote counts as invoiced once an invoice made from it with `Make invoice` is issued.

### The month for your accountant
`Pack for the accountant…` on the Dashboard makes one ZIP for a month (last month is picked): `summary.txt` with the month's totals, the documents' and payments' CSV, a PDF of every document issued here and, from the other apps on this device, [Budget](app:budget) entries with their receipt files, [Stock](app:stock) purchases and sales, and the month's orders of your WooCommerce shop (read when you make the pack; Settings › [Connected services](app:settings)). Untick what you don't want. The ZIP is saved in [Files](app:files) under Invoices/Accountant; then `E-mail to the accountant` opens [Mail](app:mail) with a message to the address you typed (kept on this device), the summary as text and the ZIP attached. Nothing is sent until you send it from Mail. A pack over 7 MB is too big for one e-mail: share it from Files instead.

## Calendar, Today and reminders
- Unpaid invoices show in [Calendar](app:calendar) on their due date, under the `Invoices` chip; tapping one opens it here.
- The [Today](app:today) summary lists invoices that are overdue or due in the next days, and recurring invoices ready to check.
- orOS reminds you once a day when invoices are overdue or due within two days, and again when there are more; tapping the reminder opens the first one. See [Notifications](help:t/notifications).

## Budget, Quote, Timesheet and CRM
- Every payment you record goes to [Budget](app:budget) by itself as freelance income (with its partner and the invoice number): the payment shows `In Budget (automatic)`. It appears the next time Budget opens on a device where you also use Invoices. Change the amount or date here and Budget follows; delete the payment and its income goes too. What you change or delete in Budget stays as you left it. Turn it off in `Settings` › `Payments go to Budget by themselves`; payments recorded before stay as they were.
- With that setting off (and for older payments), next to each payment, `Add to Budget` opens [Budget](app:budget) with the payment filled in as freelance income; check it and save it there. It shows when the payment is in Budget's currency, and remembers that you sent it.
- `Make invoice` on an accepted quote in the `Quotes` tab, and `Create invoice` in a [Timesheet](app:timesheet) report, open a new invoice here with the client and the lines filled in.
- On `Clients`, `From CRM` brings in customers from [CRM](app:crm); each one stays linked, so CRM can show its invoices.
- On an issued invoice or receipt whose lines have a product code, `Take out of stock` opens a sale in [Stock](app:stock) with those products and prices; a credit note offers `Back into stock` for a customer return. Nothing changes in Stock until you save there. The button shows only once you use Stock.

## Statements, reminders, EU clients and more businesses
- `Statement PDF` on a client (tap the client on `Clients`) lists every document that client still owes, with what was paid, the balance and how late each one is, and the open total per currency.
- On an invoice that is due within a week or already late, `Payment reminder` opens an email with a polite text, or a firm one once it is overdue. In [Mail](app:mail) the PDF is attached; edit the text before you send it.
- When the client is a business (it has a VAT number) in another EU country, the editor offers the reverse charge: `Services, 0 % VAT` or `Goods, 0 % VAT` set every line to 0 % VAT and add the standard note to the VAT exemption. Check it with your accountant; nothing is applied unless you tap it.
- A PDF in euros shows a payment QR code (EPC / SEPA, the one banking apps scan) with your first IBAN, the amount still owed and the document number. Turn it off in `Settings` (Defaults).
- More than one business? In `Settings`, `Add a business profile` keeps another set of details, logo, bank lines, terms and footer. Pick the business on each document (`Business`, shown once there are two). Link a numbering series to a profile (`Profile`, next to each series) so each business keeps its own numbers. Templates and copies keep their business.

## Quotes
Everything in this section was the Quote app. Old links and shortcuts to Quote open this tab. `Settings` (the gear) is one page for both: its `Quotes` part opens the payment terms texts and the quote templates.
Quote helps you write price quotes for your clients: line items with discounts and VAT, payment terms, instalments, and a PDF you can send.

### Getting started
The `Create` tab is a blank quote. It gets a number like OFF-2026-001 automatically; the round arrow next to `Quote #` gives it the next free number again. Fill in `Date`, `Due`, `Status` and `Currency`, then press `Save`. Any currency works: the common ones are at the top of the list, and amounts are shown the way that currency writes them (no decimals for yen, for example).

Until you press `Save`, a new quote is a draft kept only on this device. If you close the app, it's restored next time. After the first save, every change is saved as you type.

A quote can also start in another app: `Create quote` in a [Timesheet](app:timesheet) report fills in a new quote for you. Check it and press `Save`.

### Clients
Tap or click `Select client` to pick a client, or the **+** next to it to add one. `Client Details` holds `Name`, `Email`, `Phone`, `Address` and `Tax ID / VAT`. The pencil edits the chosen client; `Delete` in that window removes the client after you confirm. Quotes and invoices keep their own client lists: the list also shows, under `From Billing's clients`, the invoice clients your quotes don't have yet, and choosing one opens `Client Details` filled in (it is added only when you save). The invoice editor's `Client` list does the same with the quotes' clients.

### Line items and totals
Press **+** next to `Line Items` to add a row with `Code/SKU`, `Description`, `Qty`, `Unit Price`, `Disc %` and `VAT %`. You can type amounts with a comma or a dot.

Below the items you see `Subtotal`, `VAT` and `Total`. `Discount (%)` takes a discount off the whole quote. Changing `VAT (%)` there sets the same rate on every line, and new lines take it too.

`Show the total in` adds the total in another currency, at the day's European Central Bank rate (the same rates as the Calculator's [Convert](app:units) mode). It's only a guide for you: the quote, the PDF and the email stay in the quote's own currency. The choice stays on this device.

### Instalments
Under `Instalments`, set a `Count` and press `Generate` to split the total evenly. You can then change each date and `Amount`; a line underneath tells you whether the sum matches the total.

### Payment terms, notes and templates
Tap or click a payment preset (`Bank Transfer`, `PayPal`, `IRIS`, `Cash`) to add its text to `Payment Terms`. The pencil lets you change the preset texts, for example to add your IBAN. Use `Notes` for any other terms.

`Save as Template` stores the items, discount, payment terms, notes and instalments under a name. The `Template Library` button opens your templates, where `Use` starts from one. `Duplicate` makes a copy with a new number and the status `Draft`. On a quote marked `Accepted`, `Make invoice` opens a new invoice right here, in the `Invoices` tab, with the same client and items; check it and issue it. Nothing is saved until you press `Save draft` or `Issue`.

### Your quotes, PDF and email
The `Quotes` tab lists all your quotes. Search by number, client, item, notes or payment terms, and use the funnel to filter by status. Tap or click a quote to open it.

While a quote is open, the buttons at the top let you:
- `Export PDF`: save the quote as an A4 PDF. Your business details from `Settings` › `Your business` are printed at its top right.
- `Send via email`: when you have an account in [Mail](app:mail), a new message opens there with the client's address, the quote number, due date and total filled in, and the PDF already attached. Without a Mail account, your own email program opens instead, and you attach the PDF yourself.

### Acceptance by link
Your client can accept a quote online, without an account and without anything stored on a server.

1. Save the quote and press `Send for acceptance`. Add your name or business and, if you like, your email for the answer, then `Make the link`.
2. Send the link: `Copy link`, `Share…`, or `Email with PDF` (the message includes both the PDF and the link).
3. Your client opens the link in any browser, sees the quote, types their name and presses `Accept`. They get an answer link to send back to you.
4. Open the answer link, or press `Record acceptance` and paste it. orOS checks it against the quote you sent and offers `Mark as accepted`, then `Make invoice`.

The whole quote travels inside the link (after the `#`, which browsers never send to a server). Each link carries a random code and a fingerprint (SHA-256) of the quote; the answer must carry both back. So an answer counts only for the exact version you sent: if someone changed the link's contents, or you changed the quote and made a new link, orOS tells you and doesn't count it.

### Sync and backup
Quotes, clients, templates and payment presets sync between your devices through [orOS sync](help:t/sync). An unsaved draft and the quote you had open stay on this device. The orOS [backup](help:t/backup) keeps a copy of everything.

### Save and open an orOS file
In the `Quotes` tab, the folder button opens `Save and open`. `orOS file (.orosquote)` saves every quote (with its lines and instalments), client and template as one `.orosquote` file, for example to move them to another device without sync or to give a colleague your quotes. The file is a plain ZIP: unzipped it holds the data as `content.json` plus `preview/quotes.md` (every quote and template as text, with totals). The payment presets and the acceptance-link codes stay on this device and are not in the file. It is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere safe.

`Open orOS file…` (or `Unzipped orOS folder…`, or "Open with" orOS on the computer) shows how many records are new and how many are already here, then `Add` adds only the new ones, with their own ids: opening the same file again adds nothing. A quote already here is kept as it is, and one you deleted here stays deleted; a file never deletes anything. Tick `Update with newer versions` to let quotes that were changed later in the file replace your copy. `Undo` in the message removes what was added.

`Open as copy…` adds the file's quotes and templates as new ones, even ones you already have: each quote gets a new number and starts as a `Draft`. Use it when a file is a set of quotes to start from. Clients come in as usual (only the ones you don't have). `Undo` removes the copies.

### Keyboard shortcuts
- [[Alt+N]] starts a new quote (when you're not typing in a field).

### Tips
- orOS reminds you when a quote marked `Sent` or `Accepted` is due within two days or overdue; tapping the reminder opens the quote. See [Notifications](help:t/notifications).
- Keep the status up to date: change it to `Rejected` or `Expired` and the reminders stop.

### Limits
- The total in another currency is for information only and needs a connection to get the day's rates (the last ones stay for offline use).
- The PDF is attached automatically only in [Mail](app:mail); with another email program, attach it by hand. Very large quotes may also be too long for an acceptance link: send the PDF instead.
- Acceptance by link is not a signature: it proves that the answer matches the quote you sent, not who typed the name. Anyone who has the link can see the quote. For a binding agreement, ask for a signed copy.

## Settings
The gear button opens `Settings`: your business details and logo, the defaults for new documents (currency, VAT %, payment days, withholding, PDF language, payment terms, footer), the numbering series, and your templates.

## Save and open an orOS file
In `Settings`, under `Data`, `orOS file (.orosinvoices)` saves every document, payment, client, numbering series, template and business profile as one `.orosinvoices` file, for example to move them to another device without sync or to give your accountant a copy. Attached official PDFs and scans in `/internal/Invoices/` go into the file too. Accounts and keys of connected services (myDATA through timologisi.online, payment providers) are never saved in it. The file is a plain ZIP: unzipped it holds everything as `content.json`, the PDFs in `media/`, and `preview/documents.csv` and `preview/payments.csv` (the same columns as `Export documents (CSV)` and `Export payments (CSV)`, all dates) for other programs. It is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere safe.

`Open orOS file…` (or `Unzipped orOS folder…`, or "Open with" orOS on the computer) shows how many records are new and how many are already here, then `Add` adds only the new ones, with their own ids and numbers: opening the same file again adds nothing. A record already here is kept as it is, one you deleted here stays deleted, and a file never deletes anything. A document that is already issued, cancelled or recorded here never changes, and numbers are never changed: a document whose number is already taken here by another document, or an invoice already recorded here with the same number and ΑΦΜ, stays out. `Update with newer versions` lets drafts, clients, payments and other records that were changed later in the file replace your copy. An attached PDF is written to `/internal/Invoices/` only when it is not there yet. `Undo` in the message takes back what was added.

## Sync and backup
Documents, payments, clients, series, templates and your business details sync between your devices through [orOS sync](help:t/sync). The filters and the series you use on each device stay on that device; an unsaved draft is kept on the device until you save it.

In `Settings`, `Back up (JSON)` saves everything to a file and `Restore from backup` merges a backup with what you have: nothing is replaced or lost. The orOS [backup](help:t/backup) includes Invoices too. Attached official PDFs live on the orOS disk, not in the backup.

## Keyboard shortcuts
- [[N]] makes a new document.
- [[/]] goes to the search box.
- [[Esc]] goes back from a document, the editor or Settings.
- [[Ctrl+Z]] undoes the last change that offered `Undo`.

## Limits
- A document has at most 200 items.
- Amounts up to 1.000.000.000,00 in any currency; each document has one currency.
- The logo is made smaller (at most 600 × 300) so sync stays light.
- `Pay online` opens your own payment page; it doesn't show the amount due unless your payment page does (a Stripe Payment Link has a fixed or customer-chosen price), and payments are not recorded automatically.
