Household

Open orOS to use households.

Share with the people you live with

Make a household, invite your family or flatmates with a link, then share a shopping list or any list from To-Do. Everyone sees changes in seconds and edits from their own orOS.

Everything is encrypted on your devices. The orOS server keeps only scrambled data it cannot read, and no account is needed.

Households you are no longer in, kept on your orOS disk (folder “Household backup”). Bring their lists back to your apps or start the household again.

    Nothing is lost: everything shared here is on this device and in the copy on your orOS disk.
