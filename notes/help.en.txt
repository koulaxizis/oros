# Notes
Notes is a plain-text notebook organised as a tree of pages. Nest pages inside each other, link them wiki-style, tag them with labels and keep separate notebooks for separate parts of your life.

## Getting started
Your first notebook is called `Notes`. Tap or click the **+** button under the page tree to add a page, then type a `Title` and start writing in the big text area. There's no save button: Notes saves as you type.

On a phone the tree is hidden while you write. Tap the ☰ button (`Show tree`) next to the title to bring it back; picking a page closes it again. On a wider screen you can drag the divider between the tree and the page to make the tree wider or narrower.

## Pages and the tree
Right-click a page in the tree, or long-press it on a touch screen, to open its menu:
- `New sub-page` adds a page inside this one.
- `Rename`, `Move up`, `Move down`.
- `Move to…` lets you pick another parent page, or `Top level`.
- `Pin to top` keeps the page first among its neighbours; `Unpin` undoes it.
- `Labels` attaches or removes labels.
- `Export page (.txt)` and `Export notebook (.zip)`.
- `Delete` removes the page after you confirm.

On a computer you can also drag a page in the tree and drop it onto another page to make it a sub-page, or onto empty space to move it to the top level.

## Links between pages
Write a page title in double square brackets, like `[[Shopping]]`, to link to it. Under the text you'll see `Links` (pages this one links to) and `Backlinks` (pages that link here). Tap or click a link to open that page. If no page has that title yet, the link creates it as a sub-page of the one you're on.

## Labels and search
In the `Labels` menu of a page, type a name in `New label…`, pick a colour and press `Add`. Tap a label to attach it to the page or remove it; the × next to a label deletes it. Labels show as coloured dots in the tree and as chips above the text. Each notebook has its own labels.

The tag button under the tree opens `All labels`: every label with its number of pages. Tap one to list its pages.

The search button (or [[Ctrl+K]]) searches titles and text in the current notebook. Type at least 2 characters; title matches come first.

## Notebooks
Pick a notebook from the list at the top of the tree. The book button with a plus under the tree (`New notebook`) creates one. The ⋯ button next to the list (or a right-click on the list) has `Rename notebook` and `Delete notebook`. Deleting a notebook removes all its pages and labels.

## Sync and backup
Notebooks, pages and labels sync between your devices through [orOS sync](help:t/sync). Which page is open, which branches of the tree are unfolded and the tree's width stay on this device.

If the same page is edited on two devices before they sync, both versions are kept: one of them appears in the tree marked `conflict copy`. Compare them, keep what you need and delete the other.

The download button under the tree exports the notebook as a .zip of plain-text files, with folders for pages that have sub-pages. With more than one notebook it asks: `Current notebook (.zip)` or `All notebooks (.zip)`. There's no import. For a full copy of orOS, see [backup](help:t/backup).

## Keyboard shortcuts
- [[Ctrl+K]] opens search ([[Cmd+K]] on a Mac).
- [[Enter]] in search opens the first result.
- [[Esc]] closes search, menus and the labels panel.

## Tips
- Links ignore upper and lower case, so `[[shopping]]` finds “Shopping”.

## Limits
- Notes are plain text only: no formatting, images or attachments.
- Links and search work within the current notebook only.
- Exported files end with a short “Made with orOS” line.
