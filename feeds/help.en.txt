# Reader
Reader follows news sites, blogs and channels through their RSS, Atom or JSON feeds. Articles arrive in one place, stay readable offline, and what you've read syncs across your devices.

## Getting started
Press `Add a feed` and type a site or feed address, even a YouTube channel, then `Find`. Reader looks for the feeds itself, including those of YouTube, Reddit, Mastodon and GitHub. Pick one under `Feeds found`, choose a `Folder` if you like, and press `Subscribe`.

Moving from another reader? Use `Import from another reader (OPML)`.

## Read
- The side panel has `All articles`, `Starred`, `Read later`, your folders and feeds with unread counts, and your tags and watched words. On a phone, open it with the menu button.
- Tap or click an article to read it and mark it read. Audio and video in an article play right there.
- Pictures are off until you press `Show pictures` or `Always for this feed`.
- `Full article` loads the whole text from the site when the feed only carries a summary.
- Under an article: `Star`, `Read later`, `Tags`, `Mark as unread`, `Open the original`, `Copy the link` and `Send to…`. `Send to…` can `Save to Bookmarks`, `Save as a note` in [Notes](app:notes), `Make a task` in [To-Do](app:todo), `Send by email` or `Share…`.
- The list buttons switch the style (`List`, `Cards`, `Magazine`), toggle `Unread only` / `Show all`, and `Mark as read` everything, or what's older than a day or a week, with `Undo`.
- On a phone, swipe an article right to mark it read or unread, left to star it.

## Organise
- The ⋯ button (`Edit`) on a selected feed changes its name, folder, pictures and `Load the full article when I open one`, shows how often it posts, and has `Check now` and `Unsubscribe`. On a folder, it renames or deletes it (its feeds stay).
- Tags group articles your way. Saved articles stay for ever, even after you unsubscribe.

### Search, rules and watched words
The search button looks through titles and text, `Here` or `Everywhere`, without caring about Greek accents. `Watch these words` turns a search into a list in the side panel.

In `Settings`, `Rules and watched words` acts on new articles as they arrive: mark them read (mute), star them, add them to `Read later` or give them a tag. A rule can also `Notify me about new articles`, and so can a single feed in its `Edit` window: new articles then appear in the bell and open the article. See [Notifications](help:t/notifications). `Statistics` shows which feeds are busy, quiet or failing.

## Settings
`Settings` sets how often to `Check for new articles`, the order, list style, text size and width, pictures, `Mark as read while scrolling the list`, `Show a story that two feeds carry only once`, and the `Relay address`.

## Sync and backup
Your subscriptions, folders, tags, rules, saved articles, read state and most settings sync between your devices through [orOS sync](help:t/sync). Downloaded articles stay on this device for 60 days (up to 200 per feed); text size, width and the open view stay here too. `Export OPML` and `Import OPML` in `Settings` move your feeds to or from other readers.

## Keyboard shortcuts
- [[J]] / [[K]] (or [[↓]] / [[↑]]) open the next or previous article.
- [[S]] star, [[L]] read later, [[M]] read/unread, [[T]] tags, [[F]] full article, [[V]] open the original.
- [[R]] refreshes, [[A]] adds a feed, [[Shift+A]] marks all as read, [[/]] searches.
- [[Esc]] closes the search, or goes back to the list on a narrow screen.

## Tips
- Many sites block browsers from reading their feeds. Reader then uses the orOS relay; with its address empty, it uses the one set in [Mail](app:mail).

## Limits
- A site that blocks browsers can't be read without a relay.
- Only articles fetched on this device are available offline.
