# Forms
Forms makes surveys, sign-up sheets and order forms: questions of many kinds, pages, questions that show only for some answers, and jumps between pages. Share a form with a link, a QR code or on your website; people fill it in without an account, and the answers come to this device encrypted. Charts and export of the answers come in the next update.

## Make a form
- Press `New form`. Pick `Blank form`, or a template: contact form, event sign-up, feedback, order form, satisfaction survey or course sign-up. A template is only a start: change anything in it.
- Give the form a title and, if you like, a description. They show at the top of the form.
- Every change is saved as you type, and syncs to your other devices like the rest of orOS.

## Questions
- `Add question` opens the list of kinds. The new one goes after the question that is open, or at the end.
- Kinds: `Short answer`, `Paragraph`, `E-mail`, `Phone`, `Number`, `Date`, `Time`, `One choice`, `Checkboxes`, `Drop-down`, `Linear scale` (for example 1 to 5, or 0 to 10 with a label at each end), `Rating` (stars), `Yes / No` and `Grid` (rows and columns, one or more answers per row).
- `Text block` adds a title and text between questions. `Page break` starts a new page.
- Tap a question to open it: write the question and an optional help text, change its kind, tick `Required`, and set its limits (characters, smallest and largest number, earliest and latest date, how many checkboxes). For choices, write each answer; Enter adds the next one. `Add “Other” with a text box` lets people write their own answer.
- The buttons under an open question move it up or down, duplicate it or delete it. A deleted question can come back with `Undo` for a few seconds. `Done` closes it; so does Esc.

## Show only for some answers
- `Show only if…` shows a question, a text block or a page's questions only when an earlier answer matches: for example "Delivery address" only when "How do you want it?" is "Delivery".
- What you can ask for depends on the earlier question: is or is not one of its answers, includes (checkboxes), is more or less than (numbers, scales, stars, dates), is answered or is not answered.
- A question that is hidden never counts as answered, so questions that depend on it stay hidden too. A required question is only required while it shows.

## Pages and jumps
- With page breaks, people go through the form one page at a time, with `Back` and `Next`. Each page is checked before they can go on.
- On a page break, `After this page, go to…` sends everyone to another page, or straight to `Submit the form`.
- On `One choice`, `Drop-down` and `Yes / No`, `Go to a page by answer` sends people to a page by what they chose: for example "No" on "Will you come?" goes straight to the end. When a page has more than one such question, the last answered one decides.
- Jumps only go forward, so every way through a form has an end.

## Preview
- `Preview` shows the form exactly as people will see it, with pages, jumps, hidden questions and every check. Fill it in to try it: nothing you answer there is kept, and `Fill in again` starts over.

## Settings
- `Message after submitting` is what people read when they are done. Empty means "Thank you. Your answers have been recorded."
- `Note at the top` shows in a box under the description, for example to say what the answers are for.
- `Show a progress bar on forms with pages` shows how far along people are.
- `Page to link to after sending` puts a button on the thank-you message, for example back to your shop. It must be an https address. Nobody is sent there on their own.
- `One answer per browser`: the page remembers in that browser that the form was answered and doesn't take a second answer from it. It stops mistakes, not someone who wants to cheat (another browser can still answer).
- `Duplicate form` makes a copy to change. `Delete form` removes it on all your devices; `Undo` brings it back for a few seconds.

## Share
- `Share` › `Publish` gives the form a link (useoros.online/forms/f/…), a QR code and code to put the form on your website. People fill it in in any browser, without an account.
- The questions are encrypted with a key that is only in the link (after the #, which browsers never send), and every answer is encrypted in the person's browser for this form. The orOS relay keeps them in between and can read neither.
- After you change the form, `Publish changes` puts the new version online. Until then people see the published one.
- `Close the form` stops new answers; `Open the form` takes them again. `Close automatically on` closes it at a date and time. A closed form is removed from the relay 30 days after it closes, and one that no device has checked for 90 days too.
- Every page shows a warning: the form was made by someone who uses orOS, not by orOS, and nobody should enter passwords or card numbers. Anyone can report a form; when several people report it on the same day, it closes until you open it again.
- `Stop sharing` turns the link off for good and deletes answers still waiting at the relay. The answers already on your devices stay.

## Answers
- One device picks up the answers: the one that published the form, or the one where you press `Pick up on this device`. It checks when you open Forms and every few minutes while Forms is open; `Check for answers` checks now. With Forms closed, orOS checks every 10 minutes while it is open and shows a notice when answers wait; Today shows them too.
- `Tell me on this device even when orOS is closed` sends a notification that only says "New answer in a form", at most every 15 minutes. It needs `Notifications with orOS closed` on in Settings › Notifications.
- Answers wait at the relay for at most 30 days. Once picked up they are deleted there and kept here, and they sync to your other devices.
- `Answers` lists every answer, newest first, with the question labels. A dot on the tab means new ones you haven't looked at.
