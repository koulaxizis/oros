# Battleship
Find and sink the computer's fleet before it sinks yours. Both fleets hide on 10×10 seas, and you take turns firing one shot each.

## How to play
Each fleet has five ships: `Carrier` (5 squares), `Battleship` (4), `Cruiser` (3), `Submarine` (3) and `Destroyer` (2).

First place your fleet under `Place your ships`:
1. Pick a ship in the dock, then tap or click the square where it should start (its top or left end). Near the edge it moves back to fit.
2. `Rotate` turns it between horizontal and vertical. Tap a placed ship to pick it up again.
3. `Random` places the whole fleet for you, `Clear` empties the sea.
4. When all five ships are in, press `Start`.

Then fire at `Enemy waters`: you shoot first. Hits, misses and sunk ships are marked, and the computer answers each shot on `Your fleet`. Sink all five enemy ships to win. At the end, the enemy fleet is shown.

## Controls
- With a mouse, hovering shows where a ship would go (red when it can't).
- [[←]] [[↑]] [[→]] [[↓]] move the cursor, [[Enter]] or [[Space]] places a ship or fires.
- [[R]] rotates, [[Backspace]] picks up the ship under the cursor, and [[Enter]] starts the game once the fleet is ready.
- [[N]] starts a new game.

## Levels and scoring
- `Easy`: the computer fires at random.
- `Medium`: it searches, then closes in around a hit and follows the line.
- `Hard`: it works out where your remaining ships are most likely to be.

In `Options`, turn on `Ships may not touch` to keep ships apart, even at a corner, for both fleets. During a battle the change applies from the next game. `Records` shows wins, losses and your fewest shots in a win, per level.

## Two devices
Play a friend who has their own phone, tablet or computer. Press `Play on two devices` (the button with two screens): one of you chooses `Show a code`, the other `Enter a code` and types the 6-digit code or scans the QR code, and the first device lets the other in with `Allow`. While you're linked, `Two devices` shows in the toolbar; press it to see the link or to `Leave the game`.
- Each of you places your own fleet and presses `Start`; the battle begins when both fleets are ready, and who fires first alternates from game to game. The device that showed the code sets `Ships may not touch`.
- Your fleet never leaves your device while the game lasts: each shot is answered by the other device, which shows a ship only once it's sunk. At the end the two fleets are shown to each other and checked against every answer; if they don't match, the result says so.
- `New game` on the device that showed the code starts the next game; on the other device it asks for one when a game is over.
- If a device reloads or loses the network, the game waits and goes on where it was when it's back (for up to a day).
- Moves go straight between the two devices on the same network, or sealed through the orOS relay; nothing is kept online.

Limits: two players only, each on their own device with orOS open in this game. Games on two devices don't count in your records, and the level buttons are hidden meanwhile. `Leave the game` ends it on both devices and brings back the game you had on this device.

## Sync
Your records sync between your devices through [orOS sync](help:t/sync). `Reset records` deletes them on every device after you confirm. The game in progress, the level, `Options` and the sound setting stay on this device.

## Tips
- The game is saved as you play; reopen the app to carry on.
- Started a new game or changed level by mistake? Tap `Undo` on the message that appears.
- With `Ships may not touch` on, the squares around a sunk ship are empty: don't waste shots there.
