# Media Shelf
Media Shelf is your wishlist and tracker for books, films, series, music, podcasts and games. Keep what you want to try, follow your progress, rate what you finish and see your year in numbers.

## Getting started
Tap or click `Add` (or `Add your first title`). Choose the `Type`, then fill in the `Title` and, if you like, the creator (`Author`, `Director`, `Artist`…), `Year`, size (pages, episodes, hours…), `Tags (comma separated)`, `Where I heard of it`, a `Release date (optional)` and a `Cover colour`. Books also have a `Format`: `Print`, `E-book` or `Audiobook`; games a `Platform`. Pick the `Status` and press `Save`. Each title gets a generated cover with its initials.

## Tabs, search and sort
- `Now`: what you're reading, watching, listening to or playing.
- `Wishlist`: what you want to get to. Open a title and tap `High priority` to flag it; flagged titles come first when you sort by `Recent`.
- `History`: finished and dropped titles.
- `Statistics`: your year in numbers.

The chips filter by type. `Search title, creator, tag…` also looks in platform and source, and ignores accents. Sort by `Recent`, `Title`, `Creator`, `Rating` or `Year`.

## Progress
Tap a title to open it. Start it with a button such as `Start reading` or `Start playing`. Then log where you are: the page for books, minutes for audiobooks, episodes for series and podcasts, hours for games. Press `Update`, or use `+1 episode` or `+1 hour`.

You'll see a progress bar and percentage, and, once there's enough to go on, how long you'll take at your pace. When you reach the end, the app offers `Mark done`. You can also `Drop` a title, put it `Back to wishlist`, `Resume` it, or start it again (`Read again`, `Watch again`…). The history inside a title lists each start, progress and finish; you can remove an entry.

## Rating and review
Rate with five stars in half steps: tap the left or right half of a star. Mark a `Favourite` with the heart and write a `Review / notes`.

## Statistics and goals
Pick a year with the arrows. `Statistics` shows what you finished per type, `Pages read` (print and e-books), `Average rating`, a chart `By month`, `Top creators` and `Top tags`. With `Set goals` you set a target per type for the year, and the app tells you if you're ahead of or behind schedule.

## Find details online and the Calendar
For books and albums, `Find details online` in the form looks up the title you typed (and the creator, if any) on Open Library (books) or MusicBrainz (music). It runs only when you tap it, and the first time it tells you what is sent: just that text. Pick a match to fill in the title, creator, year, pages or tracks and, for albums, the release date; check it and press `Save`. Films, series, podcasts and games are filled in by hand.

In [Calendar](app:calendar), the `Media Shelf` chip shows the day you finished each title (rewatches and rereads too) and the release date of titles on your wishlist. Tap a row to open that title here.

## Import from Goodreads or Letterboxd
Bring your existing library in with `More` → `Import from Goodreads / Letterboxd (CSV)`.
- **Goodreads:** on the Goodreads website, open My Books → Import and export → Export library, and pick the CSV file it gives you.
- **Letterboxd:** on the Letterboxd website, open Settings → Data → Export your data. Unzip the file and pick its CSV files; you can pick several at once (`watchlist`, `watched`, `ratings`, `diary`, `reviews`).

Before anything is added, a preview shows how many titles were found per type and status, and how many are already on your shelf. Those are skipped, so importing the same file twice adds nothing. Press the import button to add the rest, or `Cancel`.

What comes in: Goodreads shelves become the status (`read`, `currently-reading`, `to-read`; a shelf such as `did-not-finish` becomes dropped), with your rating, review, pages, format, the date you finished, and your other shelves as tags. From Letterboxd you get the watchlist, every logged watch with its date (rewatches count), your rating, review and tags. Right after an import, `Undo` takes it all back.

## Sync and backup
Your titles, progress, ratings, reviews and goals sync between your devices through [orOS sync](help:t/sync). The open tab, type filter and sort stay on each device.

In the `More` menu, `Export (JSON)` saves your shelf to a file and `Import (JSON)` merges a file in, without replacing anything.

## Keyboard shortcuts
- [[/]] jumps to the search box; [[Esc]] there clears it.
- [[N]] adds a new title.
- On the stars: arrow keys change the rating by half a star, [[1]] to [[5]] set whole stars, [[0]] clears it.

## Tips
- Use tags such as `summer` or `book club` and find them later with search.
- Fill in `Where I heard of it` to remember who recommended a title; search finds it too.

## Limits
- There are no reminders.
- Covers are generated, never downloaded, and the online lookup has no source for films, series, podcasts or games.
