# Water
Water helps you drink enough during the day. Log each glass or bottle with one tap, watch the glass fill up towards your daily goal, and see how you did over the past days and weeks.

## Getting started
Water opens on today. The big glass shows how much you've drunk, your goal, the percentage and how much is left. Your goal starts at 2,000 ml; change it in `Settings` (the gear button).

Each time you drink, tap or click one of the buttons under the glass:
- `+1 glass` adds one glass (250 ml unless you change it).
- `+ Bottle` adds one bottle (500 ml unless you change it).
- `Other amount` lets you type any amount, or pick a ready one such as 330 ml or 1 litre.

Right after you add a drink, an `Undo` button appears for a few seconds.

## Entries and past days
Today's drinks are listed under `Entries` with their time. Tap or click an entry to change its amount or time, or to `Delete` it.

Use ‹ and › at the top to move to another day, and the date button to come back to today. If you forgot to log something yesterday, go to that day and add it there; you can then fix its time.

## History
Next to the day (or under it on a phone) you see:
- `Overview`: your current `Day streak`, your `Best streak`, the `Average (7 days)` and the share of `Days on goal`.
- `This week`: a bar for every day, Monday to Sunday.
- A month heatmap: the darker the day, the closer you got to your goal. Tap or click a day to open it.

## Settings
- `Daily goal (ml)`. A new goal counts from today; past days keep theirs. `Suggest` gives a starting goal from your weight (about 33 ml per kg). Your weight isn't stored.
- `Glass (ml)` and `Bottle (ml)`: the sizes of the two quick buttons.
- `Show amounts in` `ml`, `Glasses` or `fl oz`.
- `Show in Habits`: adds a read-only "Water" row to [Habits](app:habits), ticked on the days you reach your goal.

## Reminders
Reminders are off at first. Turn them on with the `Reminders` button at the top, or in `Settings`, where you also choose the hours (`From`, `Until`) and `At most every`. You only get a reminder when you're behind: for example, halfway through your hours with less than half your goal. Opening it takes you to Water. See [Notifications](help:t/notifications).

## Health Connect
In the orOS Android app, switch on `Health Connect` in Settings › `Connected services`. Your drinks of the last 30 days then go to Health Connect, and drinks logged in other health apps come into Water. A drink that came from another app and that you delete here doesn't come back.

## Save and open an orOS file
In `Settings`, under `Data`, `orOS file (.oroswater)` saves every drink and every daily goal as one `.oroswater` file, for example to move them to another device without sync or to keep a copy. Your settings (glass and bottle sizes, unit, reminders) stay on each device. The file is a plain ZIP: unzipped it holds the drinks as `content.json` plus `preview/drinks.csv` (date, time, ml) for other programs. It is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere safe.

`Open orOS file…` (or `Unzipped orOS folder…`, or "Open with" orOS on the computer) shows how many drinks and goals are new and how many are already here, then `Add` adds only the new ones, with their own ids: opening the same file again adds nothing. A drink already here is kept as it is, one you deleted here stays deleted, and a day that already has its own goal here keeps it; a file never deletes anything. Tick `Update with newer versions` to let records that were changed later in the file replace your copy. `Undo` in the message takes back what was added.

## Sync and backup
Your drinks, goals and settings sync between your devices through [orOS sync](help:t/sync). Which month the heatmap shows stays on this device. `Export CSV` (in `Settings`) saves every drink with its date, time and amount, and inside orOS `Open in Spreadsheet` opens the same drinks as a new sheet in Spreadsheet, with the total in ml; an orOS file (see above) keeps drinks and goals together. For a full copy, see [backup](help:t/backup).

## Keyboard shortcuts
- [[+]] adds one glass.
- [[←]] / [[→]] go to the previous or next day.
- [[Ctrl+Z]] undoes the drink you just added.

## Tips
- Drinks other than water count too; log whatever you'd like to track.
- The goal is a rough guide, not medical advice.

## Limits
- Reminders can't fire when the browser or the orOS tab is closed.
- Water can't import CSV files.
