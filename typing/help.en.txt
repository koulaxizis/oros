# Typing Tutor
Learn to type with all ten fingers without looking at the keys. Short lessons bring in two keys at a time, starting from the home row, for Greek, English (US) and German keyboards. A few minutes a day are enough.

## Getting started
1. Choose your keyboard at the top: `Ελληνικά`, `English (US)` or `Deutsch`. Switch your computer's keyboard to the same language (Windows: Win+Space, Linux: Super+Space, Android with a keyboard: Ctrl+Space).
2. Press `Start lesson 1`, or any lesson in the list.
3. Rest your fingers on the home row: the left index finger on F, the right one on J. Both keys have a small bump you can feel.
4. Type the text you see. The letter to type next is underlined, and the key on the screen keyboard and the finger to use light up.
5. At the end you see your speed, your accuracy and the stars you earned.

## The screen keyboard
- Each colour belongs to one finger. Every key of the same colour is typed with that finger, and both thumbs share the space bar.
- Under the keyboard, the two hands show which finger to use next.
- For a capital letter, the Shift key on the other hand lights up too.
- For a Greek letter with a tonos (ά, έ, ή…), first press the key with ΄ (right of Λ), then the letter. Shift and the same key give the dialytika (ϊ, ϋ).
- `Key hints` in the settings: `When I hesitate` lights the key only when you wait or make a mistake, `Always` lights it every time, `Never` hides the keyboard so you type from memory.

## Lessons and stars
- The lessons go row by row: the home row, the top row, the bottom row, then capitals, accents, numbers and signs. Each group ends with a review.
- Stars: one star for finishing with 90% accuracy or more, two for 95% and 15 words per minute, three for 98% and 25 words per minute.
- `Continue` always opens the first lesson without a star. You can open any lesson at any time.
- `Mistakes` in the settings: `Stop until it is right` waits for the right key (best for learning), `Go on, Backspace fixes` moves on and lets you go back.

## Speed test
- `Speed test` gives you 1, 2 or 5 minutes of common words.
- Speed is in words per minute: five characters count as one word. Accuracy is the share of right key presses.
- Your best result for each length is kept in `Statistics`.

## Statistics
- `Statistics` (the chart button at the top) shows today's practice, the days in a row, your total time and your speed of the last 7 days.
- The bars show your minutes on each of the last 14 days.
- `Your keys` colours each key by how often it goes wrong: green is good, red needs practice. The list under it names the keys to work on.

## Settings
- `Daily goal` sets the minutes for the ring on the first screen.
- `Text size` makes the lesson text smaller or larger.
- `Key sounds` and `Use the screen keyboard` apply to this device only.
- `Reset progress` clears every lesson, statistic and record on all your devices.

## On a phone
Touch typing needs a real keyboard. With a keyboard connected (Bluetooth or USB), the phone's own keyboard stays hidden and the lesson works as on a computer. Without one, `Use the screen keyboard` lets you practise the letters, but not the ten-finger method.

## Your data
- Your progress, statistics and settings are saved as you go. With orOS sync switched on in [Settings](app:settings), they follow you to your other devices, encrypted. Practice on two devices on the same day adds up.
- What you type in a lesson is not kept, only the counts (speed, accuracy, which keys went wrong).
