# Bookmarks

Save web links in folders, tag them, keep a readable copy and find them again quickly.

## Getting started

Paste a link into the bar at the top (`Paste a link — or link | title`) and press [[Enter]] or tap the + button. You don't need to type `https://`, it's added for you. To give the bookmark a title at the same time, add a `|` and the title, for example `example.com | My site`.

The link goes into the folder that's open. In `All` it goes to `Unsorted`. If you've already saved that address, the bar flashes and a note tells you which folder it's in.

Tap or click a row to open the link in a new tab.

## Folders

- Tap the + next to the folder tabs to make a `New folder`. The tabs are your top folders.
- Folders can sit inside folders, as deep as you like. When a folder is open, the strip under the search box shows its path (tap a part to go back up), its subfolders as chips, and `New subfolder`.
- `All` shows every bookmark. `Unsorted` is the default folder and can't be deleted or put inside another folder.
- To rename, move or delete the open folder, use the ⋮ button (`Folder options`) or double-click its tab. `Inside` chooses where the folder lives: `Top level (a tab)` or any other folder. A folder can't go inside itself or one of its own subfolders.
- Right-click a tab or subfolder chip (long-press on a phone) for `Rename`, `Move left`, `Move right`, `Move into…` and `New subfolder`. On a computer, drag one tab onto another to swap them, drag a tab or chip onto a subfolder chip or a part of the path to put it inside, and drag a chip onto a tab to put it there (onto `All` to make it a tab).
- `Delete folder` asks for a second tap. What was inside moves up one level: subfolders go to the parent, bookmarks go to the parent folder (to `Unsorted` from a top folder). You get `Undo`.

## Edit, move and favourites

Each row has a star (favourites), a pencil (`Edit`) and an open button. In the edit window you can change `Title`, `Address`, `Description`, `Tags` and `Folder`, or `Delete` the bookmark. Under the form you see the last link check and buttons for `Check link` and the saved copy. `Follow in Reader` opens [Reader](app:feeds) to follow that site's news. `QR code` opens [QR Generator](app:qr) with a code for the saved address.

Right-click a row (long-press on a phone) for a menu with `Open`, `Open in Maps`, `Edit`, `Add to favorites`, `Send to someone…` (the bookmark as a link that opens in any browser), `Save a readable copy` or `Read saved copy`, `Check link`, `Move to…` (a list of all your folders) and `Delete`. On a computer you can also drag a row onto a folder tab, a subfolder chip or a part of the path. Dropping it on `All` moves it to `Unsorted`.

Starred bookmarks show up as tiles under `Favorites` at the top of the list. `Open in Maps` searches for the bookmark's title and description in [Maps](app:maps).

## Readable copy

`Save a readable copy` keeps the article of a page for reading later, even offline, the way [Reader](app:feeds) shows a full article: the page's main text, without menus, ads, scripts or forms. A book icon on the row opens it.

- The copy is a file on the orOS disk, in [Files](app:files) › `Bookmarks` › `Archive`. It isn't part of the bookmarks' sync data: it reaches your other devices with Vault Drive, like any other file.
- `Keep pictures` puts the pictures inside the copy, so they show offline too. Pictures a site doesn't let the browser read stay links that only show online. Without it, the copy is text only.
- In the reader: `Original page`, `Save again` (for a page that changed) and `Remove copy`. Deleting a bookmark removes its copy too, after the moment for `Undo` has passed.
- Pages that need the relay: many sites can only be read through the relay set in [Reader](app:feeds) or [Mail](app:mail). Without one, those pages can't be saved.

## Search, tags and sorting

- The search box looks in every folder: titles, addresses, descriptions, tags and folder names.
- Add tags in the edit window: type one and press [[Enter]] or a comma. [[Backspace]] in an empty field removes the last tag.
- Tap a tag on a row to show only bookmarks with that tag. Tap more tags to narrow it down. The tag button (`All tags`) lists every tag with a count, and `Clear filters` resets the filter.
- The sort menu offers `Recent`, `Title A–Z` and `Most visited`.

## Tidy up

- The `Tidy up` button (two squares) has `Check links in this folder` (with its subfolders), `Check all links`, `Show broken and moved links` and `Duplicate bookmarks`.
- The link check runs only when you ask, ten links at a time with a short pause between, at most 500 per run; `Stop` ends it. A page that's gone (404, 410) or a site that doesn't answer is marked `Broken`; a page that now sends you somewhere else is marked `Moved`, and `Use new address` (row menu or edit window) updates the bookmark. A site that refuses robots or is down for a while is `Couldn't check` and is not counted as broken.
- `Duplicate bookmarks` finds addresses saved more than once. http and https, `www.`, a trailing slash, the order of the query and tracking parts such as `utm_source` or `fbclid` count as the same address. `Merge into oldest, remove …` keeps the oldest copy and gives it every tag, every description and the star; or use `Apply selected` / `Apply all`.
- `Select bookmarks` (the checkbox button) lets you tick several rows and use `Move to…`, `Send…` (all of them in one link) or `Delete` on all of them. `Done` ends selection. It's off while you search or filter.
- Deleting, moving, merging and importing show a message with `Undo` for a few seconds.

## Sync and backup

Bookmarks, folders and their nesting, tags, favourites, link checks and which bookmarks have a saved copy sync through [orOS sync](help:t/sync). A link check never overwrites an edit made on another device. The copies themselves are files (see `Readable copy`). Your sort choice and the `Keep pictures` choice stay on this device.

- **Import**: `Import bookmarks` reads the bookmarks HTML file of a browser, Raindrop, Pinboard or Linkding, Pocket's export (the HTML file or the CSV) and Raindrop's CSV. Folders keep their nesting and are matched by name or created; tags, descriptions, favourites and the date each link was saved come along. Pocket links go to a `Pocket` folder, archived ones to `Pocket` › `Archive`. Addresses you already have are skipped. `Undo` in the message takes the whole import back.
- **From Raindrop.io**: connect Raindrop.io in [Settings](app:settings) › `Connected services` with a test token, then the cloud button `Import from Raindrop.io…` › `Read the account` shows what will come in, and `Import` brings it (`Undo` in the message takes it back). Each collection becomes a folder (a folder with the same name is used, nested collections become folders of their own), with titles, notes (or the page excerpt), tags and favourites; links you already have are skipped. Import again whenever you like: only new bookmarks come in, and with `Update what was imported before` the title and note follow Raindrop's and new tags are added. Deleted bookmarks don't come back unless you tick `Bring back what was deleted here`. orOS only reads Raindrop: nothing there changes.
- **Export**: `Export bookmarks (Netscape HTML)` saves `oros-bookmarks.html` with the whole folder tree, tags, descriptions and dates, for a browser, Raindrop, Pinboard, Linkding or orOS.

For a copy of all your orOS data, see [Backup](help:t/backup).

## Tips

- The coloured letter on each row is made on your device. orOS doesn't download site icons, so opening the list contacts no websites. Only `Check link`, the link checks and `Save a readable copy` contact the sites, when you ask.

## Limits

- Only http and https links can be saved.
- Favourites, visits, link checks and saved copies aren't in the export file.
- Saved copies need orOS (they're on the orOS disk): Bookmarks opened on its own outside orOS can't make or show them.
- A readable copy keeps the article text, not the whole page: pages built by scripts, behind a login or a paywall may have no text to keep.
- The Raindrop CSV gives nested collections as `Parent / Child`; a collection whose own name has a `/` becomes two folders.
- A link check sees what a robot sees: some sites answer "forbidden" to it even though the page works in a browser (shown as `Couldn't check`).
