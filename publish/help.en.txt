# Publishing
Publishing is a desk for a small publisher: every book you publish with its formats, ISBNs, prices, sizes, back-cover text and cover, your ISBN blocks with the next free number, and barcodes for the back cover.

## Getting started
1. Press `New title`, write the title and, if you like, the author.
2. Under `Formats` add each edition (paperback, e-book, audiobook…). Every format has its own ISBN and price.
3. Fill in the back-cover text, the people who worked on the book and the release date. The line at the top says what is `Still missing` before the release.

## Titles
The `Titles` tab lists your books with their cover, people, ISBNs and status (`Idea`, `In production`, `Released`, `Out of print`). Filter by status, search by title, person or ISBN, and sort by release date, title or last edit.

Tap a title to edit it. Every change is saved as you type. `Delete` removes a title; `Undo` brings it back right after.

### People
A title can have authors, translators, illustrators, editors, a cover designer, a narrator or others. Use `+ Person` and pick the role.

### Formats
Each format keeps its ISBN, price, pages, size (width × height in mm), spine, weight, binding and paper; an audiobook keeps its length in minutes. The price is in the currency of the settings (⚙), where you also type the `Publisher name` for covers and press texts.

The ISBN field checks the number as you type: a wrong check digit, a missing digit, or a number another title already uses. `Next free` fills in the next unused number of your blocks.

### Barcode
`Barcode` on a format with an ISBN shows the EAN-13 for the back cover, with "ISBN …" above the bars. `Download SVG` gives a vector at the standard size (about 37 × 29 mm), `Download PNG` a sharp picture. Leave a white margin around it when you place it.

### Cover
`Add a cover picture` keeps a copy of the front cover (up to 1600 px, without location or camera data) in [Files](app:files), folder Publishing/Covers. It reaches your other devices with Vault Drive. The print file of the cover itself is made in [Layout](app:layout).

### Send to other apps
Under `Send to other apps` pick a format (when the title has more than one), then:
- `Draft in my shop` creates a draft product in your WooCommerce shop, connected with your own keys in [Settings](app:settings) › Connected services: name, SKU (the ISBN), price, back-cover text, weight and size in the shop's own units. If the shop already has a product with that ISBN, orOS links it and creates nothing. You add pictures and categories and publish it in the shop; `Open in the shop` takes you there.
- `Item in Stock` opens a new product in [Stock](app:stock) with the name, the ISBN as SKU and barcode, and the price. If Stock already has it, its sheet opens instead.
- `Block in Newsletter` adds the title, subtitle, author and back-cover text to a [Newsletter](app:newsletter) draft you pick, with a button to the book when it is in your shop.
- `Press release in Writer` opens a new [Writer](app:writer) document with a press release built from the title, the texts, the formats and your publisher name. Read it and edit it before you send it.

Nothing is saved in the other app until you save it there.

## ISBN
Your national ISBN agency gives a publisher a block of numbers, for example 978-618-5321 for 100 books. In the `ISBN` tab, `Add a block` and write the prefix with its hyphens exactly as the agency gave it.

For every block you see how many numbers are in use, the next free one (`Copy` or `New title with it`) and the list of numbers in use with their titles.

- `Used outside this app`: numbers you gave to books before you used orOS, or spoiled ones. orOS will not offer them again.
- `Numbers used twice` appears at the top when two formats have the same ISBN.
- `Check an ISBN` tells you if any number is valid, shows its ISBN-10 and whether one of your titles has it.

## Your data
- Everything stays on this device and is saved as you go.
- With orOS sync switched on in [Settings](app:settings), your titles and blocks follow you to your other devices, encrypted. Cover pictures travel with Vault Drive.

## Find it from anywhere
- The menu's search box finds a title by its words, its people or any of its ISBNs (with or without hyphens, ISBN-10 too). Choosing one opens the title.
