# Spreadsheet
Spreadsheet is a simple grid for lists, budgets and sums, with formulas, several sheets per workbook and CSV, Excel and LibreOffice files.

## Getting started
Each sheet has 100 rows and columns A to Z.

- Tap or click a cell to select it and start typing. [[Enter]] saves and moves down, [[Tab]] moves right, [[Esc]] cancels.
- To change a cell, double-click it or press [[Enter]] or [[F2]]. On a touch screen, tap a cell to select it and tap it again to edit.
- The bar at the top shows the cell name (for example A1) and its content after ƒx. You can also edit there and press [[Enter]].

## Formulas
Start a cell with = to make a formula, for example =A1*2 or =SUM(B2:B10).

- Operators: + - * / ^, & to join text, and = <> < > <= >= to compare (the result is 1 or 0).
- References: A1, ranges like A1:C5, and cells on another sheet like Sheet2!A1 (or 'My sheet'!A1 when the name has spaces).
- Functions: SUM, AVERAGE, MIN, MAX, COUNT, COUNTA, ROUND, ABS, IF, AND, OR, NOT, CONCAT. Separate arguments with a comma or a semicolon.
- Errors show in the cell, for example #DIV/0! (division by zero), #REF! (a reference that doesn't exist) or #NAME? (an unknown function).
- When you copy and paste a formula, its references move with it, as in other spreadsheets.

## Format and edit
- Select a range by dragging with the mouse or with [[Shift]] and the arrow keys.
- The toolbar has Undo, Redo, Copy, Cut, Paste, bold, italic, underline, alignment, `Number format` (`auto`, 123, 123.00, %, €), `Text color` and `Clear formatting`.
- [[Delete]] or [[Backspace]] empties the selected cells.
- Drag the right edge of a column header to make the column wider or narrower.

## Sheets
The tabs at the bottom are your sheets.

- `New sheet` (the + button) adds one.
- Double-click a tab, or long-press it on a touch screen, to rename it.
- Tap ✕ on a tab, then ✕ again within 3 seconds, to delete the sheet. The last sheet can't be deleted.

## Sync and backup
With [orOS sync](help:t/sync), your sheets, cells, formatting and column widths reach your other devices. Text and formatting in the same cell are merged separately, so a value typed on one device and bold set on another both survive.

These stay on this device: which sheet is open, the copy clipboard and the Undo history.

Import and export (buttons at the bottom right):
- `Import CSV` adds the file as a new sheet. Commas, semicolons and tabs are detected by themselves.
- `Export CSV` saves the open sheet, with each cell as you typed it (formulas stay formulas).
- `Import Excel / Calc (.xlsx, .ods, .xls)` adds every sheet of the file as a new sheet.
- `Export Excel / Calc` saves all sheets as `Excel (.xlsx)` or `LibreOffice Calc (.ods)`.

See also [Backup](help:t/backup).

## Keyboard shortcuts
- Arrow keys move; [[Shift]] + arrows select a range.
- [[Enter]] or [[F2]] edits the cell; [[Tab]] moves right; [[Esc]] cancels editing.
- [[Ctrl+Z]] undo, [[Ctrl+Y]] or [[Ctrl+Shift+Z]] redo.
- [[Ctrl+C]], [[Ctrl+X]], [[Ctrl+V]] copy, cut and paste.
- [[Ctrl+B]], [[Ctrl+I]], [[Ctrl+U]] bold, italic, underline.
- On a Mac use Cmd instead of Ctrl.

## Tips
- Start typing on a selected cell to replace its content straight away.
- Use another sheet for raw data and refer to it with Sheet2!A1.

## Limits
- Copy and paste work inside Spreadsheet only, not with other apps.
- On touch screens you can select only one cell at a time, and column widths change only with a mouse.
- Imports are capped at 500 rows × 64 columns per sheet.
- Undo history is cleared when sync brings in changes, and deleting a sheet has no Undo.
- Excel and Calc files need a helper library; if it hasn't loaded yet, you may need an internet connection.
