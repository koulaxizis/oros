# Spreadsheet
Spreadsheet is a simple grid for lists, budgets and sums, with formulas, several sheets per workbook and CSV, Excel and LibreOffice files.

## Getting started
A new sheet has 100 rows and columns A to Z. A sheet can grow to 10,000 rows and 104 columns (A to CZ): see Rows, columns and frozen panes.

- Tap or click a cell to select it and start typing. [[Enter]] saves and moves down, [[Tab]] moves right, [[Esc]] cancels.
- To change a cell, double-click it or press [[Enter]] or [[F2]]. On a touch screen, tap a cell to select it and tap it again to edit.
- The bar at the top shows the cell name (for example A1) and its content after ƒx. You can also edit there and press [[Enter]].

## Formulas
Start a cell with = to make a formula, for example =A1*2 or =SUM(B2:B10).

- Operators: + - * / ^, & to join text, % for percent, a minus sign in front (=-A1), and = <> < > <= >= to compare (the result is TRUE or FALSE).
- References: A1, ranges like A1:C5, whole columns like A:A, fixed references like $A$1, and cells on another sheet like Sheet2!A1 (or 'My sheet'!A1 when the name has spaces).
- Separate arguments with a comma or a semicolon. Text goes in double quotes; write "" for a quote inside text.
- Errors show in the cell, for example #DIV/0! (division by zero), #REF! (a reference that doesn't exist), #N/A (nothing found) or #NAME? (an unknown function).
- When you copy and paste a formula, its references move with it, as in other spreadsheets ($ parts stay).

## Functions
Over 200 functions work as in Excel and Google Sheets. Tap ƒx next to the formula bar for the full list with a line on each; while you type =SU… a short list appears: use the arrow keys and [[Tab]], or tap a name.

- Totals and conditions: SUM, AVERAGE, COUNT, COUNTA, COUNTBLANK, SUMIF, SUMIFS, COUNTIF, COUNTIFS, AVERAGEIF, AVERAGEIFS, MAXIFS, MINIFS, SUMPRODUCT.
- Conditions are written like ">5", "<>paid", "a*" (* any text, ? one character) or a cell.
- Lookups: VLOOKUP, HLOOKUP, XLOOKUP, XMATCH, LOOKUP, INDEX, MATCH, CHOOSE, OFFSET, INDIRECT, ADDRESS.
- Fixed lists go in braces: {1,2,3} is a row, {1;2;3} a column, {1,2;3,4} two rows. For example =SUM({1,2,3}) or =XLOOKUP(A1;{"a","b"};{10,20}).
- Logic and errors: IF, IFS, SWITCH, AND, OR, NOT, IFERROR, IFNA, ISBLANK, ISNUMBER, ISTEXT, ISERROR.
- Text: LEN, LEFT, RIGHT, MID, UPPER, LOWER, PROPER, TRIM, CLEAN, SUBSTITUTE, FIND, SEARCH, TEXTBEFORE, TEXTAFTER, TEXT, FIXED, VALUE, NUMBERVALUE, TEXTJOIN, CONCAT, REPT, UNICODE, UNICHAR.
- Maths: ROUND, ROUNDUP, ROUNDDOWN, MROUND, INT, MOD, QUOTIENT, POWER, SQRT, PRODUCT, GCD, LCM, FACT, COMBIN, PERMUT, EVEN, ODD, CEILING.MATH, FLOOR.MATH, SUBTOTAL, SIN, COS, TAN and the other angle functions, RADIANS, DEGREES, PI, RAND, RANDBETWEEN.
- Statistics: MEDIAN, MODE, STDEV, VAR, LARGE, SMALL, RANK, AVERAGEA, GEOMEAN, HARMEAN, CORREL, SLOPE, INTERCEPT, RSQ, FORECAST, NORM.DIST, NORM.INV, STANDARDIZE.
- Money: PMT, IPMT, PPMT, CUMIPMT, CUMPRINC, FV, PV, NPER, RATE, NPV, XNPV, IRR, XIRR, MIRR, EFFECT, NOMINAL.
- Depreciation: SLN, SYD, DB, DDB.
- SUBTOTAL counts every row, including rows a filter hides.
- TEXT(value; "0.00") writes a number as text in a pattern: 0, 0.00, #,##0, 0%, dd/mm/yyyy and so on.

## Dates
Dates are day numbers, as in Excel: 1 is 31 December 1899, so subtracting two dates gives the days between them.

- TODAY, NOW, DATE, YEAR, MONTH, DAY, WEEKDAY, DAYS, DAYS360, DATEDIF, YEARFRAC, EDATE, EOMONTH, WEEKNUM, ISOWEEKNUM, TIMEVALUE, NETWORKDAYS, WORKDAY, NETWORKDAYS.INTL and WORKDAY.INTL work with them.
- NETWORKDAYS.INTL and WORKDAY.INTL take your own weekend: 1 is Saturday and Sunday, 7 is Friday and Saturday, 11 to 17 a single day (11 Sunday), or seven 0/1 digits from Monday to Sunday, such as "0000011".
- A cell with =TODAY(), =DATE(…) and similar shows a date by itself. For other cells pick `date` in `Number format`: 2026-10-14 in English, 14/10/2026 in Greek.
- A date you type, like 2026-10-14 or 14/10/2026, stays as you typed it (text). Date functions still read it (day first: 14/10/2026 is 14 October), and DATEVALUE turns it into a day number.

## Format and edit
- Select a range by dragging with the mouse or with [[Shift]] and the arrow keys. On a touch screen, touch and hold a cell for a moment, then drag; or choose `Select a range…` in the sheet menu and tap the last cell.
- The toolbar has Undo, Redo, Copy, Cut, Paste, bold, italic, underline, alignment, `Number format` (`auto`, 123, 123.00, %, €, `date`), `Text color`, `Clear formatting` and the cell format button (see Fill, borders and merged cells).
- Tap a column letter or a row number to select the whole column or row.
- [[Delete]] or [[Backspace]] empties the selected cells.
- Drag the right edge of a column header to make the column wider or narrower, and the bottom edge of a row number to make the row taller. This works with a finger too.

## Fill, borders and merged cells
The cell format button next to `Clear formatting` has:

- `Fill colour` (a background for the cells) and `No fill`.
- `Outline border`, `All borders`, `Bottom border` and `No borders`.
- `Wrap text`: long text breaks into lines and the row grows to fit.
- `Align top`, `Align middle` and `Align bottom`.
- `Merge cells`, `Merge horizontally` (each row on its own), `Merge vertically` (each column on its own) and `Unmerge`. Only the top-left value is kept. A merge can span at most 500 rows.
- `Conditional formatting…` (see below).

## Fill handle
The small square at the bottom right of the selection is the fill handle. Drag it down, up, left or right, with the mouse or a finger:

- Numbers in a pattern (1, 2 or 10, 20) continue; one number is copied (hold [[Ctrl]] while dragging to count up instead).
- Dates continue by day, or by the step between them. Weekdays and months continue in English and Greek (Monday, Tuesday… or Ιαν, Φεβ…), and so does text ending in a number, like Item 1, Item 2.
- Formulas are copied with their references moved, as with copy and paste.
- Anything else is repeated. [[Ctrl+Z]] undoes the fill.

## Conditional formatting
`Conditional formatting…` in the cell format menu lists the rules of this sheet. `Add rule` makes a new one for the selected cells (or type a range like B2:B20):

- `Greater than`, `Less than`, `Between`, `Equal to`, `Text contains`, `Top` / `Bottom` N values and `Duplicate values` colour the matching cells with a fill, a text colour or bold.
- `Colour scale` shades every number from the lowest to the highest with two or three colours.
- `Data bars` draws a bar in each cell, as long as its value.
- `Icon set` puts an arrow or a traffic light in each cell: green for the top third between the lowest and the highest number, amber for the middle, red for the bottom.
- `Formula is true` colours a cell when a formula gives TRUE. Write the formula for the first cell of the range, for example =$B2>100 to colour whole rows where column B is over 100; it moves with each cell like a copied formula.
- When rules overlap, the one higher in the list wins. The arrow moves a rule up.
- Rules follow the values as they change, and reach your other devices with sync.

## Charts
Select the data with its headings, for example A1:C5, and press the chart button.

- Pick `Column`, `Bar`, `Line`, `Area`, `Pie`, `Donut` or `Scatter`, and give it a title, axis titles and colours. A first row or column of text gives the names of the series and of the categories; `Series in` chooses columns or rows.
- The chart floats over the sheet. Drag it to move it; drag its corner to resize it. Tap it to show its buttons: edit (or double-click it), `PNG`, `SVG` and delete. [[Delete]] deletes the selected chart and [[Ctrl+Z]] brings it back.
- A chart follows its cells: change a number and the chart changes with it. Rows hidden by a filter are left out. It uses the colours of your theme, light or dark.
- `Stacked` (column, bar, area) piles the series on top of each other. `Last series as a line` (column) draws the last series as a line over the columns, for example a total or a target. `Trend line` (column, line, area, scatter) adds a dashed straight line that fits the numbers best.
- Charts reach your other devices with sync.

## Circular references
A formula that refers back to itself, directly or through other cells, shows #CYC!. For models that need it (for example interest that depends on a balance that depends on the interest), turn on `Calculation…` › `Iterative calculation` in the sheet menu: orOS then repeats the calculation until no value changes by more than `Maximum change`, at most `Maximum iterations` times (100 and 0.001 unless you change them). The setting belongs to the workbook and reaches your other devices with sync.

## Copy and paste with other apps
- [[Ctrl+C]] or the Copy button puts the selected cells on the clipboard as text with tabs and as a table, so they paste into Excel, Google Sheets, LibreOffice, Word or an email.
- [[Ctrl+V]] pastes cells copied in Excel, Google Sheets or LibreOffice, with bold, italic, colours, fills and merged cells where the other app sends them. Plain text with tabs or lines also pastes into cells.
- Something copied here and pasted here keeps its formulas (their references move) and all its formats.
- The Paste button asks the browser to read the clipboard. If the browser refuses, press [[Ctrl+V]] instead.

## Sort and filter
- `Sort A to Z` and `Sort Z to A` sort the selected cells by the column of the cursor. With one cell selected they sort the table around it; a first row of headings (text above numbers, or bold) stays on top. Blank cells go last. [[Ctrl+Z]] undoes it.
- `Filter` puts a button on each heading of the table. Tap it to keep only some values, or rows whose text contains a word. The status bar shows how many rows are hidden; tap ✕ there or `Filter` again to show them all.
- Filters are a view of this device only: they don't reach your other devices, and nothing is deleted.

## Rows, columns and frozen panes
The `Rows, columns, freeze` button (or a right-click on the grid) has:

- `Freeze top row`, `Freeze rows 1–N` (up to the cursor), `Freeze column A` / `Freeze columns A–X`, and `Unfreeze`. Frozen rows and columns stay in view while you scroll, also on a phone. They reach your other devices with sync.
- `Insert row above` / `below`, `Insert column left` / `right`, and `Delete row` / `Delete column` for the selected rows or columns. Formulas that point at moved cells follow them; a formula that pointed at a deleted cell shows #REF!. [[Ctrl+Z]] undoes it.
- `Add 100 rows at the end`, `Add 1,000 rows at the end` and `Add 10 columns`.
- Charts and conditional formatting rules move with their cells when you insert or delete rows and columns, and their ranges grow or shrink with them.

## Sheets
The tabs at the bottom are your sheets.

- `New sheet` (the + button) adds one.
- Double-click a tab, or long-press it on a touch screen, to rename it.
- Tap ✕ on a tab, then ✕ again within 3 seconds, to delete the sheet. The last sheet can't be deleted.

## The Assistant
Ask the Assistant to change your sheets in words, such as "2026 instead of 2025", "make the first row bold", "sort by price", "insert two rows above row 5" or "freeze the first row". It shows the changes on a card first; `Apply` makes them here and opens the sheet. `Undo` on the message here, or on the Assistant's card, puts the cells back as long as they weren't changed since. See the Assistant's help for everything it can do.

## Sync and backup
With [orOS sync](help:t/sync), your sheets, cells, formatting (with fills, borders and merged cells), column widths, row heights, frozen panes, charts and conditional formatting rules reach your other devices. Text and formatting in the same cell are merged separately, so a value typed on one device and bold set on another both survive. Each chart and each rule syncs on its own: a chart added on one device and a rule added on another both stay.

These stay on this device: which sheet is open, filters, the copy clipboard and the Undo history.

Import and export (buttons at the bottom right):
- `Import CSV` adds the file as a new sheet. Commas, semicolons and tabs are detected by themselves.
- `Export CSV` saves the open sheet, with each cell as you typed it (formulas stay formulas).
- `Import Excel / Calc / orOS`, then `Open a file (.xlsx, .ods, .xls, .orossheet)…`, adds every sheet of the file as a new sheet. Nothing already here is replaced.
- `Import Excel / Calc / orOS`, then `From Google Sheets…`, signs in to Google, lets you pick one spreadsheet in Google Drive (a Google Sheet, or an XLSX, XLS or ODS file there) and adds its sheets the same way. orOS sees only that file, and the sign-in ends right after.
- `Export Excel / Calc / orOS` saves all sheets as `Excel (.xlsx)`, `LibreOffice Calc (.ods)` or `orOS file (.orossheet)`. Excel and Calc get column widths, row heights and merged cells.
- The .orossheet file keeps everything: formulas, formatting, charts, conditional formatting and every sheet. It is an open ZIP file: unzip it and you find `content.json` (the workbook) and in `preview` one CSV per sheet with the values shown, which any spreadsheet program opens. `Unzipped orOS folder…` brings back a .orossheet that was unzipped.

## Import from Notion
The `Notion` button (cloud) next to them brings in the Notion databases you shared with your Notion integration, one sheet per database. Connect Notion first in [Settings](app:settings) › `Connected services`, then in Notion open each database, ••• › `Connections`, and add your integration.
- `Read the account` lists what would come: new sheets, updated, unchanged. Then `Import`.
- Row 1 holds the property names in bold (the title first), then one row per entry: text, numbers, dates (`2026-10-13 09:30`, a range with →), checkboxes as `TRUE`/`FALSE`, tags joined with commas, a relation as the number of linked entries.
- Every value is plain text: one that starts with = + - or @ gets an apostrophe, so it never runs as a formula.
- Import again to bring in new databases. A sheet that came before is left as it is, unless you tick `Replace the values of sheets imported before`: then its values become the database's (your edits in it are replaced; its name and formats stay).
- A sheet you deleted here doesn't come back, unless you tick `Bring back what was deleted here`.
- `Undo` in the notice takes the import back.
- orOS only reads Notion: nothing changes there. Notion doesn't answer browsers, so the reading goes through the orOS relay (which keeps nothing). Up to 20 databases and 499 rows each; pages, comments and files stay in Notion ([Notes](app:notes) brings in pages).

See also [Backup](help:t/backup).

## Keyboard shortcuts
- Arrow keys move; [[Shift]] + arrows select a range; [[PageUp]] and [[PageDown]] move a screen.
- [[Enter]] or [[F2]] edits the cell; [[Tab]] moves right; [[Esc]] cancels editing.
- [[Ctrl+Z]] undo, [[Ctrl+Y]] or [[Ctrl+Shift+Z]] redo.
- [[Ctrl+C]], [[Ctrl+X]], [[Ctrl+V]] copy, cut and paste, also with other apps.
- [[Delete]] on a selected chart deletes it.
- [[Ctrl+B]], [[Ctrl+I]], [[Ctrl+U]] bold, italic, underline.
- On a Mac use Cmd instead of Ctrl.

## Tips
- Start typing on a selected cell to replace its content straight away.
- Use another sheet for raw data and refer to it with Sheet2!A1.
- On a phone, touching and holding a cell without moving opens the sheet menu.

## Limits
- A sheet holds at most 10,000 rows × 104 columns (A to CZ); imports are capped there.
- A chart shows up to 8 series and 1,000 points; a pie or donut puts slices after the 8th into Other.
- Charts, fills, borders, wrapping and conditional formatting are not saved in Excel, Calc or CSV files (an .orossheet keeps them) (merged cells, column widths and row heights are). From Excel, Sheets and LibreOffice, a paste brings fills, colours and bold, but not borders or rules.
- Merged cells don't grow when you insert rows or columns inside them; the merge keeps its size.
- Wrapped rows grow to fit when you turn wrapping on; a row whose text you change later keeps its height until you wrap it again or drag its edge.
- While one of your devices still runs orOS 4.73 or older, it shows an area chart as columns and leaves out stacking, the line over the columns and trend lines, icon sets, formula rules and iterative calculation; the updated device keeps them unless the chart is edited on the older one. While one of your devices still runs an older orOS, it doesn't show charts, rules, row heights, fills, borders, wrapping or merged cells, and the two devices may send the workbook back and forth a few times until both are updated. The updated device keeps them, except where the older one changed the same thing: setting a format on a cell there drops that cell's fill, borders, wrapping and merge, and changing a sheet there (its name, a column width) drops its row heights.
- Array formulas that fill several cells (FILTER, SORT, UNIQUE), named ranges and pivot tables are not supported. INDIRECT reads A1 references only, not R1C1. A formula from Excel that uses a function orOS doesn't know keeps showing the value Excel saved.
- Inserting or deleting rows moves cells: if another device edits the same cells before sync, its edit stays at the old place.
- Undo history is cleared when sync brings in changes, and deleting a sheet has no Undo.
- Excel and Calc files need a helper library; if it hasn't loaded yet, you may need an internet connection.
