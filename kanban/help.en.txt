# Kanban
Kanban helps you follow work from start to finish: cards move across columns such as To Do, Doing and Done. You can keep several boards, one per project.

## Getting started
A new board comes with three columns: To Do, Doing and Done.

- Type in `Add a card…` at the bottom of a column and press [[Enter]] or the + button. New cards go to the top of the column.
- Press and drag a card to reorder it or move it to another column. This works with a mouse or a finger.
- Tap or click a card to open it.

## Cards
The `Card` dialog saves as you type. It has:
- `Text` and `Notes`.
- `Start`: the day work on the card begins (optional). With a start and a due date the card is a bar in the Timeline. A start after the due date moves the due date along.
- `Due`: a date. The card shows `Today`, `Tomorrow` or `Overdue` when it's close or late.
- `Milestone`: the card is a diamond on its due date in the Timeline instead of a bar, for a deadline or an event.
- `Labels`: tap `Add / manage labels` to pick labels or create a new one with a colour. The pencil next to a label opens `Edit label`: change its name or colour, or delete it. Every card that carries it changes with it.
- `Subtasks`: a checklist. The card shows how many are done, for example 2/5.
- `Extra info`: your own fields, each with a `Field` name and a `Value`.
- `Column`: moves the card to another column, which is handy on a phone.
- `Lane`: a free name such as "Team A", used by swimlanes (see below).
- `Attachments` and `Cover`: see below.

`Track time` (when [Timesheet](app:timesheet) is installed) starts a timer there named after the card, with the project named like the card or the board if you have one; a timer already running is left alone. The card then shows `Time tracked`, the total Timesheet holds for it.

`Duplicate` makes a copy of the card. `Delete` asks first, and the message that follows has an `Undo` button for a few seconds.

## Columns and boards
- `Add column` (the + at the top) adds a column. Tap the pencil (or double-click the title) to open `Column settings`: rename it, set a `WIP limit`, add rules, or `Delete column`, which removes its cards too.
- With a `WIP limit` the column shows how full it is, for example 3/5, and turns orange when it holds more cards than the limit. It is a warning: you can still add cards.
- Drag a column by its header to move it.
- The button at the top left shows the current board. Tap it to switch boards, or drag boards in that list to reorder them. Double-click it to rename the board.
- Next to it: `New board`, `Import from other apps` and `Manage boards...`.
- In `Manage boards...` you can change a board's colour, rename it, duplicate it, archive it or delete it. Archived boards leave the board list but keep their data, and you can unarchive them there. At least one board must stay active.

## Views and swimlanes
The view button next to `Filter by label` opens `View and board options`. The view you pick is kept per board on this device.
- `Board`: the columns.
- `Table`: every card in one sortable list. Tap a column heading to sort by it; the `Column` list in each row moves the card.
- `Timeline`: a Gantt chart. Every card with a start or due date is a bar from its start to its due date (a milestone is a diamond), with today highlighted. Drag a bar to move it, or drag its left or right end to change the start or the due date; `Undo` brings the dates back. With the keyboard, the arrow keys move the selected bar by a day and Shift with an arrow changes only its due date. The darker part of a bar shows how many of its subtasks are done; cards in the last column look finished. In the view menu: `Zoom` (Day, Week, Month), `Groups` (none, by column or by lane) and `Only workdays`. Cards without dates are counted below.
- `Only workdays` is kept for the board on every device. Weekends get a darker shade, a moved bar keeps its number of workdays and does not start or end on a Saturday or Sunday, and the length of a bar is counted in workdays. Off, every day counts.
- Dependencies: in a card, `Depends on` lists the cards it waits for (cards of the same board); tap one to remove it. In the Timeline you can also drag the dot after a bar onto another bar: that card then waits for the first one. An arrow joins them, and it turns red when the next card starts before the first one ends. When a change breaks a dependency, the message offers `Move the next ones too`, which moves every card after it just enough (they keep their length, never move earlier) and has its own `Undo`. Two cards can never wait for each other.
- `Critical path` in the view menu outlines the cards that set the end of the plan: if one of them is late, the end moves. The others have some slack.
- `Swimlanes` splits the board into rows across the columns: `By label` (one row per label, plus `No label`) or `By lane` (one row per lane name, plus `No lane`). Drag a card to another row to change its label or lane.

## Attachments and cover
- In a card, `Add file` attaches photos or other files (up to 10 MB each, 20 per card). They are kept on the orOS disk in Files › Kanban › Attachments.
- Photos are made smaller (at most 1600 pixels on the long side) and saved again, which removes their camera and location data. Other files are kept as they are.
- Tap an attachment to see it, `Open` it, `Save a copy` or `Remove` it. Only photos and PDFs are shown inside orOS.
- `Cover`: a colour, or one of the card's photos (`Use as cover`), shown on top of the card.

## Automations
- In `Column settings`, `When a card arrives here` can tick all its subtasks, set its due date to today, add a label and move it to the top. A small bolt on the column shows it has rules.
- `Automations…` in the view menu lists the rules of the board and has one more: when a due date passes, add the label "Overdue". Cards in the last column (usually Done) are left alone, and if you take the label off a card it is not added again for the same date.
- Rules are part of the board, so they sync to your other devices.

## Author Portal
- When CRM sends a board's production stage to your shop (CRM › Contracts › `Production stage…`), each card ticked there shows `Portal · phase N` on that board, or `Portal · not sent` with the reason when you hold the pointer over it. Tap the chip to open the window in CRM.
- Once CRM has sent the list, Kanban sends it again by itself a few seconds after you move a card on that board, even when CRM is closed. Kanban never changes anything in CRM.

## Export, import and templates
- `orOS file (.orosboard)` saves the board as one open file named after it: columns, cards, labels, rules and the attachments themselves. It is a ZIP: unzip it and you find `content.json` (the board), `media` (the attachments) and `preview/board.md` and `preview/board.csv`, which any text editor or spreadsheet opens. `Import from other apps` opens it again as a new board, and so does opening the file with orOS from your system (`Open with`). `Unzipped orOS folder…` in the same dialog brings back a .orosboard that was unzipped, even after you changed a file in it.
- `Export board (JSON)` saves one file with the whole board: columns, cards, labels, rules and the attachments themselves (up to 25 MB in total). `Import from other apps` reads that file back as a new board, so it also works for moving a board to someone else.
- `Export cards (CSV)` saves the cards as a table for a spreadsheet, one row per card with its column, labels, due date, lane, subtasks, start date and milestone.
- `Save as template…` keeps the board's columns, labels, WIP limits, rules and cards (without due dates, attachments and ticks).
- `New board from template…` starts a board from one of your templates or from a built-in one: Personal, Software sprint or Content calendar. You can delete your own templates there.

## Search and filter
- `Search…` shows only cards whose text, notes, due date, extra info, lane, attachment names or label names match.
- `Filter by label` shows only cards that carry any of the labels you pick. From there the pencil edits a label (name and colour) and the cross deletes it; a deleted label is removed from every card.

## Sync and backup
With [orOS sync](help:t/sync), all your boards, columns, cards and labels, plus archive state, colours, WIP limits, rules and templates, reach your other devices. Changes to different parts of the same card on two devices are both kept: for example, a move on your phone and a text edit on your laptop. Which board is open, the view and swimlanes stay on this device, and your search and filter are not synced.

- Cards with a due date also appear in [Calendar](app:calendar) as all-day events, shown as "column · card text". Cards on archived boards are left out.
- `Import from other apps` reads an orOS file (.orosboard), a board exported from orOS Kanban (JSON), JSON exports from Kanri and Trello, and exports from Asana, Jira, ClickUp, WeKan and Planka. Each file becomes a board of its own, named after the project, and your existing boards are never changed. Before importing, a preview shows what was found, where it goes and what is already here. When a file or account holds several boards, each one has a tick: untick the ones you don't want, and only the ticked boards are imported.
- Asana: in the project, open the menu next to its name › `Export/Print` › `CSV` (or `JSON`). Sections become columns, completed tasks go to a `Done` column, subtasks become subtasks, tags become labels, and the assignee, start date and dependencies go to `Extra info`.
- Jira: in the issue search (Filters › `View all issues`), pick the issues, then `Export` › `Export CSV (all fields)`. Each status becomes a column (To Do, In Progress and Done in that order, others in between) and each issue a card "KEY-12 Summary", with its description as notes, its labels, and its type, priority, assignee, reporter, parent and sprint in `Extra info`.
- ClickUp: in a List or Space, `Settings` (•••) › `Export` › `CSV`, or Settings › `Import/Export` › export your Workspace. Each status becomes a column; checklists and subtasks become subtasks, tags become labels.
- WeKan: board menu (☰) › `Export board` (JSON). Lists become columns, with the cards' labels, due dates and checklists; archived lists and cards are left out.
- Planka: Planka has no export button yet; a board as the Planka API returns it (`/api/boards/ID`, JSON) is read: lists, cards, labels, due dates and tasks.
- It can also read straight from a connected account: a Trello board, a GitHub repository's issues, or a Notion database. Connect Trello (API key and token), GitHub (a personal access token) or Notion (your integration's secret) in [Settings](app:settings) › `Connected services` first, then in `Import from other apps` pick the account, `Show boards` (or `Show repositories`, `Show databases`), pick one and `Read`.
- A GitHub repository becomes a board with two columns, `Open` and `Closed`. Each issue is a card "#12 Title" with its text as notes, its labels, its milestone's date as due date and its "- [ ]" lines as subtasks. Pull requests are left out.
- A Notion database becomes a board with a column for each option of its first `Status` property (or its first `Select`), plus `No status` for entries without one. Each entry is a card named by its title, with its first date as due date, its first multi-select as labels, and its other properties as "Name: value" lines in the notes, ending with its Notion link. In Notion, share the database with your integration first (••• › `Connections`).
- Read the same board (or a newer export of the same project) again later: only new cards, columns and labels come in. Cards you already have stay as you left them, and ones you deleted don't come back. orOS only reads Trello, GitHub and Notion: nothing there changes.
- Your boards are also included in orOS [backups](help:t/backup). The attachments are files on the orOS disk, so they go with the disk.

## Keyboard shortcuts
- [[Alt+B]] creates a new board (not while you are typing in a field or a dialog is open).
- [[Enter]] adds the card, subtask or label you just typed.

## Tips
- Use labels for kinds of work (bug, idea, urgent) and the filter to focus on one kind.
- Archive a finished project's board instead of deleting it.
- Deletes and imports show an `Undo` button for a few seconds. Use it if you change your mind.

## Limits
- If the same part of a card (for example its text, or its due date) is changed on two devices before they sync, the newer change wins.
- A card changed on a device that still runs an older orOS counts as changed as a whole, so there the newer whole card can still win.
- Attachments are not sent by orOS sync: they reach your other devices with the orOS disk through Vault Drive. Until then the card shows "Not on this device".
- Only photos and PDFs can be viewed inside orOS; other attachments can only be saved.
- The "Overdue" rule leaves cards in the last column alone, and only runs while Kanban is open.
- On an older orOS, templates show up as archived boards.
