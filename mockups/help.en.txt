# Mockups
Make realistic 3D pictures of your book for a shop page, a post or a press kit. The book is built from its real measures, so the size, the page count and the binding change the picture at once.

## Getting started
1. Press `Make a book mockup` (or [[+]] at the top for another one).
2. On the `Book` tab give the binding, the size and the pages.
3. On the `Cover` tab choose the pictures of the front, the back and the spine.
4. On the `Scene` tab drag the picture to turn the camera, and pick a placement and a background.
5. On the `Save` tab pick the shape and the size of the picture and press `Save picture`.

## The book
- `Binding`: `Paperback` has a soft cover flush with the pages; `Hardcover` has boards a little larger than the pages, a groove next to the spine, and can have a `Rounded spine`, a `Headband` and a `Ribbon bookmark`.
- `Size`: ready sizes (A5, B-format, 6 × 9″ and more) or your own width and height, in mm or inches.
- `Pages` and `Paper` give the spine: pages × the thickness of one page. The white and cream papers use the figures print-on-demand services give. With `Set the spine width by hand` you type the spine your printer gave you.
- `Flaps`: on a paperback they are French flaps folded inside the cover; on a hardcover they turn the cover into a dust jacket, and `Cover under the jacket` sets the colour of the boards. Open the cover a little on the `Scene` tab to see them.
- `Finish`: matte, gloss (shines with the light) or soft-touch. `Page edges` can be white, cream or sprayed in a colour.

## The cover
- `Front, back, spine`: one picture for each part, and one for each flap when there are flaps. A picture fills its part and is cut to fit.
- `One full wrap`: the whole printed cover in one picture, as you send it to the printer (back, spine and front, flaps too). It is the sheet of the `Print` tab: the app tells you its exact size and where each part starts, and puts each part on its side of the book. `Bleed` is the extra edge around it.
- Where there is no picture the book takes the `Colour where there is no picture`, and the spine shows the `Spine text`.
- Pictures stay on your orOS disk and travel to your other devices with it.

## Several books
- `Books in the picture` (on the `Scene` tab): `One book`, `Front + back` (the same book twice, one showing its back), `Series` (side by side, fronts to the camera), `Shelf` (standing in a row, spines to the camera) or `Stack` (lying one on another).
- For a series, a shelf or a stack, `How many books` goes from 2 to 6, and `Space between them` sets the gap.
- On the `Cover` tab, `The other books` gives each book its own pictures, pages and spine text. A book without its own takes the first book's, so a stack of the same book needs nothing more.

## Scene and camera
- Drag the picture to turn around the book. Pinch, or use the mouse wheel, to come closer. The buttons under the picture jump to ready angles.
- `Placement`: standing, lying on its back or leaning on a wall. `Turn` turns the book on the floor; `Open the cover` opens it up to 160°.
- `Perspective`: low values look flat like a catalogue picture, high values look close and dramatic. `Move left / right` and `Move up / down` place the book in the frame.
- `Background`: see-through, a colour, a gradient or a photo. With `Photo`, `Choose a picture` takes one of yours and `Find free photos` searches Openverse and Wikimedia Commons (only photos that allow commercial use). A free photo is saved on your orOS disk with its credit, shown under it; `Copy credit` copies the line to give where you post the picture. `Shadow` sets how dark the floor shadow is.
- `Light`: where it comes from, how high, how strong, how soft its shadows are, and cool or warm.

## For the printer
The `Print` tab makes the cover sheet your printer asks for, from this book's numbers. The picture above turns into the flat sheet.
- A paperback sheet is back cover · spine · front cover (with the flaps at the ends), plus the `Bleed` all round, which is cut away. The spine is pages × the thickness of one page, as print-on-demand services count it.
- A hardcover case has the boards, two hinges next to the spine, and 15 mm that wraps round the boards. With flaps it is a dust jacket.
- The guides: red is the bleed (or the wrap), the red line is the trim, blue dashes are folds, purple dashes are hinges, and green dashes are the safe zone: keep text inside it. The white box on the back is the spot for the barcode.
- `With my cover pictures` puts your front, back and spine (or the full wrap) on the sheet. With `Show the guides` off you get your finished cover with its bleed, ready to send.
- `Save PNG` and `Save PDF` make it at 300 dpi; the PDF has the trim and bleed boxes print shops read. `Design it in Atelier` makes an Atelier design of the right size with the bleed; the guides show there while you work but never print or export. When it is ready, `More` › `Make a book mockup` in Atelier brings it back onto this book.
- Your printer's own template wins. If it gives another spine width, type it in `Book` › `Set the spine width by hand`; set its bleed here.

## Saving
- `Picture shape`: square, 4:5 (posts), 3:2, 4:3, 16:9 or 9:16 (stories). `Size` is the long side, up to 4096 px; some phones draw less, and the app tells you.
- PNG and WebP keep a see-through background; JPG makes it white.
- `Save to Pictures` puts it in the Pictures folder of Files. `Open in Atelier` makes a new Atelier design with the picture, to add text and more.

## All angles and video
- `Save all angles (ZIP)` makes the seven ready views (front, ¾ from both sides, spine, back, from above, low angle) in the size and format above, in one ZIP file. `All angles to Pictures` puts them in a new folder in Pictures.
- `Turntable video`: the books turn `All round` or `To and fro` for 4 to 12 seconds. `Video` makes an MP4 (or WebM where the browser has no MP4 encoder) of 720 or 1080 px; `GIF` makes a small looping GIF (480 px, at most 8 seconds). `Cancel` stops it. When it is ready, save it, or keep it in Videos (a GIF goes to Pictures).
- The video is made on your device, frame by frame; a long or large one takes a little while on a phone. A video has no see-through, so a see-through background turns white.

## Atelier
In Atelier, `More` › `Make a book mockup` sends the design here: page 1 becomes the front, page 2 the back and page 3 the spine. One wide page is taken as a full wrap. A design made in mm also gives the book its size.

## Your mockups
- Every mockup is kept with its settings and follows you to every device through orOS sync, encrypted.
- `More` (the three dots) has `Duplicate this mockup`, `Reset the camera and light` and `Delete this mockup`.
- Shortcut: Ctrl+Shift+S saves the picture. On the picture: arrow keys turn the camera, + and − come closer or move away.
