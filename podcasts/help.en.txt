# Podcasts
Podcasts lets you follow your favourite shows, see new episodes, build an `Up next` queue and download episodes for listening offline. The player keeps going after you close the app.

## Getting started
Open `Discover`, search for a podcast and press `Subscribe`. You can also paste an address into `Feed, website or Apple Podcasts address` and press `Add`. The **+** button at the top (`Add podcast`) takes you straight there.

The tabs:
- `Listening`: what's playing, the `Up next` queue and `Continue listening`.
- `New`: episodes from the last 30 days you haven't started yet.
- `Library`: your podcasts, with a badge for new episodes. Tap one to open its page. `Search all episodes` finds an episode in any of your podcasts, and `Statistics` shows how long you listened (counted on this device).
- `Downloads`: episodes saved on this device.
- `Discover`: search, add by address and OPML, and browsing like the Radio: `Popular` (the top 100 of a country, with sub-genre chips), `Genres`, `Countries` and `Surprise me` (a random popular podcast starts playing). Tap a podcast's name to try it before you subscribe: its episodes play, but nothing is saved or synced until you press `Subscribe`. The lists come from the Apple Podcasts charts.

## Listen
Press the play button on any episode. The player bar at the bottom has skip back, play/pause and skip forward; tap the episode's name there to open the full player with `Playback speed`, `Sleep timer`, chapters, `Show notes`, `Next in queue` and `Stop`. The sleep timer stops after 5 to 60 minutes or at the `End of episode`.

Tap an episode's title to read its notes; times in the notes appear under `Jump to`. The ⋯ button (`More actions`) offers `Play next`, `Add to queue`, `Mark as played`, `Download`, `Show notes` and `Open the episode page`.

In `Up next`, the arrows move episodes up or down. Your device's media controls (lock screen, headset) work too.

## Chapters, transcripts and video
- When an episode has chapters, the full player lists them under `Chapters`, with `Previous chapter` and `Next chapter`.
- `Transcript` shows the episode's text when the show offers one. Tap a line to jump there, use `Search the transcript`, and turn on `Follow playback` to keep the current line in view.
- Video episodes have a `Watch` button.
- `Share` sends a link to an episode; in the player, `Share this moment` includes the current time.
- `Note at 12:34` writes a note in [Notes](app:notes) with the episode and the time, and `Listen later (To-Do)` adds the episode to [To-Do](app:todo).

## Podcast pages
A podcast's page lists its episodes, with filters `All`, `Unplayed`, `In progress`, `Played` and `Downloaded`, and `Newest first` or `Oldest first`. From there you can `Mark all as played`, visit the `Website`, `Support the show` when the podcast offers it, or `Unsubscribe` (its downloads are deleted too). `Follow the site in Reader` follows the show's website in [Reader](app:feeds).

`Podcast settings` sets a speed for that show, `Skip the first` and `Skip the last` seconds (for intros and outros), and `Download new episodes automatically`. `Tell me about new episodes` sends a notification when that show puts out a new episode; it is off by default, and orOS checks every few hours while it is open.

## Downloads
Choose `Download` from an episode's menu. The `Downloads` tab shows how much space they take and how much is free. `Keep downloads safe from automatic clean-up` asks the browser to keep them; `Delete played downloads` tidies up.

## Settings
The gear button opens `Settings`: default speed, `Skip back` and `Skip forward` amounts, `Play the next episode in the queue`, `Delete downloads after they are played`, and which catalog `Catalog search` uses (`Apple Podcasts and fyyd`, one of them, or off).

### Skip silence and voice boost
`Skip silence` plays the quiet stretches between words faster, so episodes take less time; the full player shows how much time it saved, and `Statistics` adds it up. `Voice boost` evens out the volume and makes voices clearer, handy in a noisy room. Turn them on in `Settings` or with the two buttons in the full player.

They work on downloaded episodes and on podcasts whose server lets the browser read the audio. When a server doesn't, the episode plays normally and the full player says so; download the episode to use them.

### Sync with gpodder
`Sync with gpodder…` in `Settings` keeps your subscriptions and listening progress in step with other podcast apps (AntennaPod, Kasts, gPodder and others) through your own gpodder.net account or your own Nextcloud with the GPodder Sync app. Choose the service, enter the server address, username and password, and press `Save and sync`. After that it syncs when Podcasts opens and every hour while it's open; `Sync now` syncs straight away and `Disconnect` stops it without deleting anything.

The password stays sealed on this device and is never synced, so enter it once on each device; use an app password if your server offers one.

Without a server, move subscriptions with `Export OPML` and `Import OPML`, and listening progress with `Export listening progress` and `Import listening progress` (gpodder episode actions as a JSON file).

## Save and open an orOS file
In `Settings`, `orOS file (.orospodcasts)` saves every subscription with its own settings, its played marks and listening progress as one `.orospodcasts` file, for example to move them to another device without sync or to keep a copy. The file is a plain ZIP: unzipped it holds the podcasts as `content.json` plus `preview/podcasts.opml`, which any podcast app can import. It is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere safe. Downloads, the queue, the player settings and your gpodder login are never in it.

`Open orOS file…` (or `Unzipped orOS folder…`, or "Open with" orOS on the computer) shows how many podcasts and progress records are new and how many are already here, then `Add` adds only the new ones: opening the same file again adds nothing. A podcast already here keeps its settings and progress, and one you unsubscribed here stays unsubscribed; a file never deletes anything. Tick `Update with newer versions` to let records that were changed later in the file replace yours. `Undo` in the message removes what was added. An `.opml` file opened with Podcasts is imported as before.

## Sync and backup
Your podcasts, episode progress, played marks, queue and settings sync between your devices through [orOS sync](help:t/sync). Downloads, cached episode lists and exact positions stay on this device. `Export OPML` and `Import OPML` in `Discover` move your subscriptions to or from other podcast apps. See also [Backup](help:t/backup).

## Keyboard shortcuts
While an episode is loaded:
- [[Space]] plays or pauses.
- [[←]] / [[→]] skip back or forward.
- `[` / `]` slow down or speed up.
- [[N]] plays the next episode in the queue.

## Tips
- Podcasts refresh when you open the app and every hour while it's open; `Refresh` checks now.
- Some podcast sites block browsers. Setting up the relay in [Mail](app:mail) lets Podcasts read them.

## Limits
- Search terms go to the catalog you choose; turn search off if you prefer to add podcasts by address only.
- Some hosts don't allow downloads; those episodes can still be streamed.
- Up to 500 podcasts.
- Skip silence and voice boost only work when the browser may read the audio (downloads, or servers that allow it). Elsewhere they stay off.
- gpodder sync connects straight from the browser, so the server must allow web pages to connect (CORS). If it doesn't, you'll see a message saying so; use the files instead. It only syncs while orOS is open.
