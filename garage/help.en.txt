# Garage
Garage keeps fuel, service, tyres, costs and renewals for every vehicle you own, from a car or a boat to a bicycle. It works out consumption and cost per km, and reminds you before the KTEO, insurance or next service.

## Getting started
Tap or click `Add a vehicle`. Pick a `Type` (car, motorcycle, scooter, van, truck, camper, tractor, boat, bicycle, e-bike, e-scooter or other), give it a `Name`, and fill in what you like: `Make`, `Model`, `Year`, `Plate`, `Fuel`, tank or battery size and `Odometer now (km)`. Leave `Add the usual service plan (you can change it)` ticked to start with a ready service plan. Bicycles and e-scooters have no fuel, only maintenance.

With more than one vehicle, a bar under the toolbar switches between them.

## Log fuel, service and costs
The `Add` button, or the quick buttons on `Overview`, offer:
- `Fuel` or `Charging`: fill in any two of quantity, price and total and the third is worked out. Untick `Full tank` for a partial fill, and tick `I missed logging the previous fill` if you skipped one, so consumption stays right.
- `Service`: pick `What was done` from the list, with cost and `Garage / shop`.
- `Expense`: tolls, parking, wash, fines, parts and more.
- `Km reading`: just the odometer.

Fuel, service and expense entries can keep up to 6 receipt photos: use `Add a receipt photo` under `Receipts`. The log marks them with 📎 and the number of photos. They are kept in [Files](app:files), in the folder Garage/Receipts.

In `Fuel` or `Charging`, `Read a receipt` takes the text of a receipt (paste it, or type what it says) and fills in the date, quantity, price per litre or kWh, total and station, from Greek or English receipts. It all happens on this device; check the values, then `Save`.

From the same menu you can add a renewal, a service plan item, a tyre set or another vehicle.

## Overview
`Overview` shows the vehicle, `Coming up`, tiles for consumption, this month's cost, cost per km and km per month, then:
- `Renewals`: KTEO, insurance, road tax, emissions card, roadside assistance, driving licence or other, each with an expiry date and `Remind me before (days)`. When you renew, tap `Renewed`, enter what you paid and the new expiry is set (with `Undo`).
- `Service plan`: items due every so many km and/or months. Tap `Done` to log the service with that item already picked.
- `Tyres`: sets by season, with DOT age, tread and km run. `Mount` swaps the set on the vehicle.

`→ To-Do` next to `Coming up` sends those items to [To-Do](app:todo) as tasks, such as "Book the KTEO test" with the expiry date in the note. The editor of a renewal or a service plan item also has a `To-Do` button for that one item. To-Do shows them first and asks before it adds anything.

## Log and Stats
`Log` lists every entry month by month, with filters by kind and the consumption of each full fill. Tap an entry to edit or delete it.

`Stats` covers `12 months`, `This year` or `All time`: total cost, km driven, cost per km, average consumption and price, and charts of costs per month, consumption, fuel price and `Where the money goes`. `Show as table` turns a chart into a table.

## Logbook report
`Logbook` on the vehicle card (or `Logbook report` in `Settings`) puts the whole history of the vehicle on one page: the vehicle, a summary, every service, renewals and tyres. Tick `Fuel and charging fills` or `Other costs` to add them, and untick `Show the amounts paid` to leave out the prices, for example when you sell the car. `Print or save as PDF` opens the print window (pick "Save as PDF" as the printer for a PDF); `Save as HTML` saves one file you can open in any browser.

## Import from another app
In `Settings`, `Import from another app` reads the export of another vehicle log. Pick the file (a .csv, or a .zip of several), then check the preview: which vehicle in the file, `Add to` an existing vehicle or a new one (named from the file when it has a name), and the units of the file. Garage keeps km and litres, so miles and gallons are converted. Fills become fuel entries (full, partial, after a missed fill), services become service entries and other costs become expenses by category. An entry that is already there (same date, odometer and amount) is skipped, and importing the same file again adds nothing. `Undo` on the message takes the whole import back.
- Fuelio: Settings, Backup & sync (or Dropbox / Google Drive sync) saves `vehicle-1-sync.csv`, one file per vehicle, with fills and costs.
- Drivvo: Menu, Export (or Settings, Export data) to a CSV file with refuelling, expenses and services. Income is left out.
- Fuelly: on the Fuelly website, open your account, Export, and download the CSV. Its miles and US gallons are converted; pick UK gallons if your car logged imperial ones.
- aCar: Menu, Backup & Restore, Export to CSV, and pick the fill-up records (or the service records) file.
- LubeLogger: on a vehicle, open Gas, Service, Repair, Upgrade or Tax records and use Export to CSV; import one file at a time into the same vehicle.

## The family car
With a [household](app:household) set up, pick the vehicle, then the gear › `Household` › `Share with a household`. The vehicle moves into the household with all its fill-ups, services, plans, tyres, renewals, costs and readings: everyone who drives it logs in their own Garage, sees the same consumption and costs, and gets its reminders once. A house mark in the vehicle bar shows which vehicles are shared. Currency and reminder times stay each person's own; receipt photos stay in your Files (the others see that a receipt exists). Everything is end-to-end encrypted: the orOS relay only passes on what it cannot read.

A vehicle with more than about 2900 entries cannot be shared. `Stop sharing` takes the vehicle out of the household for everyone and brings it back to your own Garage with everything logged; the others can bring it back from the Household app for 30 days.

## Sync and backup
Vehicles, entries, renewals, plans, tyres and your currency sync between your devices through [orOS sync](help:t/sync). Receipt photos travel separately, with Vault Drive sync; until they arrive, a photo shows `Not on this device`. The reminder hour, consumption unit, tyre limits and open tab stay on each device.

In `Settings`: `Export CSV`, `Export backup` (JSON) and `Import backup`. An import merges with what you have. Inside orOS, `Open in Spreadsheet` opens the same rows as `Export CSV` in a new sheet in [Spreadsheet](app:spreadsheet), with the total cost at the bottom.

## Save and open an orOS file
In `Settings`, `orOS file (.orosgarage)` saves every vehicle, fuel and charging fill, service, service plan, tyre set, renewal, expense and km reading as one `.orosgarage` file, with the receipt photos inside: for example to move your vehicles to another device without sync, or to keep a copy. The reminder hour, consumption unit and tyre limits stay on this device. The file is a plain ZIP: unzipped it holds the records as `content.json`, the photos in `media/` and `preview/log.csv` (the same CSV as `Export CSV`) for other programs. It is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere safe.

`Open orOS file…` (or `Unzipped orOS folder…`, or "Open with" orOS on the computer) shows how many records are new and how many are already here, then `Add` adds only the new ones, with their own ids: opening the same file again adds nothing. A record already here is kept as it is, and one you deleted here stays deleted; a file never deletes anything. Tick `Update with newer versions` to let records that were changed later in the file replace your copy. A tyre set from the file is not mounted on a vehicle that already has a mounted set here. Receipt photos go to Garage/Receipts only when they are not there yet, with the same checks as adding one by hand. `Undo` in the message removes what was added.

## Keyboard shortcuts
- [[Left]] / [[Right]] on the tabs switch between `Overview`, `Log` and `Stats`.

## Tips
- In an entry, `Add to Budget` opens a ready expense in [Budget](app:budget); you check it and save it there.
- Renewals and service due dates also appear in [Calendar](app:calendar) under `Garage`.
- Choose the `Daily reminder` hour in `Settings`. See [Notifications](help:t/notifications).
- `Archive` a vehicle you sold: its history stays, reminders stop.

## Limits
- Reminders appear while orOS is open, even with this app closed. With orOS closed they wait until you open it again.
- Service intervals are only typical ones. Your vehicle's manual comes first.
- `Read a receipt` works with the receipt's text, not a photo, in this version. Receipts with an unusual layout may be read only in part: check the values before you save.
