# Crossword
A fill-in crossword with no clues: you get the list of words, grouped by length, and your job is to fit them all into the grid so every crossing agrees. Play the daily crossword or as many as you like.

## How to play
The words to place are listed next to the grid, grouped by length (`5 letters` and so on). A few letters are given. Tap or click a cell and type; tap the same cell again to switch between across and down. A word that's in place is ticked off the list. The crossword is solved when every word fits; if a puzzle has more than one solution, any filling where all the words fit counts.

Words come in English or Greek, chosen with `EN` / `ΕΛ` in the toolbar. Greek words are in capitals without accents, and a final ς is written as Σ. You can type Greek on an English keyboard and the other way round, since keys work by position, or use the on-screen keyboard.

## Controls
- `Daily` or `Free`, and in free play the size: `9×9` (8–10 words), `13×13` (14–18) or `17×17` (22–28).
- `Words`: `Random words`, or the words of a theme such as `Animals` or `Food`.
- `Check and reveal`: `Check letter`, `Check word`, `Check all` (wrong letters turn red) and `Reveal letter`.

Keyboard:
- Type letters; [[Backspace]] deletes back, [[Delete]] clears the cell.
- Arrow keys move; [[Space]] switches across / down; [[Tab]] and [[Shift+Tab]] jump to the next or previous word.
- [[Shift+L]] check letter, [[Shift+W]] check word, [[Shift+A]] check all, [[Shift+R]] reveal letter, [[Shift+N]] new puzzle.

## Levels and scoring
`Daily` is one medium crossword a day, the same on every device. `Statistics` shows the days solved, your streak, best streak and best daily time, plus how many free-play puzzles you've solved and your best time for each size. Revealing a letter means no record for that puzzle; checking is free. `Reset statistics` clears them.

After the daily, `Share` sends your result without spoilers: your time and one square per word, green for a word you found alone and yellow for a word with a revealed letter. On a phone it opens the share sheet; elsewhere it copies the text. `All games` opens the [Game Center](help:a/gamecenter), when it's installed.

## Imported crosswords
The download-tray button in the toolbar opens `Imported crosswords`: clued crosswords you bring in from a file, played with numbered squares and Across / Down clues. `Import a file…` reads:
- Across Lite `.puz` files, the format most newspaper and indie crossword sites offer as a download (look for "Across Lite" or ".puz" next to the puzzle). A puzzle that is locked (scrambled) in Across Lite has to be unlocked there and saved first.
- `.ipuz` files, an open format exported by many crossword makers and sites (often under Download or Export).

You see the title, author, size and clue count before anything is saved; `Import and play` adds it to the list and opens it, and `Undo` in the message that follows takes it out again. The same file twice is never added twice. The clue of the current word shows above the grid; tap a clue to jump to it. `Check and reveal` works against the file's solution. Squares with more than one letter (rebus) take the first letter and show the whole entry once solved; circled squares are marked. Each imported crossword keeps its own progress and best time and doesn't change the Daily or Free play records. `Free` or `Daily` takes you back.

## Sync
Your daily results, free-play records and imported crosswords sync between your devices through [orOS sync](help:t/sync). If you've already solved today's crossword on another device, you'll see its time. The puzzles in progress and your settings stay on this device.

## Tips
- Start with lengths that have only one word.
- The themes and word lists are the same as in [Word Search](help:a/wordsearch).
