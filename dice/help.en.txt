# Chance
Roll any mix of dice for board games and role-playing games, or flip a coin. Every result goes into a history you can share, export and look at as statistics.

Chance has two tabs: `Dice & coin` and `Wheel`. The Wheel was the Wheel of Fate app; old links and shortcuts to it open the Wheel tab. Chance opens on the tab you used last; [[←]] and [[→]] on the tabs switch between them.

## Getting started
Set `Count` (1 to 30 dice), `Type` (d4, d6, d8, d10, d12, d20 or d100) and a `Modifier` (−99 to +99) that is added to the total. Then tap or click `Roll`. The result shows the `Total` and each die; dice that don't count toward the total are shown dimmed.

To flip a coin, tap or click `Flip Coin` or the coin itself. The counts of `Heads` and `Tails` sit under the coin.

## Roll modes
- `Normal`: all dice count.
- `Keep Highest`: only the highest die counts.
- `Keep Lowest`: only the lowest die counts.
- `Drop Lowest`: all dice count except the lowest one.

A `MAX!` badge appears when every die shows its top number, and `Fumble` when a single die shows 1.

## Notation and presets
### Type a roll
In the box with `2d20+5 · 4d6 dl · 8d6 kh`, type a roll in dice notation and press `Apply` or [[Enter]]: it fills in the settings and rolls straight away. Add `kh` for keep highest, `kl` for keep lowest or `dl` for drop lowest. If the box flashes red, the notation wasn't understood.

### Presets
`Save preset` (the **＋** button) stores the current settings under a name you choose. Tap or click a preset to load its settings, then roll. The ✕ on a preset deletes it. Saving under an existing name replaces that preset.

## History and statistics
`History` lists your latest 50 rolls and flips with date and time.
- `Stats` shows how many dice rolls and coin flips you made, heads against tails, max rolls, fumbles and the average for each die type.
- `Export` saves the history as a text file.
- `Share` copies the latest result to the clipboard as a short text card.
- `Clear` deletes the whole history after you confirm.

## Wheel
Can't decide? Write your options on the wheel, spin it and let fate choose. Keep the wheels you use often, or start from a ready set.

### Start a wheel
A new wheel starts with two empty rows under `Options`. Type an option in each, or type in `Add an option…` and press [[Enter]] or the **+** button. The wheel updates as you type. You need at least two options to spin, and you can have up to 30, each up to 50 characters.

Then tap or click `Spin!` in the middle of the wheel, tap or flick the wheel itself, or press [[Space]] or [[Enter]].

### Spin and results
The winner is drawn fairly at random before the wheel starts turning; the spin just lands on it. While the wheel turns you can't edit the options.

When it stops, `The wheel has spoken` shows the winner, with confetti. From there:
- `Spin again` spins once more.
- `Remove from the wheel` takes the winner off, handy for drawing names one by one. An `Undo` button lets you put it back. It isn't offered when only two options are left.
- `Close` goes back to the wheel.

The pointer clicks as it passes each segment and plays a short tune at the end. The `Sound` button in the toolbar turns this off or on (on at first).

### Edit the options
- Paste several lines at once into the add box or into a row: each line becomes an option.
- Drag an option by its grip to move it, or press [[Alt+Up]] or [[Alt+Down]] in its row.
- The ✕ next to a row removes it. Empty rows disappear when you leave them.
- `Shuffle options` mixes up the order.
- The `×1` button next to a row sets the option's chance: tap it for `×2` (double) or `×3` (triple), and once more to go back to `×1`. A weighted option gets a wider slice of the wheel, so you can see its bigger chance. Weights are kept with saved wheels and sync with them.

### Ready sets and your wheels
`Ready sets` has wheels to start from: `Yes / No`, `What to eat`, `Days of the week`, `Numbers 1–10`, `Heads or tails`, `Chores`, `Movie night`, `What to do` and `Who goes first`. A set opens as a new, unsaved wheel, so your saved ones are never changed.

`Save wheel` gives the open wheel a name and keeps it under `My wheels` (up to 40). Changes to a saved wheel are saved automatically as you type. Next to each saved wheel you can rename it, make a copy or delete it; a delete can be undone for a few seconds. `New wheel` starts an empty one.

`Winners` lists the last 50 winners of the open wheel, with the time. `Clear` empties the list, with `Undo`.

### Wheels on your devices
Your saved wheels sync between your devices through [orOS sync](help:t/sync). The winners list, the unsaved wheel and the sound setting stay on this device. There's no import or export; the orOS [backup](help:t/backup) includes your wheels.

### Wheel keys
- [[Space]] or [[Enter]] spins the wheel (when you're not typing in a box).
- [[Enter]] in the add box adds the option; in a row, it moves to the next row.
- [[Alt+Up]] / [[Alt+Down]] moves the current option.

### Wheel tips
- To split chores fairly, spin, use `Remove from the wheel` and spin again until everyone has one.
- If you reduce motion on your device, the spin is short and there's no confetti.

## Sync and backup
Your history and presets sync between your devices through [orOS sync](help:t/sync), and clearing the history or deleting a preset carries over too. The sound setting stays on this device. Use `Export` for a readable copy of the history, or the orOS [backup](help:t/backup) for everything.

## Keyboard shortcuts
- [[Space]] rolls the dice.
- [[C]] flips the coin.
- [[Enter]] in the notation box applies and rolls.

## Tips
- The speaker button turns roll and flip sounds on or off (off at first).
- For a classic character stat, type `4d6 dl` and press [[Enter]].

## Limits
- The history keeps only the latest 50 results, so the coin counts and `Stats` cover those 50 only.
- Notation takes one kind of die per roll, such as `3d8+2`; mixes like `1d20+1d4` aren't understood.
