# Storage
Storage is a home inventory: it helps you remember where you put things. You build it in levels, from a space down to the shelf or drawer where each item lives.

## Getting started
Storage has five levels, each inside the one before:
1. Space, such as your home, a holiday house or a storeroom. You start with one called `Home`.
2. Room, such as the kitchen.
3. Furniture, such as a wardrobe.
4. Position, such as the top shelf or the second drawer.
5. Items, the things themselves.

Tap or click a space to open it. The button at the top right adds something to the level you're in: `Add space`, `Add room`, `Add furniture`, `Add position` or `Add item`. Give it a `Name` and press `Save`.

## Moving around
- Tap or click a card to go inside it.
- `‹ Back` takes you up one level; the path at the top lets you jump to any level above.
- On a wide screen, a tree of all your spaces, rooms, furniture and positions sits on the side, so you can jump straight to any of them.

Each card shows how much is inside it, such as `3 inside` or `5 items`.

## Items
An item has a `Name`, a `Quantity` and a `Note (optional)`, for example "winter only" or "batteries AA". Use the − and + buttons on the item to change the quantity; it never goes below 1.

## Rename and delete
Every card has a pencil to `Rename` (for items, also to change the quantity and note) and a bin to `Delete`. Deleting a space, room, furniture or position also deletes everything inside it, so Storage always asks you first.

>! A delete can't be undone. Deleting a whole space removes all its rooms and items too.

## Sync and backup
Everything you add syncs between your devices through [orOS sync](help:t/sync). Storage has no import or export of its own; the orOS [backup](help:t/backup) keeps a copy.

## Keyboard shortcuts
- [[Enter]] on a selected card opens it.
- [[Esc]] goes up one level.

## Tips
- Name positions the way you'd describe them out loud ("top shelf, left"), so they still make sense in a year.
- Use the note for details you'd otherwise forget, like a size or a battery type.

## Limits
- There's no search; you find things by browsing the levels.
- You can't move an item or a level to a different place; delete it and add it again where it belongs.
- Notes are kept to 300 characters.
