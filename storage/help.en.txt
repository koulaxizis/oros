# Storage
Storage is a home inventory: it helps you remember where you put things, what they're worth and who borrowed them. You build it in levels, from a space down to the shelf or drawer where each item lives.

## Getting started
Storage has five levels, each inside the one before:
1. Space, such as your home, a holiday house or a storeroom. You start with one called `Home`.
2. Room, such as the kitchen.
3. Furniture, such as a wardrobe.
4. Position, such as the top shelf, the second drawer or a box.
5. Items, the things themselves.

Tap or click a space to open it. The button at the top right adds something to the level you're in: `Add space`, `Add room`, `Add furniture`, `Add position` or `Add item` (on a phone it shows only `+`). Give it a `Name` and press `Save`.

## Moving around
- Tap or click a card to go inside it.
- `‹ Back` takes you up one level; the path at the top lets you jump to any level above.
- On a wide screen, a tree of all your spaces, rooms, furniture and positions sits on the side, so you can jump straight to any of them.

Each card shows how much is inside it, such as `3 inside` or `5 items`, and what it's worth when you've entered values.

## Items
An item has a `Name`, a `Quantity` and a `Note (optional)`, for example "winter only" or "batteries AA". Use the − and + buttons on the item to change the quantity; it never goes below 1.

The pencil on an item opens `Edit`, where you can also fill in:
- `Tags (optional, separated by commas)`, such as `tools, camping`. Search finds them.
- `Value of one (optional)`, such as `25` or `25,50`. Storage multiplies it by the quantity.
- `Purchase date` and `Warranty until`.
- `Photos`: up to 4 pictures. Press `+` and pick or take a photo.
- `Receipt`: one picture or PDF, with `Attach a receipt`.

Photos and receipts are kept on the orOS disk, in Files › Storage, not inside your data. Storage redraws every picture before keeping it, so the location and camera details stored in a photo are left out. Vault Drive copies the files to your other devices; until it has, a photo shows `?` there.

Spaces, rooms, furniture and positions can have a note and tags too, for example "Christmas decorations" on a box.

## Rename and delete
Every card has a pencil to `Rename` (`Edit` on items). `⋯` on a card has `Delete`. Deleting a space, room, furniture or position also deletes everything inside it, so Storage always asks you first.

>! A delete can't be undone. Deleting a whole space removes all its rooms and items too, and their photos and receipts on this device.

## Search
The magnifier at the top searches every level at once: names, notes, tags and the person you lent something to. Each result shows where it is, such as `Home › Garage › Metal shelf › Box 1`. Tap a result to go there; an item opens the position it's in and lights up so you can spot it.

The orOS menu search finds your Storage items, with their tags, as well.

## Move
Press `⋯` on a card and choose `Move…`. A tree shows every place it can go: an item goes into a position, a position onto furniture, furniture into a room and a room into a space. Pick one and it moves with everything inside it. `Undo` on the message puts it back.

Spaces don't move, and nothing can move inside itself.

## Value
A bar at the top shows the value of the place you're in (`Total value` on the first level) and how many items it holds. The currency is set in `⋯` › `Settings…`.

## Lending
Press `⋯` on an item and choose `Lend…`. Fill in `Lent to` (or pick someone with `From Contacts`), `Lent on` and, if you like, `Return by (optional)`. The item then shows "Lent to Giorgos on 14 Oct 2026".

`⋯` at the top › `Lent out` lists everything you've lent, the earliest return date first; late ones are in red. Press `Returned` when you get it back; `Undo` on the message brings the loan back if you pressed it by mistake.

## QR labels for boxes
`⋯` at the top › `Print QR labels…` prints an A4 sheet of 24 labels (70 × 37 mm), one for each space, room, furniture or position you tick. Each label has the place's name, where it is, and a QR code. `⋯` on a card › `QR label…` prints just that one.

Scan a label with your phone's camera and orOS opens Storage at that place. Where the browser can read codes itself (Chrome on Android), `⋯` › `Scan a label` does the same from inside Storage.

> A label points to the orOS address you printed it from. If you use orOS at another address, print the labels again.

## Reminders
`⋯` › `Settings…` › `Reminders on this device` turns on reminders from the hour you choose. Storage then tells you 30 days and 7 days before a warranty ends and on the day, and on the return-by date of something you lent. Reminders are off until you turn them on, separately on each device; they arrive like any other [notification](help:t/notifications).

## Sync and backup
Everything you add syncs between your devices through [orOS sync](help:t/sync). Storage has no import or export of its own; the orOS [backup](help:t/backup) keeps a copy.

## Keyboard shortcuts
- [[Enter]] on a selected card opens it.
- [[/]] opens the search.
- [[Esc]] goes up one level, or leaves the search.

## Tips
- Name positions the way you'd describe them out loud ("top shelf, left"), so they still make sense in a year.
- Use the note for details you'd otherwise forget, like a size or a battery type.
- Tag things by use (`camping`, `winter`, `tools`) and search the tag to gather them from every room.
- Stick a QR label on each box: scanning it shows what's inside without opening it.

## Limits
- Notes are kept to 300 characters; up to 10 tags of 30 characters each.
- Up to 4 photos and one receipt per item; a PDF receipt can be up to 10 MB.
- One currency for all values, chosen on each device; amounts aren't converted.
- Reminders only fire while orOS is open; a warranty end missed by more than a week isn't announced afterwards.
- Scanning inside Storage needs a browser with a built-in code reader (Chrome on Android); elsewhere use the phone's camera app.
- A device that still runs an older orOS keeps your items but drops the new details (tags, value, dates, photos, loans) when it saves them. Update every device.
