# Help
The orOS guide: how orOS works and how to use each app, in English and Greek, also offline.

## Getting started
- Open **Help** from the menu, or tap the **?** button in the top bar. Inside an app, **?** opens straight at that app's page.
- On a computer you can also press [[Ctrl+Alt+Shift+H]].
- On a wide screen the list of pages is on the left. On a phone you see the list first; tap a page to read it and use the back arrow to return to the list.

## Find what you need
- **Guide** holds the general topics: getting started, sync, backup, shortcuts and more.
- Below it, every app has its own page, grouped like the menu. An app marked `soon` has no page yet.
- Type in the search box to search every page at once. Accents and capitals do not matter, so `συγχρονισμος` finds `συγχρονισμός`. Press [[/]] to jump to the search box and [[Esc]] to clear it.
- A result opens the page at the matching section.
- On an app's page, the button next to the title opens that app.

## Sync and backup
Help stores nothing and does not sync. Its pages come with orOS itself, so they update when orOS updates and work offline once orOS has been opened online.
