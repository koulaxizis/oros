# The menu and quick search
The menu holds every app in orOS and the way into its settings. Tap or click `orOS` on the left of the top bar to open it.

## Open and close the menu
- Tap or click `orOS` to open the menu, and again to close it.
- Tap or click anywhere outside the menu, or press [[Esc]], to close it.
- While an app is open, the same button reads `Back` and takes you to the desktop. Open the menu from there.

## App categories
Under `Applications`, apps are grouped in categories such as `Office`, `Accessories`, `Games` or `Personal`. Categories are sorted by name in the language you use, and each one shows how many apps it holds.

- Tap or click a category to open or close it.
- The two small buttons next to `Applications` are `Expand all` and `Collapse all`.

All categories are closed every time orOS starts and every time you open an app, and the search box is cleared, so the menu always opens tidy.

## Quick search
The `Search apps and your data…` box at the top of the menu finds apps as you type.

- It looks at the app's name and at the category name. So `games` shows every game.
- Capitals and Greek accents don't matter: `ημερολογιο` finds `Ημερολόγιο`.
- Matching categories open by themselves, and the number next to each shows the matches.
- Press [[Enter]] to open the first match.
- Press [[Esc]] to clear the search. Press it again to close the menu.

If nothing matches, you'll see `No matching apps`.

You can also search from your browser's address bar. Add a site search (custom search engine) in your browser settings with your orOS address followed by `?search=%s`; orOS then opens with your words already in this box.

## Choose which apps the menu shows
Too many apps? Hide the ones you don't use. Open [Settings](app:settings) and choose `Menu`:
- Untick an app to hide it, tick it again to bring it back. `Find an app` narrows the list.
- `Hide all` empties the list, so you can then tick only the apps you want. `Show all` brings every app back.

A hidden app is still there. The quick search still finds it, links from other apps still open it, and its shortcut on the desktop or in the Dock still works. While some apps are hidden, a row under the list says how many, and tapping it opens `Menu` in Settings. Your choice travels to all your devices with [sync](help:t/sync). New apps always appear in the menu until you hide them.

## Settings in the menu
Below the apps you'll find a few rows:
- `Install orOS`: shown only when your browser can install orOS as an app. See [Install](help:t/install).
- `Settings`: opens the [Settings](app:settings) app with appearance, notifications, sync, backups, language and the [factory reset](help:t/reset). It is also in the `System` category.
- `Device info`: what this device and browser can do.
- `About orOS + shortcuts`: the version, the online services orOS uses and the [keyboard shortcuts](help:t/shortcuts).

> The time and the date in the top bar are shortcuts too: they open [Time](app:time) and [Calendar](app:calendar) directly.
