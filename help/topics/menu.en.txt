# The menu and quick search
The menu holds every app in orOS and the way into its settings. Tap or click `orOS` on the left of the top bar to open it.

## Open and close the menu
- Tap or click `orOS` to open the menu, and again to close it.
- Tap or click anywhere outside the menu, or press [[Esc]], to close it.
- While an app is open, the same button reads `Back` and takes you to the desktop. Open the menu from there.

## App categories
Under `Applications`, apps are grouped in categories such as `Office`, `Accessories`, `Games` or `Personal`. Categories are sorted by name in the language you use, and each one shows how many apps it holds.

- Tap or click a category to open or close it.
- The two small buttons next to `Applications` are `Expand all` and `Collapse all`.

All categories are closed every time orOS starts and every time you open an app, and the search box is cleared, so the menu always opens tidy.

## Quick search
The `Search apps and your data…` box at the top of the menu finds apps as you type.

- It looks at the app's name and at the category name. So `games` shows every game.
- Capitals and Greek accents don't matter: `ημερολογιο` finds `Ημερολόγιο`.
- Matching categories open by themselves, and the number next to each shows the matches.
- Press [[Enter]] to open the first match.
- Press [[Esc]] to clear the search. Press it again to close the menu.

If nothing matches, you'll see `No matching apps`.

You can also search from your browser's address bar. Add a site search (custom search engine) in your browser settings with your orOS address followed by `?search=%s`; orOS then opens with your words already in this box.

## Commands
Type what you want to do, in English or Greek, and the first row of the search shows the action. Press [[Enter]] or tap it. Nothing is saved yet: the app opens with the form filled in, and you check it and press `Save`.
- `dentist tomorrow 10`, `meeting Friday 3pm at the office`: a new event in [Calendar](app:calendar). A line with a day and a time is enough.
- `buy milk, eggs`, `bread to the shopping list`: the shopping list in [To-Do](app:todo).
- `todo call Nikos`, `remind me to pay the rent`: a new task in To-Do.
- `spent 12€ coffee`, `12€ coffee`, `paid 30 fuel yesterday`, `got 500 salary`: an expense or income in [Budget](app:budget), with the category guessed from the words.
- `note: wifi code 1234`: a new page in [Notes](app:notes).
- `a glass of water`: one glass in [Water](app:water), right away.
- `open maps`: opens that app.
- `timer 10 min pasta`, `timer 1h30m`: starts a timer in [Time](app:time) right away. `alarm 7:00`, `wake me at 6:30 every day`: fills in a new alarm, and you press `Add`.
- `card fix the door`, `fix the door to kanban`: a new card in [Kanban](app:kanban).
- `habit reading done`: ticks today in [Habits](app:habits) (tap the day again to undo). `new habit running` opens a new habit.
- `send hello world to writer`: hands the text or link to that app, like `Send to an app`.
- `12*3+4`, `√2`, `5 miles to km`, `100 usd to eur`: the answer appears in the row. Press [[Enter]] to copy it.
- `in 45 days`, `30 days ago`, `days until 25/12`, `days since 1/3/2020`, `what day is 1/1/2030`, `week number`: date answers, copied the same way.
- `time in Tokyo`, `New York time`: the time there now and how far ahead or behind it is.

In the bar, before you type, your last commands are listed; tap one to use it again. This list stays on this device only, and `Clear the list` empties it.

The same commands work in a small bar over any app: press [[Ctrl+K]] (in Notes and Wiki, [[Ctrl+Alt+Shift+K]]). In the orOS Android app, the magnifier button of the home-screen widgets opens the same bar. Anything that is not a command is a normal search, and with the [Assistant](app:assistant) installed the last row asks it. Commands are understood on your device; nothing is sent anywhere.

### Quick window: add a task or search from anywhere
In the bar, a line that is not a command shows `Add task: «…»` first: press [[Enter]] and [To-Do](app:todo) adds it at once to the list that is open there, with `Undo` for a few seconds. When an app's name is the whole line, opening that app comes first. Under the apps, `In your data` shows up to six matches from your apps, the same as the menu search, and only from the apps you have switched on for search on this device.

You can open the bar without opening orOS first:
- **Phone (orOS Android app):** long-press the orOS icon and choose `Add or search`, add the `orOS add or search` widget to the home screen, or add the `orOS Quick` tile to Quick Settings.
- **Installed web app (Android, Windows, Linux):** right-click or long-press the orOS icon and choose `Add or search`.
- **Windows and Linux (orOS installed with Setup.exe, AppImage or .deb):** the orOS icon in the system tray opens a small window with the bar. [[Ctrl+Alt+Shift+Space]] opens it on Windows. The tray menu can start orOS with the computer; it is off until you turn it on.

Escape in the small tray window closes it. Nothing you type there leaves your device; the screen lock still asks first.

## Choose which apps the menu shows
Too many apps? Hide the ones you don't use. Open [Settings](app:settings) and choose `Menu`:
- Untick an app to hide it, tick it again to bring it back. `Find an app` narrows the list.
- `Hide all` empties the list, so you can then tick only the apps you want. `Show all` brings every app back.
- `Choose a profile` ticks apps for you: `Home`, `Work / Business`, `School`, `Creating` or `Games`, one or more. `Everything` ticks every app. You can still tick or untick any app before you press `Save`.

The first time you open orOS, the same choice comes before the tour, as `What will you do with orOS?`. `Everything` is chosen already: `Continue`, the × or [[Esc]] keep every app in the menu. If you already use orOS on another device, choose `I already use orOS on another device` and connect [sync](help:t/sync) instead: your menu comes with it.

A hidden app is still there. The quick search still finds it, links from other apps still open it, and its shortcut on the desktop or in the Dock still works. While some apps are hidden, a row under the list says how many, and tapping it opens `Menu` in Settings. Your choice travels to all your devices with [sync](help:t/sync). New apps always appear in the menu until you hide them.

## Settings in the menu
Below the apps you'll find a few rows:
- `Install orOS`: shown only when your browser can install orOS as an app. See [Install](help:t/install).
- `Settings`: opens the [Settings](app:settings) app with appearance, notifications, sync, backups, language and the [factory reset](help:t/reset). It is also in the `System` category.
- `Device info`: what this device and browser can do.
- `About orOS + shortcuts`: the version, the online services orOS uses and the [keyboard shortcuts](help:t/shortcuts).

> The time and the date in the top bar are shortcuts too: they open [Time](app:time) and [Calendar](app:calendar) directly.
