# Notifications, alarms and reminders
orOS can tell you about alarms, calendar reminders, due tasks and more. This page explains where notifications appear, how to set them up, and what works while orOS is closed.

## Where notifications appear
New notifications pop up on screen for a few seconds (bottom right by default). They also land in the inbox: tap or click the bell in the top bar to open it. The number on the bell counts unread ones. Opening the menu closes the notifications, and the bell closes the menu. Tap a notification to open what it points to; the inbox closes as it opens. Or use `Clear all` to empty the list. Old notifications are removed after 7 days.

## Notification settings
Open the menu, choose `Settings` and go to `Notifications`:

- The `Notifications` switch turns all notifications on or off.
- `Send a test` shows a sample so you can check how it looks.
- `Position on screen`, `Style`, `Sound`, `Volume` and `Stays on screen` change where pop-ups appear, how they look, the sound they make (`None` by default; your melodies from [Ringtone Composer](app:ringtones) are in the list too, so reminders from Habits, Health and every other app can ring with one) and how long they stay.
- `Focus` holds the focus modes, below.
- `Which apps may notify` lets you switch notifications off for single apps. `orOS` stands for messages from orOS itself, such as sync and updates. `Weekly app suggestion` is the Monday suggestion of an app you have not opened yet ([Today](app:today)).

These settings travel with [sync](help:t/sync), so they are the same on all your devices.

## Interface sounds
In `Settings › Notifications › Interface sounds` orOS can play soft sounds when you tap a button, flip a switch or open and close apps and panels. Choose a `Sound pack` (`Pebble`, wood; `Keys`, piano; `Crystal`, bells), set the `Volume` and press `Try`. They are off at first and stay silent while Do Not Disturb or a Focus mode is on. The sounds are a few KB of free CC0 samples, downloaded once when you turn them on and then played offline. This device only.

## Focus modes
A focus mode keeps notifications quiet while you work, sleep or have time for yourself. While a mode is on, only the apps it lets through pop up and sound; every other notification still arrives in the bell's list, so nothing is lost.

- orOS has four modes: `Do Not Disturb` (lets nothing through), `Sleep`, `Work` and `Personal`, and you can make up to 10 of your own with `New mode`.
- In [Settings](app:settings) › `Notifications` › `Focus modes`, pick a mode to rename it, give it an icon, choose `Apps that may pop up`, turn it on now, or delete one of your own.
- `Sleep in quiet hours` turns the Sleep mode on by itself between the `From` and `To` hours (22:00 to 08:00 by default). Turning it off during the night keeps it off until the morning.
- `Turn on by schedule` turns a mode on by itself on the days and between the times you choose, in half hours; an end before the start runs past midnight (for example 22:30 to 01:30). Sleep keeps the quiet hours above.
- `Turn on during Calendar events` turns a mode on while a [Calendar](app:calendar) event with a start time runs: one with the label you choose, or with a word in its title (for example `Meeting`; capitals and accents do not matter). All-day events do not count.
- `Hide while it is on` hides the widgets and the desktop and Dock icons you tick while the mode runs, on every device. The menu and search still find every app, and nothing is removed: they come back when the mode ends.
- When you turn a mode off while its schedule or event runs, it stays off until that time is over, and comes back the next time.
- In Quick settings (the button at the right end of the top bar), `Focus` turns the last mode on or off with one tap. Its arrow lists every mode and sets how long: `For one hour`, `Until 8 in the morning`, `Until the schedule ends` (for a mode with a schedule) or `Until you turn it off`.
- A mode you turn on holds on all your devices that sync. The top-bar button shows the mode's icon while one is on (a crossed bell for Do Not Disturb).
- Clock alarms and ringing Calendar reminders always ring, in every mode.
- A notification that arrives quietly stays quiet: it does not pop up later when the mode ends, it waits in the bell. When a mode ends, one notice says how many waited while it ran, and from which apps (only when some did).
- `Notification summary` (below the modes) keeps the apps you tick quiet all day, also outside any mode: their notifications wait in the bell, and at each time you choose (up to 6) one notice says how many came and from where. orOS's own notices never wait. The summary holds on every device.
- With `Notifications with orOS closed` on, a notification of an app the running mode keeps quiet (or that waits for the summary) arrives without sound or vibration. Each time orOS is open it tells the browser the modes and schedules of the next 48 hours, so a mode you start on another device while this one is closed reaches it the next time orOS opens here.
- In [Automations](app:automations), `When a focus mode starts` and `When a focus mode ends` start a rule (`Only when it says` with the name of a mode picks that mode), and the step `Focus mode` turns a mode on, for some minutes or until you turn it off, or turns it off. Both work while orOS is open.

## Alarms
Set alarms in the [Time](app:time) app. When an alarm goes off, a box with `Alarm` appears at the top right of the screen and beeps. Tap `Snooze 9m` to hear it again in 9 minutes, or `Dismiss` to stop it. It stops by itself after 30 seconds and stays in your inbox.

Alarms belong to orOS, not to the Time app, so they ring while you're in any app or on the desktop. The alarm box shows even while a focus mode is on.

## Calendar reminders
In the [Calendar](app:calendar) app, choose a `Reminder` time when you add or edit an event. The reminder arrives as a notification at that time, even if the Calendar app is closed. If you open orOS after the reminder time but before the event starts, you still get it. Once the event has started, it's skipped.

Other apps send reminders too, for example tasks that are due or overdue in [To-Do](app:todo). Each one has its own switch under `Which apps may notify`.

## When orOS is closed
>! orOS runs inside your browser, so **without the switch below nothing rings or pops up while orOS is closed**. Alarms and reminders then only work while orOS is open in a tab or as an installed app, even if it's in the background.

### Notifications with orOS closed (optional)
`Settings` › `Notifications` › `Notifications with orOS closed` is **off** until you turn it on, and you turn it on separately on each device. With it on, these arrive even when orOS is closed:

- [Time](app:time) alarms and timers.
- [Calendar](app:calendar) reminders.
- [To-Do](app:todo) tasks that are due (at 09:00), [Water](app:water), [Habits](app:habits) and [Health](app:health) reminders.
- [Plant Care](app:plants), [Pet Health Book](app:petcare), [Garage](app:garage) and [Chore Wheel](app:chores) at each app's reminder hour, [Billing](app:invoices) documents and [Kanban](app:kanban) cards that are due (at 09:00), [CRM](app:crm) follow-ups and [Sticky Notes](app:stickies) at their time.

How it works: orOS keeps the next 7 days of reminders on the orOS relay, and at each one's time the relay sends it to your browser's push service, which wakes your device. The push service belongs to your browser's maker: **Google** in Chrome and on Android, **Microsoft** in Edge, **Mozilla** in Firefox. Each reminder is encrypted on your device first, so the push service and the relay see only an encrypted packet and the time it's sent, never what it says. Change or delete a reminder on any of your devices and the waiting copy changes or goes away too. Open orOS now and then so it can add the days ahead.

Good to know:
- On a computer the browser has to keep running in the background, although the orOS window itself can be closed. Chrome and Edge on Windows do this by default. On Linux and in Firefox, keep the browser open.
- Brave keeps its push service off by default: in `brave://settings/privacy` turn on `Use Google services for push messaging`, restart Brave, then turn the switch on. Reminders then pass through Google, encrypted.
- Android phones get them even with Chrome closed. In the orOS Android app this switch is hidden, because the app rings reminders itself (see below).
- The sound is your system's notification sound, not the orOS ringtone. Alarms stay on screen until you tap them.
- While orOS is on screen, it shows reminders itself, so you never get the same one twice.
- `Send a test` checks that it works: the test arrives through the push service a moment later, even with orOS open.
- Turning the switch off deletes the waiting reminders from the relay. Below the switch you see every device that has it on; `Remove` turns it off for that device too, from wherever you are.

On phones without the switch, browsers often pause pages that aren't on screen. For an alarm that really matters, use your phone's own alarm as a backup.

## With the orOS Android app
In the orOS app for Android, `Settings` › `Notifications` has `Reminders through the Android app`. Turn it on and tap `Allow`. The phone then rings these on its own, even when orOS is closed:

- [Time](app:time) alarms and timers, with the phone's alarm sound, until you dismiss them.
- [Water](app:water) reminders.
- Tasks due in [To-Do](app:todo), at 09:00 on the day, and [Habits](app:habits) and [Health](app:health) reminders.
- [Plant Care](app:plants), [Pet Health Book](app:petcare), [Garage](app:garage) and [Chore Wheel](app:chores) at each app's reminder hour, [Billing](app:invoices) documents and [Kanban](app:kanban) cards that are due (at 09:00), [CRM](app:crm) follow-ups and [Sticky Notes](app:stickies) at their time.

An app you switched off under `Which apps may notify` stays quiet on the phone too. Tap one to open orOS where it points. The app knows the next two days, so open orOS now and then. If Android says reminders may be a few minutes late, tap `Allow` again and switch on alarms for orOS. While orOS is on screen, it shows reminders itself, as before. Calendar events ring through the phone's calendar instead (see the [Calendar](app:calendar) help).

## System notifications
If your browser allows it, orOS also shows notifications through your system, so you see them while orOS is in the background. orOS asks for this permission only when you save a Calendar event with a reminder. You can also allow or block it any time in your browser's site settings. Clicking one brings orOS to the front and opens what it points to, such as the event. Without it, notifications still appear inside orOS.
