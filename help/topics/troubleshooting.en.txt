# Troubleshooting
Something not working as expected? Start here. Most problems are fixed with a reload or a quick look at the sync settings.

## An update isn't showing
orOS updates itself. It checks for a new version when it opens, every hour, and when you come back to its tab. The new version downloads in the background, and orOS reloads at a safe moment: when you're on the desktop with no app open, the menu closed and nothing left to sync. Afterwards you'll see `orOS was updated to` and the version number.

To check right now, press [[Ctrl+Alt+Shift+U]] (`Check for updates`). You'll see `Checking for updates…`. Then go back to the desktop and close the menu. `Check for updates` is also in `Settings` under `System`. You can also close orOS completely and open it again.

## Something looks broken after an update
Close every orOS tab or window and open it again, or reload the page. Your data is not affected by a reload.

If you're offline and an app shows `Offline — this app has not been saved for offline use yet.`, connect to the internet once and open that app again.

## Sync error messages
- `Not connected to Dropbox` or `Cloud sync is not connected`: connect in `Settings` under `Sync`, or press [[Ctrl+Alt+Shift+R]].
- `Enter your passphrase first`: type your passphrase in `Settings` under `Sync` and tap `Unlock`, or tap the sync dot.
- `Passphrase changed on another device?`: orOS couldn't decrypt the cloud data with the passphrase it has. Type the current passphrase. If you're sure it's right, update this device too, since an older orOS can't read data saved by a newer one.
- `Dropbox authorization failed — reconnect`: press [[Ctrl+Alt+Shift+R]], or use `Disconnect` and then `Connect Dropbox`.
- `Sync failed — check your connection`: check your internet. orOS tries again by itself.
- `Sync already running — try again shortly`: nothing is wrong. Wait a moment.
- `Cloud data uses an unsupported version`: the cloud data was saved by a different orOS version. Update all your devices.
- `A new passphrase needs at least 10 characters`: choose a longer passphrase.
- `Browser storage is full — sync paused so nothing is overwritten…`: see [Your data and files](help:t/data).

If the sync dot stays orange, your changes haven't reached the cloud yet. Tap or click the dot to try now. More in [Sync across devices](help:t/sync).

## Storage is full
Your browser gives orOS a limited amount of space. [Export a backup](help:t/backup), then delete data you no longer need. See [Your data and files](help:t/data).

## Notifications don't appear
In `Settings`, under `Notifications`, check that the `Notifications` switch is on, that the app is on under `Which apps may notify`, and that `Quiet hours` isn't on. Remember that orOS must be open for alarms and reminders to work. See [Notifications, alarms and reminders](help:t/notifications).

## Report a problem
If something is still wrong, open an issue on GitHub: [github.com/koulaxizis/oros/issues](https://github.com/koulaxizis/oros/issues). Say which browser and device you use, what you did, and what happened.

>! Never post your passphrase or a backup file. Backup files are not encrypted and contain all your data.
