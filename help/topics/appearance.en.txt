# Appearance
Make orOS look the way you like: language, light or dark theme, colour skin, wallpaper, seasonal decorations, a screen pet and the weather in the top bar. Most of these are in the [Settings](app:settings) app, under `Appearance`: open the menu and choose `Settings`. The top-bar weather is set in the Weather app.

## Language
orOS speaks English and Greek. The `EL` / `EN` button in the top bar switches to the other language, and so does [[Ctrl+Alt+Shift+L]].

If an app is open when you switch, it reopens in the new language. Your data isn't touched.

## Light or dark theme
In `Settings`, under `Appearance`, choose `Dark` or `Light` next to `Theme`. orOS starts in dark mode.

## Skins
The coloured dots under `System colour` are skins: each one gives orOS and its apps a different accent colour. They are named after Linux systems, such as `Ubuntu`, `Fedora`, `Mint` or `Pop!_OS`; hover over a dot to see its name. The default is `Oros`, in gold.

> If you still have the default wallpaper (`Desert Sand`), choosing a skin also switches to the wallpaper that goes with it. Once you pick a wallpaper yourself, skins leave it alone.

## Wallpapers
Under `Wallpaper`, tap or click a picture to make it your desktop background. You can choose from `Adwaita Dusk`, `Midnight`, `Aubergine`, `Forest Night`, `Ember`, `Nordic Frost`, `Aurora`, `Desert Sand`, `Monochrome`, `Nebula`, `Nordic Aurora`, `Hex Grid`, `Obsidian Veil` and `Retro Terrazzo`. `None` uses the plain background of your theme.

### Your own wallpaper (Mine)
The first tile, `Mine`, is a wallpaper you make yourself.
1. Until you've made one, the tile shows a **+** (`Make your own in Wallpaper Generator`). Tap or click it to open [Wallpaper Generator](app:wallpaper).
2. Create a design there and press `Set as wallpaper`.
3. It becomes your `Mine` wallpaper and you can pick it again in `Settings` any time.

Each device draws your wallpaper to fit its own screen, so it stays sharp on a phone and on a big monitor.

## Seasonal decorations
At some times of the year your desktop dresses up on its own: snow, a string of lights and a tree at Christmas, gold sparkles and a pomegranate at New Year (with fireworks from 23:55 to 00:10 on New Year's Eve), pumpkins, bats and a spider's web at Halloween.

The Greek holidays have their own:
- `Epiphany` (5-6 January): a gold cross over the sea.
- `Valentine's Day` (13-14 February): hearts floating up.
- `Carnival` (from Tsiknopempti to the Sunday before Clean Monday): confetti, coloured flags and a mask.
- `Clean Monday`: kites in the sky.
- `25 March` and `28 October` (and the day before each): blue and white flags and the Greek flag.
- `Easter` (Holy Thursday to Easter Monday): red eggs, an Easter candle, flowers and butterflies.
- `May Day` (30 April - 1 May): a flower wreath and falling petals.

Carnival, Clean Monday and Easter move every year; orOS works out their dates from the Orthodox Easter. When two events fall on the same day, one wins: 28 October over Halloween, Valentine's Day over Carnival, Easter over May Day.

### Extras you can turn on
These are off until you choose them:
- `My birthday`: balloons, flags and confetti on your birthday. Pick the day and month under the event; nothing shows until you do. It wins over any other event that day.
- `Seasons`: petals and tulips in `Spring`, the sun and waves in `Summer`, falling leaves in `Autumn`, light snow in `Winter`. A holiday always wins over its season.
- `Easter date`: `Orthodox` (the default) or `Western`, for those who keep Easter on the Western date. It moves only Easter; Carnival and Clean Monday stay Orthodox.

The decorations sit behind your desktop icons, never take a click and stop moving while an app is open.

They are on from the start. In `Settings`, under `Appearance` › `Seasonal decorations`:
- `Decorate the desktop in season` turns them all off or on again.
- `Motion`: `Moving`, or `Still only` for the decorations without snow or anything that moves.
- `Amount`: `A little`, `Normal` or `Lots` of snowflakes and sparkles.
- `At Christmas`: a `Tree` or the Greek Christmas `Boat`.
- `Hide for today` appears while an event is on; `Show again` brings it back.
- Under `Events`, each event shows its next dates and has its own switch. The list starts with what is on now or comes next.

### Try one before its time
Press `Try it now` next to an event. Settings closes and the decoration appears on your desktop with a small bar:
1. `Keep it` keeps it until the event's period ends (for Halloween, until 31 October).
2. `Stop` takes it away, and your desktop follows the calendar again.

A kept decoration shows in `Settings` with its own `Stop` button.

If your device asks for less motion, or the battery is low, only the still pieces show. Slow devices get fewer snowflakes on their own.

## Screen pet
In `Settings`, under `Appearance`, the `Screen pet` switch turns on a tiny companion that lives on your desktop. Feed it, pet it and let it sleep. It never dies: if you forget it for a while, it just gets grumpy. You can do more with your pet in [Pet World](app:petworld).

## Weather in the top bar
A small icon with the current temperature shows in the top bar from the start. To change it, open [Weather](app:weather), then the `Cities` dialog, and look under `Taskbar`:
1. `Show the weather in the taskbar` shows or hides it on this device.
2. The pin next to a city picks the city it shows. With no city pinned, it shows your first city.

Tap or click the icon to open [Weather](app:weather); inside Weather, it takes you back to the desktop.

When you are offline, or the reading is more than 3 hours old, the icon shows a crossed-out cloud and no temperature. orOS never shows a temperature it doesn't have. Weather data comes from Open-Meteo (see [Privacy](help:t/privacy)).

## Dock
The Dock is a bar of your favourite apps along the bottom of the desktop. Add an app with the ☆ next to it in the menu. In `Settings`, under `Appearance` › `Dock`:
- `Show the Dock` turns it on or off.
- `Size` is `Small`, `Medium` or `Large`.
- `Style` changes its look: `Classic`, `Glass`, `Frosted`, `Smoke`, `Transparent`, `Solid`, `3D shelf` or `Neon`.
- `Magnify on hover` makes icons grow under the mouse (only with a mouse or trackpad).
- `Hide automatically` tucks it away until you move the mouse to the bottom edge, or tap the small handle on a touch screen.
- `Show over open apps` keeps it visible while an app is open. It is off until you turn it on, so apps get the whole screen.

## What follows you to other devices
With [sync](help:t/sync) on, your language, theme, skin, wallpaper (including `Mine`) and the pinned weather city go to your other devices too.

The seasonal decoration settings go too. The Dock and its settings, a decoration you are trying or kept, and `Hide for today` stay on this device.
