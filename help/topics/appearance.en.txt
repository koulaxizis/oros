# Appearance
Make orOS look the way you like: language, light or dark theme, colour skin, wallpaper, seasonal decorations, a screen pet and the weather in the top bar. Most of these are in the [Settings](app:settings) app, under `Appearance`: open the menu and choose `Settings`. The top-bar weather is set in the Weather app.

`Make it yours`, the first card of `Appearance`, puts the look of orOS in one place with a small preview of the desktop: `Choose a wallpaper` (it takes you to the `Wallpaper` card below, the one place to pick it), `Choose a colour` (it takes you to `System colour`, with `Accent from wallpaper`), the look of the top bar, the Dock and its edge, the motion feel, interface sounds and the look of the lock screen. Each choice is the same setting as in its own place further down (and in `Screen lock`), so changing it either way keeps both in step.

## Quick settings
The button at the right end of the top bar (the network and power symbols) opens Quick settings, as on a Linux desktop. [[Ctrl+Alt+Shift+M]] opens it too.

- The round buttons at the top: the battery (on a laptop or phone running on it), the language, `Settings`, `Lock orOS` (when the screen lock is on) and the power button with `Restart orOS`. On orOS USB the power button also restarts or shuts down the computer.
- `Network` shows whether you are online. `Sync` shows its state; tap it to sync now, or the arrow for the sync settings.
- `Dark style`, `Large text`, `High contrast` and `On-screen keyboard` switch on and off with one tap. Settings has the full choices for each.
- `Focus` keeps notifications quiet: they still arrive in the bell's list, without a pop-up or a sound, apart from the apps the mode lets through. One tap turns the last mode on or off (`Do Not Disturb` at first); the arrow lists the modes (`Do Not Disturb`, `Sleep`, `Work`, `Personal` and your own) and sets how long: `For one hour`, `Until 8 in the morning`, `Until the schedule ends` or `Until you turn it off`. A mode holds on all your devices, and the top-bar button shows its icon while one is on. See [Focus modes](help:t/notifications).
- `Keep awake` keeps the screen on while orOS is on screen: one press until you turn it off, the arrow gives `For one hour`, `For two hours` or `Until you turn it off`. On orOS USB it also keeps the screen from going dark and the computer from sleeping. A cup on the top-bar button shows it is on.
- `Energy saver` stops what moves on the desktop (live wallpaper, Living land, the Light show, the weather desktop, seasonal decorations, the opening and closing animation of apps) and refreshes widgets once a minute. It turns on by itself when the battery falls under 20 % and is not charging, and off again on the charger; turned off on a low battery, it stays off until the next charge. Sync, notifications, alarms, timers and sound never wait for it. A leaf on the top-bar button shows it is on. Both are set on this device only.
- `Playing` lists the music, podcast, radio station or Sound Mixer mix that plays, with Play / Pause; tap its name to open the app.
- `Screenshot` takes a picture of the whole screen with one tap; its arrow offers `Whole screen` or `Region`. Screen Capture opens with the picture, and you save it to Files › Pictures. See [Screen Capture](help:a/capture).
- The `Search` field at the top hands what you type to the menu's search: apps, files and settings. With a mouse and keyboard the panel opens with the cursor in it; on a touch screen the keyboard stays closed until you tap it.
- On orOS USB, the panel also has the volume and brightness, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth.

On a narrow screen, when the top bar is full, the language button moves into Quick settings.

## Night Light
Night Light makes the screen warmer in the evening, with less blue light, which is easier on the eyes and on sleep. Turn it on in `Settings`, under `Appearance`, with `Warm screen at night`, then choose `When`:

- `Sunset to sunrise`: from this evening's sunset to the next sunrise where you are. orOS works the times out itself, without the internet, from the place of the top-bar weather (the first city of the [Weather](app:weather) app). Without a city it runs from 20:00 to 07:00.
- `Set hours`: from and to the hours you pick, in half hours.
- `Always`: warm all the time.

`Warmth` sets how warm it gets. The screen turns warm slowly, over 20 minutes, and goes back the same way. In Quick settings, the `Night Light` button turns it on now (until the next morning or the end of your hours) or off until the next evening; its arrow opens the settings. It is set on this device only.

On orOS USB the computer changes the screen's own colours, so the Internet window and Tor Browser turn warm too. In a browser or orOS Portable, orOS tints its own window: a video in full screen inside an app shows its own colours.

## Desktop widgets
Cards on the desktop that show your day at a glance: `Clock`, `Calendar` (a month; tap a day to open it in the Calendar), `Today` (what is late and what is due today, from the [Today](app:today) summary; tap a line to open it), `Focus timer` (the Pomodoro of [Time](app:time): Focus or Break, the minutes left, Start, Pause and Resume, Skip and 5 minutes more or less; Time and this card always show the same leg), `Weather` (the place of the top-bar weather), `Playing` (what plays now, large: its cover, title, progress, Previous / Play-Pause / Next and a small light that moves with the sound; other players under it with Play / Pause; music, podcasts, radio or the Sound Mixer) and `Sync` (its state and Sync now).

- On a computer they sit in a column at the right of the desktop; on a phone or a narrow window, in a row at the top that scrolls sideways.
- Right-click an empty spot of the desktop and choose `Widgets and cards…` to open the gallery: every desktop card and every top bar card, each with a live preview of what it would show now. Search by name, show `All`, `Desktop` or `Top bar`, press `Add to desktop` or `Add to top bar` (or `Remove`), and set the order with the arrows. It is the one place for them: `Settings`, under `Appearance`, opens the same gallery with its `Widgets and cards…` button.
- Right-click a card (long press on a touch screen) or use its `⋯` button to move it up or down or remove it. With a mouse you can also drag a card by its title.
- The Clock card's menu also sets its style: `Clock: digits`, `Clock: LED dots` or `Clock: automatic` (the start: LED dots with the Signal skin, digits with the others). The dots are drawn by orOS itself and never blink; with high contrast only the lit dots show.
- A computer starts with Clock, Calendar, Today and Weather; a phone starts with none. Your choice stays on this device.

### First steps
When you start orOS for the first time, a `First steps` card waits on the desktop: write your first note, add a task, choose a wallpaper or a Living land, pick an icon pack, turn on sync, meet the Assistant and, where your browser offers it, install orOS as an app. Tap a line to go there. Each line ticks itself once you have really done it, so there is nothing to tick by hand. `Hide` takes the card away; when everything is done it says so and leaves the next day. You can bring it back with `Widgets and cards…`.

On a computer, First steps also has `Use orOS on your phone too`. It adds an `On your phone` card with a QR code: scan it with the phone's camera to open orOS there. The code holds only the address of orOS (this site, or useoros.online when orOS runs from a local copy), never your data. To see the same things on both, turn on sync on each device with the same passphrase. The card can be removed like any widget.

## Smart top bar
The top bar can show a few live items next to the clock. Add them and set their order in the gallery: `Widgets and cards…` in `Settings › Appearance`, or on the desktop's right-click menu. None is on at first, and your choice stays on this device.
- `Next event`: the next timed item of today from [Today](app:today) (your Calendar events and the other apps that give Today a time). From an hour before, it counts down ("in 20 min"); while it runs it says `Now`. Tap it for this month and today's items at a glance: tap a day to open it in the Calendar, a line to open it, or `Open Calendar`.
- `Focus timer`: the minutes left of the Pomodoro running in [Time](app:time), focus or break.
- `Tasks for today`: how many tasks are late or due today.
- `Water today`: what you drank against your goal in [Water](app:water).
- `Spent this month`: this month's expenses in [Budget](app:budget).
- `Till sales today`: today's sales at the till in [Stock](app:stock).
- `Habits left today`, `Plants to look after`, `Chores for today` and `Kanban cards due`: how many are for today or late in [Habits](app:habits), [Plant Care](app:plants), [Chore Wheel](app:chores) and [Kanban](app:kanban).
- `Timesheet timer`: the time on the [Timesheet](app:timesheet) timer while it runs, and its project.
- `Overdue invoices`: how many invoices and bills in [Billing](app:invoices) are past their day and unpaid.
- `Next trip`: in how many days your next trip in [Travel](app:travel) starts (up to a month ahead).
- `Today's meals`: today's meals from your plan in [Meal Planner](app:meals).
- `The book you are reading`: the book open in [Library](app:library) and how far you are.
- `AI use this month (your own key)`: how many tokens the [Assistant](app:assistant) used this month with your own keys (Claude, OpenAI and the other services), and how many of Tavily's 1,000 free searches a month it used on this device; it turns to the accent colour from 800. Local models (on your computer or in the browser) never count. Only numbers, never what you asked.
- `Battery`: the battery's percent, and `charging` while it charges or `low` from 20 % down, on a laptop, tablet or phone whose browser reports it (Chrome, Edge and the orOS USB do; Firefox and Safari do not). A computer on mains power shows nothing. Tap it to open Quick settings.
- `Now playing`: what [Music](app:music), [Podcasts](app:podcasts), [Radio](app:radio) or the [Sound Mixer](app:mixer) plays, while it plays. Tap it for a small player: Previous, Play / Pause, Next and `Open` the app.

Live pills need no ticking: while a timer or the stopwatch of [Time](app:time) runs, or [Screen Capture](app:capture) records, a pill with its time shows at the start of the bar (the timer that ends first, with "+1" when another one runs too). Tap it to open the app; the button beside it pauses the timer or the stopwatch (resume it in Time) or stops the recording, which then waits in Screen Capture to be saved. On a phone the first live pill stays in the bar and the others go behind the button with the cards. `Live pills` in `Top bar` turns them off on this device.

Each item shows only when it has something to say, and a tap opens its app. On a phone they all sit behind one button with a small stack of cards and their number: tap it and they unfold under the bar, each with its full text. On a wider screen they stay in the bar; when it is full, the last ones move into the same button (it then reads `+2` and so on). Tap an item in the list to open its app; `Esc` or a tap elsewhere folds it again. While `Sensitive work` is on, the money items (and overdue invoices), meals, the book and the names of events and trips are hidden. Events of apps kept out of search (Health, Cycle, One-Sentence Diary and the like) never appear in the bar.

The same figures can start a rule in [Automations](app:automations): a few minutes before an event (for example, pause the music), or when the till sales or the month's spending reach an amount (a chime and a notice).

Under `Look of the bar` you can float the bar as a rounded strip: `Glass` lets the colours behind it through, `Midnight` stays dark even in a light theme, `Solid` follows your theme. `Classic` is the bar as it always was. High contrast always keeps the classic bar.

Under `Where the cards sit` each card you use can sit on the `Left` (after the menu button), in the `Centre` or on the `Right` of the bar, on wide windows. On a phone and in the Dock the cards stay together. The look `Islands` shows the left side, the centre and the right side as separate floating groups.

On Windows, with orOS installed, the tray icon's menu has `Smart bar on the desktop`: the same bar, with your chips and look, stays at the top of the screen even when the orOS window is closed, and a tap opens that app in orOS.

## Greeting at the start
The first time you start orOS each day, a short greeting appears over the desktop: good morning, afternoon or evening (with your name, if you set one under `Your name`), the date and, when [Today](app:today) has something, how many things are due and late. Tap that line to open Today. The greeting goes by itself after a few seconds, or when you tap anywhere, press a key or open an app. It never shows over an app or on your very first day. Turn it off in `Settings`, under `Appearance`, with `Greet me when orOS starts`; this stays on this device.

### A tip when you open an app
If you are new to orOS, the first time you open each app a short tip from its guide appears: at the bottom of the screen on a computer, under the top bar on a phone. `More in Help` opens that app's full guide, `×` closes it, and it also goes once you start using the app. Each app shows its tip once. `Show a tip the first time I open an app`, in `Settings` under `Appearance`, turns the tips on or off; turning them on again shows every app's tip again. This stays on this device. Games have their own first-game tours instead.

### An example in an empty app
When To-Do, Kanban, Bookmarks, Billing, Contacts, Budget, Notes or Meal Planner has nothing in it yet, its empty screen also has `Show me an example`. It shows a few made-up items, so you can see what the app looks like in use. The example is only a picture: nothing is saved, nothing syncs and your data is not touched. `Start my own` closes it and starts your first item; `Close` just closes it.

To-Do and Kanban also offer a few ready lists under `Or start from a template:`. In To-Do, `Packing list` or `Moving house` makes a new list with its tasks already in it; in Kanban, `Trip`, `Moving house` or `Personal` makes a board with its columns and a few cards. These are real items you can change or delete, and `Undo` takes them back.

## Language
orOS speaks English and Greek. The `EL` / `EN` button in the top bar switches to the other language, and so does [[Ctrl+Alt+Shift+L]].

If an app is open when you switch, it reopens in the new language. Your data isn't touched.

## On-screen keyboard
On a touch screen without a keyboard of its own (orOS USB on a touch laptop or tablet PC, a touch PC, a kiosk) orOS has its own keyboard at the bottom of the screen. It types into the text field you are in, in the shell and in every app, and the app moves up so the field stays in sight. It never shows on phones: they have their own.

- It opens when you tap a text field with a finger or a pen. A mouse click does not open it, and while you type on a real keyboard it stays away (a real key press closes it).
- The keyboard button in the top bar shows or hides it at any time. With no text field in use, its keys go to the app as key presses, for games.
- `⇧` gives one capital letter; tap it twice for Caps Lock. `?123` and `=\<` show numbers and symbols, `abc` the letters again. `⌫` deletes (hold it to keep deleting), `↵` is Enter, the arrows move the cursor.
- The key next to `?123` (`EN`, `ΕΛ`, `DE`) goes to the next layout. In Greek, `΄` puts the accent on the next vowel (`΄` then `α` gives `ά`) and `⇧` `¨` the diaeresis (`ϊ`, `ϋ`).
- In `Settings`, under `Language & search`, `On-screen keyboard` sets when it shows (`When I tap a text field`, `Only from its button` or `Never`) and which layouts it has: English, Greek and German. These choices stay on this device.

## Light or dark theme
In `Settings`, under `Appearance`, choose `Dark` or `Light` next to `Theme`. orOS starts in dark mode.

## Skins
The coloured dots under `System colour` are skins: each one gives orOS and its apps a different accent colour. They are named after Linux systems, such as `Ubuntu`, `Fedora`, `Mint` or `Pop!_OS`; hover over a dot to see its name. The default is `Oros`, in gold.

`Signal`, the last dot, is not named after a system: it gives orOS the look of an LED dot display, black with white text and a red light (in the light theme, white paper with a deep red). Its wallpaper is `Dot Matrix`, a fine grid of dots, and the Clock widget shows the time in LED dots while Signal is on.

`Accent from wallpaper`, under the dots, takes the accent colour from your wallpaper instead: the built-in wallpapers, `Living land`, `Mine` and a picture of your own. orOS adjusts its brightness so text on buttons and links stays easy to read in the light and the dark theme; backgrounds, panels and text keep the colours of your skin. When the wallpaper has several clear colours, up to four appear under `Colours from the wallpaper` to pick from; a new wallpaper starts at its strongest colour. Grey wallpapers such as `Monochrome` keep the skin's colour, and high contrast always keeps its own colours. The switch is off at first and works on this device only.

Above the wallpapers, `Find a wallpaper` narrows them by name and `Random` puts on another drawn wallpaper. Under them, `From a folder` makes the pictures of a folder in Files (at first `Pictures/Wallpapers`) take turns as the wallpaper of this device: `Every day` or `At each start`, in name order, so each comes round before one returns; `Next picture now` skips ahead. JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF and AVIF up to 20 MB count. The pictures stay on this device and nothing syncs; picking a wallpaper by hand turns it off.

> Every skin comes with a suggested wallpaper, marked with a ★ under `Wallpaper`. Choosing a skin also switches to its wallpaper, as long as you still have the default wallpaper (`Desert Sand`) or the one suggested for your previous skin. Once you pick another wallpaper yourself, skins leave it alone, and you can still choose any wallpaper with any skin.

## Wallpapers
Under `Wallpaper`, tap or click a picture to make it your desktop background. You can choose from `Adwaita Dusk`, `Midnight`, `Aubergine`, `Forest Night`, `Ember`, `Nordic Frost`, `Aurora`, `Desert Sand`, `Monochrome`, `Nebula`, `Nordic Aurora`, `Hex Grid`, `Obsidian Veil` and `Retro Terrazzo`. `None` uses the plain background of your theme.

### Your own wallpaper (Mine)
The first tile, `Mine`, is a wallpaper you make yourself.
1. Until you've made one, the tile shows a **+** (`Make your own in Wallpaper Generator`). Tap or click it to open [Wallpaper Generator](app:wallpaper).
2. Create a design there and press `Set as wallpaper`.
3. It becomes your `Mine` wallpaper and you can pick it again in `Settings` any time.

Each device draws your wallpaper to fit its own screen, so it stays sharp on a phone and on a big monitor.

### Icon packs
Settings › Appearance › `Icons` changes how app icons look on the desktop, in the Dock, the menu and search. `Classic` is the orOS look. `Colourful` gives every menu category its own colour, `Colour mix` gives the icons different colours from each other (on the desktop and in the Dock they follow their place, so neighbours always differ), `Rainbow` puts each icon on a dark tile with a rainbow rim and a glyph in a bright colour that runs through the spectrum, `Glass` makes see-through frosted tiles, `Thin` drops the tiles for a fine line, `Paper` puts a dark ink icon on a cream card, and `Round` uses circles in your system colour. The pack you pick is the same on all your devices. High contrast always shows `Classic`. `Mix your own` lets you build your own pack: pick a `Shape` (`Square`, `Rounded` or `Circle`), a `Fill` (`Accent`, `Colour mix`, `By category`, `Dark`, `Light` or `Outline`) and the `Lines` of the glyph (`Thin`, `Normal` or `Bold`); its preview shows your mix.

### Living land
`Living land` (the second tile) draws a small landscape around what is on your desktop: your shortcuts, widgets and sticky notes stand on islands, and the gaps between them become water. Add or remove a shortcut, move a sticky note or change your widgets, and the land re-forms and fades in. Its colours follow your skin and your light or dark theme.
- Under the wallpapers, `Style` picks `Islands` (mostly water), `Hills` (meadows rising to terraces, with a winding river) or `Garden` (a lawn with flowers, hedges and ponds), and `New shape` draws another land. Your choice and its shape follow you to your other devices; every device draws the land for its own screen.
- The land is still: it only changes when your desktop does, so it costs no battery while you work. With reduced motion it changes without fading.
- `Gentle motion` (off at first) adds glints on the water and a drifting cloud shadow. It stops while an app is open, with reduced motion, on a low battery (under 20 % and not charging) and on a device too slow for it.
- `Light of the day` (on at first) colours the land with the time on this device: a deep blue at night, rose at dawn, clear by day and golden in the evening, changing slowly through the day. It is drawn once over the land and costs no battery.
- High contrast shows the plain desktop instead.

### Light show
`Light show` (the third tile) makes the desktop move with what plays in Music, Podcasts or Radio: the same light as their full-screen Light show, softer, behind your shortcuts and widgets. Under the wallpapers, `Light style` picks `Glow`, `Rings`, `Bars` or `Wave`; the Wallpaper Generator has it too, as its own tab.
- Songs on this device move it with their real sound. A station or an episode whose server does not let orOS listen moves it calmly on its own; orOS never restarts a stream for the wallpaper.
- With nothing playing it rests as a still picture and costs no battery. It stops while an app is open, and stays still with reduced motion, Energy saver, a low battery (under 20 % and not charging) or a device too slow for it. High contrast shows the plain desktop instead.
- The style follows you to your other devices. The little light in the `Playing` card works the same way; `Hide the light` in the card's menu takes it away on this device.

### Weather on the desktop
Settings › Wallpaper › `Weather on the desktop` (off at first, on this device only) shows the weather over any wallpaper: sun or stars when it is clear, a few clouds, overcast, fog, gentle rain, snow, or a storm with heavier rain under darker clouds (never a flash). It follows the weather of your [Weather](app:weather) city, the same as the top bar. With no weather yet, or weather older than three hours, nothing shows. It stops while an app is open, and stays still with reduced motion, on a low battery (under 20 % and not charging) and on a device too slow for it. High contrast hides it.

## Seasonal decorations
At some times of the year your desktop dresses up on its own: snow, a string of lights and a tree at Christmas, gold sparkles and a pomegranate at New Year (with fireworks from 23:55 to 00:10 on New Year's Eve), pumpkins, bats and a spider's web at Halloween.

The Greek holidays have their own:
- `Epiphany` (5-6 January): a gold cross over the sea.
- `Valentine's Day` (13-14 February): hearts floating up.
- `Carnival` (from Tsiknopempti to the Sunday before Clean Monday): confetti, coloured flags and a mask.
- `Clean Monday`: kites in the sky.
- `25 March` and `28 October` (and the day before each): blue and white flags and the Greek flag.
- `Easter` (Holy Thursday to Easter Monday): red eggs, an Easter candle, flowers and butterflies.
- `May Day` (30 April - 1 May): a flower wreath and falling petals.

Carnival, Clean Monday and Easter move every year; orOS works out their dates from the Orthodox Easter. When two events fall on the same day, one wins: 28 October over Halloween, Valentine's Day over Carnival, Easter over May Day.

### Extras you can turn on
These are off until you choose them:
- `My birthday`: balloons, flags and confetti on your birthday. Pick the day and month under the event; nothing shows until you do. It wins over any other event that day.
- `Seasons`: petals and tulips in `Spring`, the sun and waves in `Summer`, falling leaves in `Autumn`, light snow in `Winter`. A holiday always wins over its season.
- `Easter date`: `Orthodox` (the default) or `Western`, for those who keep Easter on the Western date. It moves only Easter; Carnival and Clean Monday stay Orthodox.

The decorations sit behind your desktop icons, never take a click and stop moving while an app is open.

They are on from the start. In `Settings`, under `Appearance` › `Seasonal decorations`:
- `Decorate the desktop in season` turns them all off or on again. While it is off, the options below it and the list of events are hidden and a decoration you are trying or kept stops; your choices stay and come back when you turn it on again.
- `Motion`: `Moving`, or `Still only` for the decorations without snow or anything that moves.
- `Amount`: `A little`, `Normal` or `Lots` of snowflakes and sparkles.
- `At Christmas`: a `Tree` or the Greek Christmas `Boat`.
- `Hide for today` appears while an event is on; `Show again` brings it back.
- Under `Events`, each event shows its next dates and has its own switch. The list starts with what is on now or comes next.

### Try one before its time
Press `Try it now` next to an event. Settings closes and the decoration appears on your desktop with a small bar:
1. `Keep it` keeps it until the event's period ends (for Halloween, until 31 October).
2. `Stop` takes it away, and your desktop follows the calendar again.

A kept decoration shows in `Settings` with its own `Stop` button.

If your device asks for less motion, or the battery is low, only the still pieces show. Autumn and Winter are made only of falling leaves and snow, so they then show as a still picture of them. Slow devices get fewer snowflakes on their own; a device that stays too slow shows still pieces, and changing `Motion` or `Amount` tries moving again.

## Screen pet
In `Settings`, under `Appearance`, the `Screen pet` switch turns on a tiny companion that lives on your desktop. Feed it, pet it and let it sleep. It never dies: if you forget it for a while, it just gets grumpy. You can do more with your pet in [Pet World](app:petworld).

## Weather in the top bar
A small icon with the current temperature shows in the top bar from the start. To change it, open [Weather](app:weather), then the `Cities` dialog, and look under `Taskbar`:
1. `Show the weather in the taskbar` shows or hides it on this device.
2. The pin next to a city picks the city it shows. With no city pinned, it shows your first city.

Tap or click the icon for the next seven days at a glance (each day's high and low, and the chance of rain from 10 %), with `Open Weather` at the end; before the first forecast has arrived it opens [Weather](app:weather) straight away. Inside Weather, it takes you back to the desktop.

When you are offline, or the reading is more than 3 hours old, the icon shows a crossed-out cloud and no temperature. orOS never shows a temperature it doesn't have. Weather data comes from Open-Meteo (see [Privacy](help:t/privacy)).

## Dock
The Dock is a bar of your favourite apps along an edge of the desktop, at the bottom unless you choose another. Add an app with the ☆ next to it in the menu. In `Settings`, under `Appearance` › `Dock`:
- `Show the Dock` turns it on or off.
- `Size` is `Small`, `Medium` or `Large`.
- `Position` puts it at the `Bottom`, `Left`, `Right` or `Top` of the screen. On a phone or a small window it stays at the bottom; it goes back to your choice on a larger screen. At the side, a long Dock scrolls up and down.
- `Style` changes its look: `Classic`, `Glass`, `Frosted`, `Smoke`, `Transparent`, `Solid`, `3D shelf` or `Neon`.
- `Magnify on hover` makes icons grow under the mouse (only with a mouse or trackpad).
- `Hide automatically` tucks it away until you move the mouse to its edge of the screen, or tap the small handle on a touch screen.
- `Show over open apps` keeps it visible while an app is open. It is off until you turn it on, so apps get the whole screen.

## Motion feel
In `Settings › Appearance`, `Animate apps opening and closing` lets an app grow out of its icon and shrink back into it. `Motion feel` sets how apps, menus and panels move: `Calm` (slower and soft), `Lively` (as before) or `Bouncy` (a little spring). Reduce motion, in Accessibility or on your system, always wins. This device only.

## Accessibility
In `Settings` › `Accessibility`, for orOS and every app:
- `Text size` makes text and everything around it larger: `100%`, `115%`, `130%` or `150%`. It works in every app, in Settings, in Help and in the menu, like zooming the page; the top bar and the desktop keep their size (the Dock has its own `Size`). On a narrow screen orOS goes only as far as every app still fits (at least 320 pixels across), and the card says so.
- `High contrast` switches orOS and the apps to black and white with one strong colour, clear borders, underlined links and a plain desktop without a wallpaper. `Automatic` (the default) turns it on while your device asks for more contrast, for example with Windows high contrast; `On` and `Off` decide yourself. When Windows forces its own contrast colours, orOS follows them and marks chosen options with an outline.
- `Always show the focus ring` draws a thick outline around whatever has the keyboard focus when you move with the [[Tab]] key, also in apps that show only a faint one.
- `Colour filter` changes the colours of the whole screen: `Grey`, `Red-green, red weak (protanopia)`, `Red-green, green weak (deuteranopia)`, `Blue-yellow (tritanopia)` or `Invert colours`. The red-green and blue-yellow filters move colours that are hard to tell apart further from each other; `Strength` (25% to 100%) sets how much, also for `Grey`. The coloured strip under the list shows the result at once. `Invert colours` turns light screens dark but keeps photos, videos and drawings as they are in the orOS apps (pages from other sites, such as map tiles, turn round completely). The files you save and export keep their real colours; a screenshot or a screen recording shows the screen as you see it. The `Colour filter` button in Quick settings turns the last filter you chose on and off; its arrow opens these settings.
- `Pointer size` (only with a mouse or touchpad): `Normal`, `Large` or `Extra large`, in orOS and every app. Chrome and Edge hide `Extra large` while it touches the edge of the window. Games and drawing tools that have their own pointer keep it.
- `Reduce motion` stops animations and moving effects in orOS and every app: the live wallpaper, seasonal decorations, apps opening out of their icon, sliding panels. Games still play. When your device already asks for less motion (for example Windows `Animation effects` off, or Android `Remove animations`), orOS does this anyway and the card says so.
- `Reduce transparency` gives the top bar, the Dock, the menu, widgets and the apps' panels a solid background instead of a see-through, blurred one, so text is easier to read on a busy wallpaper. It is also on while your device asks for less transparency.

These stay on this device: a phone and a large screen often need a different size.

## Your name
In Settings › Appearance › `Your name` you can type what orOS should call you. It is empty at first. With a name, [Today](app:today) and the Start page greet you with it, for example `Good evening, Maria`. The name stays on your devices: with [sync](help:t/sync) on it goes to your other devices encrypted, like your other settings. It never goes to the [Assistant](app:assistant) or anywhere else. Empty the field to remove it.

## The Start page
The Start page is a home page for the web: the time, a search field, your favourites from [Bookmarks](app:bookmarks) and a link to orOS. Open it at [useoros.online/startpage/](https://useoros.online/startpage/) and make it your browser's home page, on a phone too.
- Type words and press [[Enter]] to search with DuckDuckGo, or type an address such as `wikipedia.org` to go there.
- Start with `oros ` (for example `oros dentist`) to search your apps and data in orOS.
- Your favourites are the bookmarks with a star in Bookmarks. `All bookmarks` opens the app.
- On orOS Portable and the orOS USB, `New Tor window` opens Tor Browser for private browsing.
- With the [screen lock](help:t/privacy) on, the page shows neither your name nor your favourites.

The page only reads what orOS already keeps on this device. Nothing you type goes anywhere until you search, and then only to DuckDuckGo.

## What follows you to other devices
With [sync](help:t/sync) on, your language, theme, skin, wallpaper (including `Mine`), your name and the pinned weather city go to your other devices too.

The seasonal decoration settings go too. The Dock and its settings, the accessibility options, a decoration you are trying or kept, and `Hide for today` stay on this device.
