# Send to someone
Send one thing from orOS to another person: a To-Do list, a note, a contact, an event, a recipe, some bookmarks or a Wiki article. orOS puts it inside a link. You send the link any way you like, and the other person opens it in their browser, even if they don't use orOS.

## Send something
Look for the send button in these apps:
- [To-Do](app:todo): the paper-plane button above the list sends the open list.
- [Notes](app:notes): the paper-plane button next to the page title, or `Send to someone…` in the page menu.
- [Contacts](app:contacts): `Share` on a contact's card.
- [Calendar](app:calendar): `Send…` in a saved event, and `More` › `Share free times…` for a link where someone picks one of your free times and emails you back.
- [Meal Planner](app:meals): `Share` on an open recipe.
- [Bookmarks](app:bookmarks): `Send to someone…` in a bookmark's menu, or select several and tap `Send…`.
- [Wiki](app:wiki): `Send to someone…` in an article's ⋯ menu. The text travels; pictures stay with you.

The `Send to someone` window shows what will travel. Then pick how to send it:
- `Share…` opens your phone's or computer's share menu (Viber, WhatsApp, Signal, email and so on).
- `Copy link` copies it so you can paste it anywhere.
- `QR code` shows a code the other person can scan from your screen.
- `Email` writes a message in [Mail](app:mail) with the link (when you have a mail account set up).
- `Save as file` saves a small text file with the link, to attach anywhere.
- `Send as text` (contacts and recipes) sends plain text, as before.

## Lock it with a password
Tick `Lock with a password` and type a password of at least 6 characters. Then the link alone shows nothing: the other person needs the password too. Tell it to them another way, in person or by phone, not in the same message as the link.

## With an expiry, and taking it back
Tick `With an expiry` and pick `1 day`, `7 days` or `30 days`. Then orOS encrypts the item on your device and keeps it on the orOS server until that day. The link holds the key, so the server cannot read what it keeps. When the day comes, the item is deleted and the link no longer opens.

You can take a link back sooner. Open `My shared links`: it is a button in the `Send to someone` window, and also in [Settings](app:settings) › `System`. Each link shows when it expires. `Copy link` copies it again, and `Take back` deletes it from the server at once. The list follows you to your other devices through sync, so you can take a link back from any of them.

Items too long for a plain link (a long note or Wiki article) always go this way: the window turns `With an expiry` on by itself and tells you why. A password works with an expiry too.

## Open something you got
Tap the link. A page opens with the item and these buttons:
- `Add to my orOS` opens the right app with the item ready. To-Do, Contacts, Calendar, Meal Planner and Bookmarks show it to you before anything is saved; Notes makes a new page; Wiki opens the article in its editor, and it is saved when you press `Save`.
- `Copy text` copies it as plain text.
- `Download` saves it as a file other programs open too: a contact as .vcf, an event as .ics, bookmarks as .html, a Wiki article as .md, the rest as .txt.

If you got a file instead of a link, open the link inside it, or use `Open a file` on that page.

## Send to another app of yours
`Send to an app…` hands something to another app of your own orOS, like the share menu of a phone. Nothing leaves your device.
- [Writer](app:writer): the arrow button in the toolbar sends the selected text, or the whole document.
- [Notes](app:notes): `Send to an app…` in the page menu sends the page, or the text you selected in it.
- [Wiki](app:wiki): `Send to an app…` in an article's ⋯ menu.
- [Mail](app:mail): `Another app…` among a message's apps.
- [Bookmarks](app:bookmarks): `Send to an app…` in a bookmark's menu.
- [Reader](app:feeds): `Another app…` when you send an article.

The window lists only the apps that can take it: text goes to Notes, To-Do (one task per line), Writer, Mail, Wiki, the Assistant, Text Tools, Case Converter and, for one short line, Label Maker; a link also goes to Bookmarks. The app opens with it filled in, and nothing is saved until you save it there. `Send to someone…` in the same window makes a link for another person, as described above.

## Privacy
A plain link carries the item in the part after the # sign, which browsers never send to a server. No orOS server sees it and nothing is stored anywhere except in the link.

A link with an expiry carries only the key after the # sign. The orOS server keeps the encrypted item until it expires and never sees the key, the title or what is inside.

>! Anyone who has the link can open it, unless it is locked with a password. A plain link cannot be taken back and does not expire; use `With an expiry` when you may want to take it back.

## Limits
- Very long items go as a link with an expiry, which needs a connection to the orOS server when you send and when the other person opens it.
- A link with an expiry lasts 30 days at most.
- A QR code holds only short links.
- Photos of contacts, pictures in Wiki articles, labels, favourites and reminders are not sent.
