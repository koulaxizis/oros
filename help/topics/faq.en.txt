# Frequently asked questions
Short answers to common questions. Each one links to a page with more detail.

## About orOS

### Is orOS free and open source?
Yes. orOS is free, open source and self-hostable. There are no cookies and no tracking. The source code is on GitHub: [github.com/koulaxizis/oros](https://github.com/koulaxizis/oros).

### Does it work offline?
Yes. orOS saves itself on your device, so it opens and works without internet. Apps that fetch live information, such as Weather, Radio, Television and Maps, need a connection for that part. See [Troubleshooting](help:t/troubleshooting) if an app says it hasn't been saved for offline use yet.

### How do I install it?
When your browser offers it, `Install orOS` appears in the menu, and `Install orOS as an app` in `Settings` under `System`. Tap or click it to add orOS to your home screen or desktop like a normal app. See [installing orOS](help:t/install).

## Your data

### Where is my data?
In your browser, on the device you're using. orOS keeps nothing on a server of its own, and there is no account. See [Your data and files](help:t/data).

### How do I get my data out?
Open `Settings`, go to `Backups` and tap `Export backup file`. You get one file with everything, including your files. Keep in mind it is not encrypted. See [Backup and restore](help:t/backup).

## Sync

### Can I use orOS on two devices?
Yes. Connect the same Dropbox account and use the same passphrase on each device, and your data stays in step. See [Sync across devices](help:t/sync).

### Is sync encrypted?
Yes. Your data is encrypted on your device with your passphrase before it goes to Dropbox. Dropbox only stores encrypted data and can't read it. Your passphrase never leaves your device.

### What if I lose my passphrase?
It can't be recovered, and nobody can decrypt your cloud data without it. The data on your devices is still there, so export a backup from one of them right away. Then see [Sync across devices](help:t/sync).
