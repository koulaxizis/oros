# Privacy and offline use
orOS is built to keep your data yours. It has no accounts, no cookies and no tracking, and it keeps working when you lose your connection.

The full privacy policy is on the web: [useoros.online/privacy.html](https://useoros.online/privacy.html).

## Your data stays in your browser
Everything you create in orOS is saved in this browser, on this device. There is no orOS server that collects it. orOS uses no cookies and no tracking or analytics of any kind.

Reading aloud ([Library](app:library), voice guidance in [Maps](app:maps)) uses only voices installed on this device, so the text is never sent anywhere. orOS has no voice input.

Because the data lives in the browser, each browser and each device has its own copy. To have the same data everywhere, use [sync](help:t/sync). To keep a copy for safety, use [backups](help:t/backup).

In the orOS Android app, [Contacts](app:contacts) and [Calendar](app:calendar) can also show your contacts and events in the phone's own Contacts and Calendar (`On this phone`). They go only into this phone, never to a server, and other apps on the phone that you allowed to read contacts or calendars can see them there. Turning the switch off removes them.

>! Clearing this site's data in your browser settings deletes your orOS data on this device. Make sure you have a backup or sync first.

### Screen lock
On a computer other people use, turn on `Screen lock` in [Settings](app:settings): orOS then asks for a PIN or a passphrase when it starts and after the idle time you choose. It keeps people away from the screen; it does not encrypt your data on the device, so someone who can read the disk or open the browser's developer tools can still get to it.

## Encrypted sync
Sync is optional and uses your own Dropbox account. orOS only works in its own app folder there.

Before anything leaves your device, orOS encrypts it with your encryption passphrase (`Encryption passphrase`). The passphrase never leaves this device, so Dropbox can't read your data. A new passphrase needs at least 10 characters.

- If you tick `Remember on this device`, the passphrase is kept locked inside this browser, so you don't have to type it every time. `Forget on this device` removes it.
- Nobody can recover a lost passphrase, not even orOS. Write it down somewhere safe.

>! Files you save with `Export backup file`, and backups in your backup folder (`Backup folder`), are **not** encrypted. Store them somewhere safe.

## Online services some apps use
orOS itself runs from your device, but some apps need the internet for what they show. They contact these services only when you use that feature:
- Weather (and the weather in the top bar): Open-Meteo, for forecasts, city search and air quality (Copernicus CAMS), MeteoAlarm for official warnings, and, only when you open the rain map, NASA GIBS (satellite rain pictures, IMERG) with OpenStreetMap map tiles.
- [Radio](app:radio): Radio Browser, a community list of stations. The stations play from their own servers.
- [Maps](app:maps): OpenStreetMap, OpenTopoMap and Esri map images, Photon place search, OSRM and FOSSGIS routes, Transitous for public transport, and AWS Terrain Tiles for the elevation of a track you plan. `Discover` asks the Overpass API, Wikipedia and Panoramax about the area you're looking at. Notes, place edits, quests and GPS trace uploads talk to the OpenStreetMap API and the Overpass API, only when you use them; writing needs your own OpenStreetMap account. `Offline maps` downloads an area you choose from Protomaps' builds of OpenStreetMap. The Maps help lists what each one receives.
- [Television](app:television): iptv-org, a community list of channels. The channels play from their own servers.
- [Atelier](app:atelier): Openverse, Wikimedia Commons, Iconify and Fontsource for free photos, icons and fonts; Pixabay and Pexels only with your own key.
- [Media Shelf](app:shelf): Open Library and MusicBrainz, only when you tap `Find details online`. Only the title and creator you typed are sent. Details and pictures can also come from Wikidata and Wikimedia Commons; films and series from TMDB only with your own key.
- [Unit Converter](app:units): Frankfurter, for the European Central Bank's currency rates, only when you open `Currency` or tap `Update rates`.
- [Budget](app:budget): the same Frankfurter rates, only when an account or an entry is in another currency (at most every 6 hours). Nothing about your money is sent.
- [Public Domain](app:pubdomain): Wikidata, for authors and their works.
- [Reader](app:feeds) and [Podcasts](app:podcasts): the sites whose feeds you add.
- [Meal Planner](app:meals): the recipe sites whose addresses you paste, and Open Food Facts when you search a food or scan a barcode for nutrition.
- [Workouts](app:fitness): wger, for exercise pictures, only when you open an exercise's details.
- [Montage](app:montage): Hugging Face, once, to download the speech model for `Make subtitles`, only when you press it. Your sound never leaves the device.
- [Podcasts](app:podcasts): gpodder.net or your own Nextcloud, only if you connect them for sync.
- [Mail](app:mail): your own mail provider, through the orOS mail relay, which passes your mail through and keeps nothing.
- [Wiki](app:wiki): Wikipedia, only when you use `Start from Wikipedia`; only the title you type is sent.
- [Library](app:library): Wiktionary, only for `Look up` and only after you agree; only the word is sent. `Online catalogues` reads Project Gutenberg, Standard Ebooks or an OPDS catalogue you add.
- [Newsletter](app:newsletter): your own mail account or sending provider, via the orOS mail relay; `Check domain` asks Cloudflare's public DNS for your domain's SPF, DKIM and DMARC records.
- [CRM](app:crm): only if you make a `Web form`, what visitors send with it goes through the orOS relay, encrypted for your CRM, and is kept there at most 30 days until your CRM picks it up.
- [Assistant](app:assistant): only the AI service you choose, with your own key, straight from your browser.
- [Chess](app:chess): Chess.com's public data, only when you open `Online accounts`; only the username you type is sent, never a password. Lichess, only when you use it there: you sign in on lichess.org (orOS never sees the password), the sign-in is sealed on this device and never synced, and a game goes to Lichess only when you press `Analyse this game on Lichess` or play on Lichess (after you press `Allow playing`).
- Sync: Dropbox, only if you connect it.

Some apps can also work with accounts you already have elsewhere, but only after you connect them in [Settings](app:settings) › `Connected services`:
- [Stock](app:stock): your own WooCommerce shop.
- [Invoices](app:invoices): your timologisi.online account, to send documents to myDATA.
- [Newsletter](app:newsletter): your MailerLite, Mailchimp or Brevo account, to sync subscribers.
- [CRM](app:crm): your HubSpot, Pipedrive, Invoice Ninja or Odoo account, read to bring customers and deals in; your WooCommerce shop's Revenue Splitter, read on a partner's card and never stored.
- [Budget](app:budget): your Stripe or Invoice Ninja account, read to bring payments in.
- [To-Do](app:todo): your Todoist account (projects and tasks) or your GitHub account (the open issues assigned to you), read only.
- [Kanban](app:kanban): your Trello account (a board) or your GitHub account (a repository's issues), read only.
- [Bookmarks](app:bookmarks): your Raindrop.io account, read to bring bookmarks in.
- [Notes](app:notes), [Spreadsheet](app:spreadsheet) and [Kanban](app:kanban): your Notion integration, read to bring in the pages and databases you shared with it.

orOS talks to them straight from your browser, or through the orOS relay when the service doesn't answer browsers. Mailchimp, HubSpot, Stripe and Notion always go through the relay, which keeps nothing. Your keys stay in Connected services.

[Contacts](app:contacts) › `From an account` is different: you sign in to Google or Microsoft once, your browser reads your contacts straight from them, and the access ends right after the reading (Google's is withdrawn, Microsoft's is forgotten and runs out within the hour). Nothing is kept and no server of orOS is involved.

`Use device location` and other location buttons ask your browser for permission first. The full list, with credits and licences, is in [Settings](app:settings) under `About orOS, credits and licences`.

## Working offline
orOS saves itself and its apps on your device, so it opens and works without a connection: your apps, your data and your settings are all there.

- Things that need the internet wait for it. The weather icon shows a crossed-out cloud instead of a made-up temperature, and online services such as maps, radio and TV catalogues come back when you are online.
- Changes you make offline are kept on the device. With sync on, they are sent when you are back online.
- If an app hasn't been saved for offline use yet, you'll see a short message instead. Open it once while online.
