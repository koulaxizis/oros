# What's new in orOS
The newest changes come first. When orOS updates itself, the update notice opens this page.

## Version 1.13.00
- Invoices: unpaid invoices in Calendar and Today, due reminders, Add to Budget, Make invoice from Quote and Timesheet, clients from CRM.
- Help: the guide pages on the menu, notifications, installing, privacy and getting started now cover the latest releases.
- Mail: other apps can open a new message with files attached, e.g. an invoice PDF.

## Version 1.12.00
- Sticky Notes: rings can repeat (every day, weekdays, every week), a Ringtone Composer melody can be the ring sound, Send to To-Do and Copy to Notes, and your notes show in the menu search and in Today.

## Version 1.11.00
- Library: Add to Media Shelf; linked books follow your reading.

## Version 1.10.00
- New app Montage (Video): edit videos on a timeline with pictures, music, your voice and titles, add transitions and filters, and export an MP4 or WebM video. Your videos stay on your device unless you turn on media sync for a project.

## Version 1.08.00
- New app Library (Personal): read EPUB and PDF books from the orOS disk with highlights, notes, bookmarks and search.
- The menu closes its categories again after you open an app, and the menu and the notifications panel no longer stay open together.

## Version 1.07.00
- New app: Invoices. Invoices, receipts, proformas and credit notes with VAT, automatic numbering and PDF; payments and overdue tracking, a revenue dashboard, clients and recurring drafts. Invoices issued in myDATA can be recorded with their ΜΑΡΚ.

## Version 1.06.00
- New app Text Tools (Accessories): sort lines, remove duplicates, clean up spaces, number lines, change the separator of a CSV, find and replace, and keep your steps as recipes.

## Version 1.05.00
- New app Unit Converter (Accessories): fifteen categories from length to fuel economy, cooking cups to grams and currencies with the ECB rates, also offline. Type "10 km to mi" in the menu search for a quick answer.

## Version 1.04.00
- New app Stock: business inventory with products, movements, reorder list and suppliers.

## Version 1.03.00
- New app: CRM. A customer book with a sales pipeline, contact history and dated follow-ups. New menu category Business (Quote and Timesheet moved there).

## Version 1.02.00
- New app: Sticky Notes. Coloured notes on the desktop that can ring at a time and fade away by themselves (Ctrl+Alt+Shift+N for a new one).

## Version 1.01.00
- New app Case Converter (Accessories): UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case and more, with Greek done right (ΤΣΑΪ, final ς), Greek in Latin letters (ELOT 743, Greeklish), names for code and Unicode styles.

## Version 1.00.00
- Photo Lab: new Filter menu (blur, sharpen, noise, pixelate, emboss, edges, vignette) and retouch tools (clone stamp, healing brush, red eye, dodge, burn, sponge, smudge)

## Version 0.99.00
- Photo Lab opens and saves Photoshop (PSD) and OpenRaster files with layers.

## Version 0.98.00
- Today now also shows your expected period and mood check-in (both only if you turn them on in Settings), today's minimalism proposal, remembrance days from your family tree, tonight's sky events and your pet's birthday.

## Version 0.97.00
- New app Photo Lab in the new Image category: edit pictures with layers, selections, 14 tools and adjustments, then export or set them as wallpaper. Pixel Avatar, Wallpaper Generator, Tree Sketcher and Aura Painter moved to Image.

## Version 0.96.00
- New app Studio (Sound): record and arrange audio on up to 8 tracks, with loop, click and WAV export. Effects come later.

## Version 0.95.00
- Today now also shows your habits, today's workout, Health reminder times, upcoming bills and income, today's meals and how many unread e-mails wait in each Inbox.

## Version 0.94.00
- Notes opens .txt and .md files; on Android, "Share → orOS" now takes files too and opens them in the right app.

## Version 0.93.00
- Sound Mixer: keeps playing after you close it, with a taskbar button for pause, volume and the sleep timer; new train, keyboard and clock sounds.

## Version 0.92.00
- Wallpaper Generator: a new picture every day, and a live wallpaper with slowly drifting light.

## Version 0.91.00
- Typewriter: send your page to Writer as a new document.

## Version 0.90.00
- Name Generator: names for pets, teams and projects, and send a name straight to Netizen ID or Characters.

## Version 0.89.00
- Today now shows your calendar events, to-dos, Kanban cards, quotes to follow up and birthdays, and you can tick To-Do and Plant Care tasks as done from it.

## Version 0.88.00
- Netizen ID: a QR code on the back of your card, Add me to Contacts, and a face straight from Pixel Avatar.

## Version 0.87.00
- Micro-Zen: make your own breathing patterns, and see your sessions as a row in Habits.

## Version 0.86.00
- New app: One-Sentence Diary. One sentence a day, and under it what you wrote on the same date in earlier years. Optional evening reminder, calendar, search and a printable year.
- Wheel of Fate: give an option double or triple chance.
- orOS opens faster on a slow or unstable connection: after 3 seconds it starts from the saved copy.
- Device Info no longer shows a fake 100% battery when the browser hides the real level.

## Version 0.85.00
- New app: Today. See what is late, due today and coming up across your apps in one place, with one short notice each morning. Open it with Ctrl+Alt+Shift+Y.
- Help: the Install guide explains how to open files from your computer with orOS.

## Version 0.84.00
- Open with orOS: once orOS is installed from Chrome or Edge, documents, spreadsheets, .ics, .vcf and more open straight from your computer in the right orOS app. See Settings › Opening files.

## Version 0.83.00
- Calendar: import .ics files from Google, Outlook or your phone, with a preview first. Contacts: on Android, add contacts straight from your phone.
- Help: the Calendar and Contacts guides now explain .ics import and "From phone".

## Version 0.82.00
- New app: Aura Painter. Paint with glowing light, in kaleidoscope symmetry if you like, keep your works, replay them stroke by stroke or make one your wallpaper.

## Version 0.81.00
- Menu: choose which apps the menu shows in Settings › Menu (hide all, show all or one by one). Hidden apps still open from search, the Dock and links.

## Version 0.80.00
- Atelier: put your designs in folders, and the Canva and Files windows now tell you what went wrong instead of waiting for ever.
- Calendar: on phones the labels and feeds now fold behind one button, so the month shows right away.

## Version 0.79.00
- Mail: your rules can now run on the mail server, and you can set an automatic out-of-office reply, so both keep working even when orOS is closed (servers with ManageSieve; Gmail, Outlook and Yahoo don't support it).

## Version 0.78.00
- New app: Tree Sketcher. Move a few sliders and a hand-drawn tree grows: choose the kind, the season and the style, keep your trees, download them or set one as your wallpaper.

## Version 0.77.00
- Mail works with your other apps: names from Contacts while you type, and a message can become a task, a note, a bookmark or a contact. Emails in Contacts and a new Email button in Calendar open Mail.

## Version 0.76.00
- Mail: undo send, send later, templates for messages you write often, and Block sender.

## Version 0.75.00
- Mail: attach files, forward with attachments, preview pictures and PDFs, and save attachments to Files.

## Version 0.74.00
- Mail: create, rename and delete folders, and save drafts on the server to finish them on any device.

## Version 0.73.00
- Mail groups replies into conversations, and with two or more accounts, All inboxes shows every Inbox together.

## Version 0.72.00
- New app: Ringtone Composer. Write a melody note by note like on the old phones, hear it, and download it as a ringtone (WAV) or as RTTTL text.

## Version 0.71.00
- Mail: search your messages (press /), write with bold, lists, links and colour, and use a signature with your logo.

## Version 0.70.00
- New app: Label Maker. Type a word and see it pressed into old-style embossed tape, then save it, download it or print a sheet of labels. In Storage, the tag button makes a label from any room, cupboard or shelf.

## Version 0.69.00
- Mail: archive, delete (with Undo), move, star, mark as spam or unread, one message or many at once (swipe on a phone). A green badge shows a verified sender, and Mail warns you about forged senders and links that lead somewhere else. Rules can file, mark or star new mail by themselves.

## Version 0.68.00
- New app Moon & Sky (Accessories): tonight's Moon, a sky map with the constellations, a month view and a red night view. Calendar can show Moon phases, solstices, meteor showers and eclipses (turn the chip on).
- Dock: magnified icons no longer cover their neighbours, and you can pick a hover effect (bounce, pulse, frame, glow, lift, wiggle) in Settings > Dock.
- Mail: a notice for new messages while orOS is open (turn it on in Settings > Notifications).

## Version 0.67.00
- Assistant: it can now prepare entries (a task, an event, an expense, a quote, a sheet, slides, a bookmark or a note) for you to check and save in that app, read a whole item when you allow it, and keep your conversations on all your devices (optional, encrypted). Ask it from the menu search or with Ctrl+Alt+Shift+A.
- Reader: Send › Email now opens a ready draft in Mail.

## Version 0.66.00
- Mail: write, reply, forward and send (plain text), with a signature per account and an Outbox that sends by itself when you are back online.

## Version 0.65.00
- Layout 1.2: type straight into text frames, and open InDesign (.idml) and PDF files.
- The guide is up to date with the latest app changes, and menu search now finds the guide page of every app.
- Time: Pomodoro focus sessions can be logged to a Timesheet project.
- Media Shelf: an optional daily reminder to keep reading (or watching, listening, playing) what you have in progress. Turn it on in More.
- Atelier: the slide show now plays videos, sound and entrance animations.
- Pet Health Book: attach photos or PDFs (prescriptions, test results) to vaccines, visits and medicines.
- QR code button in Contacts, Calendar and Bookmarks: opens QR Generator with the code ready.
- Workouts: import your history from Strong or Hevy (CSV) in Settings.

## Version 0.64.00
- New app: Typewriter. Write like on a real typewriter: only forward, a backspace strikes the letter through, key sounds and paper tints, a word goal per page.
- Timesheet: tasks, tags and an hours budget per project; reports filter by tag and group by task or tag.

## Version 0.63.00
- New app: Assistant. Chat with the AI you choose (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Mistral, OpenRouter or a local model). It can search your orOS data, but only what you allow.

## Version 0.62.00
- Media Shelf 1.2: find book and album details online (Open Library, MusicBrainz, only when you ask), release dates, and a Media Shelf chip in Calendar (finishes and wishlist releases).

## Version 0.61.00
- Atelier: a brand kit (colours, fonts, logos), video and sound in designs with video export, a better Canva import (real fonts, clear errors, retries, no duplicates), and bulk delete of designs and unused files.
- Dock: choose one of eight styles in Settings › Appearance › Dock; "Show over open apps" is now off until you turn it on.
- Garage: send your KTEO test, renewals and service appointments to To-Do.
- Family Tree: a "Family tree" button on contact cards, and death anniversaries in the Calendar.

## Version 0.60.00
- New app in Internet: One-Minute Museum shows a Wikipedia article as a museum exhibit, a new one every minute; star the ones you like to keep them, offline too.
- Media Shelf: import your Goodreads or Letterboxd CSV exports, with a preview, duplicates skipped and Undo.
- Travel: send your "Before I leave" list or what is still to pack to To-Do as a new list.
- Chore Wheel: send an open chore to To-Do from its ⋯ menu.
- Calendar: a new "Work time" label shows your Timesheet hours per project each day; tap a row to open that day in Timesheet.
- [Mind Map](app:mindmap) can save a branch or the whole map as a note in [Notes](app:notes).
- Timesheet: import your hours from Toggl, Clockify or Harvest (CSV) with a preview; re-importing adds nothing twice.

## Version 0.59.00
- [Mind Map](app:mindmap) can link any two ideas, lets you place a branch where you want it, shows small pictures, imports FreeMind and XMind files, and sends a branch to [To-Do](app:todo) or [Slides](app:slides).

## Version 0.58.00
- [Layout](app:layout) checks your document before export (preflight), starts from 7 templates, exports a page as PNG or JPG, lets you change master objects on one page, has rulers with guides, and opens Scribus files.
- [Slides](app:slides) can start a new presentation from an outline sent by another app. You pick the theme first, and nothing is created before that.
- orOS is ready for the "Send to orOS" browser add-on (Chrome, Edge, Brave, Firefox), which sends the page you are on to [Bookmarks](app:bookmarks) and searches orOS from the address bar. The add-on is not in the stores yet.

## Version 0.57.00
- [Podcasts](app:podcasts) shows chapters and transcripts, plays video episodes, searches every episode, keeps listening statistics, can tell you about new episodes, and sends an episode to [Notes](app:notes), [To-Do](app:todo) or a show's site to [Reader](app:feeds).
- [Meal Planner](app:meals) sends the shopping list to [To-Do](app:todo), and [Calendar](app:calendar) can show planned meals with the new Meals chip.
- [Travel](app:travel) reminds you the evening before a trip and before each departure, shows the destination's forecast, works out fuel cost by car with data from [Garage](app:garage), and puts trips in [Calendar](app:calendar).
- [Baby](app:baby) can remind you when a feed is due and at a daily medicine time (off by default), and [Calendar](app:calendar) can show milestones, vaccines, doctor visits and age with the new Baby chip.
- [Chore Wheel](app:chores) can remind you of your chores once a day, and [Calendar](app:calendar) can show who does what with the new Chores chip.
- While a [Timesheet](app:timesheet) timer runs, the top bar shows a clock with the time so far, and [[Ctrl+Alt+Shift+T]] starts or stops the timer. Timesheet warns you about a timer left running, and its report can turn your hours into a [Quote](app:quote).

## Version 0.56.00
- [Reader](app:feeds) can send an article to [Bookmarks](app:bookmarks), [Notes](app:notes), [To-Do](app:todo) or email, and can notify you about new articles from a feed or a watched word. In [Bookmarks](app:bookmarks), `Follow in Reader` subscribes to a site.

## Version 0.55.00
- [Pet Health Book](app:petcare) reminds you at each medicine dose time, and can send a vet cost to [Budget](app:budget) or the pet's food to your [To-Do](app:todo) shopping list.

## Version 0.54.00
- Every app and game now has its own page in Help. Inside any app, the **?** button or [[Ctrl+Alt+Shift+H]] opens it.
- New users get a short tour of the top bar the first time they open orOS. You can [take the tour](tour:start) again at any time.
- The update notice now opens this page, so you can see what changed.
- [Split](app:split) can add people from [Contacts](app:contacts) or from a [Travel](app:travel) trip.
- [Garage](app:garage) keeps receipt photos with fuel, service and cost entries.
- [Timesheet](app:timesheet) can turn a report into a new [Quote](app:quote).

## Versions 0.49.00 to 0.53.02
- Favourites: the star next to every app in the menu puts it on the desktop or in a Dock at the bottom of the screen.
- All settings moved out of the menu into the new [Settings](app:settings) app, in the System category.
- Universal search now also finds your data in 35 more apps. Private apps such as Mood, Cycle, Health, Baby and Budget stay out of it until you turn them on.
- New app: [Oracle](app:oracle), in Fun. Ask a question and get a prophecy that is always true and never helpful.
- [Budget](app:budget) has accounts and transfers, opens your entries in [Spreadsheet](app:spreadsheet), and shows recurring entries in [Calendar](app:calendar).
- [Family Tree](app:familytree) imports and exports GEDCOM files, for other genealogy programs.
- [2048](app:g2048) has a timed mode: two minutes for your best score.
- [Atelier](app:atelier) offers hundreds of free fonts, many with Greek letters.
- On mornings when one of your contacts has a name day, orOS lets you know.
- A fix for sync stopping with "Browser storage is full" on devices that kept old automatic backups.

## Version 0.48.00
- New app: [Help](app:help), in the System category. It explains orOS and its apps, works offline and has its own search.
- A **?** button in the top bar opens Help at the page of the app you are using.

## Versions 0.47.01 to 0.47.05
- Updates wait while a podcast is playing, so an update never cuts off an episode.
- Installed orOS: the app icon has shortcuts to open some apps directly.
- [Budget](app:budget) can receive expenses from other apps, such as `Add to Budget` in [Garage](app:garage).
- [Bookmarks](app:bookmarks) can receive pages from your browser with a bookmarklet, or from the Android share menu.
- The weather chip in the top bar is now set up in [Weather](app:weather), and the top bar always fits on small phones.

## Version 0.47.00
- 53 new apps and games joined the menu at once, among them [Mail](app:mail), [Reader](app:feeds), [Podcasts](app:podcasts), [Atelier](app:atelier), [Slides](app:slides), [Budget](app:budget) and many games.
- Universal search: the search box at the top of the menu also finds things inside your data.
- [Calendar](app:calendar) shows name days, public holidays and world days.
