# What's new in orOS
The newest changes come first. When orOS updates itself, the update notice opens this page.

## Version 5.02.00
- **Save new logins to Passwords.** In the orOS browser add-on, `Save the login from this page` sends the name and password you just typed to Passwords in orOS; on Android, with autofill on, answer yes to `Save to orOS Passwords?`. orOS opens it in the Passwords editor, as a new entry or as a new password for the same site and user (the old one goes to the history), and stores it only when you press Save. See [Passwords](help:a/passwords).

## Version 5.01.00
- **orOS fills your Passwords on your Android phone.** In the orOS app for Android, Passwords › Menu › `Autofill on this phone` makes orOS the phone's autofill service: tap an orOS suggestion in an app or a browser, confirm with your fingerprint, face or screen lock, and the login with its 2FA code, a card or an address is filled. The phone keeps only a sealed copy that only it can open. See [Passwords](help:a/passwords).

## Version 5.00.00
- **The orOS browser add-on fills your Passwords on websites.** Version 2 of the add-on for Chrome, Edge, Brave and Firefox fills logins with their 2FA code, cards, identities and addresses: press the orOS icon in the toolbar (or Ctrl+Shift+L), unlock with your master password and choose an entry. Your vault stays encrypted and the add-on locks itself after 15 minutes. Download it with the install steps at [useoros.online/download](https://useoros.online/download/#addon). See [Passwords](help:a/passwords).

## Version 4.99.00
- **Budget reads more bank files.** Import now takes MT940 / MT942 bank statements (`.sta`, `.mt940`, `.940`, `.942`) and the PayPal activity file (CSV, in English, Greek, German or Spanish), with the net amount after PayPal's fee and payments in another currency converted. Rows that are already in Budget another way, such as an Invoices payment, a transfer between your own accounts or the bank's line for a PayPal payment, come unticked so they are not counted twice. See [Budget](help:a/budget).

## Version 4.98.01
- **More name days and world days in Calendar, checked against the United Nations list and Wikipedia.** 23 more name days (Olga, Lydia, Polyxeni, Ermioni, Orestis, Platon, Themistoklis, Arsenios, Pelagia, Margarita…) and 54 more world days, among them the Mediterranean Diet, Tsunami Awareness, Patient Safety and Cleanup days, Olympic Day, World Voice Day and the Greek Organ Donation, National Benefactors and Armed Forces days. See [Calendar](help:a/calendar).

## Version 4.98.00
- **Passwords shows all your two-factor codes on one screen.** `Codes` at the top lists every code with the seconds it has left; tap one to copy it, and in its last 5 seconds you get the next one instead. `New` › `2FA code` keeps an account's code without a password, like an authenticator app, and codes imported from Aegis, 2FAS or Google Authenticator become such entries. `QR code` shows one account as a QR code for moving it to another app, and `Export` now also writes your codes for Aegis (encrypted) or 2FAS. See [Passwords](help:a/passwords).

## Version 4.97.02
- Credits now name Tor Browser and the Go modules that check its signature, for the coming Tor button in orOS Portable, the installers and the Live USB. Tor Browser is never part of an orOS download; it is fetched from torproject.org when you first use it.

## Version 4.97.01
- **Fuller name days and world days in Calendar.** Name days now cover many more saints and list the female and other forms of each name (1 November: Kosmas, Kosmia, Damianos, Damiani, Anargyros, Anargyri, Argyrios, Argyro…). World days grew from 65 to 168: the United Nations and WHO days and the Greek days of remembrance. Contacts who celebrate are found on the new days too. See [Calendar](help:a/calendar).

## Version 4.97.00
- **Passwords keeps cards, identities, addresses and secure notes.** `New` now asks what you want to keep. Cards show their network and last four digits, are checked for typos and are marked before they expire; `Copy in order` copies number, expiry, code and name one after another for a payment form. Identities hold your ID card, passport, driving licence, tax and social security numbers; addresses copy as lines. Everything stays in the same encrypted vault and syncs as before. Cards and identities imported earlier from Bitwarden, Proton Pass and others offer `Convert`. See [Passwords](help:a/passwords).

## Version 4.96.00
- **Encrypt my files** (orOS USB, optional, off by default): on the Live USB, every user except the first can now encrypt their own files in Settings › Users. They are encrypted at the next sign-in and can be opened only while that user is signed in, not even by the owner. Each encrypted user gets their own recovery code, because the owner can no longer reset their password. See [Settings](help:a/settings).

## Version 4.95.01
- orOS opens faster the first time: the files it needs only for background work (reminders, household, Today and similar) now load after the desktop is shown. On a slow phone the first open went from about 9 to about 6 seconds.

## Version 4.95.00
- **Users on one computer or stick** (orOS Portable and the Live USB): several people can now share one PC or USB stick, each with their own orOS, data and password. Set it up in Settings › Users (the first person becomes the owner and gets a recovery code); a guest account is optional. Sign out is in the power menu. Passwords keep users apart but do not encrypt the stick. See [Settings](help:a/settings).

## Version 4.94.03
- Every skin now suggests one of the wallpapers. When you pick a skin, orOS moves to its wallpaper only if you have not chosen one yourself; Settings › Appearance marks the suggested wallpaper with a star. See [Settings](help:a/settings).

## Version 4.94.02
- PDF export in every app now uses jsPDF 4.2.1, which closes two security holes of the old version; your PDFs look the same. Studio: the hint in an empty project no longer gets cut off on a phone. Files opened on its own no longer shows an error.

## Version 4.94.01
- Updates no longer get stuck: if one file of a new version cannot be downloaded (a slow or filtered network, common on phones), orOS tries again, picks up where it stopped, and after a few tries installs the update without that file. A phone that stayed on an old version updates by itself the next time you open orOS.

## Version 4.94.00
- Studio: the Assisted effects (Assisted EQ, Compressor, Reverb and Delay) are in `+ Effect`, and what their `Learn` finds is saved with the track and can be undone. Instrument tracks get the NULL JSFX MIDI effects (Arpeggiator, Chords, Scale and more) above the instrument. On the remote, a track with a NULL instrument gets `Pads` or `Keys` that play it on the PC, and recording MIDI keeps what you play. See [Studio](help:a/studio).

## Version 4.93.02
- Studio: NULL Drum Machine now sounds whole with `Outputs` set to Groups too. Studio mixes the group outputs (snare, hats, percussion) into the track, each with its own Drive, Grit and Volume. With Main nothing changes. See [Studio](help:a/studio).

## Version 4.93.01
- Studio: NULL Drum Machine is back in the instrument list, and projects that use it play it again. Since NULL JSFX 3.0 it has extra outputs for groups; Studio plays its main pair, so with `Outputs` set to Groups only the kick sounds. See [Studio](help:a/studio).

## Version 4.91.00

Pick the scanner, the glass or the feeder (both sides when it can), colour, gray or line art and the resolution; the pages come into the same review strip as camera scans, and `Read the text after scanning` makes them searchable. Pairing with the Bridge is shared with Studio, and nothing leaves your computer. On Linux the Bridge uses SANE (with sane-airscan for network scanners), already on orOS USB.
