# What's new in orOS
The newest changes come first. When orOS updates itself, the update notice opens this page.

## Version 0.54.00
- Every app and game now has its own page in Help. Inside any app, the **?** button or [[Ctrl+Alt+Shift+H]] opens it.
- New users get a short tour of the top bar the first time they open orOS. You can [take the tour](tour:start) again at any time.
- The update notice now opens this page, so you can see what changed.
- [Split](app:split) can add people from [Contacts](app:contacts) or from a [Travel](app:travel) trip.
- [Garage](app:garage) keeps receipt photos with fuel, service and cost entries.
- [Timesheet](app:timesheet) can turn a report into a new [Quote](app:quote).

## Versions 0.49.00 to 0.53.02
- Favourites: the star next to every app in the menu puts it on the desktop or in a Dock at the bottom of the screen.
- All settings moved out of the menu into the new [Settings](app:settings) app, in the System category.
- Universal search now also finds your data in 35 more apps. Private apps such as Mood, Cycle, Health, Baby and Budget stay out of it until you turn them on.
- New app: [Oracle](app:oracle), in Fun. Ask a question and get a prophecy that is always true and never helpful.
- [Budget](app:budget) has accounts and transfers, opens your entries in [Spreadsheet](app:spreadsheet), and shows recurring entries in [Calendar](app:calendar).
- [Family Tree](app:familytree) imports and exports GEDCOM files, for other genealogy programs.
- [2048](app:g2048) has a timed mode: two minutes for your best score.
- [Atelier](app:atelier) offers hundreds of free fonts, many with Greek letters.
- On mornings when one of your contacts has a name day, orOS lets you know.
- A fix for sync stopping with "Browser storage is full" on devices that kept old automatic backups.

## Version 0.48.00
- New app: [Help](app:help), in the System category. It explains orOS and its apps, works offline and has its own search.
- A **?** button in the top bar opens Help at the page of the app you are using.

## Versions 0.47.01 to 0.47.05
- Updates wait while a podcast is playing, so an update never cuts off an episode.
- Installed orOS: the app icon has shortcuts to open some apps directly.
- [Budget](app:budget) can receive expenses from other apps, such as `Add to Budget` in [Garage](app:garage).
- [Bookmarks](app:bookmarks) can receive pages from your browser with a bookmarklet, or from the Android share menu.
- The weather chip in the top bar is now set up in [Weather](app:weather), and the top bar always fits on small phones.

## Version 0.47.00
- 53 new apps and games joined the menu at once, among them [Mail](app:mail), [Reader](app:feeds), [Podcasts](app:podcasts), [Atelier](app:atelier), [Slides](app:slides), [Budget](app:budget) and many games.
- Universal search: the search box at the top of the menu also finds things inside your data.
- [Calendar](app:calendar) shows name days, public holidays and world days.
