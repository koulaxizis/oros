# What's new in orOS
The newest changes come first. When orOS updates itself, the update notice opens this page.

## Version 2.79.00
- Command bar: type «dentist tomorrow 10», «bread to the shopping list» or «spent 12€ coffee» in the menu search, or press Ctrl+K over any app; the app opens with the form filled in.

## Version 2.78.01
- orOS Drive on the phone: a file that could not be saved to orOS now shows a notification, and file pickers stay light until you sign in to orOS Drive.

## Version 2.78.00
- Send to someone: send a whole calendar or a date range from Calendar › More. It opens month by month in any browser, as an .ics file or straight into orOS.

## Version 2.77.00
- Notifications with orOS closed (optional): turn it on per device in Settings › Notifications and alarms and reminders arrive even when orOS is closed.

## Version 2.76.00
- Sync can now go through your own WebDAV server (Nextcloud, ownCloud, Synology, QNAP): Settings › Sync › Use WebDAV / Nextcloud.

## Version 2.75.00
- Files opens documents, spreadsheets, songs and videos straight in the orOS app that reads them (Open with…), plays songs in Music, and sends files to Mail.

## Version 2.74.00
- Music: Made for you on Home. A Daily mix, On repeat, Rewind and song radio are made on your device from what you play, and every year gets its own recap (top songs, artists and your biggest month). Today shows the recap or the Daily mix.
- Music: bring your Spotify playlists and likes. Open the my_spotify_data.zip (or its JSON/CSV files) and Music finds the songs in your library, then (if you say yes) in free music online.
- Music: find missing album covers online (MusicBrainz and the Cover Art Archive), add an album to Media Shelf, or send a free song to Montage.

## Version 2.73.00
- New users choose what the menu shows: Everything (first and chosen), or Home, Work, School, Creating, Games and any app by hand. You can change it later in Settings › Menu › Choose a profile.

## Version 2.72.01
- orOS Drive on the phone: the whole orOS disk now appears as «orOS» in Android's Files app and in every file picker (orOS Android app; not in the F-Droid version for now).

## Version 2.72.00
- Send to someone: links with an expiry (1, 7 or 30 days), stored encrypted and taken back any time from My shared links; long notes and Wiki articles can now be sent.

## Version 2.71.01
- Maps: public transport now says when an area's timetables are out of date (Athens, for now).

## Version 2.71.00
- Atelier gets Magic eraser and Magic grab (on the device), Image from text, Magic edit and Magic expand (with the image AI you choose in Assistant › Settings › Pictures: Pollinations, free, by default), Design from a description and Magic animate.

## Version 2.70.00
- New app Scanner & PDF (Office): merge PDFs, split them (each page, every few pages or page ranges), delete, duplicate, turn and drag pages into order, and make a PDF from pictures. Everything happens on your device. In Files, select PDFs or pictures and choose Open in Scanner & PDF. Camera scanning comes next.

## Version 2.69.02
- Files: big folders in grid view no longer overlap, and pictures open again. Atelier: when you delete a design you can tick a box to delete its unused files too.

## Version 2.69.01
- Settings: Connect a service now shows tiles grouped by purpose, with a search box, instead of a plain list.

## Version 2.69.00
- Send to an app: Writer, Notes, Wiki, Mail, Bookmarks and Reader can hand text or a link to another orOS app (Notes, To-Do, Writer, Mail, Spreadsheet, Wiki, Assistant…), which opens it ready to save.

## Version 2.68.01
- Prompter: Use now opens the prompt in Writer as a new document, and the Cancel and Factory reset buttons no longer touch.

## Version 2.68.00
- Music: lyrics that follow the song. Music reads a .lrc file next to a song, the lyrics in its tags, or (if you say yes) asks LRCLIB, a free lyrics library. Tap a line to jump there.
- Music: Sound settings with a 10-band equalizer and presets, crossfade, gapless playback and the same volume for every song (ReplayGain). A sleep timer stops the music after a while or at the end of the song.

## Version 2.67.00
- Writer, Spreadsheet and Slides can bring in one file straight from Google Docs, Sheets or Slides: pick it and it opens as a normal orOS file.

## Version 2.66.00
- orOS Drive on the phone: the orOS Android app can mirror folders from your phone into the Vault, so they show in Files › orOS Drive on your other devices. It syncs every 15 minutes, on Wi-Fi only unless you change it. The F-Droid version comes without it for now.

## Version 2.65.01
- Atelier AI now also works with the in-browser model chosen in the Assistant (WebLLM); it says when the model is not downloaded yet or WebGPU is missing.

## Version 2.65.00
- Mockups: show your book open at an inside spread, with your own pictures or two pages of your book's PDF and a page curve you choose, or as an e-book on a tablet, phone or e-reader in black, white or silver.

## Version 2.64.01
- Seasonal decorations: Autumn leaves and Winter snow no longer vanish when the decorations stand still (low battery, less motion, Still only, slow device). Settings says when a slow device stopped the movement.

## Version 2.64.00
- Layout and Atelier: designs keep details added by a newer orOS when an older device syncs them, so nothing is lost while your devices update at different times. A design that needs a newer orOS says so and waits instead of opening.

## Version 2.63.00

- Music: import playlists from M3U, PLS and XSPF files, and add music feeds (RSS, Podcasting 2.0 music feeds such as Wavlake).

## Version 2.62.00

Save it as a 300 dpi PNG or PDF, with the guides or as your finished cover with its bleed, or design on it in Atelier.

## Version 2.61.00

Opening only adds what you don't have and can be undone, and a Newsletter file never sends anything. Now every app that keeps records has its own orOS file.

## Version 2.60.00

- Vault: a file that failed to download on one device could be deleted from the cloud and then from every device. This no longer happens.
