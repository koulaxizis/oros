# What's new in orOS
The newest changes come first. When orOS updates itself, the update notice opens this page.

## Version 1.94.00
- The orOS Android app has home-screen widgets: orOS Today shows what's late, today and the next days, with buttons to add a task, a glass of water or a note.

## Version 1.93.00
- In the orOS Android app, your Time alarms, Water reminders and To-Do tasks due now ring even when orOS is closed.

## Version 1.92.01
- Greek everywhere: the last English labels, tooltips and screen-reader names are now in Greek, and English words such as "board" or "project" became plain Greek words. A new check keeps every new string translated.

## Version 1.92.00
- Chess plays on Lichess: find an opponent, challenge a friend or accept a challenge, and play rated or casual games on the orOS board with both clocks.

## Version 1.91.00
- Chess connects to Lichess: sign in to see your ratings and games, play the Lichess daily puzzle, or send any game to Lichess for its free analysis.

## Version 1.90.00
- Chess connects to Chess.com: type your username to see your ratings, open your games for review and play the daily puzzle.

## Version 1.89.00
- Chess opens, saves and reviews games as PGN files: step through any game, see the computer's analysis and play on from any position.

## Version 1.88.00
- Contacts: `From an account` now works with Google. Microsoft follows soon.
- orOS now has a public privacy policy page: useoros.online/privacy.html (English and Greek).
- Contacts: contacts you add or change in the phone's contacts now come back into orOS (orOS Android app, "On this phone").
- Phone sync: an event added on the phone now appears once, and contacts edited on the phone come back into orOS.

## Version 1.87.00
- Calendar: other Android apps can now add events to orOS (orOS Android app).

## Version 1.86.00
- Calendar: events you add or change in the phone's calendar now come back into orOS (orOS Android app, "On this phone").

## Version 1.85.00
- Contacts: every import now shows `Review import` first: new contacts, ones already here (skipped) and ones with other details, where you choose to merge (keeping yours or taking the imported values), add separately or skip. `Undo` puts everything back.
- Contacts: `From an account` copies your contacts from Google or Microsoft (Outlook.com, Hotmail, Microsoft 365) once and disconnects straight away; nothing is kept.

## Version 1.84.00
- Calendar: with "On this phone" on, your subscribed calendars and holidays now also appear in the phone's calendar (orOS Android app).
- Calendar: events on the phone now keep the colour of their label or feed (orOS Android app).

## Version 1.83.00
- Studio: 14 new NULL JSFX effects (NULL JSFX 2.1).
- Contacts and Calendar: another fix so "On this phone" connects in the orOS Android app.

## Version 1.82.01

- Contacts and Calendar: "On this phone" now connects in the orOS Android app.
- Studio: the Loudness Meter and Spectrum Analyzer effects now work offline, and new NULL JSFX effects arrive automatically.

## Version 1.82.00

## Version 1.80.00
