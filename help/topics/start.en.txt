# Getting started with orOS
orOS is a small operating system that runs in your web browser. It has its own desktop, a menu full of apps, and your settings, and it works on phones, tablets and computers, even without a connection.

## What orOS is
Everything in orOS runs inside the browser: there is nothing to sign up for and no account to make. Your apps and data stay on this device unless you choose to sync them. You can also [install orOS as an app](help:t/install) so it opens in its own window.

## The top bar
The bar at the top is always there:
- The `orOS` button on the left opens the [menu](help:t/menu) with all your apps and settings. While an app is open, it reads `Back`.
- The **?** button opens Help at the page of the app you are using, or at the start of Help when you are on the desktop.
- The time opens [Time](app:time) and the date opens [Calendar](app:calendar).
- The `EL` / `EN` button switches the language between Greek and English.
- The bell opens your notifications. A number on it shows how many you haven't read. See [Notifications](help:t/notifications).
- The small dot shows the sync status. Hover over it to read it, and tap or click it to sync right now. See [Sync](help:t/sync).
- If you turn on the weather tray, a small weather icon with the temperature appears too. It opens [Weather](app:weather). See [Appearance](help:t/appearance).
- After you play a station in [Radio](app:radio), a radio chip shows what's on. The radio keeps playing while you use other apps.

## Open and close apps
1. Tap or click `orOS` to open the menu.
2. Open a category and tap or click an app, or type its name in `Search apps…`.
3. The app fills the screen.
4. To go back to the desktop, tap or click `Back`, or press [[Esc]].

Only one app is open at a time: going back to the desktop closes it.

## Where your data lives
Your data is saved in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you ask for it:
- With [sync](help:t/sync) you can connect your own Dropbox and keep your devices in step. Your data is encrypted on your device first.
- With `Export data` in the menu you can save everything to a file, and bring it back with `Import data`. See [Backup](help:t/backup).

More in [Your data and files](help:t/data).

>! If you clear this site's data in your browser settings, your orOS data on this device is gone. Keep a backup or turn on sync.
