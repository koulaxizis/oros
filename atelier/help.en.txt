# Atelier
Design posts, stories, flyers, posters, cards and more, from a blank page or a ready template, and download them as PNG, JPG, PDF or PowerPoint (to keep working on them in Canva).

## Getting started
Under `What will you make?` tap or click a size, for example a square post or an A4 poster, or choose `Custom size` and enter your own width and height in pixels (screen) or millimetres (print). You can also start from one of the 50 designs under `Start from a template` (social posts, stories, presentations, posters, flyers, business cards, invitations, CVs, certificates, menus, charts, logos): pick a kind with the buttons above them or type in `Search templates` (English or Greek, with or without accents), and `Show all` lists the rest. Their texts appear in your language. Your work is saved as you go and shows up under `Your designs`.

## Add and edit elements
- The side panel (the bottom tabs on a phone) has `Templates`, `Elements`, `Text`, `AI`, `Sources`, `Uploads` and `Background`.
- `Elements` has shapes, lines and arrows, and icons you can search in English or Greek.
- `Text` adds a heading, a subheading or body text, and has ready `Styles` such as neon, shadow and curved text.
- `Elements` › `Tables and charts` adds a table or a column, bar, line, pie or donut chart. `Edit table` and `Edit chart` open a grid to type in (you can paste cells from a spreadsheet); a table has a header row, bold first column, banded rows, lines and column widths, a chart a title, a legend and `Series in` columns or rows. The bar above the canvas sets the font, size and colours. A table shrinks its text to fit its box.
- The `Templates` tab has the same library with its kinds and search; a template replaces what is on the current page.
- Tap or click an element to select it. Drag it to move, use the handles to resize, and the round handle below it to rotate. Guides help you line things up.
- Tap a selected text again, or double-click it, to type in it.
- The bar above the canvas changes with what you selected: font, size, colour, `Effects`, `Transparency`, `Position`, `Lock`, `Duplicate` and `Delete`.
- The font button of a text opens `Fonts`: Noto Sans, Serif and Mono are always available, and `More fonts (Fontsource)` has hundreds of free fonts (tick `Only fonts with Greek letters` for Greek text). A font downloads the first time you use it and then works offline on this device; letters it lacks show in Sans. Another device downloads it when it opens the design.

## Photos
- `Uploads` › `Upload an image` adds a photo from this device or from Files.
- With a photo selected you get `Filters`, `Adjust` (brightness, contrast, warmth and more), `Shape` to cut it into a circle, star or other shape, `Flip` and `Replace`.
- `Crop`, or a double-click on the photo, lets you move and zoom the photo inside its frame.
- `Background` can use the selected photo as the page background.
- `Edit in Photo Lab` opens the photo in Photo Lab for deeper changes (layers, retouching, selections). Press `Done` there and you come back to the design with the new picture in the same frame; the Photo Lab project stays, so you can change it again later. `Undo` brings back the earlier picture.

## Free photos, illustrations and icons
`Sources` searches open libraries: Openverse and Wikimedia Commons for photos and illustrations, Iconify for icons and clipart. Pixabay and Pexels appear too once you add your own free key under `Pixabay and Pexels keys`. Tap or click a result to add it.
- By default you only see pieces you may use for anything, also commercially, and change. `Show licences with limits` adds the rest, marked `Limits`.
- What you add is downloaded once and saved with your design, so it works offline afterwards. Its author and licence go to `More` › `Credits`: keep them with your design when a licence asks for credit.
- Searching needs an internet connection.

## Pages
The strip under the canvas shows the pages of your design. `+` adds a page; each page has `Duplicate page`, `Move left`, `Move right` and `Delete page`.

## Brand kit
- `Brand kit` in the side bar keeps your colours, fonts and logos, and syncs them to all your devices.
- Colours: `Add colour` or `From this design`; tap a colour to apply it to the selection. They also appear first in every colour panel as `Brand colours`.
- Fonts: select a text and tap `Use selected` next to `Heading`, `Subheading` or `Body text`. The `Text` panel then adds text in these fonts.
- Logos: `Add logo` keeps a picture; tap it to place it. `Edit` shows `×` to remove colours and logos.
- Swatches: `Import swatches (.ase, .aco)` adds the colours of a swatch file from Illustrator, Photoshop or InDesign (groups are flattened, CMYK and Lab become screen colours). `Save as swatches (.ase)` saves your brand colours as one group that Adobe apps can load. No Adobe account is needed.

## Animate and present
- Select an element and tap or click `Animate` to give it an entrance: `Fade`, `Rise`, `Pop`, `Wipe` or, for text, `Typewriter`. Elements enter one after the other, bottom layer first.
- With nothing selected, `Animate` sets how long the page shows and the transition to it (`Fade`, `Slide`, `Push`, `Zoom` or `None`).
- `Play` previews the page's animation on the canvas.
- `More` › `Present (full screen)` shows the pages as a slide show: arrow keys, a clicker or a swipe move between pages, [[Esc]] ends it.

## Video and sound
- `Uploads` › `Add video or sound` takes an MP4, WebM, MP3, M4A, OGG or WAV file (videos up to 100 MB, sounds up to 30 MB). A video becomes an element you can move, crop, mask, filter and animate like a photo; a sound becomes the sound of the current page.
- Select a video and tap `Video` to trim it, turn its sound off, set its volume, choose whether it plays again when it ends, or make the page show for as long as the video.
- With nothing selected, `Animate` › `Page sound` sets the volume and where the sound starts. `Use on all pages` gives every page the same sound, which then plays on from page to page.
- Videos and sounds play in `Play` and in `Download` › `Video` (with their sound). A GIF shows the moving video without sound. The slide show plays the videos and the page sound too, and runs the entrance animations of each page. Pictures, PDF and PowerPoint use the video's first frame.
- Video and sound files stay on your orOS disk and sync with Vault Drive like your photos, so large files take space on every device.

## Bulk create
- Write fields in curly brackets in a text box or a table, like {name} or {date}, then choose `More` › `Bulk create…`.
- Paste a list (a table copied from a spreadsheet, or one name per line) or open a CSV or Excel file. The first row has the column names; a column fills the field with the same name, and you can change which column goes where.
- Choose a separate design for each row, or one design with a page for each row, ready to save as a single PDF with `Download`.
- A name too long for its box gets a smaller text size, down to 60%. Up to 200 rows at a time.

## Download and share
- `Download` makes a PNG (normal, ×2 or ×3, optionally with a transparent background), a JPG, a PDF for printing at 150 or 300 dpi, or a PowerPoint file (see below).
- In the PDF, texts are real text in the embedded fonts (you can select, search and copy them) and shapes, lines and tables stay sharp at any zoom. Photos, icons, charts, gradients and text effects a PDF can't express (glow, blur) become pictures at the chosen 150 or 300 dpi, element by element; a text with a letter the fonts lack (an emoji, for example) also becomes a picture. Tick `Whole pages as pictures` for the old exact-look PDF.
- `Video` (MP4 or WebM, whichever your browser records) and `GIF` turn the pages into a film with their animations and transitions. A video records in real time, so keep the tab open until it is saved.
- `More` › `Resize…` makes the design in other sizes, for example a square post as a story and a banner. Tick one or more sizes (or a custom size): the elements are placed again for each one, so things at the edges stay at the edges, bars and photos across the page stretch, and nothing is distorted. With `Keep this design, make a copy in each size` on, each size becomes a new design next to this one (in the same folder); with it off, this design itself changes size (`Undo` brings it back).
- `More` › `Make a book mockup` sends the design to Mockups as the cover of a 3D book: page 1 is the front, page 2 the back, page 3 the spine, and one wide page is a full wrap. In Mockups, `Open in Atelier` brings the finished picture back as a new design.
- `More` › `Save as package (.orosdesign)` makes one file with the design, its images and clips. It is an open ZIP file: unzip it and you find `content.json` (the design), `media` (its pictures and clips) and a thumbnail.

## AI
- The `AI` tab writes for you. `Ideas for this page` gives three ideas with new words for every text box of the page (and new colours, if `New colours too` is ticked), keeping the layout: tell it what the design is about, then tap an idea to try it, or another one; `Undo` brings back your own.
- `Write a new text` adds a text box with words written from what you ask. A selected text has `Write with AI` in the bar above the canvas: write it for me, shorter, longer, fix spelling, more formal, friendlier, or translate between Greek and English. You see the answer first and choose `Use this`.
- The words come from the AI you set up in the Assistant app: your own key for Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini and others, a free online provider, a model server on your computer, the browser's built-in model, or the Assistant's in-browser model (WebLLM). Only the text in question goes to it, straight from this device; with the browser's models nothing leaves the device. An in-browser model is downloaded once in the Assistant (Settings › In-browser model) and needs WebGPU (a recent Chrome or Edge); the first answer after opening Atelier takes a little longer while the model loads.
- `Magic eraser` (on a selected photo, or in the `AI` tab): paint over something with your finger or the mouse and tap `Erase`; it is filled in from what is around it. Paint and erase as often as you like, then `Use this`. It works best on small and medium things (a person in the background, a cable, a sign); a very large area comes out blurred.
- `Magic grab` makes the photo's main subject an element of its own on top, which you can move, resize or delete, and fills in the hole it leaves in the photo. `Undo` puts everything back.
- Both run on this device and the picture is not sent anywhere. The first time they download the tools (about 40 MB, including the background tool), then work offline; `Remove the picture tools` in the `AI` tab frees the space.
- `Remove background` (on a selected photo, or in the `AI` tab) keeps the main subject and makes the rest transparent. It runs on this device and the picture is not sent anywhere. The first time it downloads the tool (about 16 MB), then works offline; `Keep more · keep less` adjusts the edge before you apply it. The original picture stays in `Uploads`, and `Undo` brings it back.

## Import from Canva and other files
`Import` on the home screen offers `From a file (.pptx, picture, package)…`, `From an unzipped package folder…` (a .orosdesign that was unzipped, even after you changed a picture in it) and `From your Canva account…`.

With `From your Canva account…` you connect once (`Connect to Canva` opens a Canva window where you sign in and allow access), then tick the designs you want and tap `Import`. Each one is exported from Canva as PowerPoint and opens as a new design; designs already brought over show `imported`. Ticking `Replace designs imported before` swaps an earlier copy for the new one (changes made to that copy here are lost). If some designs fail, the message says why and `Try the failed ones again` retries just those; a design Canva cannot export as PowerPoint (some video designs and Docs) cannot come over this way. Your Canva sign-in stays on this device; `Disconnect` forgets it. This needs the internet and the orOS relay with its Canva connection set up.

A file opens as a new design:
- An Atelier package (.orosdesign).
- A PowerPoint file (.pptx). To bring a Canva design over, open it in Canva and choose `Share` › `Download` › `Microsoft PowerPoint`, then import that file. Texts stay editable, shapes, lines and photos come over, one page per slide.
- A picture (PNG, JPG, WebP or SVG) becomes a design of its size.

>! Some things do not come over from PowerPoint: charts, tables, video, animations and some text effects. A font comes over as the same free font from Fontsource when there is one (it downloads the first time); others, such as Canva Sans or Arial, become the closest of Atelier's own fonts. The message after the import says how many elements were left out.

## Take a design to Canva or PowerPoint
`Download` › `PowerPoint` saves the design as a .pptx file, one slide per page, at the design's own size. In Canva choose `Create a design` › `Upload` (or drag the file onto Canva's home page); PowerPoint, Keynote, Google Slides and LibreOffice open it directly. No account or key is needed.
- Texts stay editable text boxes with their font, size, colour, alignment, line height and letter spacing; a shadow stays a shadow.
- Shapes, lines with their arrow heads, photos with their crop, shape and transparency, and the page background stay editable.
- Icons, curved text, other text effects (neon, outline, echo and so on), photos with a filter or adjustments, tables, charts and videos come as pictures, so they look the same but can't be edited there. The message after saving says how many.
>! The other app uses its own copy of each font: if it doesn't have it, it shows a similar one and a text may wrap a little differently. Animations, page timing and sounds stay in Atelier.
- `More` › `Credits` lists the work by others your design uses.

## Sync and backup
Designs sync with orOS sync. Uploaded images are stored once on the orOS disk and sync with Vault Drive like your other files; on another device a photo may show `Syncing…` until it arrives.
- **Folders:** the row above your designs shows `All` and your folders; `+ New folder` makes one. To put designs in a folder, tap `Select`, pick them and tap `Move`, or use a design's `⋯` › `Move to folder…`. A design you make while a folder is open goes into it. The `⋯` next to the open folder renames or deletes it; deleting a folder keeps its designs. Folders sync to your other devices.
- To delete several designs at once, tap `Select` above `Your designs`, pick them (or `Select all`, or `From Canva` for every design a Canva import made) and tap `Delete`. `Undo` in the message brings them all back.
- `Files` (also `Uploads` › `Manage files…`) lists every picture, video and sound on this device, biggest first. Files no design uses can be selected and deleted to free space; files still in a design, here or in Slides or Layout, cannot.

## Keyboard shortcuts
- [[Ctrl+Z]] undo, [[Ctrl+Shift+Z]] or [[Ctrl+Y]] redo.
- [[Ctrl+C]], [[Ctrl+X]], [[Ctrl+V]] copy, cut and paste; [[Ctrl+D]] duplicate; [[Ctrl+A]] select all.
- Arrow keys move the selection, with [[Shift]] in bigger steps. [[Delete]] removes it.
- [[Enter]] edits the selected text or crops the selected photo; [[Esc]] ends editing or clears the selection.
- [[Ctrl+=]] and [[Ctrl+-]] zoom; [[Ctrl+0]] fits the page to the screen. Hold [[Space]] and drag to move around.

## Tips
> With a mouse, drag across an empty part of the page to select several elements with a box; Shift-click adds to the selection.
> Corner handles keep the proportions; on text they also change the font size.

## Limits
- In the PDF, charts, icons, photos and effects such as glow and blur are pictures, and a text with a letter none of the fonts has (for example an emoji) is a picture of that text; curved text is real text letter by letter.
- A table has up to 30 rows and 10 columns; a chart up to 30 rows and 8 series. Cells can't be merged, and a chart's colours follow the theme (you can't colour one bar).
- Tables and charts made here don't show in an older version of orOS on another device until it updates, and changing the design there before it updates can remove them.
- A design made with a newer orOS shows `Needs a newer orOS` and does not open until this device updates. An element of a kind this version does not know shows as a grey box with the same words: it is kept as it is, and you can't select or export it.
