# Roots
Roots is a turn-based growing game for one person. You are an old tree in a glade. Under the ground you grow a network of roots from the trunk to reach springs and minerals, and on the surface you grow buds that open into leaves and flowers. Pests come in at the edges through the year, and every one of them shows what it will do before it acts. There are no dice: you answer what you see coming.

## How to play
The glade has 7 by 9 tiles: the surface on top and the soil below. A season lasts 6 turns. In each turn:
1. **You get sap.** 2 from the trunk, 2 for each spring and 1 for each mineral that a joined root reaches, and 1 for each open leaf above a joined root. The status row shows your sap and what the next turn brings (at most 20).
2. **You grow.** Choose a growth in the bar at the bottom, then tap a lit tile:
   - **Root** (1 sap, 2 on clay): a thin root on a tile next to your network. Stone stops roots.
   - **Thicken** (2): a thick root takes a bite and stays; a thin one breaks.
   - **Thorn** (3): at the end of the turn it hurts every pest on it or next to it, 2 when a mineral is under it or beside it.
   - **Leaf bud** and **Flower bud** (2): on the surface above a joined root. A bud opens after 2 turns. A leaf gives 1 sap a turn; a flower counts 1 bloom.
3. **End turn.** Thorns strike, the pests act in the order shown, cut roots count down, buds grow, and new pests come in.

`Undo` takes back the last growth, and `Reset turn` goes back to the start of the turn. Both are free, so try things out.

## The key rule
Everything counts only while it is joined to the trunk by an unbroken line of roots. A root that is cut off gives no sap, a bud above it does not grow, and after 2 turns it withers (a red number shows the turns left). Join it again in time and nothing is lost. So the question is never only where to grow: it is also which line to make thicker, where to grow a second way round, and which far branch to let go.

## The bloom
At the end of each season the tree needs its **bloom quota**: open flowers on the surface plus the springs a joined root reaches. Miss it and the year ends. Flowers fall between seasons; roots, leaves and springs stay. The quota comes from a built-in gardener that plays the season first, so every season can be met.

## The pests
- **Beetle:** walks up to 2 tiles through the soil to the nearest root and chews it. A thin root breaks, a thick one turns thin. Leaves after 3 turns.
- **Mole:** digs a straight line of up to 3 tiles and harms every root in it. Leaves after 2 turns.
- **Aphid:** walks along the surface to the nearest bud and eats it. Open leaves and flowers are safe. Leaves after 3 turns.
- **Blight** (autumn; in the old forest every season): spreads to a thin root next to it every turn and stays. Thick roots and thorns stop it; a thorn kills it.
- **Frost:** in the last 2 turns of autumn, buds with no open leaf beside them freeze.

An arrow points from each pest to where it goes, with an icon on the tile it will harm and a number for the order. Tiles in danger are red, and dashed circles at the edges show the pests that come in at the end of the turn. The base root under the trunk never breaks.

## The year and difficulty
A year moves through four glades, and each glade has 3 seasons: spring, summer and autumn. When the third season of a glade meets its quota, the tree seeds the next glade and starts again there as a bare tree with 5 sap. Winter scores the year: the bloom of every season, plus the springs reached and the longest root (the most steps from the trunk along your roots) of every glade.
1. **The meadow:** soft soil and three springs; beetles and aphids, moles from summer, blight in autumn.
2. **The riverbank:** five springs, little stone and a lot of clay; moles and aphids all year.
3. **The rocky hill:** stone everywhere, two deep springs and many minerals; beetles in numbers.
4. **The old forest:** blight in every season, and every pest at once.

The sky's colour shows the glade you are in.
- **Mild:** a lower quota, and once a season `Rewind` takes back the whole last turn.
- **Hardy:** more pests and a higher quota.

Every year has a code. The same code is the same glade and the same pests, so you can play a year again or give the code to a friend.

## The Daily glade
The calendar button in the toolbar opens the **Daily glade**: one season on Hardy from a bare tree, the same glade, season and pests for everyone that day. There is one try. Meet the bloom quota and the day counts in your streak; the score is the season's bloom, plus the springs reached, plus the longest root. A dot on the button means today's glade is still open. When it is done, the button shows your result, with `Share` (a spoiler-free line of flowers, the score and whether the quota was met) and `All games` for the [Game Center](help:a/gamecenter), where it shows under Today. Leaving today's glade before its end counts as a missed quota, and a year under way left for it counts as not finished.

## Last years
`Records` lists your last 10 years and Daily glades from every device, newest first: the difficulty, how far you got, the score, the day and the code. `Replay` starts that year again with its code; a Daily glade replays as a whole Hardy year on its glade.

## Records and sync
`Records` shows your years, the years you finished and the best year score for each difficulty, your longest root, your Daily glades (played, quota met, streak and best score) and Last years. They sync between your devices through [orOS sync](help:t/sync), adding up the years from every device, and they count in the [Game Center](help:a/gamecenter). The year in progress and the sound setting stay on this device, and an unfinished year continues where you left it. A year left for a new one counts as not finished. `Reset records` clears the records on every device.

## Keyboard
- Arrow keys move the cursor; [[Enter]] grows on the tile or describes it.
- [[1]] to [[5]]: root, thicken, thorn, leaf bud, flower bud.
- [[Z]]: undo. [[R]]: reset turn. [[E]]: end turn (or the next season). [[T]]: threats only. [[N]]: new year.
- A long press on any tile shows its text.
- The warning sign in the toolbar shows only the tiles in danger; the double arrow makes the end of a turn play faster.

## Tips
- Reach a spring early: 2 sap a turn and a bloom every season.
- A second way round costs little and saves a whole branch when a beetle bites.
- Thicken the root just behind a fork: one bite there would cut everything beyond it.
- Put thorns next to minerals, where they hit twice as hard.
- In autumn, grow leaves between your flower buds: they keep the frost away.
