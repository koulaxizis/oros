# Settings
Settings gathers the options of orOS itself in one place: how it looks, notifications, sync, backups, language and search, and the system tools. Each option still works exactly as before; it just lives here now instead of in the menu.

## Getting started
1. Open the menu and choose `Settings`, at the bottom under the apps. You'll also find it in the `System` category.
2. Pick a section on the left: `Appearance`, `Menu`, `Notifications`, `Sync`, `Backups`, `Language & search`, `Opening files`, `Connected services` or `System`.
3. Change what you like. Every change applies at once; there is no save button.

On a phone, Settings first shows the list of sections. Tap one to open it, and `Back` takes you to the list again.

Next to each card you'll see where it applies: `All devices` means it travels with [sync](help:t/sync), `This device` means it stays on this device only.

## The sections
- `Appearance`: `Theme` (`Dark` or `Light`), `System colour`, `Wallpaper`, the `Dock` when your orOS has one, and the `Screen pet`. See [Appearance](help:t/appearance).
- `Menu`: which apps the menu lists. Untick an app to hide it, or use `Hide all` and `Show all`; `Find an app` narrows the list. Hidden apps still open from the menu search, links, the desktop and the Dock. See [The menu](help:t/menu).
- `Notifications`: the `Notifications` switch, `Send a test`, position, style, sound, `Quiet hours` and `Which apps may notify`. See [Notifications](help:t/notifications).
- `Sync`: `Connect to Dropbox`, your `Passphrase`, `Get from cloud`, `Send to cloud`, `Sync automatically` and `Disconnect`. See [Sync across devices](help:t/sync).
- `Backups`: `Automatic backup`, `Backup folder`, `Back up now`, `Export backup file` and `Import backup file`. See [Backup and restore](help:t/backup).
- `Language & search`: the `Language` of orOS and which apps the menu search looks in.
- `Opening files`: which orOS app opens which file type from your device (`Open with`), the choice for types more than one app opens, and how to make orOS the default on Windows, Linux and Mac.
- `Connected services`: platforms orOS works with: a WooCommerce shop (used in [Stock](app:stock)) a timologisi.online account (used in [Invoices](app:invoices) to send documents to myDATA), MailerLite, Mailchimp or Brevo accounts (used in [Newsletter](app:newsletter) to sync a list's subscribers) HubSpot or Pipedrive accounts (read by [CRM](app:crm) to bring in customers and deals) a Stripe account (read by [Budget](app:budget) to bring in payments) an Invoice Ninja account (read by CRM for clients and by Budget for payments) an Odoo (read by CRM for contacts and opportunities) a Todoist account (read by [To-Do](app:todo) for projects and open tasks) Trello or GitHub accounts (read by [Kanban](app:kanban) to bring in a board or a repository's issues) and a Raindrop.io account (read by [Bookmarks](app:bookmarks) for collections and bookmarks). `Connect a service` asks first for the platform. For WooCommerce: the shop address and the two REST API keys (WooCommerce › Settings › Advanced › REST API › Add key, Read/Write). For timologisi.online: `Test (sandbox)` or `Live (real documents)`, the issuer's ΑΦΜ and the API key from its developer portal; `Check connection` shows the credits left. For MailerLite, Mailchimp and Brevo: the API key (MailerLite calls it a token). For HubSpot: the access token of a private app with read scopes for contacts, companies and deals (it starts with pat-). For Pipedrive: your personal API token (Personal preferences › API). For Stripe: a restricted key with Read access to Balance only (rk_live_…); a secret key (sk_) is refused. For Invoice Ninja: its address (invoicing.co for the hosted service, or your own install) and an API token (Settings › Account management › Integrations › API tokens). For Odoo (19 or Odoo Online): its address, the database only if the server has several, and an API key (your avatar › My Preferences › Account Security › New API Key). For Todoist: your API token (Settings › Integrations › Developer). For Trello: an API key and a token (trello.com/power-ups/admin › New › API key, then the Token link next to it). For GitHub: a personal access token, best a fine-grained one with read-only access to Issues of the repositories you want (Settings › Developer settings › Personal access tokens). For Raindrop.io: a test token (Settings › Integrations › For developers › Create new app › Create test token). Each account shows its `Route` (straight from this browser, or through the orOS relay when the shop doesn't answer browsers), `Check connection`, `Enter the keys`, `Rename`, `Use these keys on all my devices` and `Disconnect`.
- `System`: the `orOS version`, `Check for updates`, `Install orOS as an app` (when your browser offers it), `Keyboard shortcuts`, links to [Device info](app:device) and more, and `Factory reset`.

### Other ways in
- Tap the sync dot in the top bar while sync isn't connected or is locked: Settings opens at `Sync`.
- The `About orOS + shortcuts` window has a button that opens `System`, where the factory reset is.

## Sync and backup
Settings stores nothing of its own. Every option is saved by orOS in the same place as before, so your choices sync and back up exactly as they always did. Theme, colour, wallpaper, the apps the menu shows, screen pet, notifications, sync interval, automatic backup and language go to all your devices. The backup folder, your remembered passphrase, the menu search choices and the Dock stay on this device.

### Connected services and your keys
The keys of a connected service never go into your synced data in clear. Each device keeps them sealed with its own key; a new device asks for them once. With `Use these keys on all my devices` on, they also travel inside your encrypted sync, sealed with your sync passphrase. The account itself (name, address) syncs to all your devices. `Disconnect` removes it and its keys everywhere.

## Keyboard shortcuts
- [[Esc]] on a phone-sized screen goes back from a section to the list.
- The orOS shortcuts are listed under `System` and in [Keyboard shortcuts](help:t/shortcuts).

## Tips
- Buttons that can't be undone, such as `Disconnect` and `Factory reset`, ask you to tap a second time. If you don't, they go back to normal after a few seconds.
- Some apps keep their own settings inside the app, for example the weather in the top bar, which you set in [Weather](app:weather). `System` links to them under `More`.

## Limits
- Opening files from your device works with orOS installed from Chrome or Edge on a computer. On Android, use Share › orOS from Files, Gmail or any app. Firefox and Safari can't hand files to orOS yet: use the Import button inside the app.
- Settings works only inside the orOS desktop. Opened on its own, it asks you to open it from the orOS menu.
- The `Backup folder` options appear only in browsers that can write to a folder, such as Chrome and Edge on a computer.
