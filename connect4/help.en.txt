# Connect 4
Drop discs into a board of 7 columns and 6 rows. The first to get four in a row, across, down or diagonally, wins. Play against the computer or with a friend on one device.

## How to play
Tap or click a column to drop your disc; it falls to the lowest free space. Take turns until someone lines up four discs or the board fills up (a draw).

Choose `vs Computer` or `2 players` in the toolbar. Against the computer, pick who makes the `First move`: `You start`, `Computer starts` or `Take turns`. In 2-player games, the players alternate who starts and a running series score is kept.

## Controls
- Tap or click a column to play there. On a desktop, hovering over a column shows where the disc will land.
- `Undo move (Z)` takes back the last move. Against the computer it goes back to your turn. You can't undo after the game ends.
- `New game (N)` starts again. If a game was under way, an `Undo` button appears for a few seconds to bring it back.
- The speaker button turns sound on or off (off at first).
- Keys: [[1]] to [[7]] drop a disc in that column, the left and right arrows move between columns and [[Enter]] or [[Space]] plays the focused one, [[N]] is a new game, and [[Z]] or [[Ctrl+Z]] undoes.

## Levels and scoring
The computer has three levels: `Easy` takes a win when it sees one but otherwise plays loosely; `Medium` looks a few moves ahead; `Hard` looks much deeper and is tough to beat.

`Records` shows your wins, losses and draws against the computer for each level (`Won · lost · drawn`), and the 2-player series. `Reset records` clears them after you confirm.

## Two devices
Play a friend who has their own phone, tablet or computer. Press `Play on two devices` (the button with two screens): one of you chooses `Show a code`, the other `Enter a code` and types the 6-digit code or scans the QR code, and the first device lets the other in with `Allow`. While you're linked, `Two devices` shows in the toolbar; press it to see the link or to `Leave the game`.
- The device that showed the code plays first in the first game; after that the first move alternates, and the series score shows on both devices.
- Each device plays only on its own turn. `New game` on the device that showed the code starts the next game (it asks first if a game is under way); on the other device it asks for one when a game is over.
- If a device reloads or loses the network, the game waits and goes on where it was when it's back (for up to a day).
- Moves go straight between the two devices on the same network, or sealed through the orOS relay; nothing is kept online.

Limits: two players only, each on their own device with orOS open in this game. Games on two devices don't count in your records, and the level buttons are hidden meanwhile. `Leave the game` ends it on both devices and brings back the game you had on this device.

## Sync
Your records against the computer sync between your devices through [orOS sync](help:t/sync), and a reset clears them everywhere. The game in progress, the 2-player series, your settings and the sound setting stay on this device. A game you leave half-way is still there when you come back.

## Tips
- Columns near the middle take part in more lines of four, so they are usually the best places to start.
- Watch for two threats at once: the other side can block only one of them.
