# Tetris
Falling blocks drop into a 10×20 field. Move and turn them to fill whole rows: a full row clears. The game ends when the stack reaches the top.

## How to play
Pick a start level and press `Start` ([[Space]]). Pieces fall one at a time, and they speed up as the level rises.

- `Next` shows the next three pieces.
- A ghost outline shows where the piece will land.
- `Hold` puts the current piece aside and brings it back later. You can hold once per piece.
- A piece locks half a second after it lands; moving or turning it buys a little more time.

## Controls
- [[←]] [[→]] move; [[↓]] drops faster (soft drop).
- [[↑]] or [[X]] turns right; [[Z]] turns left.
- [[Space]] drops the piece at once (hard drop).
- [[C]] or [[Shift]] holds; you can also tap or click the `Hold` box.
- [[P]] or [[Esc]] pauses and resumes. After a pause, a 3-2-1 countdown runs.
- [[N]] or `New game (N)` starts a new game. If you leave a game in progress, an `Undo` button appears for a moment to get it back.

On a touch screen, tap the field to turn, drag to move, swipe down to drop and swipe up to hold. Buttons under the field also move, turn and drop.

If you switch to another app, the game pauses. A game in progress is kept on this device and waits, paused, for next time.

## Levels and scoring
Start at `Level 1`, `Level 5` or `Level 10`; changing it starts a new game. The level then rises every 10 lines.

- Clearing 1, 2, 3 or 4 lines at once scores 100, 300, 500 or 800 points, times the level.
- A four-line clear (a Tetris) right after another scores 1.5 times as much.
- A soft drop earns 1 point per row, a hard drop 2.

`Records` shows, for each start level, your best score, the most lines and the games played.

## Sync
Your records sync between your devices through [orOS sync](help:t/sync). `Reset records` in `Records` deletes them on every device, after you confirm. The start level, the game in progress and the sound button stay on this device.

## Tips
- Keep one column open on the side and save it for the long straight piece: Tetrises score the most.
