# Backgammon
Standard backgammon (Πόρτες in Greek Τάβλι): race your 15 checkers round the board and bear them all off first. Play the computer or a friend on the same device.

## How to play
To start, each side rolls one die and the higher one plays both. After that, roll two dice on your turn and move one checker for each die; a double is played four times.

- A single opposing checker (a blot) can be hit: it goes to the bar, and its owner must bring it back in before moving anything else.
- Once all your checkers are in your home board, bear them off with the exact die, or with a higher die from your highest point.
- You must use as many dice as you can; if only one of the two can be played, it has to be the larger one. With no legal move, the turn passes.

The first to bear off all 15 wins 1 point, a gammon (2 points) if the other side hasn't borne off any, and a backgammon (3 points) if the other side also still has a checker on the bar or in the winner's home board.

## Controls
Press `Roll`, then tap or click one of your checkers and then one of the lit destinations, or drag the checker there. `Undo` takes back any step of your turn, and `Done` ends it. The board shows each side's pip count.

The toolbar has `vs Computer` or `2 players` and the level. Against the computer you play White.

Keyboard:
- [[Space]] or [[R]] rolls; arrow keys step through the checkers you can move and then their destinations; [[Enter]] moves; [[Esc]] cancels.
- [[Enter]] ends the turn; [[Z]], [[Backspace]] or [[Ctrl+Z]] undo a step; [[N]] new game.

## Levels and scoring
The computer plays at `Easy`, `Medium` or `Hard`. `Records` shows your wins and losses against it for each level, with the points won and lost. With `2 players` the result window keeps a running `Points` score. `Reset records` clears them.

## Sync
Your records against the computer sync between your devices through [orOS sync](help:t/sync), adding up the games from every device; `Reset records` clears them everywhere. The game in progress, the 2-player score, your settings and the sound setting stay on this device.

## Tips
- Avoid leaving single checkers within easy reach.
- There's no doubling cube.
