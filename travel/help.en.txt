# Travel
Travel keeps each trip in one place: dates and travellers, a packing list that adjusts to the length of the trip, a day-by-day itinerary and your notes.

## Getting started
Tap or click `New trip`. Fill in `Name`, `Destination`, the dates (`From`, `To`) and `Travellers (comma-separated)`. Under `Start the packing list from`, tick the templates you want; `Essentials` and `Before I leave` are ticked for you. Press `Create`.

The trip list groups your trips as `Travelling now`, `Upcoming`, `No dates yet` and `Past trips`, each with a countdown and how much is packed. `Back` (or [[Esc]]) returns to the list.

## Packing
Type in `Add an item…` and press [[Enter]]. Tick items as they go in the suitcase. Tap or click an item to change its `Quantity`, `Group`, `Who takes it` and `Note`, or delete it (with `Undo`).

Quantities can follow the trip: choose `Per day` or `One every N days` instead of `Fixed`, and the number changes with the trip's dates.

- `Still to pack` hides what's already packed.
- `Show the items of` filters by traveller.
- `From a template` adds a template's items; only what's missing is added.
- `Uncheck all` clears every tick, handy for the trip home.

## Itinerary
`Add to the itinerary` adds a `Flight`, `Train`, `Bus`, `Ferry`, `Car`, `Stay`, `Activity`, `Food` or `Other`, with its day, times, places and `Booking code`. Entries are grouped by day. A `Stay` shows on every night it covers, and nights with no stay are flagged. Places have `Open in Maps`, which opens them in [Maps](app:maps).

`Notes` is free text for addresses, phone numbers or the Wi-Fi password. On a wide screen, packing and itinerary show side by side.

## Templates
`Templates` lists 10 ready ones (`Essentials`, `Beach`, `City break`, `Business`, `Hiking & camping`, `Ski`, `With a baby`, `Carry-on only`, `Road trip`, `Before I leave`) and `My templates`. You can edit or hide a ready template; `Restore ready templates` brings them back as they were. `New template` makes your own, or use `Save as template` from a trip.

## Forecast, reminders and fuel cost
- From 7 days before a trip, the trip page shows the `Forecast for` your destination, day by day. If rain is likely, orOS asks `Add an umbrella?`; `Add` puts `Umbrella` on the packing list. The forecast comes from Open-Meteo and is kept on this device for 3 hours.
- orOS reminds you the evening before you leave (from 18:00), 3 hours before a flight and 1 hour before a train, bus or ferry. The reminder opens the trip. `More` on the trip list has `Turn reminders off` and `Turn reminders on`, for this device only. See also [Notifications](help:t/notifications).
- `Fuel cost by car` in a trip's `More` menu works out the cost of driving: enter `Distance one way (km)`, tick `Round trip` if you need it, and the consumption and price (per litre, or per kWh for an electric car). If you log fill-ups in [Garage](app:garage), pick the car under `Vehicle (from Garage)` and the consumption and last price are filled in. You see the total and the cost per person; `Add to notes` saves it in the trip notes.
- Trips also show in [Calendar](app:calendar) under the `Travel` chip: each trip day and every itinerary entry with a time. Tapping one opens the trip here.

## Share and export
A trip's `More` menu has `Edit trip`, `Copy as text` (`Share as text` on a phone), `Export for a calendar (.ics)`, `Fuel cost by car`, `Print`, `Copy trip` (with new dates, nothing packed) and `Delete trip`.

The same menu can send `Send “Before I leave” to To-Do` or `Send “Still to pack” to To-Do`: the open items become a new list in [To-Do](app:todo), which shows them before anything is added. It's a copy, so ticking in To-Do doesn't change the trip.

On the trip list, `More` has `Export all (JSON)` and `Import (JSON)`. An import merges with your trips and overwrites nothing.

## Sync and backup
Trips, packing lists, itineraries and your templates sync between your devices through [orOS sync](help:t/sync). A tick on your phone and a change on your computer both survive. The open trip, tab and filters stay on each device. Besides the JSON export, the orOS [backup](help:t/backup) keeps a copy.

## Keyboard shortcuts
- [[Enter]] in `Add an item…`: add the item.
- [[Esc]]: back to the trip list, or close a menu.

## Tips
- Before a regular trip, use `Copy trip` on last year's one: you keep the list and the itinerary moves to the new dates.
- Save your own favourite packing list as a template once, and start every trip from it.

## Limits
- Up to 200 trips and 60 templates.
- Reminders show only while orOS is open on that device. Use the `.ics` export to add a trip to another calendar app.
- The forecast needs a connection and a destination Open-Meteo can find.
