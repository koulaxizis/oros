# Xeri
Xeri (Ξερή) is the classic Greek card game, you against the computer. Capture cards from the table and score points.

## How to play
You each get 6 cards and 4 cards go face up on the table. On your turn, play one card:
- If it has the same rank as the top card on the table, you take the whole table.
- A Jack always takes the table, as long as it isn't empty.
- Otherwise your card stays on top of the pile.

Taking the table when it holds just one card is a **xeri**: 10 points, or 20 for a Jack on a lone Jack. New hands come from the deck. At the end of the deal, the cards left on the table go to whoever captured last.

Points per deal: 3 for the most cards (nobody gets them at 26–26), 1 for each King, Queen, Jack and 10 (the 10♦ is worth 2), 1 for the 2♣, plus your xeri points. After each deal, press `Next deal` to carry on.

## Controls
Tap or click a card in your hand, or drag it onto the table. The toolbar has the level, `Rules`, `New match (N)`, `Records` and the sound button.

In `Rules` you can set the `Match` (`To 51`, `To 101` or `One deal`), `Cards per hand` (6 or 4), and whether a `Jack on a lone card` is a `Plain capture` or a `Xeri (10)`. Rule changes apply from the next match, or press `New match now`. Starting a new match mid-game shows an `Undo` button for a few seconds.

Keyboard: [[1]] to [[6]] play that card from your hand; with a card focused, [[←]] / [[→]] move between cards and [[Enter]] or [[Space]] plays it; [[N]] starts a new match.

## Levels and scoring
The computer plays at `Easy`, `Medium` or `Hard`; changing the level starts a new match. `Records` shows, for each level, your matches and wins, your xeri and Jack-on-Jack count, and your best deal. `Reset records` clears them.

## Two devices
Play a friend who has their own phone, tablet or computer. Press `Play on two devices` (the button with two screens): one of you chooses `Show a code`, the other `Enter a code` and types the 6-digit code or scans the QR code, and the first device lets the other in with `Allow`. While you're linked, `Two devices` shows in the toolbar; press it to see the link or to `Leave the game`.
- The device that showed the code shuffles and deals and keeps the match. Each device shows only its own hand: the other hand and the deck show as the number of cards in them, never which cards they are.
- `Next deal` and a new match follow the device that showed the code; the other device asks for them.
- If a device reloads or loses the network, the game waits and goes on where it was when it's back (for up to a day).
- Moves go straight between the two devices on the same network, or sealed through the orOS relay; nothing is kept online.

Limits: two players only, each on their own device with orOS open in this game. Games on two devices don't count in your records, and the level buttons are hidden meanwhile. `Leave the game` ends it on both devices and brings back the game you had on this device.

## Sync
Your records sync between your devices through [orOS sync](help:t/sync), adding up the matches from every device; `Reset records` clears them everywhere. The match in progress, your level and rules, and the sound setting stay on this device.

## Tips
- Save your Jacks for a big pile.
