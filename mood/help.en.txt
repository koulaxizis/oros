# Mood
Mood lets you log how you feel in a few seconds, with where you are, who you're with and a few daily habits. Over time, Insights shows you patterns.

## Getting started
The three buttons at the top switch between `Capture` (the pencil), `Entries` (the list) and `Insights` (the bar chart). Under the top bar, a row of coloured dots shows your last 7 days.

To log a mood, open `Capture`, tap or click one or more feelings under `How do you feel?`, and press `Save entry`. That's the only required step.

## Log an entry
- **Feelings:** nine to start with, from `Happy` to `Numb`, plus any you add yourself. Each one you pick gets a slider from 1 to 5 for how strong it is.
- **`Where & with whom`:** pick a `Location` and a `Person`, or `None`. The app pre-selects the value you use most at this time of day.
- **`The basics` and `Rituals`:** pairs such as `Slept well` / `Didn't sleep enough` or `Went for a walk` / `Stayed in`. Tap one side; tap it again to leave it blank.
- **`Your activities`:** the activities you added yourself (see below). Tap one to mark it for this entry; tap again to unmark it.
- **`What triggered this?`:** pick one trigger, such as `Work deadline` or `Good news`.
- **`Reflection (optional)`:** a short free-text note.

`Repeat last` fills the form with your most recent entry. If you save within an hour of your last entry, Mood asks `Logged … ago — update it?`: choose `Edit entry` to fold the new picks into that entry, or `New entry` to keep both.

### Your own values
Type in a `New value…` box and press `Add…` (or [[Enter]]) to add a location, person or trigger. To rename or delete one, long-press or right-click its chip, or tap `Manage…` and then the chip. A delete shows an `Undo` button for a few seconds.

### Feelings, activities and goals
`Moods, activities & goals…`, at the bottom of `Capture`, opens three tabs:
- **`Feelings`:** rename any feeling, give it an emoji instead of its face, move it with the arrows, or hide it with the eye. Add your own with an emoji and a name, pick its colour and tick `Positive` if it should count as a good feeling in `This week vs last` and `Weekly recap`. Clear a name to go back to the original one.
- **`Activities`:** the same for the habit pairs (each side has its own name) and for your own activities.
- **`Goals`:** pick an activity and how many days a week (Monday to Sunday) or a month you want to mark it, for example `Moved / exercised` 3 times a week.

Hidden items leave `Capture` only: entries that already use them keep them, and `Insights` still counts them. A hidden feeling or activity shows up again while you edit an entry that has it. Nothing you add can be deleted, so your old entries never lose their meaning; hide what you no longer use.

## Entries
`Entries` lists everything, newest first. Use `Search entries…` to look through dates, feelings, places, people, habits, triggers and notes. The pencil opens an entry in `Capture` for editing (`Discard` cancels); the bin deletes it, with `Undo`.

## Insights
Pick `7 days`, `30 days` or `All`. You get a `Weekly recap`, your `Goals`, `Patterns` (plain-language observations), `How you felt`, your streak, `This week vs last`, `Intensity shifts`, `Habits`, `Rituals`, `Your activities`, a month `Calendar` (tap a day to see its entries), `Year in pixels`, `Locations`, `People` and `By weekday`.

- **`Goals`:** how far you are this week or month, and in how many of the last weeks or months you reached the goal. A day counts once, however many entries it has.
- **`Year in pixels`:** one square per day of the year, coloured by that day's strongest feeling. Tap a square to see that day's entries; the arrows change the year.

`Filters` narrows most of these to one location, person or habit; `Clear filters` resets. If you use [Cycle](app:cycle) on this device, `Patterns` can also tell you how you feel on period days.

`Export PDF` saves a PDF report of the range and filters you've chosen.

## Sync and backup
Your entries, your own locations, people and triggers, your feelings and activities (names, emoji, order, hidden) and your goals sync through [orOS sync](help:t/sync), encrypted. The open tab, search and filters stay on this device. Besides the PDF report there's no import or export; use the orOS [backup](help:t/backup).

## Keyboard shortcuts
- [[Enter]] in a `New value…` box adds the value.
- [[Enter]] while renaming a chip saves the name; [[Esc]] closes the menu.

## Tips
- Your entries show up in [Calendar](app:calendar) on their day; opening one there brings you back here to edit it.
- Once you have entries, orOS reminds you once a day if you haven't logged anything that day. Turn it off in [Notifications](help:t/notifications).

## Limits
- Feelings and activities can be hidden but not deleted. Your own activities have one side only (done); the built-in pairs keep their two sides.
- Goals count days with an activity marked; they can't count "no" days or the strength of a feeling.
- The [Calendar](app:calendar) shows your own feelings under their original names only for the nine built-in ones.
- `Factory reset`, at the bottom of `Insights`, erases all entries, your own values, your feelings and activities and your goals on every synced device. It asks twice, and there's no undo.
