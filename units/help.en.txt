# Unit Converter
Convert lengths, weights, temperatures, currencies and more, on the spot. Fifteen categories, units in both languages, and it works offline (currencies use the last rates the device fetched).

## Getting started
Pick a category at the top: `Length`, `Weight`, `Volume`, `Temperature`, `Area`, `Speed`, `Cooking`, `Time`, `Data`, `Fuel economy`, `Pressure`, `Energy`, `Power`, `Angle` or `Currency`. On a phone, swipe the row sideways to see them all.

Type a number in `From` and the result appears in `To` as you type. It also works the other way: type in `To` and `From` follows.
- Tap or click a unit to change it. The list has a search field: type a name or a symbol in English or Greek (`ft`, `πόδι`, `στρέμμα`, `mpg`). [[Enter]] picks the first match.
- The ⇄ button swaps the two units.
- The line under the fields shows the rule used, for example `1 mi = 1.609344 km`.
- The copy button copies the result. [[Esc]] clears the field you are in.
- `Send to…` (the paper plane next to it) sends the conversion, like `5 km = 3.107 mi`, to another app, such as Notes.

## Typing numbers
- Use a comma or a point for decimals: `1,5` and `1.5` are the same. With both, the last one is the decimal point (`1.234,5`).
- A point (in Greek) or a comma (in English) followed by exactly three digits is a thousands mark: in Greek `1.500` is one thousand five hundred.
- Simple sums work too: `2,5*3`, `12+8`, `(3+4)/2`, and `1,5e3` for 1500.
- Results are rounded to 6 significant digits; very large or small numbers are shown as `1.2 × 10⁹`. Change the digits in `Converter settings`.

## Categories worth knowing
- `Cooking` turns cups and spoons into grams and back. Pick the `Ingredient` (water, flour, sugar, butter and more): a cup of flour weighs much less than a cup of honey. These kitchen weights are approximate.
- `Fuel economy` converts L/100 km, km/L and miles per gallon (US and UK). Lower L/100 km means higher mpg, so zero has no counterpart.
- `Data` has both kinds of units: kB, MB, GB (powers of 1000, as disks and networks use) and KiB, MiB, GiB (powers of 1024).
- Traditional Greek units: the stremma (1,000 m²), the oka (1.2829 kg) and its drachm, and the builder's cubit (0.75 m).
- `Time` months and years are averages (30.44 days, 365.2425 days).

## Currencies
`Currency` uses the euro reference rates of the European Central Bank, through the free Frankfurter service. The app fetches them when you open `Currency` (at most every 6 hours) or when you tap `Update rates`, and keeps them on this device, so currencies also work offline with the last rates. The line under the fields shows the date of the rates.

>! The ECB rates come out once a day on working days. They are for information: banks and card payments use their own rates.

## All units, favourites and recent
- `All units` shows the value in every unit of the category. Tap a row to copy it. `Hide` folds the list away.
- The ☆ button adds the pair you are using to `Favourites`, the chips above the converter. Tap a chip to go straight to that pair; tap ☆ again to remove it.
- `Recent` keeps your last 20 conversions, newest first. Tap one to bring it back. `Clear` empties the list; `Undo` in the message brings it back.

## Universal search
In the orOS menu search, type a conversion such as `10 km to mi`, `72 f to c` or `5 stremma` and the answer appears under `In your data`. Tap it to open the converter with that conversion. Your favourite pairs can be found there too.

## Sync and backup
Your favourites, recent conversions and the number of digits sync between your devices through [orOS sync](help:t/sync), and the orOS [backup](help:t/backup) keeps them. The open category, the units you last used, the ingredient and the exchange rates stay on each device.

## Tips
- To compare prices in another currency, star the pair (for example `EUR → USD`) and it is one tap away.
- Use `All units` to see a distance in metres, feet and miles at once.

## Limits
- Clothing and shoe sizes are not included: they differ between brands.
- Currencies cover the about 30 currencies the European Central Bank publishes.
