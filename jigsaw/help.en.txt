# Jigsaw
Turn any picture into a jigsaw puzzle. Cut it into 12 to 150 pieces, scatter them on the table, and drag them back together.

## How to play
Press `New puzzle (N)` and pick a picture: `From this device`, `From Files` (your [Files](app:files) disk), or `Built-in pictures`. After a puzzle, `Same picture, cut again` reuses the last one. SVG pictures aren't accepted; use PNG, JPEG or WebP.

In the `New puzzle` window, drag the picture to frame it and zoom with a pinch, the wheel or the `Zoom` slider. Then choose:
- `Pieces`: 12, 24, 48, 96 or 150. On a phone, 48 or fewer are easier to handle.
- `Shape`: `Classic` pieces with tabs, or `Squares`.
- `Layout`: `Landscape` or `Portrait`.
- `Rotated pieces (tap one to turn it)`: for an extra challenge.

Press `Start`. Pieces click together when you drop them near their place or next to a piece they fit, and then move as one group. A group in its place stays put. The status line shows the time and how much is put together.

## Controls
- Drag a piece to move it. With rotation on, tap or click a piece to turn it 90°.
- Drag the empty table to move around; pinch or use the mouse wheel to zoom.
- Helpers on the toolbar: `Preview the picture (P)`, `Faint picture in the frame (G)`, `Edge pieces only (E)` and `Tidy loose pieces to the side (T)`.
- Zoom buttons: `Zoom out (−)`, `Whole table (0)` and `Zoom in (+)`.

Keys: [[N]] new puzzle, [[P]] preview, [[G]] faint picture, [[E]] edge pieces, [[T]] tidy, [[+]] / [[−]] zoom, [[0]] whole table.

## Levels and scoring
`Records` shows, for each number of pieces, your best time and how many puzzles you've solved. `Reset records` clears them.

## Sync
Your records sync between your devices through [orOS sync](help:t/sync); `Reset records` clears them everywhere. The picture and the puzzle in progress stay on this device: your photos never leave it. If you close the app, the puzzle picks up where you left off.

## Tips
- Start with the edges: turn on `Edge pieces only`, build the frame, then switch it off.
- Use `Tidy` now and then to line up the loose pieces so they're easier to scan.
