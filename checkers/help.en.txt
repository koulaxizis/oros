# Checkers
English draughts on the dark squares of an 8×8 board. Play the computer at three levels, or a friend on the same device.

## How to play
- Men move one square diagonally forward.
- Capturing is compulsory: jump over an enemy piece to an empty square behind it, and keep jumping while you can. Men capture forward only.
- A man that reaches the far row is crowned a king, and that ends the turn. Kings move and capture one square in every diagonal direction.
- If you have no legal move left, you lose. It's a draw after 40 moves each with no capture or crowning, or when the same position comes up three times.

Black moves first.

## Controls
Tap or click one of your pieces to see where it can go, then tap a target square. A piece that must capture is highlighted.

- [[←]] [[↑]] [[→]] [[↓]] move between squares, [[Enter]] or [[Space]] picks a piece or a target.
- [[Esc]] lets go of the picked piece.
- [[Z]] (or the `Undo move (Z)` button) takes a move back. Against the computer it takes back your move and its answer.
- [[N]] starts a new game.

## Levels and scoring
Choose `vs Computer` or `2 players`. Against the computer, pick `Easy`, `Medium` or `Hard`, and your side with `You: black` or `You: white`; when you play white, the board turns round.

With `2 players`, the status bar keeps a running score of the series. `Records` shows your wins, losses and draws against the computer for each level.

## Sync
Your records against the computer sync between your devices through [orOS sync](help:t/sync). `Reset records` deletes them on every device, together with this device's 2-player series, after you confirm. The game in progress, your mode, level and colour, and the sound setting stay on this device.

## Tips
- Your game is saved as you play; reopen the app to carry on.
- Started a new game or changed a setting by mistake? Tap `Undo` on the message that appears.
- Sound is off at first; turn it on with the sound button.
- Captures are compulsory, so you can offer a piece to pull your opponent into a spot where you jump two back.
