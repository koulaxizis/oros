# Library
Library is your e-book reader. Open EPUB and PDF books from the orOS disk, keep your place on every device, and highlight, bookmark and take notes as you read.

## Getting started
Books already on the orOS disk show up by themselves: the library looks for EPUB and PDF files each time it opens. To add more, tap or click `Add` and pick `From this device…` or `From the orOS disk…`. Books you add from this device are copied to the `Books` folder of the disk. You can also drag files onto the window, or open an EPUB or PDF from Files with Library.

Tap a cover to read. The `Continue reading` row at the top takes you back to where you stopped.

## Find and organise
The chips filter by `Reading`, `Not started`, `Finished`, `EPUB` or `PDF`. `Search title, author, tag…` ignores accents. Sort by `Recently read`, `Title`, `Author` or `Progress`, and switch between covers and a list.

The info button on a cover opens the book's details, where you can `Edit` the title, author and tags, `Mark as finished`, `Export notes`, `Remove from library` or `Delete the file`.

## Reading
- Turn pages with the arrows at the bottom, by tapping the left or right edge, or by swiping. Tap the middle to hide or show the bars.
- The slider jumps anywhere in the book.
- `Contents` lists the chapters; `Search in the book` finds every match.
- `Reading settings` changes text size, font (`The book's`, `Serif` or `Sans`), line spacing, margins, justified text, hyphenation, `Pages` or `Scroll`, two pages on wide screens and the theme (`orOS`, `Paper`, `Sepia`, `Night`). PDFs have zoom (`Fit width`, `Whole page`) and `Night pages`.

## Highlights, notes and bookmarks
Select some text and pick one of four colours to highlight it. From the same bar you can `Add a note`, `Copy` or `Find in the book`. Tap a highlight to change its colour, edit the note or delete it. The bookmark button at the top marks the page.

`Notes and highlights` lists them all, with filters, and `Export notes` saves them as Markdown text to the disk, as a download or to the clipboard.

## Sync and backup
With orOS sync on, your library list, reading place, highlights, notes and bookmarks follow you to every device. The book files travel through the orOS disk sync. If a book was added elsewhere and its file hasn't arrived yet, it shows as not on this device; adding the same file here keeps your place, because the library knows a book by its content. Reading settings stay on each device.

## Keyboard shortcuts
- [[→]] / [[←]], [[PageDown]] / [[PageUp]], [[Space]]: next / previous page
- [[B]]: bookmark, [[T]]: contents, [[N]]: notes
- [[Ctrl+F]]: search in the book
- [[Esc]]: close a popup or the side panel

## Tips
> On a wide screen, the side panel stays open next to the page and the library remembers it.

## Limits
- Copy-protected (DRM) books can't be opened.
- Books larger than 50 MB can't be added.
- PDFs without a text layer (scans) can be read but not searched or highlighted.
