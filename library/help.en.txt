# Library
Library is your e-book reader. Open EPUB, PDF and FB2 books and CBZ and CBR comics from the orOS disk or free online catalogues, keep your place on every device, and highlight, bookmark and take notes as you read.

## Getting started
Books already on the orOS disk show up by themselves: the library looks for EPUB, PDF, FB2 (also `.fb2.zip`), CBZ and CBR files each time it opens. To add more, tap or click `Add` and pick `From this device…`, `From the orOS disk…` or `From online catalogues…`. Books you add from this device are copied to the `Books` folder of the disk. You can also drag files onto the window, or open a book from Files with Library.

Tap a cover to read. The `Continue reading` row at the top takes you back to where you stopped.

## Online catalogues
`Add` → `From online catalogues…` opens free e-book catalogues (OPDS). Two are built in: Project Gutenberg, with a search box, and the newest releases of Standard Ebooks. Open a catalogue, browse its sections or use `Search this catalogue`, open a book and press the format you want (EPUB, PDF, CBZ…). The book is saved in the `Books` folder of the disk and added to your library; `Read` in the message opens it.

To add another catalogue, type its OPDS address (OPDS 1 or 2) under `Add a catalogue`, with a name if you like. Your own catalogues follow you to your other devices; the bin button next to one removes it.

The first time, Library tells you what goes online: only the pages you open and the words you search for, sent to that catalogue's website. Some sites don't let a browser read them directly; Library then reads them through the orOS relay, if you've set one up in [Mail](app:mail). Without a relay, those catalogues say so and can't be read.

## Find and organise
The chips filter by `Reading`, `Not started`, `Finished`, `EPUB` or `PDF` (and `FB2` and `Comics` once you have such books). `Search title, author, tag…` ignores accents. Sort by `Recently read`, `Title`, `Author` or `Progress`, and switch between covers and a list.

The info button on a cover opens the book's details, where you can `Edit` the title, author and tags, `Mark as finished`, `Export notes`, `Remove from library` or `Delete the file`.

`Add to Media Shelf` in a book's details puts it on your [Media Shelf](app:shelf) as an e-book. From then on, the Shelf follows how far you've read here (it updates when you open it), and finishing the book here finishes it there. If it's already there, the button reads `Open in Media Shelf`.

## Reading
- Turn pages with the arrows at the bottom, by tapping the left or right edge, or by swiping. Tap the middle to hide or show the bars.
- The slider jumps anywhere in the book.
- `Contents` lists the chapters; `Search in the book` finds every match.
- `Reading settings` changes text size, font (`The book's`, `Serif` or `Sans`), line spacing, margins, justified text, hyphenation, `Pages` or `Scroll`, two pages on wide screens and the theme (`orOS`, `Paper`, `Sepia`, `Night`). PDFs have zoom (`Fit width`, `Whole page`) and `Night pages`.

## Look up and ask
Select a word and pick `Look up` in the bar: its meanings come from Wiktionary. The first time, Library asks before going online; only the word is sent, to wiktionary.org, and you can turn lookups off again under the meanings. `Open in Wiktionary` shows the full entry.

`Ask the Assistant` opens the orOS [Assistant](app:assistant) with a question ready: what the word means in its sentence, or what a selected passage means. A book's details have `Ask the Assistant` about the whole book, without spoilers past where you are. Nothing is sent until you send it there.

## Read aloud
The speaker button at the bottom left (`Read aloud`) reads the book to you from the page on screen, with your device's own voices: nothing is sent anywhere. The sentence being read is marked and the pages turn by themselves, so your place moves on as you listen.

While it reads, its controls take the place of the bottom bar: `Pause`, `Previous sentence`, `Next sentence`, the speed (tap it to go from 0.8× to 2×) and `Stop reading aloud`. If you turn to another page while paused, play starts from there. The voice follows the book's language; if your device has no voice for it, it reads with the closest one it has.

## Reading stats
`More` › `Reading stats` shows how long you've read today, in the last seven days and in all, how many days in a row, a bar for each of the last two weeks, and the time spent in each book with about how long is left at your pace. A book's details show the same for that book.

Time counts while a book is open and you turn pages, select or listen; after five minutes with none of that it stops until you carry on. Every device adds to the same totals. The [Today](app:today) summary shows the book you're reading, today's time and your streak; tap it to carry on reading.

## Highlights, notes and bookmarks
Select some text and pick one of four colours to highlight it. From the same bar you can `Add a note`, `Copy` or `Find in the book`. Tap a highlight to change its colour, edit the note or delete it. The bookmark button at the top marks the page.

`Notes and highlights` lists them all, with filters, and `Export notes` saves them as Markdown text to the disk, as a download or to the clipboard. The file starts with the title, the author, how many highlights and notes there are and the date, then lists each highlight as a quote under its chapter, with its place in the book and your note below it, ready for Obsidian or any Markdown app. From the same window, `Send to Notes` makes a page of your highlights and notes in [Notes](app:notes), in the same Markdown.

## Sync and backup
With orOS sync on, your library list, reading place, highlights, notes, bookmarks and reading time follow you to every device. The book files travel through the orOS disk sync. If a book was added elsewhere and its file hasn't arrived yet, it shows as not on this device; adding the same file here keeps your place, because the library knows a book by its content. Reading settings and the reading-aloud speed stay on each device.

## Keyboard shortcuts
- [[→]] / [[←]], [[PageDown]] / [[PageUp]], [[Space]]: next / previous page
- [[B]]: bookmark, [[T]]: contents, [[N]]: notes, [[S]]: read aloud or pause
- [[Ctrl+F]]: search in the book
- [[Esc]]: close a popup or the side panel

## Tips
> On a wide screen, the side panel stays open next to the page and the library remembers it.

## Limits
- Copy-protected (DRM) books can't be opened.
- Books larger than 50 MB can't be added; comics (CBZ, CBR) can be up to 200 MB.
- Password-protected CBR comics can't be opened. The first CBR you open loads a reader for RAR files of about 1 MB, so it needs a connection that first time.
- Catalogues that ask for a login (such as the full Standard Ebooks catalogue, for its patrons) can't be read. Through the relay, a download can be up to 5 MB.
- A comic shows one page at a time; it has no text to search, highlight or read aloud.
- PDFs without a text layer (scans) can be read but not searched or highlighted.
