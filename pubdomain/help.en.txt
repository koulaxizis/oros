# Public Domain Calculator
Public Domain Calculator tells you whether a book, song, painting or film is free to use in your country, or when it will be. It looks up the author's dates on Wikidata, or you enter them yourself, and checks the rules of 21 countries.

## Getting started
Type an author or a work in the search box, for example `Kazantzakis`, and pick the right match from the list. The app reads the dates of birth and death (and, for a work, its creators and year of publication) from Wikidata and shows the result.

Your last 10 lookups appear under `Recent`, so you can open them again with one tap. `Clear` empties the list.

## Read the result
At the top you see who or what you looked up, with links to Wikipedia and Wikidata. Then comes `My country` with a status:
- `Public domain`: free to use, and since when.
- `Protected`: still under copyright, and until when.
- `Possibly free`: it depends on the exact date or on the work.
- `Cannot tell`: a date is missing, and the app says which.

Under it you see the rule that applies, the law it comes from and a `Source` link.

`In other countries` lists the rest. Tap or click a country to see its rule and source. Sort the list `A–Z` or `Free first`.

Your country starts as Greece; change it with the `My country` menu in the result.

## Enter the dates yourself
The pencil button at the top opens `Enter the dates yourself`. Choose `One author`, `Several authors` or `Anonymous`, then type each `Year of death` (or tick `Still alive`). Add more with `+ Add author`. For an anonymous work, the `Year of first publication` is needed. The result updates as you type.

This works without an internet connection, because the rules are built into the app.

## Sync and backup
Nothing here syncs. Your country, sort order, the manual-entry choice and your recent lookups stay on this device only.

## Keyboard shortcuts
- [[/]] jumps to the search box.
- [[↓]] and [[↑]] move through the search results.
- [[Esc]] clears the search.

## Tips
- For a film, enter the director, the screenwriter, the dialogue writer and the composer: the last of them to die counts.
- If Wikidata gives only a decade or several possible death dates, the app tells you so; check the real date and use manual entry if you need to.

## Limits
- Searching needs an internet connection and sends what you type to Wikidata. See [Privacy](help:t/privacy).
- The result is about the original work only. Translations, new editions with notes, arrangements, recordings and photos of a work have their own rights.
- This is information, not legal advice.
