# Oracle
Ask the Oracle a question and get a prophecy. Every prophecy is true. None of them is helpful.

## Ask a question
Type your question in the box and press [[Enter]] or tap `Ask the Oracle`. The smoke rises and the answer appears letter by letter. With reduced motion turned on in your device settings, it appears at once.
- The Oracle reads a few words in your question, in English or Greek, and answers about time, love, work and money, yes-or-no questions, or anything else.
- The same question on the same day always gets the same answer: the Oracle does not change its mind. Tomorrow it may say something else.
- An empty question gets no answer.

## Oracle of the day
The card at the top shows one prophecy for today. It is the same on all your devices and changes at midnight.

## Keep, copy and share
Under each answer:
- The star adds it to `Favourites`. Favourites sync with orOS sync, so they follow you to all your devices. The × next to a favourite removes it, and `Undo` brings it back.
- `Copy` copies the question and the answer as text.
- `Save as image` makes a square PNG card with the question and the prophecy.

## Recent questions
`Recent questions` lists your last 100 questions with their answers. They stay on this device only and do not sync. Tap or click one to see it again. The × removes one, and `Clear` removes them all; `Undo` brings them back.

## Sound
The speaker button at the top turns the gong on or off. It is off at first.

## Keyboard shortcuts
- [[Enter]] in the question box asks.
- [[/]] jumps to the question box.

## Tips
> Ask the same thing in different words to hear more of what the Oracle knows.
