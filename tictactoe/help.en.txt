# Tic-Tac-Toe
The classic game of noughts and crosses on a 3×3 board: get three of your marks in a row to win. Play against the computer or with a friend on one device.

## How to play
Whoever moves first plays X, the other plays O. Tap or click an empty square to place your mark. Three in a row, across, down or diagonally, wins and a line is drawn through them; a full board with no three in a row is a draw.

Choose `vs Computer` or `2 players` in the toolbar. Against the computer, pick the `First move`: `You start`, `Computer starts` or `Take turns`. In 2-player games, the players alternate who starts and a running series score is kept.

## Controls
- Tap or click a square to play there. On a desktop, hovering shows your mark first.
- `Undo move (Z)` takes back the last move; against the computer it goes back to your turn. You can't undo after the game ends.
- `New game (N)` starts again. If a game was under way, an `Undo` button appears for a few seconds to bring it back.
- The speaker button turns sound on or off (off at first).
- Keys: [[1]] to [[9]] play a square in number-pad layout ([[7]] is top left, [[3]] bottom right), the arrow keys move between squares and [[Enter]] or [[Space]] plays the focused one, [[N]] is a new game, and [[Z]] or [[Ctrl+Z]] undoes.

## Levels and scoring
The computer has three levels. `Easy` takes a win when it sees one, otherwise plays at random. `Medium` also blocks your threats, but a double threat beats it. `Hard` plays perfectly and never loses: the best you can get is a draw.

`Records` shows your wins, losses and draws against the computer for each level (`Won · lost · drawn`), and the 2-player series. `Reset records` clears them after you confirm.

## Sync
Your records against the computer sync between your devices through [orOS sync](help:t/sync), and a reset clears them everywhere. The game in progress, the 2-player series, your settings and the sound setting stay on this device. A game you leave half-way is still there when you come back.

## Tips
- To beat `Medium`, set up two lines at once (a fork): it can block only one.
- Against `Hard`, try to make every game a draw. That's a perfect result.
