# Workouts
Workouts is a training log: follow a program or train freely, tick your sets as you go, and watch your records and progress grow.

## Getting started
The tabs at the bottom are `Train`, `History`, `Progress`, `Programs` and `Body`. On `Train`, choose `Empty workout` to add exercises as you go, or `Start` a day from one of your programs. If a program day is set for today's weekday, it shows under `Today`.

A workout keeps running while you switch tabs or close the app; `Resume workout` brings you back to it.

## During a workout
- `Add exercise` opens the `Exercise library`. Search, pick an exercise, or make a `New exercise` with a `Muscle group` and a `Measure`: `Weight × reps`, `Reps`, `Time` or `Distance + time`.
- Sets are filled in from `Last time`. Change the numbers and tick a set when it's done. `Add set` adds one; you can also add a `Warm-up set`.
- When every set last time reached the top of its rep target, a `Try …` chip suggests a heavier weight. Tap it to use it for every set.
- Ticking a set starts the rest timer. Use `−15 s`, `+15 s` or `Skip`. It can play a sound or vibrate when the rest is over.
- New records, such as `Heaviest weight` or `Best estimated 1RM`, pop up as they happen.

`Finish` ends the workout; unticked sets are left out. You then see a summary with `Duration`, `Volume`, `Sets` and `New records`. `Discard workout` throws it away.

## History and progress
`History` lists finished workouts. Filter by exercise or program, or `Search notes`. Open a workout to `Edit` it or `Delete workout`, with `Undo`.

`Progress` shows a chart for one `Exercise`, a `Measure` (such as `Estimated 1RM`, `Volume` or `Pace (min/km)`) and a `Period`, plus your `Records`, `Workouts per week`, `Sets per muscle group, this week` and `Training days, last 12 months`. `Show as table` gives the numbers as text.

## Programs and body
On `Programs`, copy one of the `Ready programs` (`Full Body 3×`, `Upper / Lower`, `Push / Pull / Legs`) with `Copy to my programs`, or make a `New program`. A program has days, each with exercises, sets, reps, an optional weight, rest time and `Weekdays`.

On `Body`, `Add measurement` logs `Body weight`, `Waist`, `Chest` or `Arm`.

## Supersets and circuits
Tap the link button on an exercise (`Superset with the next exercise`) to join it to the one below. Two linked exercises are a `Superset`, three or more a `Circuit`. You can link them in a program day or during a workout.

During the workout the linked exercises share one box with `Round 1 of 3`. Tick a set and the next exercise of the same round is marked; the rest timer starts only when the round is over (`Rest after the round`, the rest of the last exercise in the group). History shows the group, and records count every set as usual.

## Progression programs
On `Programs`, `New progression program` sets up a program that works out every session's sets and weights for you:
- `5/3/1 (Wendler)`: press, deadlift, bench and squat days; weeks of 5s, 3s and 5/3/1, then a deload week. Enter a `Training max` for each lift (about 90% of your 1RM). After each 4-week cycle the training max goes up one step; if a `+` set missed its reps during the cycle, that lift goes down 10% instead. `Boring But Big` adds 5 × 10 at 50%.
- `GZCLP`: four days in three tiers. Enter your `5-rep max`: T1 starts at 85%, T2 at 65%. A missed T1 session goes from 5×3+ to 6×2+ to 10×1+ at the same weight, then starts again at 85%; T2 goes from 3×10 to 3×8 to 3×6. T3 goes up when the last set reaches 25 reps.
- `Linear 5×5`: days A and B. Every session with all sets at 5 reps adds weight; three misses in a row take 10% off.

`+` sets mean as many reps as you can: type the reps you did. The steps are your `Step, upper body` and `Step, lower body` from Settings, and every weight is rounded to the plate step you choose (`Round weights to`). The next session shows on `Train`; `Start` it, and `Finish` moves the program on, with a summary of what changes next time. A program counts each session once. `Change numbers` edits the training maxes or weights; `Skip this session` moves on without it.

## Outdoor walks, runs and rides
On `Train`, under `Outdoor activity`, choose `Walk`, `Run` or `Ride`. Workouts records your route with this device's location and shows `Distance`, `Time`, `Pace` (or `Speed` on a ride), the current kilometre or mile, a route preview and your `Splits`. `Pause` and `Resume` leave the pause out of the time and the distance. `Finish` saves it as a workout of `Running`, `Walking` or `Cycling`, so records and progress charts include it. Open it in `History` to see the route and splits, or `Export GPX` to use it in Maps or another app.

> Keep the screen on and Workouts open. Most browsers stop the location when the screen turns off or the app goes to the background; the route then jumps in a straight line from where it stopped to where it starts again.

## Exercise pictures
`Show picture` (the picture button on an exercise, also in the `Exercise library`) looks for a picture of the exercise on [wger.de](https://wger.de), a free exercise database. It is fetched only when you ask, then kept on this device. The author and licence (CC BY-SA) show next to the picture with a link to the source. The match is by name, so the picture may show a variation; `Remove from this device` forgets it.

## Import workouts
Bring your history over from another training app or from your watch. In `Settings`, choose `Import workouts (Strong, Hevy, FitNotes, JEFIT, FIT, TCX, GPX)` and pick the file. How to export:
- **Strong**: `Settings` › `Export Strong Data` (CSV).
- **Hevy**: `Settings` › `Export & Import Data` › `Export Workouts` (CSV).
- **FitNotes**: `Settings` › `Spreadsheet Export` (CSV). The sets of one day become one workout.
- **JEFIT**: on the JEFIT website or app, the data export under your profile or settings (a CSV with sections that start with `###`). JEFIT does not write its units, so choose them in the summary.
- **Garmin**: one activity as `.fit` (Garmin Connect, an activity › the gear menu › `Export Original`), or the whole account (Account › Data Management › `Export Your Data`, a ZIP).
- **Strava**: one activity as `.gpx` or `.tcx` (an activity › `…` › `Export GPX` / `Export Original`), or `Settings` › `My Account` › `Download or Delete Your Account` › `Request Your Archive` (a ZIP).
- Any other app or watch that saves `.fit`, `.tcx` or `.gpx` files, one at a time or in a ZIP.

The summary shows the source, how many workouts or activities, the dates, which exercises were found here and which are new, and what is skipped. If the file does not say its units, choose `Metric (kg, km, cm)` or `Imperial (lb, mi, in)` under `Weights and distances in this file`. Tap or click `Import`; the toast that follows has `Undo`.

Warm-up sets stay warm-up sets, notes come along, and records, charts, Calendar and Habits include the imported workouts at once. Runs, walks and rides keep their route (thinned, like the ones you record here) with distance and time; swims and rows get their distance and time; other sports come in with their time and a note (calories, average heart rate). Importing the same file again skips what is already here, and an activity that starts within a minute of a workout you already have (recorded here or from Health Connect) is skipped too. A workout you delete is not brought back, unless you tick `Also bring back … workouts deleted after an earlier import`.

> New exercises land in the `Exercise library`, where you can rename them or change their muscle group. Very large exports keep the routes of the first few hundred activities only, so sync stays light; the rest come in without a route.

## Health Connect
In the orOS Android app, switch on `Health Connect` in Settings › `Connected services`. Your finished workouts of the last 30 days then go to Health Connect as strength training, and workouts from your watch or other apps (a run, a ride, a swim) come in with their times and no exercises. A workout that came from another app and that you delete here doesn't come back.

## Sync and backup
Your exercises, programs, progression programs, workouts (with their routes), body measurements and settings sync through [orOS sync](help:t/sync). A running workout reaches sync every few minutes, so you can finish it on another device. The open tab, chart choices, rest timer and exercise pictures stay on this device.

In `Settings` › `Export`, save `All sets (CSV)` or a `Backup (JSON)`. `Restore from backup` merges a backup into what you already have.

## Keyboard shortcuts
- [[1]] to [[5]] switch between the five tabs (when you're not typing).

## Tips
- In `Settings`, pick `Metric (kg, km, cm)` or `Imperial (lb, mi, in)`, the weight steps for the suggestions, and your `Default rest (s)`.
- Finished workouts show in [Calendar](app:calendar) and as an automatic row in [Habits](app:habits). Turn the Habits row off with `Show workouts in Habits`.
- Body weights also appear in [Health](app:health).

## Limits
- The rest timer only runs on the device you're training on.
- The weight suggestion is a hint for `Weight × reps` exercises only.
- Outdoor sessions record only while Workouts is open on screen; the browser may stop the location when the screen is off. The route preview is a line without a map underneath.
- Progression programs follow the fixed exercises of their template; add other exercises during the workout as you like.
- Pictures need an internet connection the first time, and wger.de may not have one for every exercise.
- Older orOS versions on your other devices don't know supersets, progression programs or routes; update every device so they keep them.
