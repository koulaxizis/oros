# Chess
Play chess against the computer at three levels, or against a friend on the same device. All the rules are there: castling, en passant, promotion, check and every kind of draw.

## How to play
Choose `vs Computer` or `2 players` in the toolbar. Against the computer, also pick a level and `Your pieces`: `As white`, `As black` or `Random side`. Changing any of these starts a new game.

To move, tap or click a piece, then the square to go to. Dots show where the piece can legally go. With a mouse you can also drag the piece. If a move isn't allowed, a message tells you why, for example when it would leave your king in check.

When a pawn reaches the last row, pick the piece it becomes under `Promote to`.

The game ends by checkmate, or as a draw by stalemate, the 50-move rule, threefold repetition or when there aren't enough pieces left to mate.

## Controls
- Tap, click or drag to move.
- Arrow keys move around the board; [[Enter]] picks up a piece and puts it down. [[Esc]] drops the selected piece.
- [[U]] or `Undo move (U)` takes back a move. Against the computer, it takes back your move and its answer.
- [[F]] or `Turn the board (F)` turns the board around. When you play black, the board is already turned for you.
- [[N]] or `New game (N)` starts a new game. If you leave a game in progress, an `Undo` button appears for a moment to get it back.

A game in progress is kept on this device and waits for you next time.

## Levels and scoring
`Easy` sometimes picks a weaker move on purpose, `Medium` thinks a few moves ahead and `Hard` searches deepest. `Records` (`Records vs computer`) shows your wins, draws and losses for each level.

>! Games where you used undo, and all `2 players` games, are not recorded.

## Games as PGN
PGN is the file format every chess site and program uses for games. Open `Open or save a game (PGN)` in the toolbar:
- `Open file…` opens a .pgn file, or paste a game in the box and press `Open pasted`. A file with many games lists them, so you pick one.
- `Save this game (.pgn)` saves the game on the board; `Copy PGN` copies it, to paste into Lichess, Chess.com or another program.

On a computer with orOS installed, .pgn files also open with orOS from the system's "Open with".

## Reviewing a game
An opened game shows its players, the result and the move list. Step through it with the buttons under the moves, with [[←]] [[→]] (and [[Home]] [[End]]), or by tapping any move in the list.
- `Analysis` shows how the computer sees each position (the `Hard` level): the score from white's side (+1.00 is about a pawn ahead for white, #3 a mate in three) and the best move it finds.
- `Play from here` continues the game from the position on the board: against the computer with the side to move, or with 2 players.

>! A game played on from a position is not counted in the records.

A game you were reviewing also waits for you next time. If a PGN has a move that isn't legal, the game opens up to that move and a message says where it stopped.

## Sync
Your records against the computer sync between your devices through [orOS sync](help:t/sync). `Reset records` deletes them on every device, after you confirm. The mode, level, side, board turn, the game in progress and the sound button stay on this device.

## Tips
- Start on `Easy` to warm up; the computer still never misses a mate in one.
- In `2 players`, turn the board with [[F]] when you hand the device over.
