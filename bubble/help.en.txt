# Bubble Shooter
Shoot coloured bubbles into the wall hanging from the ceiling. Join three or more of one colour to pop them, and clear the whole wall before it reaches the line.

## How to play
Your bubble flies straight, bounces off the side walls and sticks where it touches the wall or the ceiling.
- Three or more bubbles of one colour that touch pop.
- Bubbles that no longer hang from the ceiling fall, for extra points.
- Every few shots the ceiling drops one row. The dots by the launcher count down, and the status line warns you on the last shot.
- You lose when a bubble crosses the dashed line, and win when the wall is empty.

The next bubble waits bottom left. New bubbles only come in colours still on the board; each colour also has its own symbol.

## Controls
- **Touch:** drag on the field to aim; the dotted line shows the path with one bounce. Let go to shoot. Let go below the launcher to cancel.
- **Mouse:** the aim follows the pointer; click to shoot.
- Tap or click the next bubble, or the `Swap bubbles (S)` button, to swap the two.
- [[←]] [[→]] aim (hold to keep turning), [[Space]], [[Enter]] or [[↑]] shoot, [[S]] or [[↓]] swaps, [[N]] starts a new game.

The toolbar also has `New game (N)`, `Records` and the sound button (`Sound off` / `Sound on`; sound is off at first).

## Levels and scoring
- `Easy`: 4 colours, 5 rows, the ceiling drops every 10 shots.
- `Normal`: 5 colours, 6 rows, every 8 shots.
- `Hard`: 6 colours, 6 rows, every 7 shots.

A popped bubble scores 10, a falling one 20 (double when 5 or more fall at once, triple from 10). Clearing the wall adds 1,000. Each level keeps its own records.

## Sync
Your records (best score, games and walls cleared per level) sync between your devices through [orOS sync](help:t/sync). `Reset records` in `Records` deletes them on every device after you confirm. The game in progress, the level you picked and the sound setting stay on this device.

## Tips
- A game in progress is saved; close the app and pick it up later.
- Started a new game or changed level by mistake? Tap `Undo` on the message that appears.
