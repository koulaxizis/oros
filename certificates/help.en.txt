# Certificates
Make certificates of attendance, completion and merit, diplomas, thank-you certificates and kids' awards for a whole list of people at once. You design one certificate, add the names, and get a numbered PDF for every person.

## Getting started
1. Press `Make certificates` (or [[+]] at the top), pick a `Kind`, type the seminar or course and press `Create`.
2. In `Design`, fill in `What it is for`: the organisation, the dates, the place and the hours.
3. In `People`, add the names.
4. In `Issue`, press `Give numbers`, then `PDF with everyone` or `ZIP, one PDF each`.
On a phone the preview sits above the form; on a wide screen it is on the right. The arrows next to it go from person to person.

## Design
- Ten kinds come with ready texts in English and Greek: `Attendance`, `Completion`, `Merit`, `Excellence`, `Diploma`, `Participation`, `Training`, `Thanks`, `Volunteering` and `Kids' award`.
- `Language of the certificate` is the language of its texts and dates, whatever language orOS is in.
- Six looks: `Classic`, `Modern`, `Elegant`, `Bold`, `Minimal` and `Playful`. Each sets its own colours and font; change them after with `Main colour`, `Second colour` and `Font`.
- `Paper` is A4 or Letter, landscape or portrait.
- Under `Texts` you can change every line. Tap a field such as `Name` or `Dates` to put it where the cursor is; each person's details go in its place. A part between double square brackets shows only when its fields have a value, so in `[[{place}, ]]{date}` the place and its comma disappear when the place is empty.
- `Ready texts for this kind` puts the original texts back.

## Logo, signatures and seal
- `Choose a picture` adds a logo at the top and a seal or stamp at the bottom. With no seal, `A medal when there is no seal` draws a medal with the year.
- Up to three signatures, each with a name, a role and a signature: `Sign on the screen` with a finger, pen or mouse, or `Picture of a signature`.
- A new set of certificates starts with the organisation, logo, signatures and seal of your last one.

## People
- Type a name and an email and press `Add`, or use `Paste a list`: one person per line, or a table copied from a spreadsheet with a first row of column names (name, email, grade…).
- `Open a file (CSV, Excel)` reads a .csv, .xlsx, .xls or .ods table the same way. "First name" and "Last name" columns are joined.
- `From Contacts` and `From CRM` add everyone, or the people of one label or tag.
- A name already on the list with the same email is not added twice.
- Tap a person to change the details, add a `Grade or distinction`, mark the certificate `Revoked` or remove the person.
- `Other form of the name` is for your own texts that need, for example, the name in the accusative in Greek. The ready Greek texts use the name as written, so you never have to fill it in.

## Numbers
- `Give numbers` numbers everyone who has none, in list order: CERT-2026-0001, CERT-2026-0002… Change the start under `Numbers` › `Number starts with`.
- A number is given once and never reused, even when a person is removed.
- If two devices give numbers at the same moment, `Check` shows the number given twice and `Give a new number` fixes it.

## Check code
- Tick `A QR code that shows the certificate is genuine` under `Check code (QR)`. Each certificate gets a QR code at the bottom left.
- Anyone who scans it with a phone sees a check page with the details you signed: number, name, course, dates, organisation and date of issue. If someone changes even one letter, the page says `Not valid`.
- The code is signed with a key that orOS makes the first time and keeps in your synced data, so all your devices sign with the same key. The check page shows that key, for example `714C-C0EF-E8A4-2098`.
- `Copy the key` and put it on your website or under your emails, so people can see the certificate really comes from you. `Open the check page` shows the page for the person in the preview.
- The check needs no server and nothing is sent anywhere. In the PDF the code is also a link.

## Attendance
- `Sign-in sheet (PDF)` under `People` prints the list with a signature column for each day of the seminar (up to 7 days) and a few blank rows for people who come without signing up.
- Tick the box next to each person who came, or press `Everyone came`. `Clear the ticks` removes them all.
- Under `Issue`, tick `Only for people ticked as present`: the PDFs, the numbers and the email list then cover only them. `Check` shows how many are ticked.

## Open Badges
- `Open Badges (ZIP)` makes one Open Badges 3.0 credential for each person, a .jwt file signed with the same key as the check code.
- People can add it to a badge wallet or a course platform that accepts Open Badges 3.0, and anyone can check the signature without a server of ours.
- The email is stored only as a hash. If the check code is on, the badge also carries the link to the check page.

## Save, print and send
- `PDF with everyone` makes one file with a page per person; `ZIP, one PDF each` one PDF per person, named with the number and name.
- `Save each in Files` puts the PDFs in Files › Certificates › the name of the set.
- `Print` prints up to 100 certificates at a time.
- `PDF of this one` and `Picture (PNG) of this one` save the certificate shown in the preview.
- `Send by email…` opens one message per person. With orOS Mail set up on this device the PDF is attached; otherwise the PDF is saved and your mail app opens, and you attach it yourself.
- `Register (CSV)` saves the list of numbers, names, grades, dates and revoked certificates.
- When the design shows numbers, orOS first offers to number the people who have none. `Leave out revoked certificates` keeps revoked ones out.

## Email everyone
- With an account set up in orOS Mail on this device, `Email everyone…` under `Issue` sends each person one message with their own certificate attached. Each message has a single recipient.
- Messages go out one at a time, at most the number per hour you set (mail providers limit this, often 100 to 300 an hour). Keep orOS open and in front while it sends.
- If the mail server refuses more messages, the password is wrong or the connection drops, sending stops. Press `Go on` later. People already sent theirs are not sent again, even after closing orOS, unless you tick `Also people who were already sent theirs`.
- An address the server refuses is skipped and named at the end.

## Sync and backup
- Your certificates and lists follow you to every device through orOS sync, encrypted, and are part of the orOS export.
- Logos, signatures and seals stay on your orOS disk and travel with it.
- [[⋯]] › `orOS file (.oroscert)` saves one set of certificates as an open file named after it: the design, the texts, the list of people with their numbers, and the logo, signatures and seal themselves. It is a ZIP: unzip it and you find `content.json` (the set), `media` (the pictures) and `preview`, with `thumbnail.png` (the design) and `people.csv` (the register, which any spreadsheet opens). The key that signs the check QR codes is never put in the file: certificates opened on another device sign with that device's key.
- `Open an orOS file (.oroscert)…` on the first screen, or opening the file with orOS from your system (`Open with`), brings it in as new certificates; it never replaces the ones you have. `Unzipped orOS folder…` next to it brings back a .oroscert that was unzipped, even after you changed a file in it.
- Nothing is sent anywhere unless you send an email yourself.

## Tips
> [[⋯]] › `Duplicate (design only)` copies a design without its people, ready for the next seminar.

## Limits
- Up to 2,000 people in one set and 5,000 in all; up to 200 sets.
- The fonts are Noto Serif and Noto Sans, which cover Greek and Latin letters.
- A certificate you revoke after it went out still shows as signed on the check page, because there is no server to ask. Tell people to contact you when in doubt.
