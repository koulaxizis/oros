# CRM
CRM is a small customer book for a freelancer or a small business: your customers, the people you talk to there, what was said, what you promised to do next and the deals you are working on.

## Getting started
Tap or click `Add your first customer`. Pick `Company` or `Person`, write the name and choose a `Status`: `Lead` (not a customer yet), `Active` or `Past`. Under `Billing details` you can keep the `VAT ID (ΑΦΜ)`, `Tax office (ΔΟΥ)`, `Profession`, `Address` and `Country`. For a Greek VAT ID the app checks the check digit and warns when another customer already has it.

The tabs are `Customers`, `Pipeline`, `Agenda` and `Dashboard`. On a phone they sit at the bottom of the screen.

## Customers and people
The list has a search field, a sort menu and filters: `Leads`, `Active`, `Past`, `Starred`, `Not contacted` and your tags. Tap a customer to open the page with `Call`, `Email`, `Map`, `Log contact`, `Follow-up`, `Deal`, `Invoice` and `Quote`.

Under `People` tap `+ Person`. Pick someone `From Contacts` (their phone and email are read from [Contacts](app:contacts) and stay up to date), or write a name with a phone and email. `Add to Contacts` opens Contacts with that person filled in; it is saved there only when you press Save.

## History, follow-ups and the agenda
`Log contact` records a call, email, meeting, message or note with its date and time, who you spoke to and the deal it was about. Pick `Next follow-up` to plan the next step in the same window.

A follow-up has a day, an optional time and what to do. `Agenda` shows the late ones, today's and the next 7 days. Tap the circle to mark one done; the note that appears offers `Log it`. The menu of a follow-up moves it to `Tomorrow` or `In a week`, or sends it to [To-Do](app:todo).

Open follow-ups also show in [Today](app:today), where the tick marks one done in CRM, and in the [Calendar](app:calendar) under `Customers`, together with the expected close day of open deals. A contact that is a person of a customer gets a `Customer:` button in [Contacts](app:contacts).

## Pipeline
A deal has a title, a value, a stage (`New`, `Contacted`, `Proposal`, `Negotiation`, `Won`, `Lost`), a chance in percent and an expected close day. On a wide screen drag a card to another column. On a phone pick a stage at the top and use the `⋯` button of a deal to move it. The totals show the value and the weighted value (value × chance).

Under `Settings` › `Pipeline stages` you can rename a stage, change its default chance, move it up or down, add your own (up to 8 open stages) or hide the ones you do not use. A stage that still has deals cannot be hidden or deleted. `Won` and `Lost` stay as they are.

## Invoices and quotes
`Invoice` on a customer's page opens [Invoices](app:invoices) with a new invoice for that customer: name, VAT ID, tax office, profession, address and contact details are filled in. `Quote` does the same in [Quote](app:quote). Open a deal to find `Invoice this deal` (won deals) and `Quote for this deal`: they add the deal as one line with its value. Nothing is saved until you press Save in that app. Quote works in EUR or USD only.

The `Documents` tab of a customer lists their invoices with what was invoiced, paid and is still unpaid, and the quotes made for a client with the same name or VAT ID. Tap one to open it. CRM only reads them; you change them in their own app.

## Dashboard
Open pipeline, won this month and this year, win rate, late follow-ups, customers you have not contacted for a while, new leads and, once you invoice from CRM, what your customers still owe. Tap a tile to see the list behind it.

## Sync and backup
Customers, people, history, follow-ups, deals, tags and the currency sync between your devices through [orOS sync](help:t/sync). The open tab, filters and sort stay on each device.

In `Settings`: `Export customers (CSV)`, `Backup (JSON)` and `Restore a backup`. A backup is merged into what you have; nothing is deleted.

## Tips
- Follow-up reminders reach the [notifications](help:t/notifications) inbox even when CRM is closed: on the day, or at the time you set.
- Change `"Not contacted" after` in `Settings` to decide when a customer counts as forgotten.
- Deleting a customer also deletes its deals, history and follow-ups; `Undo` brings them all back.
- CRM is not in the menu search until you switch it on under `Search in`, because it holds other people's details.

## Limits
- Reminders need orOS to be open in a tab or window; a reminder missed while it was closed arrives the next time you open it.
- Up to 2,000 customers and 20,000 history entries, to leave room for the other apps.
