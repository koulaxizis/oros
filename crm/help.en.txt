# CRM
CRM is a small customer book for a freelancer or a small business: your customers, the people you talk to there, what was said, what you promised to do next and the deals you are working on.

## Getting started
Tap or click `Add your first customer`. Pick `Company` or `Person`, write the name and choose a `Status`: `Lead` (not a customer yet), `Active` or `Past`. Under `Billing details` you can keep the `VAT ID (ΑΦΜ)`, `Tax office (ΔΟΥ)`, `Profession`, `Address` and `Country`. For a Greek VAT ID the app checks the check digit and warns when another customer already has it.

The tabs are `Customers`, `Pipeline`, `Agenda` and `Dashboard`. On a phone they sit at the bottom of the screen.

## Customers and people
The list has a search field, a sort menu and filters: `Leads`, `Active`, `Past`, `Starred`, `Not contacted` and your tags. Tap a customer to open the page with `Call`, `Email`, `Map`, `Log contact`, `Follow-up`, `Deal`, `Invoice` and `Quote`.

Under `People` tap `+ Person`. Pick someone `From Contacts` (their phone and email are read from [Contacts](app:contacts) and stay up to date), or write a name with a phone and email. `Add to Contacts` opens Contacts with that person filled in; it is saved there only when you press Save.

## History, follow-ups and the agenda
`Log contact` records a call, email, meeting, message or note with its date and time, who you spoke to and the deal it was about. Pick `Next follow-up` to plan the next step in the same window.

A follow-up has a day, an optional time and what to do. `Agenda` shows the late ones, today's and the next 7 days. Tap the circle to mark one done; the note that appears offers `Log it`. The menu of a follow-up moves it to `Tomorrow` or `In a week`, or sends it to [To-Do](app:todo).

Open follow-ups also show in [Today](app:today), where the tick marks one done in CRM, and in the [Calendar](app:calendar) under `Customers`, together with the expected close day of open deals. A contact that is a person of a customer gets a `Customer:` button in [Contacts](app:contacts).

## Pipeline
A deal has a title, a value, a stage (`New`, `Contacted`, `Proposal`, `Negotiation`, `Won`, `Lost`), a chance in percent and an expected close day. On a wide screen drag a card to another column. On a phone pick a stage at the top and use the `⋯` button of a deal to move it. The totals show the value and the weighted value (value × chance).

Under `Settings` › `Pipeline stages` you can rename a stage, change its default chance, move it up or down, add your own (up to 8 open stages) or hide the ones you do not use. A stage that still has deals cannot be hidden or deleted. `Won` and `Lost` stay as they are.

## Invoices and quotes
`Invoice` on a customer's page opens [Invoices](app:invoices) with a new invoice for that customer: name, VAT ID, tax office, profession, address and contact details are filled in. `Quote` does the same in [Quote](app:quote). Open a deal to find `Invoice this deal` (won deals) and `Quote for this deal`: they add the deal as one line with its value. Nothing is saved until you press Save in that app. The quote takes the CRM's currency, whatever it is.

The `Documents` tab of a customer lists their invoices with what was invoiced, paid and is still unpaid, and the quotes made for a client with the same name or VAT ID. Tap one to open it. CRM only reads them; you change them in their own app.

## Dashboard
Open pipeline, won this month and this year, win rate, late follow-ups, customers you have not contacted for a while, new leads and, once you invoice from CRM, what your customers still owe. Tap a tile to see the list behind it.

## Sync and backup
Customers, people, history, follow-ups, deals, tags and the currency sync between your devices through [orOS sync](help:t/sync). The open tab, filters and sort stay on each device.

In `Settings`: `Import customers (CSV)`, `Export customers (CSV)`, `Backup (JSON)` and `Restore a backup`. A backup is merged into what you have; nothing is deleted. Inside orOS, `Open in Spreadsheet` opens the same customer list as `Export customers (CSV)` in a new sheet in [Spreadsheet](app:spreadsheet), with the open deals and their value totalled at the bottom.

`Import customers (CSV)` reads a list from Excel, HubSpot, another program or CRM's own export. The first row must name the columns (for example Name or Company, VAT ID, Email, Phone, Address, City, Status, Tags). You see how many customers are new before anything is added; customers you already have (same VAT ID or name) are skipped, and `Undo` removes the whole import.

## Save and open an orOS file
In `Settings`, `orOS file (.oroscrm)` saves every customer with its people, history, follow-ups and deals, your tags and your own pipeline stages as one `.oroscrm` file, for example to move them to another device without sync or to keep a copy. Your settings (currency, `"Not contacted" after`, the names of the built-in stages) and the web form with its keys stay on each device, and no account of a connected service is ever in the file. The file is a plain ZIP: unzipped it holds the records as `content.json` plus `preview/customers.csv` (the same list as `Export customers (CSV)`) for other programs. It holds other people's details and is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere safe.

`Open orOS file…` (or `Unzipped orOS folder…`, or "Open with" orOS on the computer) shows how many records are new and how many are already here, then `Add` adds only the new ones, with their own ids: opening the same file again adds nothing. A record already here is kept as it is, one you deleted here stays deleted, and a file never deletes anything. Tick `Update with newer versions` to let records that were changed later in the file replace your copy. `Undo` in the message takes back what was added.

## HubSpot, Pipedrive, Invoice Ninja and Odoo
`Settings` › `Import from HubSpot, Pipedrive, Invoice Ninja or Odoo` brings in an account you connected in [Settings](app:settings) › `Connected services`. Pick the account and `Read the account`: you see how many customers, deals and follow-ups are new and how many changed there since the last time, before anything is added.

- Companies become customers, with their contacts as people. A contact without a company becomes a person customer.
- Deals keep their stage: won and lost stay won and lost; an open deal goes to the stage nearest its probability (New, Contacted, Proposal or Negotiation). A deal in another currency than CRM's comes in without a value, with the amount in its note.
- Invoice Ninja's active clients become customers, with their contacts as people and their VAT ID; a customer with the same VAT ID here is matched first.
- From Odoo come its contacts (companies with their people, people on their own, VAT ID) and its opportunities as deals; your own company and its staff are left out. It needs Odoo 19 or Odoo Online.
- Pipedrive's open activities become follow-ups. One you mark done here stays done.
- A customer you already have (same name or e-mail) is used as it is, never changed.
- `Update what was imported before` refreshes only the platform's fields (name, phone, e-mail, website, address, people, deal stage and value). Status, tags, notes, stars and history stay yours.
- What you delete here doesn't come back on the next import, unless you tick `Bring back what was deleted here`.
- `Undo` takes back the whole import. orOS only reads HubSpot, Pipedrive, Invoice Ninja and Odoo: nothing changes there.

## Web form
`Settings` › `Web form` makes a contact form for your own website. Leave `Thank-you page (optional)` empty or give an https address on your site, then `Create the form`. Copy the `HTML for your site` (in English or Greek) into a page of your site. It is a plain form with Name, Email, Phone, Company and Message, with no script, so you can style it like the rest of your site. Keep the hidden field: it stops spam robots.

- A message someone sends waits at the orOS relay, encrypted with a key made on your device, until CRM picks it up. Only your CRM can read it. A message that isn't picked up within 30 days is deleted.
- `Check for new leads` picks them up. CRM also checks by itself when you open it, at most every 10 minutes.
- Each message becomes a new customer with status `Lead` and source `Web form`, with the message in its history. When the e-mail already belongs to a customer (or to one of its people), no new customer is made: the message goes into that customer's history, with any new name, phone or company written under it.
- The form and its keys sync with your CRM. One device collects the messages; on another one press `Collect on this device`.
- `Turn the form off` stops the form on your site and deletes the messages still waiting at the relay.

## Update from online sources
`Update online` on a customer's page (or `Update customers from online sources` in Settings, for all of them) looks only where the customer already points: the Gravatar profile of their own e-mail the website saved in the customer, and the public GitHub, Bluesky or Mastodon profiles these point to, Wikidata entries that name them, and, for a company, OpenStreetMap by name and address or phone. It suggests a website, phone, e-mail, address and, for a person, a job title. An empty field is filled; anything else goes into the note as a line such as "Instagram: https://…", so nothing you typed is replaced. Each suggestion says where it came from and how sure it is; nothing changes until you tap `Apply selected`, and `Undo` puts the customers back. When you check a single record, the window also offers DuckDuckGo search links (the web, LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, X, TikTok) that you open yourself. If the Assistant is set to search with your own SearXNG server, a few records at a time are also searched there by name; those results always start unticked. Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X and TikTok are never read, and no one is searched for by name alone.

## Tips
- Follow-up reminders reach the [notifications](help:t/notifications) inbox even when CRM is closed: on the day, or at the time you set.
- Change `"Not contacted" after` in `Settings` to decide when a customer counts as forgotten.
- Deleting a customer also deletes its deals, history and follow-ups; `Undo` brings them all back.
- CRM is not in the menu search until you switch it on under `Search in`, because it holds other people's details.

## Limits
- Reminders need orOS to be open in a tab or window; a reminder missed while it was closed arrives the next time you open it.
- Up to 2,000 customers and 20,000 history entries, to leave room for the other apps.
- Web form: one form per CRM. A message is at most 2,000 characters and every other field 200. The relay keeps at most 500 messages waiting and takes at most 100 a day per form; beyond that it shows the thank-you page but keeps nothing. Messages are picked up only while CRM is open on the device that collects them.
- A device still on an older CRM drops the web form from its own copy until it updates; your other devices keep it.
