# Music
Music plays the songs on your device, in the way you know from the big streaming apps: Home, Search, Your library, albums, artists, playlists, Liked songs and a queue with shuffle and repeat. The player keeps going after you close the app.

## Getting started
Press **+** at the top (`Add music`) and choose `Add songs` or `Add a folder`, or drop music files on the window. The files are copied to `Files › Music` on the orOS disk, so they play without a connection. With Vault Drive on, they reach your other devices too. Music reads MP3, M4A/AAC, FLAC, Ogg/Opus, WAV and WebM files.

Songs you put in the Music folder with [Files](app:files) appear as well: Music reads the folder each time it opens. `Read the music folder again` in the settings does it on request.

The tabs:
- `Home`: your playlists and Liked songs, `Jump back in`, `Recently added`, `Your most played` and your artists.
- `Search`: songs, albums, artists and playlists; with an empty field, `Browse by genre`.
- `Your library`: `Playlists`, `Songs`, `Albums`, `Artists`, `Genres`, `Folders`, `Offline` and `Servers`.

## Listen
Tap a song to play it, with the rest of its album or list after it. The big play button plays a whole album, artist, playlist or genre, and `Shuffle` plays it in random order.

The player bar at the bottom shows the song, a heart and play/pause and next. Tap the song's name for the full player: position, `Previous`, `Next`, `Shuffle`, `Repeat` (all, then one), volume and `Queue`. Your device's media controls (lock screen, headset, media keys) work too. While a song plays, a music note shows on the orOS top bar, also with the Music window closed: tap it for a small control panel with the song, its position, `Previous`, play/pause, `Next`, `Stop`, `Open Music`, volume and `Mute`. Tap outside it or press Esc to close it. Radio and Podcasts pause when music starts, and the other way round.

## Queue
`Queue` in the full player shows `Now playing`, `Next up` (songs you added by hand) and what follows from the list you started. Tap a song there to jump to it, or press × to take it out of `Next up`. The ⋯ button on any song offers `Play next` and `Add to queue`.

## Liked songs and playlists
The heart saves a song to `Liked songs`. `New playlist` in `Your library` (or under **+**) makes a list; `Add to playlist` on a song's ⋯ menu, or on an album page, adds songs to it. On a playlist page, a song's ⋯ menu has `Move up`, `Move down` and `Remove from this playlist`; the playlist's own ⋯ button has `Rename` and `Delete playlist`.

Liked songs and playlists sync to your other devices. A song that is not on a device shows as `Not on this device` there until its file arrives.

## Your files
A song's ⋯ menu has `Details` (file, format, size, length) and `Delete from this device`, which deletes the file from `Files › Music`. Old tags in Greek written with the Windows code page are recognised automatically; if names look wrong, choose `Always Greek` or `Always Western` under `Old tags` in the settings.

## Free music
Besides your own songs, Music plays free and legal music from four open catalogues, with no account:
- **Jamendo**: independent artists who share their music under Creative Commons. Popular this week, new albums, all-time favourites, moods and Jamendo's own radios. Jamendo songs always stream (Jamendo does not allow offline copies), and each one links back to its page on Jamendo. If a stream stops (the connection dropped, or the song's address went stale), Music asks for it again and goes on from where it stopped; without a network it waits and goes on when the network is back. Only a song that still cannot play is skipped, with the notice "This song cannot play here." Tap an artist's name (also in the full player) for the artist's page: on Jamendo it has every album, newest first, and every song, most played first.
- **Internet Archive**: netlabel albums, classical recordings and the Live Music Archive (concert tapes of bands that allow free, non-commercial sharing). Only items with a Creative Commons or public-domain licence are shown.
- **Openverse**: songs with Creative Commons or public-domain licences from Wikimedia Commons, Freesound, Jamendo and more.
- **ccMixter**: Creative Commons remixes, instrumentals and a cappellas.

On `Home`, `Free music` has shelves such as `Popular this week`, `Jamendo radios`, `Live concerts`, `Netlabel albums`, `Classical` and `Remixes`; `Free music by genre` has genres and `Moods (Jamendo)` has moods. In `Search`, `Free music online` adds results from the catalogues when you stop typing (or press Enter); the chips choose `All` or one catalogue. Nothing is asked of them until you open a shelf or search, and the requests go straight to them.

Every song shows its licence (for example `CC BY-SA`). Its ⋯ menu has `Copy credit`, the line that names the song, the artist, the licence and the source, ready to paste wherever you use the song; `Details` shows it too, and so does the licence line under the song in the full player. `Open on …` opens the song's page at its source.

Songs from the catalogues go into `Liked songs` and playlists like your own, and play on your other devices too. `Save for offline` (on a song, or the arrow on an album) keeps a copy on this device, only where the licence and the source allow it: songs that Openverse lists from Jamendo always stream. Saved songs are under `Offline` in `Your library`, with the arrow mark; `Remove offline copy` or `Delete all offline copies` in the settings frees the space. To turn the catalogues off, untick `Free music online` in the settings.

## Your own servers
If your music lives on a server of your own, Music plays it from there. Press **+** and `Connect a server`, or go to `Your library › Servers`:
- **Subsonic / Navidrome**: Navidrome, Airsonic, Gonic, Ampache and other servers with the Subsonic (OpenSubsonic) API. Enter the address, your user name and password. Music keeps a salted token, not the password itself.
- **Jellyfin**: the address, user name and password. Music signs in once and keeps only the access token.
- **Funkwhale**: the address of a pod. Public pods work without a login; for your own library, create a token in Funkwhale under `Settings › Applications` (read access) and paste it.
- **WebDAV / Nextcloud**: the WebDAV address (Nextcloud shows it in `Files › Settings`), your user name and an app password, and an optional start folder. The server has to allow requests from orOS (CORS); on Nextcloud this needs an app or a setting by its admin.
- **Dropbox**: a folder of your Dropbox, through the connection you made in [Files](app:files) (`Files › Dropbox`).

Use an `https://` address: browsers block plain `http://` addresses in orOS, except a server on the same computer. The address and the login stay on this device, sealed with a key that never leaves the browser, and are never synced. A server's page has its albums, artists and playlists (or its folders), and `Shuffle the server`. Its ⋯ button has `Rename` and `Remove from this device`. `Home` shows `New on …` for your servers, and `Search` asks them too once you stop typing.

Songs from a server go into `Liked songs` and your playlists like any other. They play on your other devices once you connect the same server there too; until then they show as `From a server not connected here`.

## Playlist files and music feeds
`Import a playlist (M3U, PLS, XSPF)` makes a new playlist from a playlist file or its address. Each entry becomes the song with the same file name (or title and artist) on this device, the song at an `https://` address, or, for a file kept on a WebDAV or Dropbox server, the song next to it on that server. Entries that cannot be found are left out, and Music says how many. Playlist files inside a server's folders open the same way.

`Add a music feed (RSS)` adds a feed with songs, for example a Podcasting 2.0 music feed (Wavlake and others) or any RSS or Atom feed with audio. The feed's page lists its songs; its ⋯ button has `Add to playlist`, `Open website` and `Remove feed`. Feeds are kept on this device.

## Lyrics
`Lyrics` (the lines button at the top of the full player) shows the words of the playing song. Lines with timing follow the song: the line being sung is bright, and tapping a line jumps there. Music looks first for a `.lrc` file with the same name next to the song in `Files › Music` (add it together with the song, or drop it in the folder), then for lyrics inside the song's tags.

For other songs Music can ask LRCLIB (lrclib.net, a free and open lyrics library). It asks you once; it sends only the title, artist, album and length of the song you open lyrics for. Lyrics found online are kept on this device. Turn it on or off with `Look up lyrics online (LRCLIB)` in the settings.

## Sound
`Sound` in the full player (also in the settings) has:
- `Equalizer`: ten bands from 31 Hz to 16 kHz, with presets (Bass boost, Vocal, Rock, Late night and more) or your own curve. It works on songs on this device, songs saved offline and songs from WebDAV or Dropbox; songs that stream from other sites play without it, because their sites do not allow it.
- `Crossfade`: the end of a song fades into the next one, up to 12 seconds. Very short songs are not faded.
- `Gapless playback`: the next song is loaded before this one ends, so live albums and mixes run on without a pause. It is on unless you turn it off.
- `Same volume for every song (ReplayGain)`: uses the ReplayGain tags in your files so loud and quiet songs play at the same level. `Automatic` uses the album's level while you play an album. Songs without the tags keep their volume.

These settings stay on this device.

## Play on a speaker or TV
In the full player, the button with the screen-and-waves icon (`Play on…`) chooses where the music plays: another output of this device, a Bluetooth speaker paired in your system settings, Chromecast or AirPlay through the browser, or a smart TV or network speaker through orOS Bridge.
- **Smart TV or network speaker (DLNA):** with [orOS Bridge](https://useoros.online/download/) 0.10.0 or newer running on a Windows or Linux computer at home, the same window lists the smart TVs, Kodi boxes and network speakers on your network. The first time, `Connect to orOS Bridge` asks for the 6-digit code the bridge shows (Studio and Scanner & PDF use the same pairing). Choose a device and it plays; the window then has pause, volume and `Back to this device`. The device fetches the stream itself, so only http and https addresses go. `Search again` looks once more.
The device gets the song's own https address, so free music and songs from your own servers can go; songs on your orOS disk and offline copies play only on this device. While a TV or speaker plays, Music here stays quiet and works as its remote: next, previous or another song is sent to it.

## Share a song
`Share…` in a song's ⋯ menu, or the share button at the top of the full player, sends the song with [Send to someone](help:t/share): your phone's share menu (WhatsApp, Viber, Messenger and so on), a link, a QR code, or the `Send with` buttons.
- A song from free music (Internet Archive, Jamendo, ccMixter, Openverse) or a public link goes with its address, cover, licence and credit, so it plays on the page the other person opens, even without orOS. `Add to my orOS` there lets them play it, like it or add it to a playlist.
- A song from your own server goes with its name only: the server's address and your login never leave your device.
- A song on this device asks each time: send its name as a link, or the file itself through the share menu (where the device can send files, up to 100 MB). Send a file only if it is yours to give.
- `Share…` in an album's ⋯ menu (yours or from free music) or a playlist's ⋯ menu sends the whole list the same way: songs from free music play one after another on the page, songs of your own go with their names. `Add to my orOS` there offers `Play all` or `Save as my playlist`.
- `Show the cover in chats` puts the cover and title in the chat's preview (see [Send to someone](help:t/share)).

## Sleep timer
`Sleep timer` in the full player stops the music after 5 to 90 minutes, or at the end of the playing song. The last ten seconds fade out. The button shows how long is left; choose `Turn off the timer` to cancel it.

## Made for you
`Home › Made for you` has mixes made from what you play on this device: `Daily mix` (songs you love and more, new every day), `On repeat` (what you play most this month) and `Repeat rewind` (old favourites you have not played for a while). `Song radio` on a song's ⋯ menu plays songs like it: more by the same artist, the same genre, and artists you keep in the same playlists. `Save as playlist` keeps a mix.

`My year in music` shows your minutes of music, top songs, top artists and your months, from the plays on this device; in December it also appears in Today. Nothing about what you play leaves the device.

## Import from Spotify
`Import from Spotify` (under **+**) reads your own Spotify data. In Spotify, go to Account › Privacy settings › Download your data; Spotify sends a ZIP file by e-mail. Choose that ZIP (or the Playlist and YourLibrary .json files in it), or a CSV from Exportify or TuneMyMusic. Each Spotify playlist becomes a playlist here with the songs Music can play (songs on this device, on your servers, or already in your music), and your Spotify liked songs become likes. The songs that were not found are listed, and you can copy the list. `Look for the missing songs on Jamendo and your servers` sends each missing song's title and artist there and adds only exact matches.

## Artists worldwide
Turn on `Artist pages online (MusicBrainz, Wikipedia)` in the settings (or `Turn on` under `Artists worldwide` in Search) and Search also finds any artist in the world. An artist's page has a photo, a short bio from Wikipedia in your language when there is one, the genres, the members (or the bands a person played in) and the whole discography from MusicBrainz: albums, singles and EPs, live albums and compilations, with covers from the Cover Art Archive.

**What plays.** orOS plays only music that is free to play: your own files, your servers, and the free catalogues (Internet Archive with its Live Music Archive, Jamendo, Openverse with Wikimedia Commons, ccMixter). `Free to play here` on the page lists the songs of that artist found there; an album's page plays the songs found there and greys out the rest. A greyed song, and the `Listen in full` links on the page, open the artist's official site, Bandcamp or the store where it can be heard in full. There are no 30-second previews and no YouTube links.

`Follow` keeps an artist under `Artists you follow` on Home and in Your library › Artists, on all your devices. `Artist page` on a song's ⋯ menu, or on an artist of your library, opens that artist's worldwide page.

What is sent: the words you search for, the artist's MusicBrainz and Wikidata ids and the Wikipedia title; nothing about your music or what you play. Answers are kept on this device for up to 30 days.

**Discover.** With the switch on, Home also has `New from artists you follow`, `Top artists this week` and `Fresh releases` (ListenBrainz, open data of the MetaBrainz Foundation). An artist's page adds `Fans also like` and a button for radio stations of the artist's genre in Radio. `Artist radio` (the antenna button) plays a shuffled queue of free songs by this artist and by up to six artists their fans also like. Ask the Assistant to play an artist you follow and it starts that artist radio.

## ListenBrainz
`ListenBrainz account…` in the settings connects your own free ListenBrainz account: paste your user token from listenbrainz.org › Settings. The token stays on this device, locked, and goes only to ListenBrainz; each device is connected on its own. With `Send what I listen to` on, a song counts after half of it or four minutes, and its title, artist, album, length and source go to your ListenBrainz profile; songs heard offline are sent later. `From your ListenBrainz` on Home shows the playlists ListenBrainz makes for you (such as Weekly Jams) and your own: songs that are on this device play, `Save the songs that play here as a playlist` keeps them, and the others show where they can be heard in full. `Disconnect this device` deletes the token here.

## Covers and other apps
`Find missing covers online` in the settings, or `Find cover online` on an album's ⋯ button, asks MusicBrainz and the Cover Art Archive for covers of albums that have none; only the album and artist names are sent. An album's ⋯ button also has `Add to Media Shelf`. `Use in Montage` on a song opens it in Montage: a song of this device as it is, a free song whose licence allows changes saved first to `Files › Music › Free music`, with its credit copied for your video.

## orOS Music on its own
Music can also be installed as its own app, orOS Music, with its own icon and window: Settings › Single apps, or on Android the APK `oros-music.apk` (also in the orOS F-Droid repository). It uses the same songs, likes and playlists as Music in orOS on the same device. Music keeps playing with the app in the background or the screen off, with the song and its buttons in the notification and on the lock screen. Long-press the icon for `Liked songs` or `Shuffle my music`. Songs open with orOS Music from a file manager (they are copied into `Files › Music` and play), and a link shared to it plays a song at once, or opens Import for a playlist file (M3U, PLS, XSPF) or Add a music feed for any other address; nothing is added before you tap.

## Keys
Space plays or pauses, ← and → move 10 seconds, Shift+← and Shift+→ go to the previous or next song, S turns shuffle on or off, R changes repeat and L likes the song.
