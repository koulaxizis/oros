# Case Converter
Change the case and the letters of any text: capitals, small letters, Title Case, Greek without accents, Greek in Latin letters, names for code and fancy Unicode letters. Greek is done right: ΤΣΑΪ, ΜΑΪΟΣ, capitals without accents and a final ς.

## Getting started
1. Type or paste your text in `Text`. `Paste` takes what you copied; `Clear` empties the box (`Undo` brings it back).
2. Pick a conversion. The `Result` changes as you type.
3. Press `Copy`, or `Use as text` to convert the result again (for example `Remove accents`, then `UPPERCASE`).
Under the text you see its characters, words and lines. When you have the Text Tools app, `Text Tools` next to the result opens the result there.

## The conversions
- `Basic`: `UPPERCASE`, `lowercase`, `Sentence case` (a capital at the start of each sentence), `Title Case` (small words such as "of", "the", "και", "του" stay small), `Capitalize Each Word`, `tOGGLE cASE` (fixes text typed with Caps Lock on) and `aLtErNaTiNg`.
- `Greek`: `Remove accents` (also diaereses and the accents of other languages), `Greek → Latin (ELOT 743)` as on Greek passports (Θεσσαλονίκη → Thessaloniki), `Greeklish` as in messages, and `Fix mixed letters`, which finds Latin letters hiding in Greek words (an English A in ΑΘΗΝΑ) and the other way round.
- `Code`: camelCase, PascalCase, snake_case, kebab-case, CONSTANT_CASE, dot.case, path/case and Train-Case. Each line becomes one name. Greek is written in Latin letters unless you tick `Keep Greek letters`.
- `Styles`: bold, italic, monospace, double-struck, small caps, fullwidth, circled, strikethrough and underline letters for posts. `Plain text` turns them back.

## Language and selection
- `Language` is `Automatic` at first: the app looks at the letters. Choose `Türkçe` for the Turkish i and İ, or `Deutsch`, when the text needs it.
- Tick `Selection only` and select part of the text: only that part changes; the rest of the result stays as it is.

## My words
The button at the top right opens `My words`: words that always keep their capitals in `Sentence case`, `Title Case` and `Capitalize Each Word`, such as iPhone, NASA or ΔΕΗ. Type a word and press `Add`; the × next to a word removes it (`Undo` brings it back). A few common words are there at first.

## Sync and backup
- `My words` follows you to all your devices through orOS sync, encrypted like the rest of your data, and is part of the orOS export.
- Your text is never synced. It stays on this device, so it is still there the next time you open the app; `Clear` removes it. Very long texts (over 100,000 characters) are kept only while the app is open.

## Keyboard shortcuts
- [[Ctrl+Enter]]: copy the result.
- [[←]] and [[→]] on the tabs or the conversions: move between them.
- [[Esc]]: close `My words`.

## Tips
> Writing a title in Greek capitals? Use `UPPERCASE`: the accents go away by themselves and the diaereses stay where they belong.

## Limits
- Small letters cannot get back the accents of a text written in capitals (ΚΑΛΗΜΕΡΑ → καλημερα).
- Greeklish to Greek is not available: the same Latin letters can be several Greek ones.
- Many screen readers read the `Styles` letters badly; keep them for short titles. Letters a style has no form for, such as Greek in monospace, stay as they are.
