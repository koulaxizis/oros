# Screen Capture
Take a picture of your whole screen, one window or a browser tab, crop it, and keep it in Files › Pictures, or record it as a video in Files › Videos. Works on a computer with Chrome, Edge or Firefox; phones and tablets do not let a web page capture the screen.

## Take a screenshot
- Press `Choose what to capture`. Your browser asks what to share: the whole screen, a window or a tab, including the orOS tab itself.
- The live view shows what you share. Press `Take screenshot`.
- With a delay (3, 5 or 10 seconds) you have time to open a menu or switch to another window before the picture is taken.
- While you share, you can take as many pictures as you like. `Stop sharing` ends it, as does the browser's own stop button.

## Crop, save and copy
- Drag on the picture to keep only part of it. `Whole picture` brings back all of it.
- `Save to Pictures` saves a PNG in Files › Pictures, named with the date and time.
- With `Open in Photo Lab after saving` switched on, the picture opens in [Photo Lab](app:photolab) to draw on it or add text.
- `Copy` puts the picture on the clipboard, to paste it in another app.
- `Take another` goes back to the live view; `Discard` drops the picture.

## Record a video
- Choose `Recording` at the top, then `Choose what to capture`.
- `Record the sound of the screen or tab` adds what you hear. In Chrome and Edge, tick "Share audio" in the browser's question; a tab or the whole screen can share sound, a single window cannot.
- `Record the microphone` adds your voice. Both sounds are mixed into one track.
- Press `Start recording` (after the delay, if you chose one). `Pause` and `Resume` skip a part; `Stop recording` ends it. You can take screenshots while you record.
- Watch the video, then `Save to Videos`. It is saved as MP4 where the browser can write it, otherwise as WebM.
- With `Open in Montage after saving` switched on, the video opens in [Montage](app:montage) to cut it or add music and titles.
- A recording stays in memory until you save it, and stops by itself at 1 GB. Closing the app before saving loses it.

## Privacy
- Nothing leaves this device. orOS only sees what you choose to share, and only while you share it.
- With orOS sync switched on in [Settings](app:settings), your Pictures and Videos folders follow you to your other devices, encrypted, like any other file.
