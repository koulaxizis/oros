# Photo Lab
Edit photos and pictures with layers, selections, brushes and adjustments, in the spirit of Photoshop, GIMP and Photopea. Everything works offline.

## Getting started
- `New picture` starts an empty picture: pick a size and a background. `Open picture` opens a photo from your device (or a Photoshop or OpenRaster file with its layers), and `Paste` opens a picture you copied. You can also drop a picture file anywhere on the app.
- Your pictures are under `Projects`. Tap or click one to open it; the pencil renames it and the bin deletes it (`Undo` in the message brings it back).
- In the editor, the button at the top left goes back to `Projects`. Your work is saved as you go.

## Tools
The tool bar is on the left (at the bottom on a phone). The bar above the picture shows the options of the current tool.
- `Move`: drag the layer. With a selection, it lifts and moves the selected pixels.
- `Marquee selection` and `Lasso`: select an area. Press the tool again to switch between `Rectangle` and `Ellipse`, or between a freehand and a point-by-point lasso. Hold Shift to add to the selection, Alt to subtract.
- `Magic wand`: selects similar colours; set the `Tolerance` and whether only `Contiguous` pixels count.
- `Brush` and `Eraser`: `Size`, `Hardness`, `Opacity` and `Flow`. A pen paints wider when you press harder.
- `Paint bucket` and `Gradient` (`Linear` or `Radial`) fill with the foreground colour.
- `Eyedropper`: click picks the foreground colour, Alt+click the background colour.
- `Text` and `Shape` add their own layers; click a text to change it.
- `Crop`: drag the frame, choose a `Ratio`, then `Crop` or [[Enter]].
- `Hand` and `Zoom` move around. Two fingers pinch to zoom on touch screens.

## Layers
`Show or hide panels` (the layers button at the top right) opens `Layers` and `History`.
- Each layer has an eye (show or hide), a lock and a name (tap it to choose the layer). `Blend` and `Opacity` apply to the chosen layer.
- The buttons below add, duplicate, move, merge down and delete layers. The `Layer` menu has more: `Layer via copy`, `Rasterize layer`, `Flatten image` and more.
- `History` lists your steps; tap one to go back to it.

## Menus
- `Image`: `Image size…`, `Canvas size…`, rotate, flip, `Crop to selection`, `Trim transparent edges`.
- `Select`: `Select all`, `Invert selection`, `Feather…`, `Grow…`, `Shrink…`.
- `Adjust`: `Light and colour…`, `Looks…`, `Hue / saturation…`, `Levels…`, `Invert`, `Black and white`, `Threshold…`, `Posterize…`. Dialogs show a live preview; with a selection, only the selected area changes.
- `File`: `Export…` saves a PNG, JPG, WebP, PSD or OpenRaster file to your device, `Save to Pictures…` saves it in the Pictures folder of the orOS disk, and `Use as wallpaper` makes it the wallpaper of this device.
On a phone all menus are under the menu button.

## Photoshop and OpenRaster files
- Photoshop files (`.psd`) and OpenRaster files (`.ora`, from GIMP, Krita and MyPaint) open with their layers: names, positions, opacity, blend modes and visibility.
- Groups are flattened into the layer list (a hidden group hides its layers) and layer masks are applied to the layers' pixels. Text, smart objects and effects come in as the pixels the file saved for them.
- `Export…` with `PSD (Photoshop, with layers)` or `OpenRaster (GIMP, Krita, with layers)` keeps your layers for Photoshop, Photopea, GIMP or Krita. Text and shapes go as pixels.

## Sync and backup
- The list of projects follows you through [orOS sync](help:t/sync). The pictures themselves are kept on the orOS disk, in the `Photo Lab` folder, and Vault Drive syncs them file by file, encrypted.
- If the same picture changed on two devices before they synced, nothing is lost: the other device's version is kept as its own project.
- Undo history, tool settings and colours stay on this device.

## Keyboard shortcuts
- [[Ctrl+Z]]: undo. [[Ctrl+Shift+Z]] or [[Ctrl+Y]]: redo.
- [[Alt+N]]: new picture. [[Ctrl+O]]: open picture. [[Ctrl+Shift+S]]: export.
- [[Ctrl+C]], [[Ctrl+X]], [[Ctrl+V]]: copy, cut, paste. [[Delete]]: clear the selection.
- [[Ctrl+A]]: select all. [[Ctrl+D]]: deselect. [[Ctrl+Shift+I]]: invert selection.
- [[Alt+T]]: free transform. [[Alt+Shift+N]]: new layer. [[Ctrl+J]]: layer via copy. [[Ctrl+E]]: merge down.
- [[Ctrl+U]]: hue / saturation. [[Ctrl+L]]: levels. [[Ctrl+I]]: invert.
- [[Ctrl+=]], [[Ctrl+-]], [[Ctrl+0]], [[Ctrl+1]]: zoom in, zoom out, fit, 100%.
- Tools: [[V]] move, [[M]] marquee, [[L]] lasso, [[W]] magic wand, [[C]] crop, [[B]] brush, [[E]] eraser, [[G]] paint bucket ([[Shift+G]] gradient), [[I]] eyedropper, [[T]] text, [[U]] shape, [[H]] hand, [[Z]] zoom.
- [[X]] swaps the colours, [[D]] resets them, `[` and `]` change the brush size. Hold [[Space]] to move around.

## Tips
> Want to edit only part of a photo? Select it first: brushes, fills and adjustments stay inside the selection.

## Limits
- Pictures up to 8192 px a side on computers, and 4096 px (about 16 megapixels) on phones. Bigger pictures are made smaller when they open.
- Up to 100 layers a picture. Text and shape layers move and scale but do not rotate; rasterize them first.
- Old undo steps are dropped when they would use too much memory.
- Photoshop files: RGB, Grayscale and CMYK at 8 or 16 bits (CMYK colours are converted simply, without a colour profile). Large documents (PSB) and other colour modes do not open.
