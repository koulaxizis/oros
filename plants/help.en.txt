# Plant Care
Plant Care keeps a watering schedule for each of your plants and reminds you when they're thirsty. It can also track fertilizing, misting and repotting.

## Getting started
Tap or click `Add a plant` (or the `Plant` button at the top). Choose a `Species` from the list of ready plants to fill in sensible intervals and a tip, or pick `Other / my own`. Give it a `Name`, an `Icon`, a `Room or spot` and say `Where` it lives, `Indoor` or `Outdoor`. Set `Water every (days)` and, if you like, `In winter every (days, 0 = same)`. Under `More care` you'll find fertilizing, misting and repotting. For a new plant you can enter when it was `Last watered`.

## Today
`Today` lists what's `Late`, due `Today` and in the `Next 7 days`.
- `Done` marks the task as done today, with `Undo` for a few seconds.
- `Water all (n)` waters everything that's due in one go.
- The three-dots button opens more choices: `Postpone 1 day`, `Postpone 2 days`, `Skip this time`, `Mark as done` and `Open plant`.

When you have plants in more than one room, room chips at the top filter both tabs.

## My plants
`My plants` shows a card for each plant with its next watering. With six plants or more, `Search plants…` appears.

Tap a plant to see its details: the schedule with the next day for each task, how often you really water on average, a `Last 30 days` strip and the `History`. You can remove a history entry, `Edit` the plant or `Delete` it (with `Undo`).

## Weather hints
If you use [Weather](app:weather), Plant Care reads its latest forecast on this device for your outdoor plants. When rain is expected today you'll see a banner with `Skip outdoor watering`; on a very hot day it suggests checking them earlier. Plant Care makes no network calls of its own.

## Share your plants with your household
With a [household](app:household) set up, the gear › `Household` › `Share with a household` moves your plants into the household: everyone in it sees the same plants in their own Plant Care, and `Done` on one phone moves the next watering for everyone, so nobody waters twice. In a plant's editor, `Reminder for` picks who gets its daily reminder: everyone in the household, or one person (their initials show on the plant's tasks). The reminder hour and hemisphere stay on each device. Everything is end-to-end encrypted: the orOS relay only passes on what it cannot read.

Only the last six months of history travel with the plants (plus the last time each task was done, which the schedule needs). When there is more than one set of plants (your own and a household's), chips at the top switch between them. `Stop sharing` takes the plants out of the household for everyone: you keep your own copy under `Only mine`, and the others can bring them back from the Household app for 30 days.

## Save and open an orOS file
In `Settings`, under `Your plants`, `orOS file (.orosplants)` saves every plant (schedule, room, notes) and its care history as one `.orosplants` file, for example to move them to another device without sync or to keep a copy. The reminder hour, hemisphere and your Pl@ntNet key stay on each device. The file is a plain ZIP: unzipped it holds the plants as `content.json` plus `preview/plants.md`, `preview/plants.csv` and `preview/history.csv` for other programs. It is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere safe.

`Open orOS file…` (or `Unzipped orOS folder…`, or "Open with" orOS on the computer) shows how many plants and history entries are new and how many are already here, then `Add` adds only the new ones, with their own ids: opening the same file again adds nothing. A plant already here is kept as it is, and one you deleted here stays deleted; a file never deletes anything. Tick `Update with newer versions` to let plants that were changed later in the file replace your copy. `Undo` in the message takes back what was added.

## Sync and backup
Your plants (with their scientific names), schedules and history sync between your devices through [orOS sync](help:t/sync). The reminder hour, hemisphere, chosen room and open tab stay on each device.

In `Settings`, `Export` saves your plants to a file and `Import` merges a file with what you have. An orOS file (see above) moves plants and history between devices.

## Keyboard shortcuts
- [[Left]] / [[Right]] on the tabs switch between `Today` and `My plants`.

## Tips
- In `Settings`, choose the `Daily reminder` hour, or `Off`. One notification a day lists the plants that need care. See [Notifications](help:t/notifications).
- Set your `Hemisphere (for the winter interval)` so winter watering starts in the right months.
- Watering and due days also appear in [Calendar](app:calendar) under `Plants`.

## Limits
- Reminders appear while orOS is open, even with this app closed. With orOS closed they wait until you open it again.
- Weather hints only work when the Weather app has a fresh forecast on this device.
- History older than two years is removed.
