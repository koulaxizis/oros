# Banner
Banner is a turn-based card battle on a small board of 5 by 7 tiles. Each side plays cards from its deck: a card puts a unit on the board, changes a tile, or casts a spell. The hand is the card game, and where your units stand is the board game.

## How to win
Each side has a Keep with 15 Health in the middle of its home row: Blue at the bottom, Red at the top. You win when you break the other Keep.

There are three shrines, the flags on the middle row. You also win when you hold all three at the start of three of your turns in a row. You hold a shrine while one of your units stands on it.

From a side's 15th turn, its own Keep loses 1 Health at the start of its turn, then 2, then 3 and so on. So every battle ends.

## A turn
1. You draw 2 cards (not on the first turn of the battle). You start with 4 cards, and you can hold 6. A card drawn into a full hand is lost. When your deck is empty, your discard pile is shuffled back into it.
2. You get energy: 1 on your first turn, one more each turn up to 6, and 1 more for each shrine you hold. Energy you do not use is gone at the end of the turn.
3. You play cards while you have the energy for them. The number at the top left of a card is its cost.
4. Each of your units may move once and attack once, in either order.
5. `End turn` (or [[E]]) passes the turn to the other side.

## Cards
- **Units** have Attack, Health and Move. A unit goes on your home row, or on a tile next to one of your units on your half of the board or the middle row. A new unit waits a turn before it can act, unless it has Charge.
- **Tiles** change the ground for good. They cannot go on a Keep, a shrine or the other side's home row, and a rock or water can never cut off the way between the Keeps.
- **Spells** act once and go to the discard pile.

Hold a card or a tile (or press [[I]] on the board) to read what it does.

## Fighting
Tap one of your units. The light tiles are where it can go, and the red ones are what it can hit. Tap one of them. A unit attacks a unit or the Keep next to it.

Damage is the attacker's Attack, +1 if it stands on a tower and +1 under Rally, then −1 if the target is in a wood and −1 if a friendly Guard stands next to the target. It is always at least 1. A unit that survives a hit from a unit next to it hits back. There are no dice: chance comes only from the shuffle and the board, and both come from the battle code.

Keywords:
- **Reach:** attacks 2 tiles away in a straight line, not past a rock.
- **Guard:** your units next to it take 1 less damage.
- **Charge:** can move and attack on the turn it is played.
- **Climb:** passes rocks and water.
- **Heal:** at the end of your turn, heals 1 to each of your units next to it.

## Tiles
- **Wood:** a unit in it takes 1 less damage.
- **Rock:** blocks the way (Climb passes).
- **Water and Pond:** only Climb passes. A **Bridge** on water makes it passable.
- **Tower:** +1 Attack for the unit on it.
- **Mire:** a unit that walks in stops, and cannot move on its next turn.
- **Spring:** heals 1 to the unit on it at the end of its turn.
- **Road:** +1 Move for a unit that starts its move on it.

## Spells
- **Spark:** 2 damage to an enemy unit.
- **Rally:** your units get +1 Attack this turn.
- **Recall:** one of your units goes back to your hand.
- **Mend:** heals one of your units by 3.
- **Haste:** one of your units may move and attack again this turn.
- **Quake:** turns a tile back to plain ground.
- **Storm:** 1 damage to every enemy unit.
- **Fortify:** your Keep gets 3 Health back (at most 15).

## Decks
There are four ready-made decks of 20 cards, and every card is open from the start:
- **Iron:** guards, towers and tough units.
- **Thorn:** woods, healers and big beasts.
- **Tide:** fast units, water and haste.
- **Ember:** spells, charges and a drake.

`Random` lets the battle code choose a deck.

## A new battle
`New battle` (or [[N]]) asks who you play against:
- **Computer:** you are Blue. `Easy` plays the first card it can and walks toward your Keep, `Medium` weighs every play of its turn, and `Hard` also looks at your best answer.
- **Two players:** Blue and Red on one device. At the end of each turn the hand is hidden, and the next player taps `Show my hand` when they have the device.

Pick the decks, and type a battle code to play the same board and shuffle again, or leave it empty for a new battle. Each battle shows its code when it ends, with `Same battle again`. Starting a new battle while one is in progress counts that one as a loss.

## Daily battle
`Daily battle` (the calendar button) is the same battle for everyone each day: the same board, the same decks and the same shuffle, against the computer on Medium. You play Blue. It has one try: leaving it before the end, or starting another battle, counts as lost. A dot on the button means today's battle is still open.

When it ends you see whether you won, the turn it ended on, what was left of your Keep and your streak of won days. `Share` copies a line without spoilers: five squares for your Keep and the turn. The Daily battle also appears in the [Game Center](help:a/gamecenter) under Today's dailies. Daily battles do not count in the computer levels in `Records`; they have their own line there.

## March
`March` (the flag button) is eight battles in a row against the computer, each one stronger: three on Easy, three on Medium, then two on Hard. Pick a starting deck. After each win you choose 1 of 3 cards to add to your deck, or remove one card, or keep the deck as it is (a deck stays between 15 and 30 cards). One loss ends the march. Leaving a march battle before its end, or starting another battle, counts as a loss. A march carries on across battles on this device, and `Records` shows your marches and your best one.

## Undo and keys
`Undo` (or [[Z]]) takes back what you did in this turn, step by step, until you end it.

Keys: [[1]] to [[6]] pick a card from your hand. On the board, the arrow keys move the cursor, [[Enter]] acts on the tile under it, and [[I]] reads it. [[Esc]] lets go of the chosen card or unit.

## Records and sync
`Records` shows your battles and wins against each computer level, your two-player battles and your marches. `Last battles` lists your 10 most recent battles with their codes, and `Replay` plays one again (a March battle replays as a free battle with the starting decks). They sync between your devices through [orOS sync](help:t/sync), adding up the battles from every device, and they count in the [Game Center](help:a/gamecenter). The battle in progress and the sound setting stay on this device, and an unfinished battle continues where you left it. `Reset records` clears the records on every device.

Banner is an original game: its rules, cards and drawings are made for orOS.
