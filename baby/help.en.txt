# Baby
Baby is a log for your baby's feeds, sleep and nappies, with growth charts and milestones. It works for one or more children and syncs, so both parents can log from their own phones.

## Getting started
Tap or click `Add your baby`, type a `Name` and `Date of birth`, and optionally a `Sex (optional)` and a `Colour`. The child's name and age show at the top; tap it to switch child, `Edit this child` or `Add another child`.

The tabs at the bottom are `Today`, `History`, `Growth` and `Milestones`.

## Today
### Quick buttons
- `Breastfeed` starts a feed timer on the side you pick; the other side from last time is marked `suggested`. While it runs you can switch side, `Pause` and `Resume`, and `Done` when you finish.
- `Sleep` starts a sleep timer; tap `Wake up` when the baby wakes.
- `Bottle` (`Breast milk` or `Formula`, with amount), `Nappy` (`Wet`, `Dirty`, `Wet + dirty`), `Solids` and `Pump`.
- `More` has `Medicine`, `Temperature`, `Bath`, `Tummy time`, `Note`, `Milk stash`, and `Log a past sleep` or `Log a past feed` for things you forgot.

Cards show how long ago the last feed and nappy were, and how long the baby has been awake or asleep. Below come today's totals and the `Log`.

### Pumping and the milk stash
`Pump` logs a session: the amount, the side (`Left`, `Right` or `Both`) and, if you like, `Duration (min, optional)`. Tick `Add to the milk stash` and pick `Fridge` or `Freezer` to store that milk as a bag at once.

Once you pump or store milk, a `Milk stash` card on `Today` shows how many bags and how much milk are in the fridge and the freezer, which bag to use next, and any bag past its use-by date. Tap it (or `More` → `Milk stash`) to see every bag:
- The bag to use first is on top, marked `Use first`: fridge bags before freezer bags, then the earliest use-by date, so the oldest milk goes first.
- `Use` marks a bag as used, with `Log a bottle` to log it as a breast-milk bottle and `Undo`. `Thaw` moves a freezer bag to the fridge, to use within a day. A bag past its date offers `Throw away`.
- `Add a bag` adds one by hand. Tap a bag to change its amount, `Pumped` day, place or `Use by` date, or to delete it.

The use-by date is filled in from usual times: fridge 4 days, freezer 6 months (up to 12), thawed milk within 24 hours and never refrozen. You can change it. Used and thrown-away bags are kept under `Used and thrown away`.

### Edit or delete
Tap any entry to change its start, end or values, or to `Delete` it. After a delete, `Undo` appears for a few seconds.

## History and growth
`History` shows any day's totals and entries; use the arrows to move a day. `Last 7 days` has bars for `Sleep`, `Feeds` or `Nappies`, and `24-hour pattern` shows when the baby slept and fed.

In `Growth`, `Add measurement` records weight, length and head size (fill in any of the three). Each gets a chart by age in months. These are your own measurements only, not compared with growth standards.

## Milestones
Tick the ready milestones as they happen, or `Add` your own. You can also record `Vaccines` and `Doctor visits`.

## Reminders and Calendar
In `Settings`, `Reminders on this device` has two optional reminders, both off at first:
- `Feed reminder`: a notice 2 to 6 hours after the last feed.
- `Daily medicine or vitamin`: a notice at the same time every day, with an optional `Name`. Leave the time empty to turn it off.

Reminders come from orOS on this device only, and only while orOS is open; the Baby app itself can be closed. Each parent turns them on on their own device. See also [Notifications](help:t/notifications).

[Calendar](app:calendar) can show milestones, vaccines, doctor visits and your child's age each month (up to 2 years, then birthdays) under the `Baby` chip. Tapping one opens Baby.

## Import from another app
Bring your log over from another baby tracker. Pick the child at the top first, then in `Settings` press `Import from another app` and choose the file you exported. Baby recognises the app and shows what it found: how many feeds, sleeps, nappies and measurements are new, how many are already here, the dates they cover, and anything it leaves out. Press `Import`; a message tells you how many entries were added, and its `Undo` button takes the whole import back.

How to export from each app:
- **Huckleberry**: in the app's settings, look for exporting your data as CSV (on the website it's under your account). Feeds (breast with left and right times, or bottles with their amount), sleep, nappies, pumping, growth, medicine, solids, temperature, baths and tummy time come over.
- **Baby Buddy**: in the admin pages, open a list such as Feedings, Sleep, Diaper changes, Weight, Height, Head circumference, Temperature, Tummy time, Pumping or Notes, and use `Export` (CSV or JSON). Import each file in turn. A full JSON dump of the database works too. Baby Buddy keeps no units, so values are read in the units you chose in `Settings`.
- **Baby Tracker** (Nighp): in the app's settings, export your data as CSV. You get one file per activity (Nursing, Formula, Pumped, Pump, Sleep, Diaper, Growth, Medicine and more); pick the ZIP with all of them, or one file. If the file has more than one baby, choose which one goes to this child.

Entries the child already has at the same minute are not added twice, and importing the same file again adds only what's new. Your own entries are never changed. Kinds that Baby doesn't keep, like potty or play time, are listed and left out. The file is read on your device; nothing is uploaded anywhere except through your own sync.

## Save and open an orOS file
In `Settings`, under `Data`, `orOS file (.orosbaby)` saves your children with every entry, measurement, milestone, vaccine, doctor visit and milk-stash bag as one `.orosbaby` file, for example to move them to another device without sync or to keep a copy. Your units and reminders stay on each device, and a timer that is running right now is left out. The file is a plain ZIP: unzipped it holds the data as `content.json` plus `preview/log.csv` and `preview/growth.csv` (the same as the CSV exports) for other programs. It is not encrypted and holds your child's health details, so keep it somewhere safe.

`Open orOS file…` (or `Unzipped orOS folder…`, or "Open with" orOS on the computer) shows how many records are new and how many are already here, then `Add` adds only the new ones, with their own ids: opening the same file again adds nothing. A record already here is kept as it is, one you deleted here stays deleted, and a milestone already ticked here keeps its date; a file never deletes anything. Tick `Update with newer versions` to let records that were changed later in the file replace your copy. `Undo` in the message takes back what was added.

## Sync and backup
Children, every entry, growth, milestones, the milk stash and units sync between your devices through [orOS sync](help:t/sync). A running timer syncs too, so one parent can stop a timer the other started. Night view and the open tab stay on each device.

In `Settings` you'll find `Export log (CSV)`, `Export growth (CSV)`, `Back up (JSON)` and `Restore from backup`. A restore merges: it adds and updates, and never removes what's already there.

## Two parents, one baby
With a [household](app:household) set up, the gear › `Household` › `Share with a household` moves your children into the household, with their log of the last 90 days, growth, milestones and the milk stash: both parents (or a grandparent) log feeds, sleep and nappies on their own phone and see the same day, and a running feed or sleep timer started on one phone shows on the other. A record someone else logged shows their initials. Units and reminders stay each person's own, and a shared child reminds only once. Chips at the top switch between `Only mine` and the household's Baby. Everything is end-to-end encrypted: the orOS relay only passes on what it cannot read.

Older log entries stay on the device that shared them. `Stop sharing` takes the children out of the household for everyone and brings them back to your own Baby with everything logged; the others can bring them back from the Household app for 30 days.

## Keyboard shortcuts
- [[1]] to [[4]] switch tabs.
- [[Left]] / [[Right]] in `History` go to the previous or next day.
- [[Ctrl+Z]] does the same as `Undo` while it is showing.

## Tips
- The moon button turns on night view, a dim palette for the middle of the night.
- `Summary for the paediatrician` in `Settings` gives a text you can `Copy`, `Share` or `Save as text`.
- In `Settings`, choose units for weight (kg or lb), temperature (°C or °F) and volume (ml or fl oz).

## Limits
- Baby keeps a log; it's not medical advice. If you're worried about your baby, call your paediatrician.
- Reminders only show while orOS is open on that device.
- Milk storage times are general guidance, not rules for your milk or your freezer. When unsure, throw it away, and follow your paediatrician or lactation consultant.
- The CSV exports don't include the milk stash; the JSON backup does.
