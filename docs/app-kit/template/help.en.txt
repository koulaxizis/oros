# My App
A short list of things you want to keep. Replace this page with a guide to your own app: what it is for, how to start, and where its data goes.

## Getting started
- Write something in the box at the top and press `Add`.
- Tick an item when it is done. `Delete` removes it.

## Your data
- Everything stays on this device and is saved as you go.
- With orOS sync switched on in [Settings](app:settings), your list follows you to your other devices, encrypted.

## Find it from anywhere
- The menu's search box finds your items by their words. Choosing one opens the app on that item.
