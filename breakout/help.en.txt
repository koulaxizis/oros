# Breakout
Bounce the ball off your paddle and break every brick. Clear a level to move on to the next.

## How to play
Press `Start`, then launch the ball. It bounces off the walls, the bricks and your paddle. Where the ball meets the paddle sets the angle: near the middle it goes up almost straight, near the ends it flies off to the side. Every paddle hit makes the ball a little faster.

Some bricks take two or three hits; their colour shows how many are left. If the ball gets past your paddle you lose a life. You start with 3 lives, and the game ends when they're all gone.

## Controls
- **Mouse:** the paddle follows the pointer; click to launch.
- **Touch:** drag anywhere to move the paddle; tap to launch.
- **Keyboard:** [[←]] [[→]] or [[A]] [[D]] move the paddle, [[Space]] or [[↑]] launches.
- [[P]] or [[Esc]] (or `Pause (P)`) pauses and resumes; [[Space]] pauses too while the ball is flying.
- [[N]] starts a new game.

The game also pauses when you switch away from it. When you resume, it counts down 3-2-1 first.

## Levels and scoring
There are 8 levels, each with its own brick layout. After the eighth they start over, 15% faster each time round.

Each brick hit scores 10 points, and clearing a level adds 100 × the level number.

With `Power-ups` on (the default), broken bricks sometimes drop a capsule. Catch it with the paddle:
- `Wide paddle` for 15 seconds.
- `Slow ball` for 10 seconds.
- `Extra life`, up to 5 lives.

Choose `No power-ups` for the plain game. Each mode keeps its own records: `Records` shows the best score with its date, the best level reached and the games played.

## Sync
Your records sync between your devices through [orOS sync](help:t/sync). `Reset records` deletes them on every device after you confirm. The game in progress, the power-up choice and the sound setting stay on this device.

## Tips
- A game in progress is kept and reopens paused, so nothing happens until you're ready.
- Started a new game or switched mode by mistake? Tap `Undo` on the message that appears.
- Hit the ball with the edge of the paddle to send it into the corners.
