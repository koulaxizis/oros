# Today
Today gathers what your apps have for you today in one place: what is late, what is due today and what is coming up in the next three days. Every morning it can also leave one notice in the bell.

## Getting started
Open Today from the menu or with [[Ctrl+Alt+Shift+Y]]. At the top you see the date and short lines such as the weather, who celebrates a name day, a running timer, how much water you've drunk, today's meals from [Meal Planner](app:meals) or how many unread e-mails wait in [Mail](app:mail). Tap or click one to open its app.

Below come three groups:
- `Overdue`: tasks whose day has passed, with how many days late.
- `Today`: what is due today. Rows with a time come first, in time order.
- `Coming up`: the next three days, day by day.

Tap or click a group's title to fold it. Tap or click a row to open it in its app, on that event, task, card, quote, contact, plant, pet, car, trip, recurring entry or day. A [Health](app:health) reminder opens a new reading of that kind, a [Workouts](app:fitness) session opens the Train tab and a [Habits](app:habits) row opens this week's habits, where you tick it. `Refresh` (the round arrow) builds the summary again.

Tasks from [To-Do](app:todo) and [Plant Care](app:plants) also have a tick button, `Done (opens the app)`. It opens that app, which marks the task done itself: To-Do ticks it (untick it there to take it back) and Plant Care logs it, with `Undo` in its message.

## What each app adds
- [Health](app:health): today's reminder times that have no reading yet. A reading taken up to an hour early, or later, ticks its time.
- [Workouts](app:fitness): the program days planned for today, until you finish a workout of that day.
- [Habits](app:habits): habits planned for today and not ticked yet. Habits for any day of the week are not "due", so they stay out.
- [Budget](app:budget): recurring entries (rent, salary, a yearly bill) that fall today or in the next three days, with the amount.
- [Meal Planner](app:meals): today's plan as top lines, one per meal.
- [Cycle](app:cycle) (off until you switch it on): the expected start of your next period when it falls in the next three days, or a top line when it seems late (for up to 14 days). It is an estimate from your own history.
- [Mood](app:mood) (off until you switch it on): a check-in row while you haven't logged a mood today.
- [Minimalism](app:minimalism): a row while today's proposal is neither done nor skipped. Its text stays in the app.
- [Family Tree](app:familytree): death anniversaries of today and the next three days.
- [Moon & Sky](app:sky): main moon phases, equinoxes and solstices, meteor shower peaks and eclipses; today's as top lines.
- The screen pet's birthday comes with the Calendar, unless you turned off the pet's Calendar feed on this device.
- [Mail](app:mail): only the number of unread messages in each account's Inbox, never a sender or a subject. Mail rows have no Assistant button; inside Mail you can still ask about one message.

## The daily notice
On your first open of orOS each day after the start time (06:00 unless you change it), Today leaves one notice in the bell when something is late or due today. It only shows numbers, for example "2 overdue · 1 today", never the names of your tasks. Opening it brings you here.

The notice comes once a day, even with several devices: they all see the same one. When sync is on, it waits until your data has synced, so it never announces yesterday's state. On a day with nothing late or due, no notice comes.

## Ask the Assistant
When the [Assistant](app:assistant) is set up, rows have a speech-bubble button, `Ask the Assistant about this`. It opens the Assistant with a question about that row already typed. Nothing is sent until you press `Send` there. The button follows the Assistant's own permission for each app, so it never appears for an app the Assistant may not read.

## Settings
Open them with the gear button:
- `Daily summary` turns the daily notice on or off. The Today tab works either way.
- `From` is the start time, from 00:00 to 12:00.
- `Quiet the morning reminders the summary covers`: once the summary has come, the separate reminders of Plant Care, Chore Wheel, Pet Health Book, Garage, To-Do, Quote, Minimalism, name days and the weather briefing stay quiet. Reminders at a set time, such as alarms or medicine doses, still ring.
- `Apps in the summary`: choose which apps take part. Apps marked `sensitive` show only when you want them: Health, Baby and Budget are on, Cycle and Mood are off until you switch them on.

## Sync and backup
Your Today settings sync between your devices through [orOS sync](help:t/sync). Today stores nothing else: every row is read from the app it belongs to, at the moment you look. Which groups you folded stays in this tab only.

## On the Android home screen
In the orOS app for Android, long-press the home screen, choose `Widgets` and add `orOS Today`. It shows what is late, today's events and tasks and the next days, as Today does. Tap it to open Today. Its buttons add a task, a glass of water or a note. There is also a small `orOS quick add` widget with just the buttons.

The widget fills in while orOS is open, and keeps up on its own when orOS is closed: tasks whose day passed show as late, and events that are over leave. Open orOS now and then so it learns about new things.

## Keyboard shortcuts
- [[Ctrl+Alt+Shift+Y]] opens Today from anywhere in orOS.

## Tips
- To see your day without the notice, turn `Daily summary` off and open Today when you like.
- A notice from an earlier day still opens today's summary.

## Limits
- The notice can only come while orOS is open: if you don't open it, nothing arrives, and it comes on your next open.
- The Mail count is the one Mail saw on this device the last time it refreshed its folders, so the line says when that was. Open Mail to bring it up to date.
- Only To-Do and Plant Care tasks have the `Done` button; other tasks are done in their app.
- From Kanban, cards in each board's last column (usually "Done") are left out.
