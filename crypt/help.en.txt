# Crypt
Crypt is a turn-based dungeon crawler with a backpack puzzle. Go down ten floors of a crypt, fight what lives there and pack your finds well: what you carry, and how it fits together, is your whole hero.

## How to play
Every action is one turn: a step, an attack, opening a chest, using an item, or waiting. The monsters move only when you do.

You win when you take the key from the Warden on floor 10 and reach the stairs there, which are the way out.

Each awake monster shows what it will do next. A red cell with a cross is a cell it will hit, and a light arrow shows where it will step. An attack lands only if you are still on the marked cell when the monsters move, so you can step out of it, or hit first.

Damage is always the attacker's Attack minus the target's Defence, and at least 1. There are no dice in a fight. Chance decides only the floors, what you find and what you meet, and it all comes from the crypt code.

## Moving and fighting
- Tap a cell you have seen to walk there. The walk stops when a monster comes into view, when you are hurt, or when you find something.
- Swipe, or use the arrow keys or [[W]] [[A]] [[S]] [[D]], for one step.
- Tap a monster next to you to attack it. Tap a monster further away to read what it is and how it behaves.
- Step into a chest to open it. What it holds drops on its cell.
- `Wait a turn` (the hourglass, or [[Space]]) lets the monsters move.

Gold and items are picked up when you step on them. An item goes into the first free place in the backpack. If it does not fit, it stays on the floor until you make room.

Traps are hidden until they go off (3 damage) or until something shows them. You slowly get Health back as you walk (1 every 12 turns), and Focus too (1 every 10 turns).

## The backpack
`Backpack` (or [[I]]) opens a grid of 6 by 4 cells. It grows to 7 by 5 on floor 4 and to 8 by 5 on floor 7. Every item has a shape of 1 to 5 cells, and it works only while it is in the backpack.

- Tap an item to choose it, then tap a free cell to put it there, or drag it.
- `Rotate (R)`, or tapping the chosen item again, turns it.
- `Take out` puts it on the floor. `Pack it` puts a floor item in the first place it fits.
- `Use` uses a rune, a potion or a scroll.
- `Undo (Z)` takes back the last change.

Packing takes no turn, unless an awake monster can see you: then closing the backpack after a change takes one turn.

**Links:** the dots on an item's edges are its ports. When two items touch and their ports face each other, they link and the dot lights up. Each link gives a bonus:
- blade and blade: +1 Attack
- ward and ward: +1 Defence
- a rune with anything: +1 Focus
- charm and charm: 5 % more gold
- any other pair: +2 Health

**Curses:** some strong items are cursed (striped). A cursed item does not link, and it cannot leave the backpack until you read a `Cleansing scroll` or a shrine lifts the curse. A cursed item lying on the floor is not picked up by itself: put it in by hand if you want it.

## Items
- **Blades** add Attack. **Wards** add Defence and Health.
- **Runes** are skills that cost Focus:
  - `Bolt` hits the nearest monster in sight;
  - `Push` throws back the monsters next to you;
  - `Ward` stops the next hit;
  - `Blink` jumps away;
  - `Mend` heals;
  - `Storm` hits every monster in sight.
- **Charms** work all the time: more light, traps shown, more gold, more Focus or Health, healing over time.
- **Potions** and **scrolls** are used up: healing, Focus, lifting curses, showing the whole floor, escaping next to the stairs, fire all around you.

The quick slots at the bottom hold the first four runes, potions and scrolls in the backpack, read left to right, row by row. Tap one or press [[1]] to [[4]]. A rune's slot shows its Focus cost.

## Shrines
Floors 3, 6 and 9 each have a shrine (and, in Endless, every third floor past 10), a small stone altar near where you arrive. Step on it to trade your gold:
- `Heal to full`;
- `Lift every curse` in the backpack;
- buy one of three items (never cursed). What you buy goes into the backpack, or onto the floor if it does not fit.

The shrine takes no turn, but it stays silent while an awake monster can see you. Prices grow with the floor. Tap your cell to open it again.

## Monsters
Each kind has one habit:
- **Rat:** plain.
- **Rat swarm:** splits off a rat when you hit it.
- **Bat:** takes two steps a turn.
- **Skeleton:** plain, but sturdier.
- **Archer:** shoots along a row or a column and keeps its distance.
- **Slime:** acts every other turn.
- **Bell ghoul:** rings its bell when it wakes, and everyone near it wakes too.
- **Mimic:** looks like a chest until you are next to it.
- **Spider:** leaps next to you along a straight line.
- **Wisp:** its hit also drains 1 Focus.
- **Stone guard:** only moves while you are not in its row or column.
- **Knight:** plain and armoured.
- **Brute:** acts every other turn and hits very hard.
- **Warden:** on floor 10. It calls skeletons every 6 turns and holds the key.

Monsters sleep until they see you (closed eyes), and they get a little stronger every three floors.

## Modes and runs
`New run (N)` asks for the mode:
- `Gentle`: more Health, a potion to start, and a save point when you reach floors 4, 7 and 10. After a fall you can go back to the last save point.
- `Grim`: one life. A fall ends the run.
- `Endless`: one life, like Grim. With the Warden's key the stairs on floor 10 do not lead out: they go on down, floor after floor, as deep as you can. The monsters keep getting stronger every three floors. Your deepest floor is your score.

`New run` also asks for the hero:
- `Fighter`: the full backpack, a dagger and a buckler.
- `Mystic`: an L-shaped backpack (the 3 by 2 cells at its top right are missing), 3 more Focus, a dagger and a Bolt rune. Fewer cells, more spells.

Every crypt has a code, shown in the result window and in `Records`. Type a code in `New run` to go down the same crypt again: the same floors, chests and monsters. Leave it empty for a new crypt.

## Lore pages
Each of floors 1 to 10 hides one torn page, a pale sheet on the floor, away from where you arrive. Step on it to read it. The ten pages tell the crypt's story, and once found a page stays found: `Records` shows how many you have, and `Read` opens the journal with every page you found, on every device.

## Daily crypt
The calendar button (with a dot while today's crypt is not played yet) opens the Daily crypt: the same short crypt for everyone that day. It has five floors, Gentle Health and a potion to start, no save points and one life. Reach the stairs on floor 5 to get out; no key is needed. There is one try a day: leaving it for a new run counts as a fall where you were.

When it ends you see whether you got out, the turns, and your streak of days you got out. `Share` copies a line without spoilers: five squares for the floors and your turns. The Daily crypt also appears in the [Game Center](help:a/gamecenter) under Today's dailies. Daily runs do not count in the Gentle and Grim records; they have their own line in `Records`.

## Records and sync
`Records` shows your runs, wins and the deepest floor you reached in each mode, and the last 10 runs from all your devices: how each one ended and its code, with `Replay` to go down the same crypt again. They sync between your devices through [orOS sync](help:t/sync), adding up the runs from every device, and they count in the [Game Center](help:a/gamecenter). The run in progress, its save point and the sound setting stay on this device, and an unfinished run continues where you left it. `Reset records` clears the records on every device.

## Keyboard
- Arrow keys or [[W]] [[A]] [[S]] [[D]]: one step. [[Space]]: wait.
- [[I]]: backpack. In the backpack, [[R]] rotates and [[Z]] undoes.
- [[1]] to [[4]]: quick slots. [[N]]: new run. [[+]] and [[-]]: zoom. [[Esc]]: stop walking.

## Tips
- Read the marks before you step: a cell without red is safe for one more turn.
- Fight in corridors, where only one monster can reach you.
- A small blade that links can beat a big one that does not: watch the dots.
- Keep a potion in a quick slot, and leave room in the backpack for what the next chest holds.
