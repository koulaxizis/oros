# Assistant
Assistant is a chat with the AI assistant you choose: Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Mistral, OpenRouter, a service with a free plan (Groq, Cerebras, GitHub Models, Cohere, Hugging Face, SambaNova, Pollinations), a model that runs on your own computer, or the browser's own built-in model. It can search your orOS data to answer questions about your notes, tasks, events and more, but only what you allow. It can also prepare an entry for you, such as a task, an event or an expense, which you check and save yourself in that app. It can also change or delete a task, an event, an expense, a bookmark, a contact, a Kanban card and more that you already have, but only after you see the change and press Apply.

## Getting started
1. Open `Assistant` and tap or click `Set up the assistant`.
2. Under `Assistant`, choose who you want to talk to.
3. Paste your `API key` from that service and press `Save key`. `Get a key` opens the page where you create one.
4. Check the `Model`. `Load models` lists the models your key can use and also tells you that the key works (it runs by itself once a key is saved). Pick one from the list under the box, or type a model name yourself.
5. Press `Test the connection`. It sends one short message and tells you if the service answers here. If it says the service does not accept calls from a web browser, pick another one: that can't be fixed from orOS.
6. Press `Close`, type your question and press `Send`.

## Free services
These need only a free account and a free key, with daily limits. Nothing is charged unless you add a way to pay on their site:
- `Groq`, `Cerebras` and `SambaNova`: fast open models such as Llama and Qwen.
- `GitHub Models`: GPT, Llama, Mistral and more with any GitHub account. The key is a fine-grained personal access token with the `Models` permission set to read.
- `Cohere`: Command models. The free trial key is for personal use and testing only.
- `Hugging Face`: many open models through one free token.
- `Pollinations`: use a publishable key (it starts with `pk_`).
- `OpenRouter`: tick `Only free models`, then `Load models`: only the models whose name ends in `:free` are listed.

Every one of them can search your data, prepare entries and search the internet with orOS's own search. Photos and PDFs go to them as the text orOS reads on this device.

## Open-source models on your computer
Choose `Local / OpenAI-compatible`, then your program under `Server`: Ollama, LM Studio, llama.cpp, Jan, LocalAI, vLLM or KoboldCpp. orOS fills in its address and shows how to let this site call it (CORS), which every one of them needs once. `Other / my own address` takes any OpenAI-compatible server. A key is optional, except in Jan. Then press `Load models` and `Test the connection`. If the model can't use tools (many small ones can't; the error says so), tick `This model can't use tools`. Chrome may ask to let the site reach devices on your local network: allow it. Nothing leaves your network.

## A model inside the browser
`In-browser model (WebLLM)` runs an open model (Llama, Qwen, Phi, Mistral, Hermes and more) on this device's graphics card, with no key and no server. Pick a model and press `Download the model`: it comes once from huggingface.co (from about 0.9 GB to 6.3 GB, better over Wi-Fi) and stays on the device, so after that it works without internet and nothing you write leaves the device. `Remove from this device` frees the space. It needs WebGPU: recent Chrome or Edge on Windows, Linux, a Mac or Android. Bigger models answer better but need a stronger graphics card. Like the built-in browser model, it can't call tools, so orOS searches your data for it (see below).

`Built-in browser model` needs no key and no internet: recent Chrome on Windows, Linux or a Mac has a small model of its own. Settings say whether this device has it; `Download the model` fetches it once (about 2 GB). It can't call tools, so orOS searches your data for it (see below).

## Ask about your data
When a question needs your data, the assistant searches your apps, much like the menu search. Before anything is shared you see a card, `Share with …?`, listing every result it found:
- untick what it should not see, then press `Share`;
- `Don't share` keeps everything on this device; the assistant answers without it;
- `Always share from … without asking` stops the questions for those apps.

In the answer, titles in the accent colour are links: tap one to open that item in its app. A grey line above the answer shows what was searched and what you shared.

Each result also shows its labels (tags in Bookmarks and Media Shelf), and for a task whether it is done and its priority. So you can ask "which tasks have no label?" or "show my cards with the label Bug": the assistant then lists every such item of the apps that have labels (To-Do, Calendar, Kanban, Contacts, Notes, Bookmarks, Media Shelf), up to 200 per app. Labels are changed only to ones you already have (tags in Bookmarks are free words); new labels are made in the app.

When a short result is not enough, the assistant may ask for the whole item, for example a long note. For an app set to `Ask first` you see one more card with the beginning of the item and its size; press `Share` or `Don't share`.

Models that can't call tools (the built-in browser model, the in-browser model, and a local model marked `This model can't use tools`) can't search by themselves. For them orOS does it before your message goes: it takes a few words from your question, searches the apps you allow, shows the same `Share with …?` card for `Ask first` apps, and sends what you share along with the message. A grey line says what was searched. They can also do a `Briefing`, and read photos and PDFs as text. They can't prepare entries or change anything.

## Summaries
For totals and overviews, such as "how much did I spend on groceries this month?", "how many hours did I work in September?" or "what's on this week?", the assistant asks an app for a summary instead of searching item by item. orOS works it out on this device:
- Budget: income, expenses and each category of a month.
- Timesheet: hours and amounts of a month per project.
- To-Do: open, late and due-today tasks per list, and what is due next.
- CRM: "what is open with …?" for one partner: what they owe you and you owe them, stock on consignment, orders, hours not billed, royalties, contracts and the next follow-up. When several partners match, the assistant asks which one.
- Calendar, Habits, Plant Care and the other apps that appear in `Today`: what is late, today and in the next days.

For an app set to `Ask first` you see the summary on a card before it is shared; press `Share` or `Don't share`. Only those lines leave the device, not the entries themselves. Budget starts at `Never`, so allow it in `Settings` → `Your data` first.

## Add entries
Ask, for example, "remind me to call the plumber", "I spent 12.50 on lunch" or "make an invoice for ACME: 3 hours of design at 50". The assistant answers with a card that shows exactly what it would add, and two buttons:
- `Open in …` opens that app with the entry filled in. For To-Do, Calendar, Budget, Quote, Invoices and Bookmarks nothing is saved until you press Save or Add there; `Cancel` leaves no trace.
- `Dismiss` drops the card.

Notes and Spreadsheet make the new note or sheet when you press `Open in …`, and Slides asks you to pick a theme first; you can change or delete them there.

You can also ask for a contact («add Maria from ACME, 6971234567»), a CRM customer, a Kanban card, a habit, a finished workout, a Garage service or renewal reminder («KTEO for the Golf is due on 1 March»), a new plant, a pet's vaccine or vet visit, a book, film, series, album, podcast or game for Media Shelf, a place on the map, a shared expense in Split («I paid 64 for dinner in the Crete trip»), or a book you publish («new book Night Sea by Ann Writer, out on 15 March»: Publishing opens its New title window with the release date and the release plan). For a book you publish you can also ask for posts («three posts for Night Sea: a week before, on the day, a week after»: one card per post, each opening Publishing's post window filled in, which you save and later post yourself), for a consignment settlement («settle with Corner Books»: Stock opens that shop's settlement, where you count what is left), and how much a book sold in a month, in total and in each bookshop (from Stock, with your permission to share the summary). Each one opens that app's own form filled in, and nothing is saved until you save it there. A Kanban card waits in the column's "Add a card" box until you press Enter, and a place opens on the map, where you save it from its pin if it is the right one.

## Automations
For work you repeat in [Atelier](app:atelier), [Layout](app:layout) or [Photo Lab](app:photolab), describe it, for example "every photo 1080 px on the long side, my name as a watermark bottom right, saved as JPG" or "replace 2025 with 2026 in all my posters and download them as PNG". The assistant answers with a card that lists the steps in plain words:
- `Open in …` opens the app's Automations editor with these steps. Check them, press `Save`, then `Run` when you want it: nothing runs before that.
- `Save as file` saves the steps as a `.orosrecipe` file to send to someone (they open it with `Import` in the app's Automations).
- `Dismiss` drops the card.

The assistant can only use the steps the app's Automations offer (the same list as `Add a step`), never code. If you ask for something they cannot do, it says so.

## Newsletter
Build a [Newsletter](app:newsletter) issue by chat: "add the interview from https://…", "a short paragraph about our new release with a button to the shop". With a link, the assistant reads the page first (when it may search the internet) and writes a short summary in its own words, with the page's main picture beside it when it finds one. "A banner to https://…" with a picture address becomes a linked picture. "Add our latest release" asks Newsletter for the newest product of your connected shop; "3 books of other publishers" become product cards from the pages the assistant read. The card lists the blocks; `Open in Newsletter` lets you pick the draft they go into (or a new one) and adds each one in its section (top story, news, products, banners, closing note) before the footer. Nothing is added before that. "It's ready" or "send it" gives a `Ready to send?` card: `Open the check` opens Newsletter's check of the draft and, when nothing is missing, its send screen. The assistant never sends a newsletter; you press Send there.

## Book cover
Attach the front picture (and your logo, if you want it on the cover) and ask, for example: "set up the cover of «Title» by Author, 240 pages". The card lists the details and the pictures; `Open in Layout` saves the pictures to Files › Pictures › Covers and opens [Layout](app:layout)'s Book cover window filled in, where you check everything and press Create. Pictures you attached in an earlier session are not kept, so attach them again or pick them in Layout.

## New contacts and CRM customers
The assistant can add people and companies straight to [Contacts](app:contacts) and [CRM](app:crm), many at once: "add everyone from this list to my contacts", "make Ann Writer a CRM customer and link her to her contact". It looks them up first, then shows one card with every new record and a tick on each.
- `Add` saves the ticked ones. `Don't add` leaves everything as it is. Nothing is saved before you press it.
- Someone who looks like a record you already have (same e-mail, phone, tax number or name) is marked `Already there` and starts unticked; the assistant then updates that record instead, with a `Before` and `After` card.
- A CRM customer can be linked to a contact: the contact becomes one of the customer's `People` (shown live in CRM), and Contacts shows the customer on the contact's card. The link has its own card with `Link`; Contacts itself doesn't change. A missing contact is added first.
- `Undo` takes the new records (or the links) back until you leave the Assistant, as long as you haven't changed them in the app (a customer that got a deal or a note of a call stays).

## Tasks, events, expenses, notes, bookmarks and cards
The assistant can also add straight to [To-Do](app:todo), [Calendar](app:calendar), [Budget](app:budget), [Notes](app:notes), [Bookmarks](app:bookmarks) and [Kanban](app:kanban), one item or many at once: "put the 12 deadlines from this PDF in my calendar", "add these receipts to my expenses", "make a task for each step of this plan in the Rome trip list", "save these five links in Reading". You get the same card with a tick on each item.
- `Add` saves the ticked ones, in the app's own way, so they sync like anything you add there. Nothing is saved before you press it.
- An item that looks like one you already have (the same open task in that list, an event with the same title on that day, an entry with the same amount on that day, a note with the same title, a card with the same title on that board) is marked `Already there` and starts unticked. A link you already have is never added twice.
- Your own lists, notebooks, folders, boards, columns, labels and categories are used; the assistant doesn't invent new ones, except a To-Do list, which is made when no list has that name (the card says so). Without one, the item goes where the app puts new items: the open To-Do list, the first notebook, Unsorted, the first column of the first board, today for an expense.
- Events are one-off (a repeating event is set in Calendar); an expense is in your main currency.
- `Undo` takes them back until you leave the Assistant, as long as you haven't changed them in the app.

## Change what you already have
Ask, for example, "mark the plumber call as done", "move the dentist to Friday at 10:00", "the lunch was 14.50, not 12.50" or "delete the old bookmark about trains". The assistant first finds the item with a search (you see and share it as usual), then shows a card: what changes, `Before` and `After`, or what is deleted.
- `Apply` (or `Delete`) makes the change. `Don't change` leaves everything as it is. Nothing changes until you press it, even for apps set to `Always share`.
- After a change the card says what happened and has `Undo`. Undo works until you leave the Assistant; after that, change it back in the app itself.
- If the item changed in the meantime (for example on another device), nothing is changed and the card says so.
- The same change to several items (for example "give every event without a label the label Work") comes as one card, up to 200 items: on top what every item gets, then the first 10 items (`Show all` lists the rest); one `Apply` changes them all, and `Undo` takes them all back.
- Different changes to several items (for example the new details of ten people from a list) also come as one card, each item with its own `Before` and `After`. In Contacts every row has a tick: untick what should stay as it is.

What it can change:
- [To-Do](app:todo): mark a task done or not done, move its date, rename it, change its note, labels or priority, delete it.
- [Calendar](app:calendar): move an event (day, start, end), rename it, change its place, label or note, delete it. It can also list your events by label, or the ones without a label ("which events have no label?"). New labels are made in Calendar.
- [Budget](app:budget): change an entry's amount, date, category or note, delete it.
- [Bookmarks](app:bookmarks): rename a bookmark, change its note or tags, delete it.
- [Contacts](app:contacts): change a contact's name, nickname, organisation, job title, birthday, note or labels; add a phone number, e-mail, website or address (the old ones stay), or replace one that changed; add a line to the note; delete it. Attach a business card, a form, a list, a vCard or an e-mail signature and ask "update my contacts from this": the assistant looks everyone up first, proposes only what is new or different, and adds anyone who isn't there on one card (see New contacts and CRM customers).
- [CRM](app:crm): fill in a customer's VAT or tax number, tax office, job title, phone, e-mail, website or address, change its name or alias, add a line to its note (bank details or a bio go there). A phone, e-mail, website or address it already has is replaced only when you say it changed. Customers are deleted in CRM itself.
- [Kanban](app:kanban): move a card to another column ("move the invoice card to Done"), change its due date, title, note or labels, delete it. A move runs the column's own automations, and the card shows what they change.
- [Media Shelf](app:shelf): change a title's status (want to, in progress, done, dropped), name or note, delete it.
- [Habits](app:habits): tick or untick a yes/no habit for today, rename a habit.
- [Plant Care](app:plants): mark a plant watered today, rename it, change its notes.

Some changes stay in the app, and the assistant says so: ticking a repeating task (the app works out its next date), moving or deleting a repeating event (you choose this time or the whole series), recurring Budget entries, entries with receipts or typed in another currency, bookmarks with a saved copy, Kanban cards with files on the orOS disk or on an archived board, habits that count an amount, and deleting a habit or a plant (that removes its whole history). Only the result goes back to the AI service ("applied" or "not applied"), never what the item looked like before.

## Photos, PDFs and documents
Press the paper clip next to the message box to attach a photo, a PDF or a document, `From this device…` or `From the orOS disk…`. You can also paste a photo or drop it on the message box. Up to 30 files go with one message, each up to 30 MB.
- Ask, for example, "add this receipt to my expenses", "save this business card as a contact", "put this poster's concert in my calendar" or "summarise this contract in a note". The assistant reads the file and prepares the entry card, which you check and save as usual.
- Above the message box you see each file and its size, and where it goes. The file goes only to that service, only when you press `Send`. Large photos are made smaller first.
- Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini and OpenRouter read photos and PDFs themselves. Mistral reads photos itself.
- Each service takes only so much in one request (Claude about 22 MB of files, at most 100 PDF pages and 100 files; ChatGPT about 30 MB; Gemini about 13 MB; the others about 12 MB). What doesn't fit goes as the text orOS reads on this device, and its chip says `text only`. Newer files go first; older ones in the conversation are left out first.
- Every other case works too: orOS reads the file's text on this device first and sends only that text. PDFs go through PDF.js; a scanned PDF (no text inside) and the words in a photo go through Tesseract, in English and Greek. This is how Mistral reads PDFs, a local model reads PDFs and photos, and the built-in browser model reads both. The chip then says `text only`.
- The first time a photo is read this way, the text engine is downloaded once from orOS itself (about 7 MB). Nothing else is contacted, and the file never leaves the device.
- For a local model with vision (llava, gemma3, qwen2.5-vl and others) tick `This model can see photos` in the settings: photos then go as pictures.
- Documents are read on this device, always, and only their text goes to the service: Word (.docx, .doc), LibreOffice (.odt, .ods, .odp), Excel (.xlsx, .xls), PowerPoint (.pptx), RTF, e-books (.epub), web pages, vCards, e-mails (.eml), CSV, Markdown and plain text. Tables keep their columns, spreadsheets come sheet by sheet. Nothing in a document runs.
- Photos in other formats (BMP, AVIF, SVG, and HEIC or TIFF where the browser can open them) are redrawn as JPEG or PNG first.
- Files are not kept with the conversation and are never synced. After you leave the Assistant the conversation shows them as `not kept`, and they are not sent again. Files cost tokens like text: a page of a PDF or a photo is roughly as much as a page of writing.

## Search the internet
In `Settings` → `Internet`, `Let the assistant search the internet when an answer needs it` lets the assistant look things up on the web: opening hours, news, prices, a restaurant for your trip. It is off unless you switch it on, and it works with every AI service except the built-in browser model.
- `Search with` → `Automatic`: Claude uses Anthropic's own search; every other service uses Wikipedia.
- `Wikipedia`: free, with no key. Good for facts, places and people.
- `Tavily`: searches the whole web, news and opening hours too. Make a free key at tavily.com (1,000 searches a month at no cost) and save it under `Tavily key`.
- `My own SearXNG server`: open source, on your own computer or server. Enter its address; in its settings.yml add `json` to `search.formats` and let this site call it (CORS).
- `Test the search` tries the chosen source and says whether it works.
- The assistant decides when a question needs a search. The line above the message box says `Web search on`. Each answer shows what was searched (`Searched the web (…): …`) with the pages found, and the pages it read. The links open in a new tab.
- When a short result is not enough, the assistant reads the whole page: Wikipedia pages through Wikipedia, others through Jina Reader (r.jina.ai). It can open only a page that a search returned or that you wrote yourself.
- Anthropic's search costs $10 per 1,000 searches on top of the tokens; `Used this month` counts them. Wikipedia and SearXNG cost nothing; Tavily is free up to its monthly limit.
- Search words go to the source you chose. The assistant is told never to put your orOS data in them unless you ask for that exact search.

## Pictures (image AI)
`Settings` → `Pictures` chooses who makes pictures for Atelier's `Image from text`. The choice stays on this device.
- `Default: Pollinations`: free, with no key and no account. A free Pollinations key (enter.pollinations.ai) makes it faster.
- `Hugging Face`: a free account and an access token with "Make calls to Inference Providers" (small monthly credit). It is the same key as Hugging Face for text.
- `OpenAI gpt-image-1`: your own OpenAI key, paid per picture (the same key as ChatGPT). It can also do Magic edit and Magic expand.
- `Stability AI`: your own key from platform.stability.ai, paid with credits. It can also do Magic edit and Magic expand.
- `Stable Diffusion WebUI / Forge`: free and open source, on your own computer with a graphics card. Start it with `--api --cors-allow-origins=` followed by this site's address (Settings shows the exact line). Nothing leaves your network.
- Each choice shows its steps under `How to switch it on`. `Test` checks the key or the address. Keys are kept like the AI keys (sealed on this device, and synced only if you switch on key sync).
- Only your description (and, to edit, the picture) goes to the service, straight from this device. No Google service is offered.

## Briefing
On a new conversation, `Briefing` sums up your day in one answer: today's agenda, what is late, birthdays and other dates this week, and how this month's spending looks. [Today](app:today) has the same button (the sparkles at the top).
- It runs only when you press it, never by itself, so it never costs anything you didn't ask for.
- orOS works out each app's summary on this device first. Apps set to `Ask first` show on one card, with the lines that would go out: untick what should stay, then press `Share`. `Without these` sends only the apps set to `Always share`. Apps set to `Never` are not read at all, and apps with nothing for today or this week are left out.
- Under your message you see what was shared and what you held back. The answer links each item to its app, and may suggest up to 3 entries (for example a reminder to buy a present), which you save yourself as usual. A briefing never changes or deletes anything.
- It needs `Chat only` off. Every AI service can do it, also the models that run on this device (they get a shorter summary and suggest no entries).

## Ask from anywhere
- Type a question in the menu search: the last row, `Ask the Assistant: …`, puts it in the assistant's message box. Nothing is sent until you press `Send`.
- [[Ctrl+Alt+Shift+A]] opens the assistant from anywhere in orOS.
- In Writer, select some text and press the sparkle button: `Summarize`, `Rephrase`, `Fix spelling & grammar` or `Ask something else…` put the text and the request in the message box. Nothing is sent until you press `Send`.
- `Copy` under every answer copies it, ready to paste into your document.

## Desktop menu
Right-click an empty spot of the desktop (on a phone or tablet, press and hold) for a small menu: `Ask the Assistant…`, your tasks, your apps, `Change wallpaper…` and `Edit this menu…`.
- A task is a text for the assistant, such as `What do I have today?`. Tapping it opens the assistant with the text in the message box; nothing is sent until you press `Send`, unless you ticked `Send on tap` for that task. Apps set to `Ask first` still ask before anything of theirs goes out.
- In a task's text, `{date}` becomes today's date and `{clipboard}` the text you copied (read only when you tap the task).
- `Edit this menu…` (also in the assistant's Settings, under `Desktop menu`) adds tasks and apps, puts them in order and removes them. `Restore the examples` brings back the three examples; your own items stay.
- The menu syncs with your other devices. Tasks for the assistant hide on a device without it, apps hide where they are not installed.
- The menu's Assistant items open a small Assistant window over the desktop. [[Ctrl+Alt+Shift+Q]] opens the same window from anywhere, also over an open app. Its buttons start a new conversation, open the conversation in the full Assistant (with your list of conversations), or close the window. When the full Assistant is already open, everything goes there instead.

## Selected text in an app
Select some text in any orOS app, then right-click it for a menu: `Copy`, `Explain`, `Summarize`, `Translate`, `Fix spelling and grammar`, `Ask about it…` and your own tasks that use `{selection}`. On a phone or tablet, or in a text field, a small `Assistant` button shows under the text you selected; tap it for the same menu. With text selected, [[Ctrl+Alt+Shift+Q]] opens the menu too.
- Each choice puts the text and the request in the assistant's message box (the small window, or the full assistant if it is open). Nothing is sent until you press `Send`, unless your task says `Send on tap`.
- `Translate` translates into your orOS language, or into English when the text is already in it.
- In a task's text, `{selection}` becomes the selected text. Such tasks show only in this menu, not on the desktop. Up to 8000 characters are taken.
- [[Shift]]+right-click keeps the browser's own menu. Text fields keep their own right-click (for cut and paste); use the button there. Apps with their own right-click menu, such as Spreadsheet, keep it. Writer has its own sparkle button for this. Passwords and password fields are never offered.
- Don't want the button? Untick `Show an Assistant button under text I select` in `Edit this menu…` (per device).

## From outside orOS
- On a computer, right-click the orOS icon in the taskbar or dock (on Android, press and hold the orOS app icon): `Ask the Assistant` opens orOS with the small Assistant window.
- Share text or a link to orOS from another app (Android's `Share`): orOS asks where it should go, and `Assistant` is one of the choices. A link can also go to Bookmarks, Notes and the other apps as before.
- With the `Send to orOS` browser add-on (1.1 and later), select text on any web page and right-click it: `Ask the orOS Assistant about …` opens orOS with the text in the Assistant's box. The text travels after the `#` of the address, which browsers never send to a server.
- In the Android app: an Assistant button on the `orOS Today` and `orOS quick add` widgets, and an `orOS Assistant` tile you can add to Quick Settings (pull down the notifications, then the pencil).
- Each way only opens the Assistant with the text ready; nothing is sent until you press `Send`.

## Privacy: what leaves your device
- Your messages and the results you share go only to the service you chose, straight from your browser. No orOS server is in between. The line above the message box says where they go.
- In `Settings` → `Your data` you choose, per app, `Ask first`, `Always share` or `Never`. The three buttons under `All apps at once` set every app in one go; you can still change single apps after. Health, Mood, Cycle, Baby and Budget start at `Never`. Mail and Passwords are never offered.
- `Chat only` turns off every search: the assistant then sees only what you type.
- With `Built-in browser model` or `In-browser model (WebLLM)` nothing leaves the device at all (WebLLM downloads its model once).
- A photo or PDF goes out only when you attach it and press `Send`, and only to that service.
- Internet search is off unless you switch it on; then search words go to the source you chose in `Search with`.
- Your key is kept encrypted on this device and is sent only to that service. You pay the service for what you use, so set a spending limit on its site.

>! The answers come from the AI service, not from orOS. Check anything important before you rely on it.

## Sync and backup
The choice of assistant and model, the permissions per app and `Chat only` go to all your devices through [sync](help:t/sync). Conversations stay on this device unless you tick `Sync conversations to my other devices`. Each conversation is then also an encrypted file in Files (internal › Assistant), and Vault Drive carries it to your other devices; it needs sync connected and unlocked. A conversation you delete is then deleted everywhere. If two devices continue the same conversation at once, you keep both: the older one shows up as a second conversation with the same title. Turning the switch off stops it; the files stay in Files until you delete them.

Turn off `Keep conversations on this device` to keep none on this device, or use `Delete all conversations`.

Keys stay on each device unless you tick `Sync API keys to my other devices`. They then travel inside your encrypted sync, sealed with your sync passphrase: anyone who knows that passphrase can use them. Removing a key while this is on removes it on every device.

## Import conversations
Bring your history from another AI chat. In the Assistant's settings, under `This device`, press `Import conversations` and pick the file.
- ChatGPT: in ChatGPT open Settings › Data controls › Export data. You get an e-mail with a link to a `.zip`; pick that file (or the `conversations.json` inside it).
- Claude: on claude.ai open Settings › Privacy › Export data. The e-mail brings a `.zip` with `conversations.json`; pick either.
- You first see how many conversations and messages the file has, from which dates, and a list to choose from (`Select all`). Conversations that are already here are greyed out and never come in twice.
- Your messages and the answers come in as text, with their bold, lists and code. System messages, tool calls and the model's thinking are left out; pictures and files show as [image] and [file: name]. For ChatGPT, the version of each conversation you last saw is kept (not the edited-away branches).
- An imported conversation is marked with ChatGPT or Claude in the list and keeps its own date. You can read it, and go on with it using the assistant you have set up.
- `Undo` on the message removes what came in; a conversation you have continued meanwhile stays. The file is read on this device only.

## Keyboard shortcuts
- [[Enter]] sends, [[Shift+Enter]] starts a new line.
- [[Esc]] stops an answer that is still coming.
- [[Ctrl+Alt+Shift+A]] opens the assistant from anywhere in orOS.
- [[Ctrl+Alt+Shift+Q]] opens it in a small window over what you are doing; with text selected in an app, it opens the menu of selected text.

## Tips
- `New chat` starts a fresh conversation; older ones are in the list on the left, or behind the menu button on a phone.
- `Used this month` in Settings shows how many tokens this device has used per service.
- If a service says the key or the model is wrong, open Settings and press `Load models` to check both.

## Limits
- The assistant needs the internet, except a model on your own network, the in-browser model (after its download) or the built-in browser model.
- It can change or delete items only in the apps listed under `Change what you already have`, one at a time, and it can't send anything (no email).
- Undo of a change works only until you leave the Assistant.
- If an app is open in another browser tab while you change one of its items here, that tab may still show the old version until you reload it.
- A long answer is not cut off: when the service stops at its length limit, the assistant asks it to go on, up to 4 times, and you see one answer. Only a very long one (or the in-browser model, which has little room) can still end with "The answer was cut off"; then write `go on`.
- Some services may refuse calls from a browser: `Test the connection` tells you. A local server must allow this site; `Server` in the settings shows how for each program.
