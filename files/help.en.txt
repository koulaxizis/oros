# Files
Files is the orOS disk: folders and files kept in this browser. Import files, preview and edit text, search inside them, pack and unpack ZIP files, download copies and, with orOS sync on, find the same disk on your other devices.

## Getting started
Folders are on the left, the current folder's contents on the right. On a phone, the folder button at the top opens the tree. Use the path or the up arrow to go back.

- At the top of the tree, `Recent` lists the files you opened or changed lately, and `Favourites` the folders and files you pinned. `Trash` sits below your folders.
- Tap or click a row to select it. On a computer, double-click to open a folder or file. On a touch screen, tap once to select and tap the same row again to open it.
- The toolbar buttons are `Import files`, `New folder`, `New file`, `Rename`, `Delete` and `More actions`.
- The button at the right of the search box switches between `List view` and `Grid view`. The grid shows small pictures of images and of the first page of PDFs. Each device remembers its own choice.
- The main folder also shows `Recent files`, the last 10 files you opened or changed.

## Manage files and folders
- `New folder` and `New file` ask for a name. A name can't contain / \ : * ? " < > |.
- Select several items with Ctrl+click (Cmd+click on Mac), or a range with Shift+click.
- Right-click an item, or select it and tap `More actions`, for `Preview`, `Add to Favourites`, `Download`, `Move to…`, `Copy to…`, `Compress to ZIP`, `Extract here` (ZIP files), `Copy name`, `Copy path`, `Copy content` (text files only), `Rename` and `Delete`.
- `Convert to orOS` (in the menu and in the selection bar) appears when the selection holds documents or spreadsheets the [orOS Converter](app:converter) can turn into orOS files (.docx, .odt, .xlsx, .csv and more). It opens the Converter with those files; your files here stay as they are.
- On a computer you can also drag items onto a folder in the tree to move them there, or onto `Trash`.
- Tap the column headers `Name`, `Size` or `Modified` to sort; tap again to reverse.

### Select several items on a touch screen
- Tap the check box at the left of the column headers (`Select`), or hold your finger on a row for half a second.
- Each tap now ticks or unticks a row. The bar at the bottom shows how many are selected and offers `Select all`, `Add to Favourites` (one item), `Compress to ZIP`, `Move to…`, `Copy to…` and `Delete`.
- Tap `Done` (or press Esc) to leave Select mode.

### Favourites
- `Add to Favourites` pins a folder or file to the top of the tree. Tap the star next to it there, or choose `Remove from Favourites`, to unpin it.
- Favourites follow a rename or a move made in Files, and are synced to your other devices. A favourite that has not reached a device yet is shown faded.

### ZIP files
- `Compress to ZIP` packs the selected files and folders into one .zip next to them, named after the item (or "Archive.zip" for several). Text and other files are compressed where the browser can; photos, videos and other already compressed files are stored as they are.
- Open a .zip to see what is inside. `Extract` (or `Extract here` in the menu) unpacks it into a new folder next to it. A file never overwrites anything: names that are taken get " (1)".

## Trash
`Delete` moves items into the `Trash` instead of erasing them. You can get them back for 30 days.

- Open `Trash` in the tree. Each item shows the folder it came from and when it was deleted.
- Select items and choose `Restore` to put them back in their folder (the folder is made again if it is gone; a taken name gets " (1)"), or `Delete forever`.
- `Empty Trash` deletes everything in it at once. Items older than 30 days are deleted for good automatically.
- With orOS sync on, the Trash is the same on all your devices: a deleted file disappears from its folder there too and shows in their Trash, and restoring it on any device puts it back everywhere.

>! `Delete forever`, `Empty Trash` and Shift+Delete can't be undone. Other apps that delete their own files on the disk don't use the Trash.

## Import, preview and edit
- Tap `Import files` to pick one or more files, or drop files from your computer onto the list. If a file with the same name already exists, choose `Overwrite` or `Keep both`.
- Open a file to preview it. Images (PNG, JPG, GIF, WebP, BMP, ICO, AVIF) are shown as pictures. Markdown files are shown formatted, with a `Source` button for the raw text.
- Tap `Edit` to change a text file and `Save` to write it to the disk. Markdown files show a live preview next to the editor. Closing with unsaved changes warns you first; close again to discard them.

## From Google Drive and Google Photos
- `Import files` › `From Google Drive` or `From Google Photos`. Sign in to Google in the small window, then pick the files in Google's own picker (for Photos, press `Open Google Photos` first, pick, then `Done`).
- orOS sees only what you pick, reads it once and lets go of the sign-in right away; nothing stays connected. You see a list with sizes and the total, and what is left out, before anything is saved.
- The files go into the folder "Imported from Google Drive" or "Imported from Google Photos" and sync like any other file. `Undo` on the message removes exactly the files that came in.
- Google Docs, Sheets, Slides and Drawings arrive as .docx, .xlsx, .pptx and .png. Folders, Forms, Sites and My Maps are left out.
- Picking the same files again adds only what is missing: a file with the same name and size already in the folder is left out.
- From Google Drive works with orOS's own Google app. With your own app id (Contacts › From an account › Use my own app id), it appears only once you also add your own Google API key there. If Google answers 403, the Google Drive API and Google Picker API (or the Google Photos Picker API) are not turned on in that Google Cloud project yet.

## Search
- Type in `Search in this folder…`. It looks through the current folder and all its subfolders. Capital letters and Greek accents don't matter.
- Names are searched by default. Turn on `Contents` to also search inside text files (TXT, Markdown, CSV, JSON, HTML, code and more) and PDFs. A line shows how far the search has got, and each match inside a file shows a snippet with the words found.
- The first 100 results are shown; `Show more` adds the next 100.
- Tap a folder to open it, or a file to open its folder and preview it.

## Your Dropbox
Below `Files` in the folder tree, `Dropbox` shows your whole Dropbox, not only the orOS disk. It is optional and works on each device separately.

- Tap `Dropbox`, then `Connect Dropbox`, and allow access in the Dropbox window. orOS sync is not affected: it keeps using its own folder.
- Browse folders, search names with `Search in Dropbox…` and sort by `Name`, `Size` or `Modified`. Tap a file to preview it (text and images) and `Download` to save a copy.
- orOS sync's own folder shows a lock. Its contents are encrypted and only orOS sync reads them, so they are not listed.
- The account button at the top right shows your Dropbox name and `Disconnect Dropbox`.

### Copy or move into the orOS disk
The orOS disk is the encrypted place: with orOS sync on, every file on it is encrypted before it leaves your device.

- Tick the boxes next to files or folders (the box in the header selects everything shown), then choose `Copy to orOS disk` or `Move to orOS disk`. A file's preview has the same two buttons.
- Pick the disk folder, check the summary and tap `Start`. Folders come with everything inside them.
- A copy never replaces a file on the disk: if the name is taken, the new file gets " (1)".
- `Move` removes each Dropbox original only after its encrypted copy is confirmed in orOS sync. The originals go to Dropbox's deleted files, where you can restore them for 30 days. A file that changed in Dropbox during the move stays there.
- Moving needs orOS sync connected and unlocked. Copying works without it.
- `Stop` ends a transfer: files already copied stay on the disk and no original is removed. If a transfer is cut short (closed tab, no connection), Files offers to `Continue` it next time.

### Send from the orOS disk to Dropbox
- On the disk, select files or folders and choose `Send to Dropbox…` (right-click menu, or the selection bar).
- Pick the Dropbox folder and tap `Send here`, then `Start`. Files then shows that Dropbox folder.
- Dropbox gets ordinary, unencrypted copies, so you can share them. The files stay as they are in orOS.
- Nothing in Dropbox is overwritten: if the name is taken, the copy gets " (1)". orOS sync's own folder can't be chosen, and the Trash is never sent.

- To use a Dropbox app of your own instead, open `Use my own app key`: create a Dropbox app with Full Dropbox access, tick the permissions shown, add the redirect address shown and enter the app key.

> Files go straight between this browser and Dropbox. The connection is kept on this device only and is not synced.

## orOS Drive
- `orOS Drive` in the folder list shows the folders you mirror from your computers and phones with the orOS Drive app.
- They stay in the cloud until you open them, so this device does not fill up. A cloud mark next to a name means the file is not on this device yet.
- Opening a file downloads it, and from then on it stays up to date here. Opening it from another place in Files works the same way once it is downloaded.
- `Keep on this device` downloads the whole folder and keeps it. `Free up space` removes the downloaded copies of a folder from this device; nothing is deleted from your other devices or from the cloud. Files you changed here and haven't synced yet always stay.
- Deleting a downloaded file in Files deletes it everywhere, including on the computer it came from.

## Sync and backup
The disk lives in this browser. When [orOS sync](help:t/sync) is connected and unlocked, Vault Drive syncs it file by file, whether Files is open or not. Names and contents are encrypted before they leave your device.

- The pill in the status bar shows `Disk synced`, `Changes pending sync` or `Disk sync unavailable`.
- If the same file changed on two devices, both versions are kept. The other copy gets "(conflict" plus the date and time in its name.
- Favourites and the Trash are synced. Your sort order, list or grid view, the `Contents` switch, last folder, `Recent` and the storage figure stay on this device.
- orOS [backups](help:t/backup) include the whole disk, Trash included. For a single file, use `Download`.

## Keyboard shortcuts
- [[Enter]] opens the selected folder or file.
- [[Space]] adds or removes the focused row from the selection.
- [[Delete]] moves the selection to the Trash, after you confirm. Inside the Trash it deletes forever.
- [[Shift+Delete]] deletes the selection forever, after you confirm.
- [[Esc]] closes the preview, or leaves Select mode.

## Tips
- The toolbar shows how much space your files use, for example "120 KB used". Items in the Trash count too, until it is emptied.
- To move files on a phone, use `Move to…`. Dragging works only with a mouse.
- To send a whole folder somewhere, compress it to ZIP and download the .zip.

## Limits
- Text previews go up to 512 KB and images up to 10 MB. Bigger files can still be downloaded.
- Files that aren't UTF-8 text can't be edited here. Download them instead.
- Searching inside files reads text files up to 4 MB and PDFs up to 30 MB (the first 300 pages); bigger or unreadable files are skipped and counted. Scanned PDFs without a text layer can't be searched.
- A new ZIP holds up to 10,000 files and 300 MB. A ZIP opens here up to 300 MB, with up to 20,000 files, 1 GB in all and 80 MB per file once unpacked. Encrypted and ZIP64 archives aren't supported, empty folders inside a ZIP are not recreated, and file names are read as UTF-8 (very old Windows ZIPs may show odd names).
- Grid pictures are made on this device each time and are not stored. Only images and PDFs up to 20 MB get one.
- `Recent` keeps the last 30 files on this device.
- An empty folder moved to the Trash on one device reaches the others' Trash without being in their folders, because empty folders don't sync.
- Very large files are slow to sync, as each is encrypted in one piece.
- The Dropbox view needs a connection to the internet.
- One transfer, either way, takes up to 1000 files and 1 GB, and files up to 300 MB each (the disk encrypts each file whole in memory).
- One import from Google takes up to 100 files, 100 MB per file and 500 MB in all. Google exports Docs, Sheets and Slides only up to 10 MB.
