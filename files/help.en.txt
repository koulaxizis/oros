# Files
Files is the orOS disk: folders and files kept in this browser. Import files, preview and edit text, search inside them, pack and unpack ZIP files, download copies and, with orOS sync on, find the same disk on your other devices.

## Getting started
Folders are on the left, the current folder's contents on the right. On a phone, the folder button at the top opens the tree. Use the path or the up arrow to go back.

- At the top of the tree, `Recent` lists the files you opened or changed lately, and `Favourites` the folders and files you pinned. `Trash` sits below your folders.
- Tap or click a row to select it. On a computer, double-click to open a folder or file. On a touch screen, tap once to select and tap the same row again to open it.
- The toolbar buttons are `Import files`, `New folder`, `New file`, `Rename`, `Delete` and `More actions`.
- The button at the right of the search box switches between `List view` and `Grid view`. The grid shows small pictures of images and of the first page of PDFs. Each device remembers its own choice.
- The main folder also shows `Recent files`, the last 10 files you opened or changed.

## Manage files and folders
- `New folder` and `New file` ask for a name. A name can't contain / \ : * ? " < > |.
- Select several items with Ctrl+click (Cmd+click on Mac), or a range with Shift+click.
- Right-click an item, or select it and tap `More actions`, for `Preview`, `Add to Favourites`, `Download`, `Move to…`, `Copy to…`, `Compress to ZIP`, `Extract here` (ZIP files), `Copy name`, `Copy path`, `Copy content` (text files only), `Rename` and `Delete`.
- `Convert to orOS` (in the menu and in the selection bar) appears when the selection holds documents or spreadsheets the [orOS Converter](app:converter) can turn into orOS files (.docx, .odt, .xlsx, .csv and more). It opens the Converter with those files; your files here stay as they are.
- `Open in Scanner & PDF` (in the menu and in the selection bar) appears when the selection holds a PDF, or several pictures. It opens [Scanner & PDF](app:scanpdf) with their pages, to merge, split or arrange them, or to make one PDF from the pictures; your files here stay as they are.
- On a computer you can also drag items onto a folder in the tree to move them there, or onto `Trash`.
- Tap the column headers `Name`, `Size` or `Modified` to sort; tap again to reverse.

### Select several items on a touch screen
- Tap the check box at the left of the column headers (`Select`), or hold your finger on a row for half a second.
- Each tap now ticks or unticks a row. The bar at the bottom shows how many are selected and offers `Select all`, `Add to Favourites` (one item), `Compress to ZIP`, `Move to…`, `Copy to…` and `Delete`.
- Tap `Done` (or press Esc) to leave Select mode.

### Favourites
- `Add to Favourites` pins a folder or file to the top of the tree. Tap the star next to it there, or choose `Remove from Favourites`, to unpin it.
- Favourites follow a rename or a move made in Files, and are synced to your other devices. A favourite that has not reached a device yet is shown faded.

### Tags
- Give any file or folder of the disk coloured tags: right-click it (or select it and open `More actions`) and choose `Tags…`. Tick the tags it should have, or type a new name and press `Add`. With several items selected, a dash means only some of them have that tag; leave it so to keep it that way.
- Tagged items show small coloured dots after their name. `Tags` in the tree lists every tag with how many items have it; open one to see all of them, wherever they are.
- The pencil next to a tag (or a right-click on it) renames it, gives it one of twelve colours, or deletes it. Deleting a tag takes it off every item; the files themselves stay as they are.
- It is one list with `Documents`: a tag made here shows there, and a document's tags are the tags of its file.
- Tags follow a rename or a move made in Files, stay with an item in the `Trash` so a restore brings them back, and are synced to your other devices. A file moved on another device is found again by its content. In `Photos`, the photos show their tags, `Select` › `Tags…` tags many at once, and `⋯` › `Show a tag…` shows only the photos with one tag.
- The search in the orOS menu, the Assistant and orOS Gate find an item by its tag names too (names only; a private document's tags never show).

### Smart folders
- A smart folder is a saved search: it always shows the items that fit right now. Nothing is copied or moved, so a file can show in several smart folders and still have one place on the disk.
- Make one with the `+` next to `Smart folders` in the tree, or finish a search and choose `Save as smart folder…` under the results.
- It can ask for tags (any of them, or all together), kinds (pictures, videos, sound, documents, PDF, spreadsheets, text, archives, code, folders), when the item changed (last 7 or 30 days, last year, or between two dates), words in the name, a folder to look in, and a size in MB. Every condition you fill in must fit.
- Smart folders look at names, tags, dates and sizes, not inside files. They cover the disk of orOS, not `This computer` or Dropbox.
- The pencil next to a smart folder (or a right-click on it) changes it or deletes it; deleting it removes only the saved search. Smart folders are synced to your other devices.

### Remove hidden details
- `Remove hidden details` (in the menu) makes a clean copy, "name (clean).ext", next to each selected photo (JPEG, PNG, WebP), PDF, or Word, Excel, PowerPoint or OpenDocument file. The original stays as it is.
- It removes what the file says about who made it, where, when and with what: GPS location and camera details from photos; author, program, dates and earlier saved versions from PDFs; author, last editor, dates, company, template, custom properties and Word's editing-session codes from office files. The picture and the text stay byte for byte; a turned photo keeps only its orientation.
- Comments and tracked changes are not removed, because you can see and decide on them: the report says when a file still has them. Read the content itself too, see [Sensitive work and sources](help:t/sensitive).

### Files that delete themselves
- `Delete itself…` (in the menu, one file) sets a file to go in 1 hour, 1 day, 7 days or 30 days. An hourglass next to its name shows that it is set; `Keep it` takes it back. It follows the file when you move or rename it in Files, also into the Trash.
- When the time comes, the file is deleted for good on every device the first time orOS runs there, also while Files is closed. A copy you sent, downloaded or backed up before stays where it is. Encrypted pieces in your cloud are removed only by `Unused files in the cloud`.

### ZIP files
- `Compress to ZIP` packs the selected files and folders into one .zip next to them, named after the item (or "Archive.zip" for several). Text and other files are compressed where the browser can; photos, videos and other already compressed files are stored as they are.
- Open a .zip to see what is inside. `Extract` (or `Extract here` in the menu) unpacks it into a new folder next to it. A file never overwrites anything: names that are taken get " (1)".

## Trash
`Delete` moves items into the `Trash` instead of erasing them. You can get them back for 30 days.

- Open `Trash` in the tree. Each item shows the folder it came from and when it was deleted.
- Select items and choose `Restore` to put them back in their folder (the folder is made again if it is gone; a taken name gets " (1)"), or `Delete forever`.
- `Empty Trash` deletes everything in it at once. Items older than 30 days are deleted for good automatically.
- With orOS sync on, the Trash is the same on all your devices: a deleted file disappears from its folder there too and shows in their Trash, and restoring it on any device puts it back everywhere.

>! `Delete forever`, `Empty Trash` and Shift+Delete can't be undone. Other apps that delete their own files on the disk don't use the Trash.

## Duplicates
`Duplicates` in the folder tree finds files that are exactly the same, byte for byte, whatever their names, dates or folders. To look in one folder and its subfolders only, right-click it (or select it and use `⋯`) and choose `Find duplicates here`.

- It compares sizes first, then the contents (SHA-256). Files only in the cloud (orOS Drive) are compared with the fingerprint orOS sync already keeps, so nothing is downloaded. The Trash, hidden folders and empty files are left out.
- The groups that free the most space come first. In each group one copy stays (unticked, marked `Stays`): a favourite, a document, a tagged file or an album photo first, then one on this device, then the oldest. The others are ticked; untick any you want to keep. One copy of every file always stays.
- `Move to Trash` asks first. Right before each move, the copy that stays and the copy that goes are compared again byte by byte; a copy that changed in the meantime is left alone. Everything goes to the Trash, so you can restore it for 30 days.
- `Identical folders` come first: folders where every file is the same, byte for byte. The whole folder goes to the Trash at once, and its files are not listed again under `Identical files`.
- `Same picture or sound, other hidden details` lists JPEG, PNG and WebP pictures and MP3 files whose picture or sound is the same byte for byte while the hidden details differ (camera, date, place, tags). Nothing there is ticked, because the details differ: tick what you do not need. Files only in the cloud are not compared this way.
- `Photos that look alike…` opens the finder in Photos, for bursts and retakes that are not the same file.
- In `Dropbox` and in `This computer`, the duplicates button in the bar searches the folder you are in. The Dropbox lists a fingerprint for every file, so nothing is downloaded there. A section `Already on the orOS disk` lists files that the orOS disk holds too, byte for byte; nothing there is ticked, and the orOS disk's copy always stays. Removing in the Dropbox deletes the file there (Dropbox keeps deleted files for a while; restore them on dropbox.com), and only if it did not change since the search; on this computer files go to its own Trash.
- Tap a name to preview the file, or the folder button to see it in its folder. `Search again` runs a new search; a second search is quick, because the fingerprints are kept on this device.

## Import, preview and edit
- Tap `Import files` to pick one or more files, or drop files from your computer onto the list. If a file with the same name already exists, choose `Overwrite` or `Keep both`.
- Open a file to preview it. Images (PNG, JPG, GIF, WebP, BMP, ICO, AVIF) are shown as pictures. Markdown files are shown formatted, with a `Source` button for the raw text.
- Tap `Edit` to change a text file and `Save` to write it to the disk. Markdown files show a live preview next to the editor. Closing with unsaved changes warns you first; close again to discard them.

### Copy text from a picture
- In a picture's preview, and in the Photos viewer, press `Text` (the frame with a T; in Photos the round button at the bottom right). orOS reads the text in the picture on this device: the words found are marked on the picture.
- Drag from one word to another, or draw a box over the text, to select it; a tap selects a line, a second tap its paragraph. Then `Copy`. `Copy all` copies everything, `Text` shows the whole text in a box where you can correct it before copying, and `Send to…` hands it to Notes, To-Do, Writer, Mail and the other apps that take text. On a keyboard, Ctrl+A selects all the words, Ctrl+C copies, Esc closes.
- Above the buttons, what the text holds that another app can use shows as small buttons: a web address opens in a new tab, a day and time opens a new event in Calendar, an e-mail address a new message in Mail, a phone number calls it (where the device can), and `New contact` opens a new contact in Contacts filled from the text. Nothing is saved until you save it there.
- The first time, orOS asks before it downloads its text recognition engine (Tesseract, about 7 MB, Greek and English) from its own site; it is the same download as Documents and Scanner & PDF, so one yes is enough. After that it works offline. The picture and its text never leave your device.
- Printed text reads best. Handwriting, very small or decorative letters and text at a strong angle are often read wrongly; check the text before you use it.

## Open a file in another app
- A file the preview cannot show (a Word or orOS document, a spreadsheet, a song, a video, an e-book…) opens straight in the orOS app that reads it when you double-click it, or tap it twice on a touch screen. Text, pictures and ZIP files still open in the preview, unless you picked an app for that type in [Settings](app:settings) › `Default apps` (for example Pad for .txt): then they open there.
- The preview has `Open in …` (one app reads the file) or `Open with…` (several do: pick one, and tick `Always use this app` to skip the question next time). Right-click a file, or use `More actions`, for `Open with…` too.
- Songs play in [Music](app:music). A song outside the `Music` folder is copied there first, so Music keeps it.
- `Send to an app…` in the same menu sends the selected files (up to 10): as attachments of a new message in [Mail](app:mail), ticked on the Send tab of [Transfer](app:transfer), as pages in [Scanner & PDF](app:scanpdf) (PDFs and pictures), or one file to the app that reads it: a picture to Photo Lab, Layout, Atelier or the wallpaper of this device (it asks first), a book to Library, a song to Music.
- `Send photos to someone…` (right-click or long-press a picture or a folder) makes a photo album link from the selected pictures and the pictures right inside the selected folders, up to 200. The photos are made smaller and lose their hidden details (place, camera, time) before they leave; see [Send to someone](help:t/share).
- The app opens its own copy: the file on the disk does not change until you save it there.

## From Google Drive and Google Photos
- `Import files` › `From Google Drive` or `From Google Photos`. Sign in to Google in the small window, then pick the files in Google's own picker (for Photos, press `Open Google Photos` first, pick, then `Done`).
- orOS sees only what you pick, reads it once and lets go of the sign-in right away; nothing stays connected. You see a list with sizes and the total, and what is left out, before anything is saved.
- The files go into the folder "Imported from Google Drive" or "Imported from Google Photos" and sync like any other file. `Undo` on the message removes exactly the files that came in.
- Google Docs, Sheets, Slides and Drawings arrive as .docx, .xlsx, .pptx and .png. Folders, Forms, Sites and My Maps are left out.
- Picking the same files again adds only what is missing: a file with the same name and size already in the folder is left out.
- From Google Drive works with orOS's own Google app. With your own app id (Contacts › From an account › Use my own app id), it appears only once you also add your own Google API key there. If Google answers 403, the Google Drive API and Google Picker API (or the Google Photos Picker API) are not turned on in that Google Cloud project yet.

## Search
- Type in `Search in this folder…`. It looks through the current folder and all its subfolders. Capital letters and Greek accents don't matter.
- Names are searched by default. Turn on `Contents` to also search inside text files (TXT, Markdown, CSV, JSON, HTML, code and more) and PDFs. A line shows how far the search has got, and each match inside a file shows a snippet with the words found.
- The first 100 results are shown; `Show more` adds the next 100.
- Tap a folder to open it, or a file to open its folder and preview it.

## Your Dropbox
Below `Files` in the folder tree, `Dropbox` shows your whole Dropbox, not only the orOS disk. It is optional and works on each device separately.

- Tap `Dropbox`, then `Connect Dropbox`, and allow access in the Dropbox window. orOS sync is not affected: it keeps using its own folder.
- Browse folders, search names with `Search in Dropbox…` and sort by `Name`, `Size` or `Modified`. Tap a file to preview it (text and images) and `Download` to save a copy.
- orOS sync's own folder shows a lock. Its contents are encrypted and only orOS sync reads them, so they are not listed.
- The account button at the top right shows your Dropbox name and `Disconnect Dropbox`.

### Copy or move into the orOS disk
The orOS disk is the encrypted place: with orOS sync on, every file on it is encrypted before it leaves your device.

- Tick the boxes next to files or folders (the box in the header selects everything shown), then choose `Copy to orOS disk` or `Move to orOS disk`. A file's preview has the same two buttons.
- Pick the disk folder, check the summary and tap `Start`. Folders come with everything inside them.
- A copy never replaces a file on the disk: if the name is taken, the new file gets " (1)".
- `Move` removes each Dropbox original only after its encrypted copy is confirmed in orOS sync. The originals go to Dropbox's deleted files, where you can restore them for 30 days. A file that changed in Dropbox during the move stays there.
- Moving needs orOS sync connected and unlocked. Copying works without it.
- `Stop` ends a transfer: files already copied stay on the disk and no original is removed. If a transfer is cut short (closed tab, no connection), Files offers to `Continue` it next time.

### Send from the orOS disk to Dropbox
- On the disk, select files or folders and choose `Send to Dropbox…` (right-click menu, or the selection bar).
- Pick the Dropbox folder and tap `Send here`, then `Start`. Files then shows that Dropbox folder.
- Dropbox gets ordinary, unencrypted copies, so you can share them. The files stay as they are in orOS.
- Nothing in Dropbox is overwritten: if the name is taken, the copy gets " (1)". orOS sync's own folder can't be chosen, and the Trash is never sent.

- To use a Dropbox app of your own instead, open `Use my own app key`: create a Dropbox app with Full Dropbox access, tick the permissions shown, add the redirect address shown and enter the app key.

> Files go straight between this browser and Dropbox. The connection is kept on this device only and is not synced.

## Documents
`Documents` in the folder list is an archive of your papers: receipts, bills, contracts, warranties, certificates. Any file can be a document; it stays where it is in Files.
- Select one or more files, open the menu (right-click or `⋯`) and choose `Add to Documents`. Or open `Documents` and press `+` to add new files from your device: they go to the folder `Documents` › (this year).
- Tap a document to fill in its title, type, tags, who it is from, the document date, an amount, when it expires and how long to keep it. `Save` keeps the changes; `Open` shows the file and `Show in folder` takes you to it.
- Choosing a type suggests a `Keep until` date, for example five years for invoices and tax papers or until it expires for a warranty. It is only a suggestion, not legal advice: change it as you need, or tick `Keep for ever`.
- The chips at the top show `All`, `Expires soon` (within 30 days), `Can be thrown away` (past its keep-until date) and `No type`; the lists next to them filter by type, tag, sender and year. The search field finds words in titles, notes, types, tags and senders, and in the text of each document once it is read.
- `Text` in a document: `Read text` reads the words of a PDF, a picture or a text file. Scans and photos need the text recognition engine (English and Greek, about 7 MB): it is downloaded once, after you agree, and runs on this device, so the file never leaves it. `Show text` shows what was read, to copy it.
- `From the text:` suggests a type, the document date, an amount, an expiry date and a sender you already have. `Fill in` fills only the fields that are empty (and a date that was only the file's own time).
- `⋯` › `Read the text of all documents` reads the ones not read yet, one after another; the same item stops it. `Read the text of new documents` (on or off for this device) reads each new document by itself.
- In Scanner & PDF, saving `In Files` with `Also add it to Documents` ticked makes the PDF a document the next time Files runs.
- Reminders: orOS tells you 30 days and 7 days before a document expires, on the day it expires, and when its `Keep until` date has passed, from 09:00 on this device (`⋯` › `Reminders of dates` sets the hour or turns them off). The same dates show in [Today](app:today) and as `Documents` in [Calendar](app:calendar); a private document (medical, ID, or linked to Health, Cycle or Baby) shows only its type there. Nothing is ever deleted: you decide what to throw away. In [Automations](app:automations), the ready rule `Expiring document to To-Do` turns each reminder into a task.
- `Link to…` in a document links it to an entry of [Budget](app:budget), an invoice, a vehicle or renewal in [Garage](app:garage), a record in [Health](app:health) or a customer in [CRM](app:crm): pick the app, then the item (type to search). Tap a link to open that item; the item in turn lists its `Documents`, and a tap there brings you back here. A document linked to Health is private, like medical ones.
- The [Assistant](app:assistant) and orOS Gate (when you let Claude read Files) find your documents through search: they see each document's details (type, date, sender, tags, amount, dates, file) and the first 300 characters of its text, nothing more. Private documents (medical, IDs, or linked to Health, Cycle or Baby) never reach them.
- `⋯` › `Types, tags and senders` renames or deletes them and adds your own types with their own suggestion. A type can be private: its documents are never shown to the Assistant or to Claude. Medical and ID documents are private from the start.
- `Remove from Documents` forgets the details only; the file stays. Moving or renaming a file in Files keeps its document. If another app or device moved it, `⋯` › `Find moved files` finds it again by its content.
- `⋯` › `Export documents (.zip)` saves the documents shown, with their files, a `documents.json` and a `documents.csv` for a spreadsheet. `Import an archive (.zip)` brings such an archive back, on any device: the files go to `Documents` › `Imported` (date).
> Nothing in Documents deletes a file. The details sync encrypted with orOS sync, the files through the disk as always. The text read from each document is kept as a small hidden file on the disk, so your other devices can search it without reading it again.

## Photos
`Photos` in the folder list shows every photo and video on the disk by the day it was taken, newest first, like a phone's gallery. The files stay where they are.
- The date comes from the camera (the photo's details), else from the video, else from a name like `IMG_20261112_143015.jpg`, else from the file's time. `Details` in the viewer says which.
- Tap a photo to open it. Swipe or use the arrows to go to the next one, double-tap to zoom in, and play videos right there. The round `Text` button at the bottom right copies the text in the photo (see Copy text from a picture). The buttons at the top are `Edit in Photo Lab`, `Show in folder`, `Send…`, `Details` (camera, size, place) and `Move to Trash`.
- `−` and `+` make the pictures smaller or bigger. On a wide screen the years on the right jump to that year.
- `Select` (or a long press on a phone) picks several. `Send…` sends the photos with a link, as `Send photos to someone…` does in Files. `Move to Trash` asks first; they can be restored from `Trash` for 30 days.
- Photos that orOS Drive keeps only in the cloud (for example your phone's `Camera backup`) show a cloud mark. When one comes into view it is downloaded once for its small picture, then it goes back to the cloud, so the disk does not fill up.
- `⋯` › `Folders shown…` chooses which folders appear. Folders where other apps keep their own pictures (covers, receipts, designs) are left out at first; tick the ones you want, or add any other folder. Documents and the Trash never appear. `⋯` › `Look for new photos` checks again.
- `Albums` at the top lists your albums. An album lists photos without moving or copying them, so one photo can be in several albums. Select photos in `All` and press `Add to album…` (or use the album button in the viewer), or make one with `New album…`.
- In an album, `Select` › `Remove from album` takes photos out of it, and the star in the viewer makes a photo the album's cover. `⋯` has `Send the album…`, `Rename album…` and `Delete album`. The photos always stay on the disk.
- Albums sync to your other devices. A photo that sync carries is found again there even after it is moved or renamed; an album shows how many of its photos are not on this device yet.
- `Map` shows the photos that carry the place they were taken, as small pictures on the map; a number means several were taken close together. Tap one to see those photos, and `‹` goes back to the map. The map pictures come from OpenStreetMap, as in Maps; the places of your photos are never sent anywhere.
- `⋯` › `Find duplicates…` looks for the same file saved twice and for photos that look alike and were taken within a few minutes (bursts, retakes). In each group the best copy (most pixels) is left unticked and the others are ticked, so `Move to Trash` clears them in one go after asking; untick any you want to keep. Nothing is removed without you, and the Trash keeps them for 30 days.
> The small pictures and the dates are made on this device and stay on it. Only the albums sync (their names and which photos they hold); the place a photo was taken never leaves your device.

## orOS Drive
- `orOS Drive` in the folder list shows the folders you mirror from your computers and phones with the orOS Drive app.
- They stay in the cloud until you open them, so this device does not fill up. A cloud mark next to a name means the file is not on this device yet.
- Opening a file downloads it, and from then on it stays up to date here. Opening it from another place in Files works the same way once it is downloaded.
- `Keep on this device` downloads the whole folder and keeps it. `Free up space` removes the downloaded copies of a folder from this device; nothing is deleted from your other devices or from the cloud. Files you changed here and haven't synced yet always stay.
- `Keep on all my devices` does the same on every device where you use orOS with sync: the folder is downloaded and kept on each of them, and it becomes a full two-way mirror of the folder on your computer (what changes on the computer changes in orOS, and what you change in orOS reaches the computer). Turn it off and each device goes back to its own choice.
- To delete a folder or a file, tap the bin at the end of its row (or select the row and press Delete) and confirm. It is deleted everywhere, including in the cloud: copies on this device go to the `Trash`, and orOS Drive on your computers and phones moves its copies to its own trash. Files that are only in the cloud can't go to this Trash, so the dialog says how many leave orOS for good.
- Deleting a downloaded file elsewhere in Files deletes it everywhere too, including on the computer it came from. Deleting a Drive folder elsewhere in Files removes only the files this device holds, so use the bin in `orOS Drive` for whole folders.
- On a computer, deleting a file or folder inside a mirrored folder deletes it in orOS as well (more than 50 files at once wait for your OK in orOS Drive's settings). `Stop mirroring` in orOS Drive's settings deletes nothing.
- On Android, orOS Drive is an app of its own (from Koulaxizis Apps or the download page); the phone button in the orOS Android app opens it, or its download. It backs up folders of the phone the same way, in the background every 15 minutes (on Wi-Fi only, unless you change it), keeps files or folders offline (press and hold one), takes files from Share › orOS Drive, and can back up new camera photos and videos to `Drive › (phone name) › Camera backup` (upload only).
- With orOS Drive signed in and unlocked, the whole orOS disk also appears as «orOS» in the phone's Files app and in every app that opens or saves files. A file downloads when you open it and uploads when the app saves it; if it changed in orOS meanwhile, both versions are kept, and a notification tells you.

## This computer
On the orOS USB stick, on a computer where orOS is installed from it, in orOS Portable and in the orOS apps for Windows and Linux, `This computer` in the folder list shows the computer's own files: the home folder (Documents, Downloads, Pictures, Music, Videos, Desktop) and its disks. It does not appear in the browser or on macOS.
- orOS Portable asks once on each computer whether to show its files; the orOS apps for Windows and Linux show them from the start. Change it any time in [Settings › System](help:a/settings), `This computer`. The orOS Portable folder itself never shows.
- Tap a folder to open it and a file to preview it (pictures, video, sound and text). `⋯` on a row copies it to the orOS disk, renames it or moves it to the Trash.
- Under `Disks`, tap a USB stick or another disk to open it, and the eject button before you unplug a stick. Windows disks open read-only, so nothing on Windows can be damaged. USB sticks and other disks can be written to.
- When you plug in a USB stick, a notification says so; `Open` shows it here. Nothing opens by itself.
- The bin button at the top is the Trash of this computer, separate from the orOS Trash. `Put back` returns an item to its folder; `Delete for good` and `Empty the Trash` free the space.
- On Windows (orOS Portable and the orOS app), disks are the drive letters, such as `Windows (C:)` and `STICK (E:)`, and all of them can be written to. Windows' own folders (Windows, Program Files, ProgramData) and hidden files do not show. Deleting moves items to the Windows Recycle Bin, and the bin button at the top opens it in File Explorer, where you put things back or empty it. A USB stick has no Recycle Bin, so orOS does not delete there. Before you unplug a stick, use `Safely remove` in Windows' taskbar.
- To bring files into orOS, choose `Copy to the orOS disk…`. To take files out, select them on the orOS disk and choose `Send to This computer…` (right-click menu or the selection bar). Names that are taken get " (2)"; nothing is overwritten.
- Every orOS app opens and saves straight on this computer: `Open…` and `Save…` show this computer's folders and disks (Windows disks only for opening). Saving a file you opened from here replaces it in place; if it changed on the computer since you opened it, orOS offers to save your version as a copy and never overwrites the other one. Replacing any other file asks first.
- The menu of a file or folder also has `Compress to ZIP` (the .zip lands next to it), and a .zip has `Extract here` (into a new folder named after it). A read-only disk cannot take the result.
> Files on this computer are never synced. Only what you copy to the orOS disk joins orOS sync. On a stick without a persistence partition the home folder is emptied when the computer shuts down.

## Sync and backup
The disk lives in this browser. When [orOS sync](help:t/sync) is connected and unlocked, Vault Drive syncs it file by file, whether Files is open or not. Names and contents are encrypted before they leave your device.

- The pill in the status bar shows `Disk synced`, `Changes pending sync` or `Disk sync unavailable`.
- If the same file changed on two devices, both versions are kept. The other copy gets "(conflict" plus the date and time in its name.
- Favourites, Documents and the Trash are synced. Your sort order, list or grid view, the `Contents` switch, last folder, `Recent`, the storage figure and everything in `Photos` stay on this device.
- orOS [backups](help:t/backup) include the whole disk, Trash included. For a single file, use `Download`.

## Keyboard shortcuts
- [[Enter]] opens the selected folder or file.
- [[Space]] adds or removes the focused row from the selection.
- [[Delete]] moves the selection to the Trash, after you confirm. Inside the Trash it deletes forever.
- [[Shift+Delete]] deletes the selection forever, after you confirm.
- [[Esc]] closes the preview, or leaves Select mode.

## Tips
- The toolbar shows how much space your files use, for example "120 KB used". Items in the Trash count too, until it is emptied.
- To move files on a phone, use `Move to…`. Dragging works only with a mouse.
- To send a whole folder somewhere, compress it to ZIP and download the .zip.

## Limits
- Text previews go up to 512 KB and images up to 10 MB. Bigger files can still be downloaded.
- Files that aren't UTF-8 text can't be edited here. Download them instead.
- Searching inside files reads text files up to 4 MB and PDFs up to 30 MB (the first 300 pages); bigger or unreadable files are skipped and counted. Scanned PDFs without a text layer can't be searched.
- A new ZIP holds up to 10,000 files and 300 MB. A ZIP opens here up to 300 MB, with up to 20,000 files, 1 GB in all and 80 MB per file once unpacked. Encrypted and ZIP64 archives aren't supported, empty folders inside a ZIP are not recreated, and file names are read as UTF-8 (very old Windows ZIPs may show odd names).
- Grid pictures are made on this device each time and are not stored. Only images and PDFs up to 20 MB get one.
- Photos lists up to 50,000 files. HEIC photos from an iPhone show only a placeholder in most browsers, and RAW files only where the browser can read them. Small pictures take up to 300 MB on this device; the oldest unused ones make room.
- `Recent` keeps the last 30 files on this device.
- An empty folder moved to the Trash on one device reaches the others' Trash without being in their folders, because empty folders don't sync.
- Very large files are slow to sync, as each is encrypted in one piece.
- The Dropbox view needs a connection to the internet.
- One transfer, either way, takes up to 1000 files and 1 GB, and files up to 300 MB each (the disk encrypts each file whole in memory).
- One import from Google takes up to 100 files, 100 MB per file and 500 MB in all. Google exports Docs, Sheets and Slides only up to 10 MB.
