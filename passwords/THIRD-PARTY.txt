3. ../vendor/hash-wasm/argon2.umd.min.js: hash-wasm 4.12.0 (the Argon2
   build only), by Dani Biró, https://github.com/Daninet/hash-wasm
   License: MIT, full text in ../vendor/hash-wasm/LICENSE.
   Changes: none. Used for the vault's master password (Argon2id) and to
   open KeePass files that use Argon2d or Argon2id.

4. ../vendor/jsqr/jsQR.js (shared with the QR app): jsQR by Cosmo Wolfe,
   Apache-2.0. Loaded only to read a two-factor QR code when the browser
   has no BarcodeDetector.

The KeePass reader and writer (kdbx.js), ChaCha20, Salsa20 and the vault
crypto (pmcrypto.js) are orOS's own code.
