# Mahjong
Mahjong solitaire: take matching pairs of free tiles until the layout is empty. Every deal can be solved.

## How to play
Tap or click two matching tiles to take them away. A tile is free when no tile lies on top of it and its left or right side is open. If you pick a tile that isn't free, a message tells you why.

- Most tiles match only an identical tile.
- Any flower matches any other flower, and any season any other season.

`Tiles` shows how many tiles are left, and the status line tells you how many pairs you can take right now. When no free tiles match, `No moves left` offers to `Shuffle` the remaining tiles (the game stays solvable), `Undo` or start a `New game`.

## Controls
- Tap or click a tile to select it, then its match.
- Arrow keys move a cursor over the free tiles; [[Enter]] or [[Space]] takes the tile under it. [[Esc]] clears your selection.
- [[H]] or `Hint (H)` shows a pair you can take.
- [[U]], [[Backspace]] or `Undo (U)` puts the last pair back.
- [[S]] or `Shuffle (S)` shuffles the tiles left on the board.
- [[Z]] or `Zoom (Z)` makes the tiles bigger; the board then scrolls.
- [[N]] or `New game (N)` deals a new game. If you leave a game in progress, an `Undo` button appears for a moment to get it back.

The clock starts with your first move and runs only while the app is on screen. A game in progress is kept on this device for next time.

## Levels and scoring
Choose a layout: `Turtle` (144 tiles), `Pyramid` (82) or `Fortress` (70, the default on phones). Changing it deals a new game. On a phone the `Turtle` is bigger than the screen, so you move around inside it.

`Records` shows your best time and number of wins for each layout.

## Sync
Your records sync between your devices through [orOS sync](help:t/sync). `Reset records` in `Records` deletes them on every device, after you confirm. The layout, the zoom, the game in progress and the sound button stay on this device.

## Tips
- Free the long rows from the ends and clear the tall stacks early; tiles buried under others are the ones that block you later.
